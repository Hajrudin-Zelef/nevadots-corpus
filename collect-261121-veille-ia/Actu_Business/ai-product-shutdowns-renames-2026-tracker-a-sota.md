## Before you switch or migrate

Search by the product, company or type of change. Start with the affected surface: an app, an API, a hosted service and an open-source repository can have different outcomes. Confirm account access, billing, data export and integration endpoints separately.

For a rename, follow the official destination and verify your existing account before creating another. For an acquisition, look for a closing announcement and a product-continuity statement. For a shutdown, export data before the stated deadline and evaluate a replacement using a small, non-confidential workload.

## Shutdowns

Even products from the largest labs can close quickly. OpenAI discontinued the Sora web and app experience in April 2026 and its API in September 2026, and shut down the ChatGPT Atlas browser in August 2026 after announcing plans to combine it with the ChatGPT desktop app and Codex. Roo Code shut down its VS Code extension in May 2026 to focus on a cloud agent. Khoj shut down its hosted cloud on April 15, 2026; the open-source app still runs if you host it yourself. SurfSense retired its hosted web app in 2026: the cloud service is export-only until October 18, 2026, when stored data is deleted, and the free desktop app continues. Void deprecated its editor and stopped accepting contributions; its repository was archived on June 2, 2026. TensorZero, an open-source LLM gateway and optimization stack, is no longer maintained; its repository was archived on June 12, 2026, and the code stays available on GitHub. bloop, the company behind the Vibe Kanban coding-agent board, shut down on April 10, 2026; its hosted services ended after 30 days, and the open-source project continues under community maintenance. Refact announced on April 30, 2026 that its hosted Refact Cloud is being retired, with the final date to be published separately; the open-source, local-first coding assistant continues with your own model keys, maintained by the community. Flowise, which Workday bought in 2025, wound down operations: feature development stopped on July 29, 2026, the repository was archived in August and end of life was August 31; the Apache 2.0 code remains available to fork. Moondream announced on October 14 that Cloud inference and Lens will close on November 13, 2026, with refunds for unspent purchased credits; its models remain open.

If a tool you rely on closes, the cost is in migration: exported data, rewritten workflows and retrained habits. Prefer tools that let you export your data in standard formats.

## Renames, moves and pivots

Renames are common and often unannounced in search results. In 2026 Cognition renamed Windsurf to Devin Desktop, Mistral renamed Le Chat to Vibe, Freepik renamed itself Magnific, MGX became Atoms, the open-source notetaker Hyprnote became Char and then Anarlog, and Google's Vertex AI became Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Azure AI Foundry became Microsoft Foundry and Grammarly renamed its company Superhuman. Earlier, the app builder Create rebuilt itself as Anything in August 2025 and moved to anything.com that December. Open-source projects move too: goose moved from Block to the Agentic AI Foundation. Llama Stack became OGX in April 2026 to show it is no longer tied to Llama models, and the document-extraction library Kreuzberg was rebuilt and rebranded as Xberg, with v1.0.0 released in July 2026. Gitpod announced its rename to Ona in September 2025, reflecting its shift from an online IDE to a platform for software engineering agents.

Some products keep their name but change what they are. Hume AI, known for expressive voice models, now presents itself as human evaluation and data for voice AI, and its docs say the TTS and EVI APIs end on November 13, 2026, after which account data is deleted. When a product's homepage no longer describes what you signed up for, check whether the part you use is still supported.

For builders, the lesson is in our SEO basics guide: redirect every old URL permanently and keep the old name on your site for a while, because people and AI assistants keep using it.

## Free plans that changed

Free tiers are not permanent. Qwen Code's free Qwen OAuth tier ended on April 15, 2026, so users now need an API key or a paid coding plan. Check free-plan terms again before you build a workflow on them.
