- Work without a credit card.

## What Are the Best Free AI Chat Assistants?

ChatGPT is the best free AI chat assistant for most people, and Claude is the better free writer. The big chatbots ration different things: ChatGPT rations features, Claude rations sessions, and Gemini now rations models, so pick by what you’ll run out of first.

### 1. ChatGPT

OpenAI's chatbot is the best free all-rounder in 2026, mainly because everyday text chat on the free plan has no cap.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** Unlimited everyday text chats on OpenAI's Luna model, plus web search, data analysis, file uploads, image creation, voice, and deep research, each with its own limit.
- **Catch:** Ads may appear on the free plan in some countries, and OpenAI publishes no numbers for uploads, images, voice, or research.
- **Paid from:** ChatGPT Go (price varies by country, from $8/month in the US)
- **Best for:** Everyday chat, drafting, and quick questions

### 2. Claude

Anthropic's Claude writes the most natural prose of the free chatbots, and its free plan includes more than most people expect.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** Claude's Sonnet and Haiku models, web search, memory, connectors, file creation and code execution, Artifacts, voice mode, Claude Docs, Slides, and Design, and up to 5 Projects.
- **Catch:** Usage caps reset every 5 hours and weekly, with no published message count. Opus, Claude Code, and Research are paid.
- **Paid from:** $20/month (Pro), or $17/month billed annually
- **Best for:** Writing, editing, and careful reasoning

### 3. Gemini

Google's Gemini app is still a solid free assistant, but it gave up the most ground this year.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Google account
- **Free tier:** Auto model selection (most prompts go to Gemini Flash-Lite, harder ones to a stronger model), a 32K-token context window, Nano Banana image generation, Deep Research, and Gemini Live.
- **Catch:** Since October 2026, free users can't choose Flash or Pro. There's no video generation, Deep Research may be unavailable at peak times, and limits refresh every 5 hours up to an unpublished weekly cap.
- **Paid from:** Google AI Plus (price varies by country, from $4.99 in the US)
- **Best for:** People who live in Gmail, Docs, and Drive

### 4. DeepSeek

DeepSeek is the only major chatbot here with no paid chat plan at all, and its models are open-weight under the MIT license.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan (open-weight models) ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** The web chat and the iOS and Android apps, running DeepSeek's V4.1 Flash model since October 2026.
- **Catch:** According to DeepSeek's privacy policy, your data is processed and stored in China and used to train its models, with no opt-out described. Rate limits exist but aren't published.
- **Paid from:** No paid chat plan; the API is pay-per-use
- **Best for:** Free chat on non-sensitive topics

### 5. Mistral Vibe

Mistral renamed Le Chat to Mistral Vibe in May 2026, and the free plan now leans into agents and connectors.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** Limited messages and web searches, basic image generation and editing, 100+ connectors, limited coding sessions plus the Vibe CLI, and up to 5 scheduled tasks.
- **Catch:** Mistral uses your inputs for training by default until you opt out in settings, and it publishes no message limits.
- **Paid from:** $14.99/month (Pro), or $5.99/month for verified students
- **Best for:** A European alternative with lots of app connectors

Grammarly is the best free grammar checker for English, and LanguageTool wins as soon as you write in more than one language. Unlike a general chatbot, these tools check your text as you write.

### 6. Grammarly

Grammarly checks grammar, spelling, and clarity across web, desktop, and mobile, and adds a monthly allowance of AI rewrites.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** Grammar and spelling checks, a tone indicator, and 100 AI prompts per month.
- **Catch:** You can see your tone on the free plan but not adjust it; tone rewrites are Pro-only.
- **Paid from:** $12/month (Pro, billed annually)
- **Best for:** Polishing emails and reports in English

LanguageTool checks spelling, grammar, and style in 30+ languages, which makes it the stronger pick for multilingual writers.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** No
- **Free tier:** Spelling, basic grammar, and some style suggestions on texts up to 2,000 characters, plus 3 AI sentence rephrasings per day.
- **Catch:** The browser extensions are now Premium-only, so the free version works in LanguageTool's own editor. Advanced suggestions are also paid.
- **Paid from:** LanguageTool Premium
- **Best for:** Writing in German, French, Spanish, and other non-English languages

### 8. QuillBot

QuillBot is a paraphrasing tool first, useful when a sentence is correct but reads badly.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Optional
- **Free tier:** Paraphrasing up to 125 words in 2 modes, a humanizer up to 125 words, basic grammar fixes, summaries of up to 1,200 words, and a limited AI Detector.
- **Catch:** The 125-word cap means one paragraph at a time. Custom summary modes and longer inputs (up to 6,000 words) are Premium.
- **Paid from:** $8.33/month (Premium, billed annually)
- **Best for:** Rewording awkward sentences and condensing long reports

Lara Translate is the best free AI translator for volume, with 60,000 characters a month that also cover documents and audio. DeepL is the pick for short snippets you paste in. I’ve found both very high-quality, but their free plans ration very differently.

### 9. Lara Translate

Lara Translate is the most generous free translator here, because one monthly pool covers text, documents, images, audio, and its live interpreter.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** 60,000 characters per month, shared across every feature, with no daily or per-feature caps.
- **Catch:** When the pool runs out, translation pauses until it resets on your billing date.
- **Paid from:** $9.99/month (Pro, billed annually)
- **Best for:** Translating whole documents

### 10. DeepL

DeepL remains the reference for text translation quality, but its free plan is built for snippets instead of files.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Optional
- **Free tier:** Up to 1,500 characters per translation without an account, or 5,000 with a free account, plus 1 file every 30 days (DOCX, PPTX, PDF, or RTF up to 5 MB; PNG or JPG up to 3 MB).
- **Catch:** Translated files carry a "Translated by DeepL" watermark diagonally across every page.
- **Paid from:** €7.49/month (DeepL Pro Individual, billed annually)
- **Best for:** Quick, high-quality translations of text you paste in

### 11. Google Translate

Google Translate covers nearly 250 languages and over 60,000 language pairs, far more than any other tool here.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** No
- **Free tier:** Text, document, and camera translation, live translation through headphones, and pronunciation practice on Android (US and India, for now).
- **Catch:** It offers none of Lara's style or context controls.
- **Paid from:** Free for consumers
- **Best for:** Rare languages and translating signs or menus with your camera

### 12. Doclingo

Doclingo specializes in translating PDFs without breaking tables, charts, or two-column layouts.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** No
- **Free tier:** Document translation for files up to 50 MB and 200 pages, with no login needed.
- **Catch:** Free jobs are queued, guests appear limited to 1 translation, and OCR on scanned files shows only a partial preview until you pay.
- **Paid from:** Doclingo membership
- **Best for:** Academic papers and reports with complex layouts

Gemini Notebook is the best free AI research tool for your own sources, while Perplexity and Consensus cover the web and published papers. Unlike chatbots, all three ground answers in sources you can check.

### 13. Perplexity

Perplexity answers questions with a written summary that cites the web pages it read.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Optional
- **Free tier:** Practically unlimited basic searches, 3 Pro Searches per day, 1 research query per month, and basic file uploads.
- **Catch:** Perplexity picks the model for you, and image generation is paid.
- **Paid from:** $20/month (Pro), or $10/month for verified students and educators (Education Pro)
- **Best for:** Quick, cited overviews of a topic or tool

### 14. Gemini Notebook

Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) answers only from the sources you upload, which keeps it grounded in your material.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Google account
- **Free tier:** 100 notebooks with 50 sources each, plus Audio Overviews (podcast-style discussions of your sources), study guides, and flashcards.
- **Catch:** Usage is compute-based, refreshing every 5 hours up to a weekly cap, so Google publishes no fixed number of Audio Overviews. Watermark removal is paid.
- **Paid from:** Google AI Plus (price varies by country, from $4.99/month in the US)
- **Best for:** Studying papers, documentation, or course notes

We cover Gemini Notebook step by step in our Practical AI with Google Gemini and NotebookLM course.

### 15. Consensus

Consensus searches over 200 million academic papers and summarizes what the research says.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** Unlimited searches, unlimited research quality indicators, and 10 Pro Analyses per month.
- **Catch:** The deeper analyses run out after 10 a month.
- **Paid from:** $11.99/month (Premium), or $8.99/month billed annually
- **Best for:** Checking whether a claim holds up in the literature

### 16. Humata

Humata lets you ask questions about long PDFs and answers from the document itself.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** 60 pages per month.
- **Catch:** The free plan has no OCR, so scanned documents won't work.
- **Paid from:** $9.99/month (Expert, 500 pages)
- **Best for:** Occasional questions about a long, text-based PDF

### 17. ChatPDF

ChatPDF is the lightweight option: upload a PDF and chat with it.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Optional
- **Free tier:** 2 documents per day.
- **Catch:** ChatPDF publishes no limits on questions or pages for the free plan.
- **Paid from:** ChatPDF Plus
- **Best for:** A quick look at a single paper or report

### 18. Google Antigravity

Antigravity is Google's agent-first coding platform: you describe a task, and its agents plan, write, run, and test the code. It replaced Gemini CLI for free users in June 2026, so older guides pointing you to Gemini CLI's free tier are out of date.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Google account
- **Free tier:** The Antigravity app, CLI, IDE extensions, and Python SDK, with parallel background agents, subagents, browser use, and scheduled tasks, on a baseline quota that refreshes weekly.
- **Catch:** The free plan runs non-flagship Gemini models only, and Google publishes no quota numbers. Quota scales with the work an agent does, so small prompts barely dent it and big refactors drain it; type`/usage` in the CLI to see what's left.
- **Paid from:** Google AI Pro (price varies by country, from $19.99/month in the US)
- **Best for:** Building a working app without setting up an environment

### 19. GitHub Copilot

Copilot is the autocomplete most engineering teams use, and the free plan is enough to learn it properly.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** GitHub account
- **Free tier:** 2,000 inline code completions per month, an allowance of GitHub AI Credits for chat and agent mode, and Copilot Chat on GitHub.com and mobile.
- **Catch:** Models are picked automatically, and GitHub doesn't publish the size of the free AI Credit allowance.
- **Paid from:** $10/month (Pro), free for verified teachers and maintainers of popular open-source projects
- **Best for:** Autocomplete inside VS Code

- **Free tier:** Limited Agent, Chat, and Tab completions using Cursor's own Grok and Composer models.
- **Catch:** No Claude, GPT, or Gemini models on the free plan, no trial, and no published limits. The old Hobby plan is closed to new users.

## What Are the Best Free AI App Builders?

Lovable and Google AI Studio are the only AI app builders here with a real free plan; most others now offer one-time credits, which you’ll find in the trials box below. Both turn a plain-language description into a working web app, so you don’t write the code yourself.

### 22. Google AI Studio

Google AI Studio is Google's free playground for Gemini models, and its Build mode turns prompts into deployable apps.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Google account
- **Free tier:** AI Studio is free unless you link a paid API key. Eligible accounts can publish up to 2 full-stack apps on the Google Cloud Starter Tier without a billing account.
- **Catch:** Prompts sent on the free Gemini API tier are used to improve Google's products, and only some models are free.
- **Paid from:** Pay-as-you-go API (prepay from $5)
- **Best for:** Prototyping apps built on Gemini

### 23. Lovable

Lovable builds full-stack web apps from a chat, including the database and hosting.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** 5 build credits per day (up to 30 a month), a daily chat allowance, 20 Cloud credits and 4 AI credits per month, private projects, unlimited workspace members, and public publishing.
- **Catch:** You don't know a build's credit cost before you send it. In SaaSCRMReview's October 2026 test, one build used about 2 of the 5 daily credits. Custom domains are paid.
- **Paid from:** $25/month (Pro), or $21/month billed annually; 50% off for verified students and teachers
- **Best for:** Turning an app idea into a working prototype in an afternoon

## What Are the Free AI APIs and Local Models?

The Gemini API has the best free tier for hosted models, and Ollama or LM Studio are the best free ways to run models locally. Hosted APIs are faster to start; local models have no limits and keep your data on your machine.

### 24. Gemini API (free tier)

The Gemini API is the most generous free hosted API from a major lab, and you get a key in a few clicks from Google AI Studio.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Google account
- **Free tier:** Free access, up to per-model rate limits, to Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Gemini Flash models up to 3.8, and the Gemini 3.5 Transcribe Live and Gemini 3.8 Live models.
- **Catch:** Free-tier prompts and responses are used to improve Google's products; only the paid tier keeps them out.
- **Paid from:** Pay-as-you-go (prepay from $5)
- **Best for:** Prototyping an app or agent on a frontier-class model

### 25. OpenRouter (free models)

OpenRouter gives you one OpenAI-compatible API for hundreds of models, including a rotating set of `:free` variants.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** 50 requests per day on free models, rising to 1,000 per day once you've bought at least 10 credits.
- **Catch:** Free models only route to providers that match your privacy settings, so some need the model-training toggle turned on.
- **Paid from:** Pay per token
- **Best for:** Testing many models without managing many API keys

OpenRouter is also one of the routes in our guide on how to use Claude Code for free.

### 26. Ollama

Ollama runs open-weight models on your own computer from the command line, with no account and no usage limits.

- **Free type:** Open source ·**Signup:** No
- **Free tier:** Unlimited local use of any model your hardware can run.
- **Catch:** You need a capable GPU or a lot of RAM for larger models. Ollama's cloud models are pay-per-token, with no free cloud allowance.
- **Paid from:** $20/month (Pro, with $60 of cloud credits)
- **Best for:** Private, offline LLMs for coding and scripting

### 27. LM Studio

LM Studio is a desktop app for downloading and chatting with local models, no terminal needed.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** No
- **Free tier:** The full app, free at home and at work since July 2025.
- **Catch:** Like Ollama, it's only as fast as your hardware.
- **Paid from:** Enterprise (contact sales)
- **Best for:** Trying local models through a graphical interface

## What Are the Best Free AI Image Generators?

Adobe Firefly is the best free AI image generator for commercial work, and Gemini’s Nano Banana is the pick for everything else. With free image generators, rights matter more than quality: Firefly and Leonardo allow commercial use on the free plan, while Recraft doesn’t and keeps ownership of free images.

For a deeper comparison, see our guide to the best free AI image generators.

### 28. Gemini (Nano Banana)

Google's Nano Banana models generate images inside the Gemini app, with no separate account or tool.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Google account
- **Free tier:** Nano Banana image generation within the Gemini app's standard limits, which refresh every 5 hours up to a weekly cap.
- **Catch:** Images count as high-cost requests, so they use up your allowance faster than text, and Google publishes no image count. Redoing images with Nano Banana Pro is paid.
- **Paid from:** Google AI Plus (price varies by country, from $4.99/month in the US)
- **Best for:** Quick, photorealistic images for slides and mockups

### 29. ChatGPT Images

ChatGPT generates images in the same chat window, and you can edit them by describing the change.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** Limited image creation, with its own limit separate from text chat.
- **Catch:** OpenAI publishes no number; once you hit the limit, you wait for the reset or upgrade.
- **Paid from:** ChatGPT Go (price varies by country, from $8/month in the US)
- **Best for:** Iterating on an image through conversation

### 30. Leonardo.Ai

Leonardo.Ai offers fine-grained creative control, including steering an image from your own sketch.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** 150 tokens per day, resetting every 24 hours with no rollover.
- **Catch:** Everything you generate on the free plan is public.
- **Paid from:** Leonardo.Ai paid plans
- **Best for:** Artistic images you're allowed to use commercially

### 31. Recraft

Recraft specializes in icons, illustrations, and brand-style assets.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Yes
- **Free tier:** Free image generation (Recraft doesn't publish the credit amount).
- **Catch:** According to Recraft's terms, Recraft owns images made on the free plan, they're public in its community gallery, and they're not licensed for commercial use.
- **Paid from:** $12/month (Basic), or $10/month billed annually
- **Best for:** Testing design ideas before you pay for rights

### 32. Adobe Firefly

Adobe Firefly is the safest free choice for commercial work, because Adobe trains its own Firefly models on licensed content.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Adobe account
- **Free tier:** A limited number of free generations per day for images, video, and audio.
- **Catch:** The commercially-safe promise covers Firefly's own models, not the partner models also available in the app. Adobe doesn't publish the daily count.
- **Paid from:** $9.99/month (Firefly Standard, 2,000 credits)
- **Best for:** Images for client work or products you sell

### 33. Ideogram

Ideogram renders text inside images more reliably than most generators, which makes it the pick for posters and social graphics.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Google or Apple account
- **Free tier:** 30 credits per week, resetting every Saturday at 00:00 UTC.
- **Catch:** Everything on the free plan is public, and accounts created with email or Microsoft get no free credits.
- **Paid from:** $20/month (Plus), or $15/month billed annually
- **Best for:** Graphics with words in them

## What Are the Best Free AI Video Generators?

Google Flow is the best free AI video generator, with 50 credits a day across its Omni Flash and Veo models. Video is the most expensive thing to generate, so it has the tightest free tiers and the most churn: Pika, Kling, and Invideo all dropped off this list in 2026. What’s left either gives you a small daily allowance or runs on your own GPU.

For a deeper comparison, see our guide to the best free AI text-to-video generators.

### 34. Google Flow

Google Flow is Google's AI filmmaking studio, and the free tier gives you access to both its Omni Flash and Veo video models.

- **Free type:** Ongoing free plan ·**Signup:** Google account
- **Free tier:** 50 credits per day, with no rollover. Omni Flash costs 1 credit per second at 360p or 3 at 720p (clips up to 10 seconds), and Veo Lite costs 10 credits per clip, so 50 credits buys about 50 seconds of 360p video, about 16 seconds at 720p, or 5 Veo Lite clips.
- **Catch:** Free users may be blocked from generating video at peak times (roughly 2 to 5 PM UTC), and Veo Quality (100 credits per clip) is out of reach.
- **Paid from:** Google AI Pro (price varies by country, from $19.99/month in the US)
- **Best for:** Short, high-quality clips for presentations and social posts

### 35. Seedance (via Dreamina)

ByteDance's Seedance video models are among the most realistic available, and you can use them free through ByteDance's Dreamina app.

Free AI tools cost you something other than money: your data, your rights to the output, or your time. Here's what to watch for before you put any of them into real work.

### Your data may train the model

The big chatbots train on free-plan chats too, unless you change a setting. A few examples:

- **ChatGPT:** OpenAI uses your conversations for training unless you opt out in Data Controls or its privacy portal. Business plans like ChatGPT Enterprise are excluded by default.
- **Claude:** Anthropic lets you choose under Settings > Privacy. If you allow training, chats are kept for 5 years; if you don't, 30 days.
- **Gemini:** While Gemini's History setting is on, Google uses your chats to train its models, and human reviewers read a subset, which Google keeps for up to 3 years even if you delete them.
- **DeepSeek:** Uses conversation data for model training, stores data in China, and does not offer an opt-out.

Never paste customer data, API keys, or unreleased financials into a free tool unless you've checked its data settings.

### Limits are vague and shrink without notice

Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and Mistral describe many free limits only as "limited" or "standard." They also change: in 2026 alone, Gemini's free users lost model choice, Antigravity's free plan dropped its Claude models, and Pika, Kling, and Invideo stopped offering usable free plans.

### Free output often isn't yours to sell

Check the license before you ship anything:

- **Ownership:** Recraft owns images made on its free plan.
- **Commercial use:** ElevenLabs and Recraft ban it on free plans; Firefly and Leonardo allow it.
- **Attribution:** ElevenLabs requires "elevenlabs.io" or "11.ai" in the title of anything you publish.
- **Watermarks:** DeepL files, HeyGen, Synthesia, Dreamina, and Descript exports all carry one on the free plan.
- **Visibility:** Leonardo, Recraft, and Ideogram make free-plan images public.
