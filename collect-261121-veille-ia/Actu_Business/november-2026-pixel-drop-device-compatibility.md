Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop is rolling out, but the headline features do not arrive on every Pixel. Some require a Pixel 10, others are limited to the Pixel 11 series, and a few reach models as old as the Pixel 6a. The update also launches Pixel Tag in the United States on November 11.

Rollout began November 10 and continues over the next several weeks, so an eligible phone may not show every feature immediately. Here is the compatibility breakdown that matters before you start hunting through Settings.

## What is new for Pixel 10 phones?

Pixel 10 devices gain the latest Gemini Intelligence features, although Google excludes the Pixel 10a from this group. Rambler improves voice typing by removing filler words and formatting dictated text before you send it. Gemini can also surface proactive suggestions and handle more complex tasks, with availability varying by country, language, age, and account setup.

Create My Widget is rolling out to the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, and the Pixel 11 family in supported markets. You describe the widget you want, such as a trip countdown or monthly spending tracker, and the phone builds a personalized version without coding.

This is a feature drop, not a full Android replacement. If you remember the rougher launch problems covered in our older Android 15 Pixel update report, the practical difference is that monthly drops add selected capabilities on top of the current operating system.

## Which features require a Pixel 11?

The conversational Call Screen upgrade is limited to the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold in the United States. When an unknown caller reaches you, Gemini can ask why the person is calling while displaying a live transcript. You decide whether to answer or end the call.

HiLight support for Google Messages is narrower. It works on the Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. When the phone is face down, the HiLight indicator can glow for messages from favorite contacts. Standard Pixel 11 and older Pixel models do not receive this hardware-dependent alert.

## What reaches older Pixel phones?

Two useful additions go much further back:

- **Custom icon styles:** available on Pixel 6a and newer models across supported regions and languages.
- **Pixel search:** available on Pixel 6a and newer, letting you search photos, email, messages, calendar events, and device settings from one place.

Those features are more broadly useful than the flashier AI demos because they do not require the newest Pro hardware. Results and exact availability can still vary, and a staged rollout means two identical phones may receive the update on different days.

## Pixel Tag price and compatibility

Pixel Tag goes on sale November 11 for $29.99 each or $99.99 for a four-pack in the United States. Google says its replaceable battery lasts more than a year. It uses the Find Hub network, which includes more than one billion Android devices, to locate an item when it is far away.

Nearby precision finding uses Ultra Wideband, so the visual direction and distance guidance requires compatible UWB hardware. The tag itself works with Android 9 phones, but an older or lower-end phone may offer basic network location without the precise arrow-style experience.

## Pixel Watch features in the November drop

Phone-based watch unlocking is coming over the next few weeks to Pixel Watch 3, Pixel Watch 4, and Pixel Watch 5 models with UWB. It also requires a supported Pro-series Pixel with UWB. When the watch is on your wrist and the nearby phone is unlocked, the phone can unlock the watch without another PIN entry.

Step-by-step workouts are rolling out to Pixel Watch 3 and newer. They can display exercise instructions, log repetitions and weights, and track metrics. Google notes that workouts created by Google Health Coach require a Google Health Premium subscription, which is separate from the free software update.

## How to get the Pixel Drop

Open **Settings > System > Software updates** and check for the latest system update. Also update Google Play system components and apps through the Play Store. Restart the phone after installation if a feature does not appear.

If the phone is eligible but a feature is still missing, the most likely explanation is staged availability, not a failed update. Google’s official November Pixel Drop announcement says distribution will continue over several weeks and lists device, market, and language restrictions in its footnotes.

## Which upgrade matters most?

Pixel 10 owners get the broadest software expansion through Gemini Intelligence and custom widgets. Pixel 11 Pro owners gain the most visible hardware-specific addition through HiLight messaging. Pixel 6a and newer owners still receive practical search and personalization improvements, while Pixel Tag is a separate purchase rather than a feature included with the update.
