Google called the November Pixel Drop a big one, and on paper it is. Gemini Intelligence on the Pixel 10, custom widgets, new icon styles, the Pixel Tag tracker and a Google Fi network switch all landed in one announcement on November 10, 2026. A week later, plenty of owners are still staring at an October security patch.

If your phone has not shown an update prompt, or you installed it and cannot find Create My Widget, you are in large company. Android Authority's rollout check on November 16 describes the same pattern across Pixel 6a to Pixel 11 owners. Here is what Google actually promised, who is eligible for what, and what you can do while you wait.

## What the poll says about the rollout

Android Authority ran a reader poll asking whether people had the November Pixel Drop. With 908 votes, 61% said they were still waiting. Another 18% had installed it but barely saw anything new, 16% had it with some features missing, and just 5% had the update and every announced feature. It is a self-selected poll, so treat it as a mood check rather than a measurement.

The confusion started before the announcement. Google normally pushes its monthly Pixel update on the first Tuesday of the month, but November 3 passed with nothing, even though the November Android Security Bulletin was already out on November 2. Android Authority had reported that clues in Google's security database pointed to Android 17 QPR2 arriving early, and it did. Google then rolled the drop out to the Pixel 6a and newer, with the Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro no longer on the list. We covered the earlier gap in our look at the missing November Pixel update.

Google's own wording is careful. Its announcement says updates start rolling out on November 10 and "will continue over the next several weeks." Nothing there promises a day-one update on every phone.

## Why installing it may not show everything

Readers on r/pixel_phones describe a few different situations. Some got only a Google Play system update, with no sign of the Pixel Drop itself. Some saw a "Your phone was updated" notification from the Pixel app without any OS update behind it. Others installed the update and reported only the extended theme color palette, with no custom widget, no extended Pixel Search and no Rambler. A Pixel 11 Pro owner said the only change they noticed was fully selectable accent colors.

Android Authority notes that this is a familiar Pixel Drop pattern: many features depend on app updates and server-side switches that Google flips separately from the system update. The OS update lays the groundwork, and features can show up weeks later. Some users believe Google paused the rollout to fix bugs, but Google has not said that.

Eligibility matters too. The table below pulls the device and market limits from the footnotes in Google's announcement, so you can see whether a missing feature is a delay or simply not meant for your phone.

| Feature | Devices Google lists | Catch | 
|---|---|---|
| Gemini Intelligence (Rambler, proactive suggestions) | Pixel 10 and newer, except Pixel 10a | Needs the Gemini app; select countries and languages, users 18+ | 
| Create My Widget | Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, 10 Pro Fold and the Pixel 11 series | Select markets only | 
| Call Screen upgrade | Pixel 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, 11 Pro Fold | Listed for the U.S. | 
| Custom icon styles and Pixel search updates | Pixel 6a and newer | Listed for all regions and languages | 
| HiLight for Google Messages | Pixel 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, 11 Pro Fold | Face-down glow for favorite contacts | 
| Pixel Tag | Works with Android 9 and newer phones | $29.99 for one or $99.99 for four, from November 11 | 

A Pixel 10a owner will never see Create My Widget, and a Pixel 9 owner will never see Rambler, no matter how long they wait.

## What the drop actually contains

Google's announcement groups the drop around Gemini Intelligence on the Pixel 10. Rambler removes filler words like "um" from voice typing and formats your thoughts into polished text. Gemini can also handle multi-step tasks such as groceries, ride booking or restaurant reservations, and Google says that is available in Japan, the UK and India. Proactive suggestions include sharing your ETA with a tap when someone asks when you will arrive.

Create My Widget lets you describe a widget that does not exist yet, for example a monthly spending tracker, a game day widget in your team colors, or a getaway countdown that pulls in flight and reservation details. On the Pixel 11 series, Call Screen gets more conversational: Gemini asks unknown callers why they are calling, and you see a live transcript while you decide whether to pick up.

Beyond the phone, Google says your Pixel Watch will skip the PIN or pattern when it is on your wrist and your nearby phone is open, coming in the next few weeks. Step-by-step workouts are rolling out to Pixel Watch 3 and newer. Google Fi also announced a network that switches between two U.S. 5G networks on eligible Pixels, with 50% off select unlimited plans for 12 months for existing Pixel owners until December 1, 2026.

## What to do while you wait

You do not need to do anything exotic. A simple routine every couple of days is enough.

1. Open Settings, then System, then Software updates, then System update, and tap Check for update. Android Authority recommends this manual check if the prompt has not appeared.
2. Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon and run Update all, so Pixel Launcher and related system apps are current. Several features depend on app updates, not the OS build.
3. Restart the phone after those updates finish, then look again in the widget picker or Settings.

If you would rather not wait, you can sideload Google's official OTA image for your device from the Android developer site. Be clear about what that buys you: it installs the system update, but features Google has not switched on for your account or region will still arrive on Google's schedule. Sideloading also carries the usual risk of choosing the wrong image, so match it to your exact model.

For Pixel Tag, the situation is simpler. Google says it is available from November 11 and works with Android 9 phones and newer through the Find Hub network of more than 1 billion Android devices, with UWB giving direction and distance guidance nearby. Our guide to setting up a Find Hub Tag covers the setup steps.

## Questions Pixel owners are asking

### Which Pixels get the November 2026 Pixel Drop?

Android Authority reports the rollout covers the Pixel 6a and newer, with the Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro dropped from the list. Individual features have their own device limits, shown in the table above.

### Why did my Pixel say it updated but nothing changed?

Some owners received a Google Play system update or a notification from the Pixel app, neither of which is the Pixel Drop OS update. Check the manual update path in Settings to see whether a system update is actually pending.

### Will sideloading get me Create My Widget right away?

No. Sideloading installs the system update only. Google's server-side switches and app updates decide when Create My Widget appears, and it is limited to Pixel 10 models other than the 10a and the Pixel 11 series.
