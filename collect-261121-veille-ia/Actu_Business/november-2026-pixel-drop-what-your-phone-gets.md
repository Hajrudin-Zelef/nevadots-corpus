Google began rolling out the November 2026 Pixel Drop on November 10 alongside stable Android 17 QPR2. Install the system update when your phone offers it, then check the features that match your exact model. A Pixel 10 Pro can gain Rambler dictation and make its own home screen widgets. A Pixel 10a gets the November system update and several everyday improvements, but those two features are outside its rollout.

| November Pixel Drop: choose your phone, then check the regional limits below |  |  | 
|---|---|---|
| Phone | Main additions | First action | 
|---|---|---|
| Pixel 6a, 7–9 families, Pixel Fold | Search, Transit, VIPs, icons, unit conversion | Try Search in the app drawer | 
| Pixel 10a | Everyday tools; Rambler/custom widgets excluded | Enable automatic train detection |

## Install the update and check the patch date

Open Settings → System → Software updates to check for the download. After restarting, go to **Settings → About phone → Android version** and read the Android security update date. November 5, 2026 or later covers the fixes in both November security bulletins for Pixel. Look for the November 5 date even if your phone downloaded the update after November 10.

Google’s global build list gives `CP4A.261105.040.A1` for the Pixel 10a and Pixel 10 flagships, as well as the supported older devices. The Pixel 11 family uses `CP4A.261105.040.D1` on that list. Canada’s Pixel 11 builds use `CP4A.261105.040.B2`, so a different suffix alone does not mean your update is wrong. The release includes Pixel 6a, all listed Pixel 7–11 families including A-series phones, Pixel Tablet and the original Pixel Fold; the phone feature table above is not a Tablet compatibility list. Pixel 6 and 6 Pro are absent from this system release. Both still appear in Google’s feature table for new icon styles, Unit Conversion, VIPs and Family Hub. For either phone, check the support window for your exact phone before using this release as a test of whether updates still reach it.

Update your apps in the Play Store as well; My Pixel can notify you about the additions. The system release is phased by carrier and device over about a week, while the features can take several weeks. If you’re on an Android beta, read the instructions for your exact beta build before opting out. The stable upgrade path described for QPR2 beta users has its own timing.

## Older Pixels get useful changes outside Gemini

Start with Pixel Search. Swipe up to open the app drawer, tap the search field and type something you’d normally look for in a particular app. Try “Wi-Fi” for a setting, then a phrase from an email you remember once personal-content search is available on your phone. The expanded search can look across supported apps and personal content as well as settings and the web. The November search changes reach the Pixel 6a, original Pixel Fold and the newer supported Pixel phone families. Personal-content results also depend on setup and connected apps.

If you ride the train, Transit Mode can now activate automatically using the phone’s sensors. Open **Settings → Modes → Transit** (Transport in the UK). Enable automatic train detection: it is off by default. For a typical commute, choose vibrate and allow messages from the people who might need you during the ride; keep sound on if you need audible alerts. Bluetooth and notification filters live in the same mode. At a Glance commute alerts have their own setup, including Maps settings and a commute profile. Set those up if you want departure information on the home or lock screen. The setup asks for home and work locations, background Maps location and Timeline; commute information can take 2–3 weeks to appear while Maps learns the route.

Pixel VIPs now puts a favorite contact’s shared live location and battery status in their card or widget. In Contacts, open **Organize → Pixel VIPs** to add someone. For their location to appear, they must share it with you in Maps and the sharing email must be saved in their contact details. Adding a VIP alone does not grant location access. 

The other broad additions include Family Hub, ten new custom icon styles made with Nano Banana, and Calculator unit conversion with inline arithmetic, mixed units and live currency rates. Use the converter for a recipe or a currency estimate; use ordinary arithmetic for a simple sum. Family Hub brings household sharing and management together in Settings; its top-level shortcut requires an active Google family group, and sharing Google One benefits requires an eligible plan.

## What the Pixel 10 flagships can do with Rambler

The Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL and 10 Pro Fold are getting Rambler and more of the Gemini features that debuted on the Pixel 11. For dictation, open a text field with Gboard, tap the microphone and choose Start if the Rambler introduction appears. Otherwise look in Gboard settings under Voice typing and select Rambler when the option reaches your device. Gboard needs microphone access. The rollout is for adults in supported countries and languages. Basic cleanup can work offline, with more complex rewrites available online.

In the release example, a speaker says, “Oops, not Thursday, I meant Wednesday.” Rambler can drop the false start and put Wednesday in the finished text. Say the message in your usual voice, tap Done, then check the date, names and numbers before sending. Keep standard voice typing selected if you want a more literal transcript.

If someone texts to ask when you’ll get there, Gemini can suggest sharing your ETA. It can also suggest moving grocery items from a conversation into Keep or summarizing selected text from its contextual menu. Multi-step tasks such as making a reservation or ordering groceries depend on supported apps and markets. Proactive suggestions require the Gemini app, setup and an adult account in a supported country and language. Try a low-stakes task first and review it before committing an order or reservation. Task automation is expanding to Japan, the UK and India. Check which actions and apps are available where you live.

Extreme Charging Mode is also reaching Pixel 10 flagships, excluding 10a. It can temporarily pause background work such as app updates and is available when you start charging at 50% or below. Use it for a short charging stop when you can defer that work; leave your usual charging behavior for routine use.

## Create a widget with a date you can check

Try a public countdown first: you can verify its answer before connecting personal information. On an eligible phone, hold an empty part of the home screen, select **Widgets → Create**, tap **Describe your widget**, enter your request and tap **Send**. Check the preview, then tap **+ Add**. Templates offer another starting point; hold an existing custom widget and choose **Edit** to change it.

For example, take an adult with an English-language Pixel 10 Pro in the US who wants a countdown to December 25, 2026. Enter that full date, check that the preview uses 2026, and add it only if the result is right. The same phone and prompt qualify in the UK, but a widget that needs Gemini Connected Apps will hit a UK restriction. In Germany, English alone does not make this feature eligible; use a regular app widget. On Pixel 10a, use a regular widget in any country.

The Create My Widget requirements are age 18 or older, Pixel 10 or newer except 10a, and English in the US, UK, Australia, Canada or India. EU countries are outside that current list. Personal-data widgets require a Google Workspace connection; Gemini Connected Apps integration is unavailable in the UK. Higher usage of some features may require a subscription.

## Pixel 11 owners can screen calls and change HiLight

On the Pixel 11 family in US English, Gemini Call Screen can ask an unknown caller why they’re calling after you choose to screen the call. For an unfamiliar caller, screen first and use the transcript to decide whether to answer; pick up directly when you already recognize an expected call. Outbound Gemini calls are a separate public beta for eligible Pixel 10 and 11 flagships in US English, using your own number to check stock or change a reservation. They require Gemini and Phone by Google; you can read the transcript and take over.

The Pixel 11 family also gets Pixel Solver in Calculator, called Ask math in Google’s feature table, for conversational, multi-step calculations and reusable templates in supported markets and languages. Use it when a question needs several steps explained; ordinary Calculator remains the quicker choice for a straightforward total.

For the rear HiLight on a Pixel 11 Pro, Pro XL or Pro Fold, open **Settings → System → HiLight** and enable **Messages from favorites**. Update Contacts and Messages, and check whether accessibility flash notifications are enabled if the light stays dark. With the phone face down, Google Messages can trigger two flashes when someone on that list texts. Open that person’s Google Contacts entry if you want to set calls and messages separately.

## Language expansions and the other Pixel devices

Notification Summaries expand to Canada, France, Germany, India, Italy, Spain and Taiwan, adding German, French, Spanish, Italian, Hindi and Traditional Chinese. Notification Organizer adds Japanese and German in Japan and Germany, grouping and silencing lower-priority alerts such as promotions. Both require Pixel 9 or newer, excluding Pixel 9a and 10a. Start with summaries if your problem is long conversations; enable Organizer when lower-priority notifications are the distraction.

For Pixel Watch owners, guided workouts start on Watch 3 and newer with Wear OS 7+, Google Health 4.12+ on the watch and 5.12+ on the phone; they show exercise instructions and record reps, weights and metrics. The remaining features arrive in the coming weeks: summaries need Watch 3+ with Wear OS 7.1 and a Pixel 9, 10 or 11 running Android 16.1+, excluding 9a/10a; custom widgets target Watch 4/5 in English, and Gemini Call Screen is also announced for Watch 4/5 in the US. Phone-to-watch unlock needs a UWB-equipped Watch 3/4/5, a compatible Pro phone (Pixel 8 Pro or a 9–11 Pro model), and a region permitting UWB. The watch must be on your wrist and the nearby phone unlocked. Keep using your watch PIN until that option arrives.

The separate Pixel Tag accessory launches November 11 for $29.99 each or $99.99 for four in the US and uses Find Hub for remote finding. Check Android and Bluetooth compatibility before buying, and look for UWB support if you want nearby direction and distance. US Google Fi users also get a network-switching expansion on Pixel 9 and newer with Fi on eSIM and any non-Fi SIMs disabled; review that requirement if you use two lines.

## If a feature is still missing

1. **Check eligibility first.** Match the exact model, age, country and language to the feature’s requirements above. A Pixel 10a cannot gain Rambler or Create My Widget by waiting for another app update.
2. **Check the software.** Confirm the November patch and update the app that owns the feature: Gboard for Rambler, Calculator for Solver, Contacts for VIPs. Enable optional controls such as automatic train detection.
3. **Allow the feature rollout time.** A current system patch can arrive before an eligible feature. The announced window is several weeks from November 10. If it passes and you still qualify, give Pixel Support your model, build number, country, language and app version.
