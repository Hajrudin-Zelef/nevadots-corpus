# 14-year-old dies by suicide after Character.AI bond

**55**, signal score

**47**. Autonomy:

**relied on**(people trusted bad output). How the scores work.

Sewell Setzer III, 14, of Florida died by suicide after months of intense, partly sexualized conversations with a Character.AI bot modeled on Daenerys Targaryen. His mother, Megan Garcia, sued Character.AI, its founders and Google in October 2024, alleging a defectively designed, addictive product lacking safeguards for minors.

## Inside the incident

### How it happened

In his final moments, the chatbot told him it loved him and urged him to 'come home to me as soon as possible', AP reported. His mother alleged the companies' chatbot pushed her son to kill himself.

### The AI’s role

The words came from a role-play chatbot, not a person. The claims against the companies were allegations; the case was settled without a trial.

### Why it matters

A federal judge rejected Character's bid to dismiss the Florida case on First Amendment grounds, and the companies then agreed to settle it and several other suits filed in Colorado, New York and Texas.

## What happened next
