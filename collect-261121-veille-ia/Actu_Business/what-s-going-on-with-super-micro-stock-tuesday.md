# What's Going On With Super Micro Stock Tuesday?

**Super Micro Computer Inc.** SMCI shares are moving higher Tuesday, following SambaNova’s announcement of a strategic collaboration with the server maker to build and sell AI inference systems combining SambaNova’s SN50 chips with Nvidia Corp. NVDA GPUs. Investors also may be positioning ahead of Super Micro’s earnings report, due after the close.

- Super Micro shares are powering higher. Why are SMCI shares rallying?

**SambaNova’s SN50 Chips Will Power Supermicro’s New Inference Racks**

Super Micro will work with SambaNova to develop SN50 inference racks that operate alongside Nvidia GPUs as part of its Data Center Building Block Solutions portfolio. The collaboration initially plans for 10,000 SN50 RDU chips, SambaNova’s reconfigurable dataflow units, to build and sell AI disaggregated inference systems that include both RDUs and GPUs. The systems pair Nvidia GPUs, which read the prompt, with SambaNova chips built to write the response quickly.

Supermicro will integrate, test, deliver and support the systems, including on-site service, and SambaNova engineers will help tune models on the SN50 and support deployments. The companies will start with neoclouds and sovereign clouds, then expand to enterprises that run AI in their own data centers.

"Our collaboration with SambaNova expands our broad DCBBS portfolio by enabling heterogeneous architectures that integrate GPU computing with specialized inference acceleration," said **Charles Liang**, Super Micro’s president and CEO.

**Tech Stocks Are Also Trying to Rebound After a Selloff**

The move could also reflect a rotation back into tech after a rough stretch for the group. The Nasdaq 100 closed Monday more than 10% below its record high, a technical correction, according to Bloomberg and Reuters, and the Nasdaq fell 1.07% on the day. Nasdaq 100 futures were up about 0.2% to 0.4% this morning. Supermicro fell 4.3% Friday and about 5% Monday as AI server stocks including Dell and Hewlett Packard Enterprise sold off, according to 24/7 Wall St.

**Super Micro to Report Fiscal First-Quarter Results Tonight**

Super Micro is scheduled to report fiscal first-quarter earnings today after the market closes, with a call at 5 p.m. ET. Analysts estimate earnings per share of $1.23 and revenue of $16.39 billion. On Oct. 20, Super Micro said preliminary first-quarter revenue is estimated at $18.5 billion to $19.5 billion, compared with its guidance of $14.5 billion to $15.5 billion, and gross margin at 12.0% to 12.5%.
