Alongside releasing iOS 27.2.1 update, Apple has today released new updates for devices that are stuck on iOS 26 and iOS 18.

Apple has released iOS 27.2.1 with important bug fixes and improvements. Now it looks like the security fixes were not limited to iOS 27, as the company has also made iOS 26.7.3 and iOS 18.7.12 available for older models of iPhone and iPads.

Here’s the complete list of legacy iOS versions that have an update released today. Interestingly, Apple has also released a new update for watchOS 26.

- iOS 26.7.3
- iPadOS 26.7.3
- iOS 18.7.12
- iPadOS 18.7.12
- macOS Tahoe 26.7.3
- macOS Sequoia 15.8.3
- watchOS 26.6.1

If you have a compatible iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Watch that runs any of the above operating system versions, then head over to the Software Update section of your device and download the update.
