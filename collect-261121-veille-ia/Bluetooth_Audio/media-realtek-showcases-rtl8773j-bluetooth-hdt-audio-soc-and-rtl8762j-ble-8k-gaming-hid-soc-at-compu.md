2026-05-19

Realtek Semiconductor Corp. will showcase its latest Bluetooth innovations at COMPUTEX 2026, featuring two flagship solutions: the RTL8773J HDT Audio SoC and the RTL8762J BLE SoC for 8K HID applications. These platforms deliver high data throughput, ultra-low latency, and energy-efficient performance for next-generation audio, gaming, and advanced human interface devices.

The RTL8773J is Realtek's next-generation Bluetooth HDT Audio SoC designed for TWS, gaming headsets, speakers, and AI-enabled devices. It supports Bluetooth 7, including Bluetooth Classic, Bluetooth Low Energy, LE Audio, and HDT, along with advanced capabilities such as PAwR, channel sounding, and AoA/AoD. Featuring dual MCU cores, flexible embedded and external memory configurations, and dual HiFi-class DSPs, the RTL8773J enables advanced features such as adaptive hybrid ANC and premium audio processing. Realtek's HDT technology further enhances HDT data rates while reducing end-to-end audio latency, delivering immersive and highly responsive gaming audio with ultra-low power consumption for extended wireless use.

The RTL8762J is a high-performance HID SoC designed for gaming keyboards, mice, and controllers, supporting 8K reporting rates for precise, low-latency input. It integrates BLE, 2.4 GHz wireless, 4 Mbps PHY, and USB 2.0 High-Speed, providing manufacturers with a unified platform for both wired and wireless peripherals while maintaining consistent performance. Optimized for efficiency, it features deep low-power standby and supports single-cell battery operation without external boost components, enabling longer battery life and compact device designs. Together, the RTL8773J and RTL8762J enable OEMs and ODMs to create cohesive ecosystems that combine high-resolution, low-latency audio with ultra-responsive input devices for next-generation gaming experiences.
