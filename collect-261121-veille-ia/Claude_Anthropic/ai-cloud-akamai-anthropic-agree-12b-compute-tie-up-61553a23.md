Cloud company Akamai struck a seven-year deal worth $11.6 billion with Anthropic for computing power, in addition to issuing a warrant for the sale of 5% of its stock to the AI company.
As part of the multi-year agreement, Akamai is to provide Anthropic access to CPUs using its cloud’s distributed AI and software to support Anthropic’s services.
There is scope to expand the partnership by a further $9 billion and the latest tie-up builds on a previous $1.8 billion computing deal struck between the pair earlier this year.
Akamai stated it secured more than $2.8 billion in multi-year cloud infrastructure commitments across its customer base, underscoring “a growing demand” for it “to enable customers to build, deploy and operate AI workloads at scale”.

In what is a deepening relationship, Akamai stated it also issued a warrant to Anthropic for the purchase of Series-B stock, representing 7.7 million shares amounting to approximately 5% at $111.33 each.
Around 2% of the stock is tied to the $11.6 billion commitment, while the remaining 3% will vest if the companies expand the agreement by a further $9 billion.
Total capex related to the initial agreement is estimated to amount to $5.5 billion, Akamai added.
Akamai co-founder and CEO Tom Leighton said Anthropic is advancing the AI revolution “and we are thrilled they chose Akamai’s capabilities for building an operating AI infrastructure at scale”.
