# Anthropic Commits $3.4 Billion to 1.7 GW of Gas Generators for Data Centers

According to ChainCatcher citing the Financial Times, Anthropic agreed in late September to spend $3.4 billion on a commitment to purchase 1.7 gigawatts of heavy-duty generators from OnePWR Solutions, a San Antonio, Texas-based equipment supplier. The deal also includes engineering and construction capabilities to install the generators; Anthropic did not acquire OnePWR, and no OnePWR employees joined the AI company.

OnePWR had ordered the generators from Caterpillar two years ago but needed financing to complete the deal. Anthropic initially planned to buy from OnePWR, then chose to take over its order with Caterpillar.
