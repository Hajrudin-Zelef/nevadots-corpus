Anthropic has launched the Claude Marketplace, a platform that allows enterprise customers to access and purchase AI tools built by partner companies using the Claude model family. The marketplace is currently available in limited preview and aims to simplify how organisations adopt AI-powered applications across different workflows.

The company said the marketplace enables enterprises to extend their existing Anthropic spending commitments to third-party tools built on Claude. This approach allows companies to manage AI procurement through a single platform while accessing specialised software designed for different industries and use cases.

What the Claude Marketplace offers

The Claude Marketplace allows enterprises to use AI tools developed by partner companies that run on Claude models. These tools are designed for tasks such as software development, legal research, finance workflows and enterprise data analysis.

According to Anthropic, the goal is to reduce the complexity organisations face when evaluating multiple AI products. Instead of negotiating separate contracts with each provider, companies can apply part of their existing Anthropic commitment toward these partner solutions.

The platform also aims to provide a curated ecosystem of enterprise-focused AI tools that meet security and compliance requirements commonly required by large organisations.

Partner companies available at launch

At launch, the marketplace includes several partners offering Claude-powered tools for different sectors. These include:

GitLab for software lifecycle and development workflows

Harvey for legal research and analysis

Lovable for building applications using natural language

Replit for software creation and deployment

Rogo for financial modelling and research

Snowflake for enterprise data analysis through AI agents

Anthropic said additional partners will join the marketplace over time as more companies build products powered by Claude.

How billing and access will work

Anthropic said that purchases made through the Claude Marketplace will count against a portion of an organisation’s existing Anthropic commitment. The company will handle invoicing for partner purchases, allowing enterprises to manage spending through a single relationship.

Companies that already have agreements with Anthropic can access the marketplace through their account teams. Organisations that build products using Claude can apply to join the marketplace through a partner waitlist.
