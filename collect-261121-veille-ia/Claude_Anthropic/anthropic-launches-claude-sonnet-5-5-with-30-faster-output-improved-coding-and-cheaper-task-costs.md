Anthropic has launched **Claude Sonnet 5.5**, the second model in its Claude 5.5 family. The company says the new model is designed for well-defined everyday tasks, coding, document creation and other knowledge-work applications, while sitting below Claude Opus 5.5 in the company's lineup.
According to Anthropic, Sonnet 5.5 generates responses **more than 30% faster** than its predecessor, Claude Sonnet 5. The company also says it can cost up to 30% less per task because the model typically requires fewer tokens to complete the same work.

**70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0**, compared with 10.3% for Sonnet 5. It also scored 55.5% on CursorBench 4.0, up from 34.1% for its predecessor.

**Focus on coding and knowledge work**
Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 is built to handle multi-step coding tasks and can understand codebases while reducing the number of steps required to complete tasks. The company also highlights improvements in knowledge work, including document creation, presentations, spreadsheets and computer-based tasks.

The model is priced at the same token rates as Sonnet 5, but Anthropic says its lower token usage can reduce the overall cost of completing a task by up to 30%. Output generation is also more than 30% faster.

**Availability**
Claude Sonnet 5.5 is available across Anthropic's platforms, including **Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure**. Developers can access it through the Claude Platform using the `claude-sonnet-5-5` model identifier. Anthropic also says the model is available with zero data retention.

The company has also introduced additional cybersecurity safeguards for Sonnet 5.5, reflecting what it describes as an increase in the model's cybersecurity capabilities compared with Sonnet 5.
