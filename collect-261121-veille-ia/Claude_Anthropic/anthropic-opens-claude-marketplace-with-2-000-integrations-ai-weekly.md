## TL;DR

- Anthropic's Claude Marketplace went live September 23, 2026 with more than 2,000 plugins and connectors from Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Notion, and Salesforce.
- The agents and products section sells Claude-powered software from CrowdStrike, Cursor, Harvey, Legora, Lovable, and Snowflake, with Accenture, BCG, and Deloitte listed as service partners.
- Anthropic accepts submissions from any developer via Model Context Protocol and Agent Skills, framing the open model against OpenAI's underperforming app marketplace.

Anthropic has opened a Claude Marketplace stocked with more than 2,000 plugins and connectors from Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Notion, and Salesforce, BleepingComputer reported. The storefront also sells Claude-powered agents and products from CrowdStrike, Cursor, Harvey, Legora, Lovable, and Snowflake, and lists Accenture, Boston Consulting Group, and Deloitte as consulting partners for deployments.

Anthropic says the goal is to "make it easier for teams to discover products that already work with Claude, while giving developers and partners a direct way to reach existing Claude customers." Developers build connectors or plugins with the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Agent Skills, then submit through Anthropic's documentation.

The move is explicitly framed against a rival that flopped. Anthropic is positioning the store as a response to OpenAI's previous app marketplace, which underperformed, by "opening the catalog to outside builders rather than curating it only with large partners."

The store went live on September 23, 2026, with three sections: connectors and plugins, agents and products, and service partners. The coverage does not say whether Anthropic takes a cut of partner revenue, or how MCP-built connectors are vetted before appearing in the catalog.
