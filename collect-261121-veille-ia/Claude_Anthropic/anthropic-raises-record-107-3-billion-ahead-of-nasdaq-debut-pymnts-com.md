Anthropic has raised a record $107.3 billion in its inital public offering, per an early Thursday (Nov. 19) morning report by Bloomberg News.

The company has priced its initial public offering of its Class A common stock at $83 per share and expects the shares to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Thursday, Anthropic said in a Wednesday (Nov. 18) announcement.

The offering includes 980 million shares of Class A common stock, with 956,351,354 being sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 being sold by certain selling shareholders, according to the announcement.

The Bloomberg report calls the listing a watershed moment for both AI and the world’s financial markets, which have become more and more tied to the technology’s development.

The Wall Street Journal reported Wednesday that Anthropic’s offering is expected to value the company at around $1.5 trillion. The $83 share price is above the price range Anthropic had guided, the company sold more shares that it had planned and the deal raised $81 billion, which is a record high for an IPO and tops the $75 billion raised by SpaceX in June, according to the report.

Options on Anthropic shares could begin trading as soon as Monday (Nov. 23), Reuters reported Wednesday.

Cboe Global Markets expects to list Anthropic options two business days after the company’s stock begins trading, according to the report.

“The Anthropic IPO could be yet another catalyst, bringing a new wave of investors and trading activity to the options markets,” Rob Hocking, global head of derivatives at Cboe, said in the report.

The Wall Street Journal also reported on Cboe’s plans, adding that options contracts have reached record volumes amid a surge of interest among individual investors, and that the options tied to Anthropic could drive those volumes higher.

Wednesday’s news about Anthropic’s IPO marks a turnaround from two weeks earlier.

It was reported Nov. 5 that the valuation or timing of Anthropic’s IPO could be impacted by the selloff of tech stocks. The Wall Street Journal reported at the time that three major investors who were planning to participate in the IPO said Anthropic was unlikely to achieve the expected valuation of $1.5 trillion after the selloff. One investor said the valuation could be closer to $1 trillion.

Reuters reported the same day that four people familiar with investor discussions said potential investors were skeptical that Anthropic could move ahead with the roadshow it had planned for the following week and a November listing.

By Tuesday (Nov. 17), both the Journal and Bloomberg reported that Anthropic was planning to price the shares in its IPO at or above the top of the initial price range, which was $70 to $78 each.
