Anthropic has signed a $3.4 billion deal for Caterpillar gas generators.

The Financial Times reports that the 1.7GW contract will see the company partner with Texas-based equipment supplier OnePWR Solutions.

OnePWR ordered the generators two years ago, prior to recent price increases, but needed financing to complete the transaction. Instead of simply becoming a OnePWR customer, Anthropic has taken over the Caterpillar equipment orders.

The generators are expected to be available within the next two years. OnePWR will help with the installation of the generators at Anthropic data centers, including engineering and construction needs.

Earlier this month, Anthropic revealed $895.4bn in cloud and data center commitments through 2045. Alongside the cloud deals, the company has increasingly looked to extend further down the data center stack, growing more involved in facility construction and power deals.

The generator deal, the largest known single purchase of power-generation equipment by a frontier lab, is aimed at giving Anthropic greater control over its data center portfolio.

Anthropic is expected to have more than 3.5GW of compute this year, the New York Times reports, with a similar amount due in 2027.

According to its prospectus, it has committed $121.3bn to lease compute from Google, $120.5bn from SpaceX, $110bn from Amazon, and $44.9bn from Microsoft.

Outside of the hyperscalers, Anthropic has signed a $44.6bn deal with neocloud Nscale until 2033 and $41.3bn with Lambda until 2034. However, Nscale is required to try to obtain qualifying financing for its Nvidia GPUs, while Lambda will have to secure its facility lease in a timely manner.

It has also entered into a $50bn deal with Fluidstack, as well as contracts with TeraWulf and Hut 8.

Anthropic did not mention its $11.6bn cloud deal with Akamai, nor its compute agreement with CoreWeave.
