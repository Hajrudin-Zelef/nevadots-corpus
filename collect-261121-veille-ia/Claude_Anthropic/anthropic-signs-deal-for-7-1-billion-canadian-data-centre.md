Anthropic signed its first data centre lease in Canada, agreeing to take capacity in a new project valued at more than $7.1 billion in Nova Scotia. The company's head of public policy in Canada, Jacob Glick, stated on LinkedIn that Canadians use Claude at one of the highest levels globally, and the deal moves infrastructure closer to those users.

EverWind Fuels announced plans on 16 October 2026 to build the data centre complex and onshore wind infrastructure, calling it the largest private investment in the province's history. The clean energy company will direct more than $5.6 billion to build the data centre at its Point Tupper industrial complex, located northeast of Halifax, and $1.4 billion to expand onshore wind generation. The project plans to create 2,000 construction jobs and 215 permanent operational roles.

The data centre is scheduled to start operating with an initial phase of 95 megawatts at the end of 2027, followed by a 325-megawatt expansion in 2028, reaching a total capacity of 420 megawatts. Minister of Energy Tim Hodgson stated that the investment illustrates how Canada is realising its energy potential.

Anthropic also told prospective investors that its third-quarter revenue reached $16.8 billion, according to people familiar with the matter. The company is preparing to start formally pitching its initial public offering during the week of 9 November 2026.
