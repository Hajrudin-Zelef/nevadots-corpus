# Anthropic Signs First Canada Data Center Deal With EverWind

**Officially launched on October 16, 2026.**

Anthropic has secured its first data-center lease in Canada, partnering with clean energy developer EverWind Fuels on a C$10 billion ($7.1 billion) project in Nova Scotia. The deal marks a significant infrastructure expansion for the AI company as it moves Claude closer to one of its most active user bases outside the United States.

## Release Date and Capacity Timeline

Officially announced on October 16, 2026, the data-center campus will be built at EverWind's existing Point Tupper industrial site, northeast of Halifax. The facility is expected to begin with an initial 95-megawatt phase in late 2027, followed by a 325-megawatt expansion in 2028. At full build-out, the campus will deliver 420 megawatts of capacity, making it one of the largest AI infrastructure investments in Canadian history.

Jacob Glick, Anthropic's head of public policy in Canada, emphasized the strategic importance of the move: "Canadians use Claude at some of the highest rates in the world, and this brings our infrastructure closer to the people who rely on it."

## Clean Energy and Economic Impact

EverWind will invest more than C$8 billion in the data-center development itself, with an additional C$2 billion allocated to expanding onshore wind generation infrastructure. The project is designed to operate on renewable energy, aligning with Anthropic's commitment to sustainable AI operations. EverWind has pledged to protect existing electricity ratepayers, safeguard local water resources, and establish a community benefit program worth tens of millions of dollars.

The staged build-out is projected to support 2,000 construction positions and create 215 permanent operating jobs. Energy Minister Tim Hodgson called the investment "a powerful example of Canada's energy opportunity in action, and exactly the kind of investment our government has been working to attract from day one."

## Context: Anthropic's Growth Trajectory

The Canadian data-center deal comes as Anthropic accelerates its global infrastructure footprint. The company recently disclosed to prospective investors that its third-quarter 2026 revenue hit $16.8 billion, according to people familiar with the matter. Anthropic is preparing to formally market its initial public offering as soon as the week of November 9, 2026, Bloomberg previously reported.

The Nova Scotia facility will give Anthropic dedicated capacity to serve Canadian enterprises and government clients, reducing latency for Claude users across the country. The deal follows similar data-center commitments by major AI labs seeking to localize compute resources in key markets.

## What This Means

Anthropic's first Canadian data-center lease signals the company's recognition of Canada as a strategic growth market for enterprise AI. By co-locating compute with renewable energy infrastructure, the deal sets a template for sustainable AI expansion in regions with strong clean energy portfolios. For EverWind, the partnership validates its positioning as a clean energy enabler for next-generation technology infrastructure. The phased rollout starting in late 2027 will provide a testbed for integrating large-scale AI workloads with wind-powered data centers, a model likely to be replicated as compute demand continues to surge globally.
