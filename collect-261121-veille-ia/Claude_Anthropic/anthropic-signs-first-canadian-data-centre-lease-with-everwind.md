Anthropic has signed a lease with Canadian renewable energy company EverWind for data centre capacity at Point Tupper in Nova Scotia, marking the AI company’s first data centre agreement in Canada.

Jacob Glick, Anthropic’s head of public policy for Canada, confirmed the agreement in a LinkedIn post, citing strong adoption of its Claude AI platform among Canadian users and the need to bring computing infrastructure closer to them.
