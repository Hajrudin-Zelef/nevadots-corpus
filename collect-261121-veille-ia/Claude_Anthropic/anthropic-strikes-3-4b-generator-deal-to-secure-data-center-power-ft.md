# Anthropic strikes $3.4B generator deal to secure data center power: FT

Anthropic ANT agreed to spend $3.4 billion to secure gas-fired generators through Texas supplier OnePWR Solutions, the Financial Times reported. The September transaction covers 1.7 gigawatts of generating capacity and includes engineering and construction capabilities to install the equipment.

Anthropic ANT took over orders OnePWR placed with Caterpillar CAT two years earlier. That secured equipment available soon and over the next two years. Anthropic did not acquire OnePWR, and no employees transferred. Both companies declined to comment.

For investors, the deal highlights how electricity shortages are pushing AI developers to commit billions beyond chips and computing facilities. Controlling power equipment could help Anthropic ANT avoid delays, but adds to its financial commitments as it prepares for a possible stock market listing.

The purchase reflects a broader effort by technology companies to secure electricity directly as equipment backlogs grow. The Financial Times described it as the largest known single purchase of power-generation equipment by an AI lab.

Anthropic ANT has disclosed nearly $900 billion in computing commitments through 2045. The newspaper reported that a listing could come this month, while Blackstone BX warned investors that Anthropic ANT could receive a speculative-grade credit rating.

Anthropic ANT is an artificial intelligence company that develops AI models with an emphasis on safety and reliability. Its Claude brand includes AI models and an assistant that can help users write, analyze documents, conduct research and develop software.
