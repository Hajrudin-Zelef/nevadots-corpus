# Anthropic to train 10,000 AI engineers with $100m programme

Anthropic is launching Claude Frontier Academy, a training programme backed by a $100 million commitment, with the aim of producing 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027.

The company said in an official blog post that the programme addresses a shortage of people able to put AI systems into production inside large organisations. Engineers from Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, and Novo Nordisk are in the first cohorts, which are running in San Francisco, New York, and London.

The academy is modelled on medical training, says Anthropic. Participants learn from practising engineers, work through realistic cases, and are assessed before receiving a credential. The first programme, the Frontier Deployed Engineer Residency, begins with a multi-day in-person session alongside Anthropic engineers, covering a simulated enterprise deployment from use-case selection through security review to handover. Participants then complete a graded practical.

Those who pass earn a Claude Resident Engineer badge and enter a 12-week residency, during which they lead a real Claude project at their own organisation with support from Anthropic engineers. A second assessment follows, with successful candidates earning the Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer badge. The first are expected in early 2027.

Entry is by nomination. Anthropic said it is looking for hands-on software engineers with strong fundamentals and a record of building with large language models. Prior experience with AI agents is not required, though each participant arrives with a named project to lead on their return.

The academy builds on Anthropic's partner network, where professionals across 46,000 firms have earned more than 175,000 Claude certifications. Steve Corfield, the company's global head of business development and partnerships, said a small team with the right skills could transform an entire company, and that no AI company had invested at this depth in developing talent inside its customers and partners.
