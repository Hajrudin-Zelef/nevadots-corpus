# Anthropic’s Claude Tops AI Rankings as OpenAI Limits Cyber Model

The frontier changed hands again this week. Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.2 moved past Google’s Gemini 4 Carbon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, the independent scorecard both companies cite. Meta went the other direction with a model priced at $0.10 per million input tokens, built to do the grunt work while a bigger model plans. OpenAI kept its most capable security model behind a vetting process.
