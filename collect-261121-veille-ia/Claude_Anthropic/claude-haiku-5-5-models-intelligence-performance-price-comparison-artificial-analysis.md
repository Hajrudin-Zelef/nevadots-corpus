# Claude Haiku 5.5: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

Claude Haiku 5.5 (Max) is amongst the leading models in intelligence and well priced when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also slower than average per task and very verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

At 14.9 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Claude Haiku 5.5 (Max) is slower than average compared with the median of 6.5 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
