By Matt Frankel, CFP® –

## Key Points

- Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, above its $70-$78 range.
- At the original share count, the IPO would raise about $68.5 billion, plus a separate $26 billion private placement.
- Reports say the deal was upsized, but the final size and valuation aren’t official yet.

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude family of AI models, priced its initial public offering at $83 per share on Wednesday, according to Reuters, Bloomberg and CNBC, which all cited people familiar with the matter. That's well above the $70-$78 range the company set when it launched its roadshow last week.

Shares are expected to start trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT. Pricing above the range is a strong sign of demand and sets up one of the largest IPOs on record, as well as the first from a major developer of frontier AI models.

Image source: Getty Images.

## **What the $83 IPO price means**

Anthropic originally planned to sell 825 million shares, about 801 million of which would be offered by the company and the remainder from existing shareholders. At $83 apiece, that share count alone would raise about $68.5 billion. Anthropic also lined up a $26 billion private placement with major chip, cloud, and software companies, bringing the combined total to more than $94 billion. And at $83 per share, Anthropic would be valued at approximately $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

## **How Anthropic's IPO compares with SpaceX**

**SpaceX** (SPCX -7.69%) set the IPO record in June, raising $75 billion by selling about 555.6 million shares at $135 each. Including the extra shares sold to underwriters, the total came to about $86.2 billion. At its original share count, Anthropic's IPO would come in slightly below SpaceX's base deal, but it would require only about 10% more shares to match it. With the private placement included, Anthropic's total fundraising would already top what SpaceX raised.

## **What we don't know yet**

A few important details aren't official yet. Bloomberg and Reuters both reported that Anthropic was likely to upsize the offering (sell more shares than originally planned), but Anthropic hasn't disclosed how many shares it's selling. Until it does, we don't know the final amount raised, whether this becomes the largest IPO ever, or the exact valuation. We also don't know whether the underwriters will buy additional shares or where the stock will open on Thursday. As with many hot IPO stocks, the first trade could come well above $83.

I've said before that Anthropic is arguably the most impressive pre-IPO company I've ever seen, and this pricing and reports that it's highly oversubscribed suggest big investors agree. Having said that, a strong first day and a strong long-term investment are two different things. I'm not in a rush to buy, and I'll be watching how the stock trades once the initial excitement fades and how Anthropic manages its enormous computing costs as a public company.
