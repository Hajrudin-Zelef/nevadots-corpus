Anthropic has $807 billion in AI infrastructure deals
Collectively, Anthropic is set to pay as much as $807 billion over the next several years for the infrastructure it needs to run and improve its artificial-intelligence models.
The company has a $44.9 billion deal with Microsoft, as well as a $110 billion agreement with Amazon.com and an up to $120.5 billion deal with SpaceX, it said in a filing. It also has various deals with Advanced Micro Devices (worth at least $20 billion), Nscale (worth $44.6 billion), Lambda (worth $41.3 billion) and Broadcom (worth $161.2 billion).
Anthropic will pay at least $114.5 billion to Alphabet through July 2033. The company said it could spend between $125 billion and $150 billion on computing infrastructure from providers of Google's tensor processor units, or TPUs, that aren't included in its contractual deal with Alphabet.
