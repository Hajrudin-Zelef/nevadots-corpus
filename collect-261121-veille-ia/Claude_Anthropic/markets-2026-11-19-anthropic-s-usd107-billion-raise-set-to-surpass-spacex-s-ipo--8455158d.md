Anthropic’s $107 billion sale set to top SpaceX’s IPO as tokenized stocks gain traction
The maker of Claude priced its Nasdaq debut at $83 a share for a $1.5 trillion fully diluted valuation, with the Nasdaq 100 down 14% from record highs.
- Anthropic raised $81 billion in its IPO at $83 a share. A planned $26 billion private placement takes the total to $107 billion, above the $86 billion SpaceX raised in its listing.
- AI neocloud stocks have fallen as much as 70% from their highs.
- Nasdaq 100 futures rose more than 2% ahead of the debut. Bitcoin gained more than 1% over 24 hours to trade just below $64,000.
Anthropic 
The AI company said it priced the shares at $83 each, giving it $81 billion through an initial sale of stock to the public. Adding a planned $26 billion private placement with strategic investors including Nvidia 
The company and some shareholders sold 980 million shares at $83 apiece, above the $78 top of its initial range. The offering priced Anthropic at a $1.5 trillion fully diluted valuation, with a basic market capitalization just above $1 trillion. Underwriters can sell another 147 million shares, which would add about $12 billion.
Tokenized exposure to the shares will also go live. Changpeng “CZ” Zhao, the founder of crypto exchange Binance, said BNB Chain will carry versions of the stock via bStocks, Ondo and xStocks. A version issued by Backpack Securities will be available on Solana.
