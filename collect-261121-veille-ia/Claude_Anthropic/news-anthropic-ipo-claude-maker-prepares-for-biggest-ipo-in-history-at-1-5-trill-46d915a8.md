Anthropic ANT -8.50% ▼ is set to make history with the largest initial public offering (IPO) on record. The company behind the Claude AI chatbot priced its shares at $83 each, above its initial range. The IPO values Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis. Anthropic is set to begin trading on the Nasdaq NDAQ -0.99% ▼ on November 19 under the ticker symbol ANT.
The company raised $81.3 billion through the sale of 980 million shares. It is also raising another $26 billion through a private placement, taking the total deal size to $107.3 billion. This puts Anthropic ahead of SpaceX SPCX -7.69% ▼, which had set the prior record with its IPO.
Strong Demand Drives Record IPO
The IPO was six times oversubscribed, helping Anthropic price its shares above the top end of its first range. The company had first planned to sell shares at $70 to $78 each, which would have valued it at about $1.4 trillion.
The record IPO comes amid growing concerns about the high cost of AI development and the large sums tech firms are spending on chips and data centers. Anthropic’s public debut will give investors a chance to assess the value of a major AI model maker in the public market.
Claude Growth Drives Revenue Higher
Demand for Claude and Claude Code, Anthropic’s AI tool for software developers, has helped drive rapid business growth.
Anthropic’s third-quarter revenue reached $17 billion, up 47% from the prior quarter and more than 12 times higher than a year earlier. Its annualized revenue run rate (not reported annual revenue) reached $81 billion as of October 31, up $10 billion from September 30.
The company also reported 11,900 customers generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue as of September 30. This compares with more than 1,500 such customers at the end of last year.
High Computing Costs Remain a Key Risk
Despite strong growth, Anthropic faces high costs as it builds and runs its AI systems. The company spent nearly $9 billion on computing and infrastructure in the first half of 2026. It also reported an operating loss of $567 million in the third quarter, compared with operating income of $974 million in the prior quarter.
Anthropic has nearly $900 billion in future computing commitments with partners, including Amazon AMZN -4.10% ▼ and Google GOOGL -2.70% ▼. These costs will be important to watch as the company expands its AI services.
What Is the Forecast for ANT Stock?
Currently, three research firms have initiated coverage of ANT stock with Buy ratings and bullish price targets. Needham analyst Scott Berg set a $150 target, while New Street Research’s five-star analyst Pierre Ferragu set a $105 target. Citizens JMP analyst Patrick Walravens set a $125 target.
Based on the $83 IPO price, these targets point to potential upside of about 81%, 26.5%, and 50.6%, respectively.
