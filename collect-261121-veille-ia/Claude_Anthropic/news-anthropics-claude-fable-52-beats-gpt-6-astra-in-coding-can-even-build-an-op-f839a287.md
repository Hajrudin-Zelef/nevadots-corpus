Anthropic today announced two new frontier AI models, Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, with significant improvements in coding, reasoning, scientific research, and more. The new models are also faster and cheaper than their predecessors, making them more attractive for complex, long-running AI tasks.
As with the previous Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 models, both new models share the same architecture. Fable 5.2 comes with strict cybersecurity and biology safeguards for general users, while Mythos 5.2 offers fewer restrictions for verified organizations working on cybersecurity and biomedical research.
These new models generate output up to 30% faster than the previous generation. They also cost 20% less, with pricing dropping from $10 to $8 per million input tokens and from $50 to $40 per million output tokens. A new optional fast mode delivers up to 2.5 times faster token generation at double the standard API price.
On the FrontierCode 2.0 coding benchmark, Claude Fable 5.2 scored 49.1%, ahead of OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra at 42.8%, Google's Gemini 4 Carbon at 41.1%, and Opus 5.5 at 45.5%. Fable 5.2 also scored 74.2% on Humanity's Last Exam with tools, compared to 57.2% for GPT-6 Astra.

Apart from topping benchmarks, Anthropic highlighted the capabilities of these new models through several real-world tasks. For example, Fable 5.2 coordinated over 1,000 AI agents for eight days to build a working operating system from scratch. It can boot on a laptop and includes device drivers, file storage, a windowing system, and an installer. Also, a team of 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalized the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in just 30 hours, compared to 11 days for an earlier Claude research model.
Fable 5.2 designed three working electronic devices, including a voice-assistant hub, USB speakerphone, and virtual pet. And these worked without electrical modifications after manufacturing. Anthropic also claimed that Fable 5.2 solved two previously open theoretical physics problems involving black holes.
Claude Fable 5.2 is available now through Claude, Claude Code, the Claude API, AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. Developers can access it using the claude-fable-5-2 model ID. Anthropic is also offering additional Fable usage to eligible paid subscribers this week. Claude Mythos 5.2 is available through Anthropic's Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs, while Enterprise customers can access its capabilities through Claude Security.
