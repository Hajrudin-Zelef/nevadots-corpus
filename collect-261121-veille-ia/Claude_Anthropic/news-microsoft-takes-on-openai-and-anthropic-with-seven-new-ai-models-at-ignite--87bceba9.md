Microsoft's Ignite 2026 event is already in full swing, with a huge emphasis on AI. After announcing more features for Copilot, the company has now introduced a big refresh of its MAI lineup of LLMs. The lineup includes MAI models specialized for coding, cybersecurity, image generation, voice interactions, and more.
Here's the full lineup of the new MAI models:
- MAI Cyber 2 Flash matches GPT-6 Astra’s quality at less than 2% of the cost per task on the CyberGymE2E benchmark.
- MAI Code 2 reaches 98% of the coding quality of GPT-6.1 Sol at about 40% of the price. And against Opus 5.5 it reaches 96% performance for just 5% of the price.
- MAI Code 2 Flash matches of the performance of GPT-6 Luna at 65% of the price, and Haiku 5.5 at 20% of the price.
- MAI Image 2.7 is #1 on Artificial Analysis image editing, outperforming GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (max) at just one fifth of the price.
- MAI Realtime 1 outperforms GPT-Realtime 2.1 and starts at just $0.01 per audio minute.
- Dragon Copilot Transcribe is built on MAI Transcribe 2, the world’s fastest and most accurate multilingual transcription model, and already powers 10 million patient encounters a month — with multilingual error rates halved.
- Decision 1.1 the latest decision model from Microsoft, is 50x faster and 79x cheaper than GPT-Sol 6.1.
Along with the new models, Microsoft also said that its second-generation AI accelerator Maia 200, which was announced earlier this year, is now in production, running its largest models with 1.7 times more tokens per watt. Additionally, the company is announcing MAI Licensed Weights, which allow organizations to customize MAI models and run them in their own Azure environments.
