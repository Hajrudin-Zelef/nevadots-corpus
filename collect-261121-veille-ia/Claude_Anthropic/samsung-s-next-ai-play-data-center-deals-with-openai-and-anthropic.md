## Gumi AI data center prepares for high-density GPU deployments

Samsung SDS is already constructing an AI data center in Gumi, South Korea.

According to its announcement, the facility will initially support 60 megawatts of power capacity, with plans to expand to as much as 120 MW.

Construction is scheduled for completion by the end of 2028.

The facility will use a combination of air and liquid cooling to support high-performance GPU servers. Samsung SDS plans to operate its own GPU infrastructure while also offering colocation services that allow customers to install their own equipment.

Samsung has not confirmed whether Gumi is among the facilities being discussed with OpenAI or Anthropic.
