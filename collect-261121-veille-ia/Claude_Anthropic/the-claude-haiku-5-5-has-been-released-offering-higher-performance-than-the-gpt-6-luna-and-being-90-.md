# The 'Claude Haiku 5.5' has been released, offering higher performance than the GPT-6 Luna and being 90% cheaper than its predecessor.


 Anthropic released its AI model ' **Claude Haiku 5.5** ' on October 7, 2026 (local time). Its performance is a dramatic improvement over its predecessor, 

**Introducing Claude Haiku 5.5 \ Anthropic**

**https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5**

Anthropic's AI models are offered in four categories: the flagship 'Fable/Mythos,' the high-performance 'Opus,' the balanced 'Sonnet,' and the inexpensive and fast 'Haiku.' Claude Haiku 5.5, released this time, is the first update to the Haiku series in a year.

The table below shows the benchmark scores for 'Claude Haiku 5.5', 'Claude Haiku 4.5', 'GPT-6 Luna', and 'Claude Sonnet 5.5'. Claude Haiku 5.5 significantly improved its score compared to Claude Haiku 4.5 and also surpassed the score of GPT-6 Luna.


 Claude Haiku 5.5 is the first in the Haiku series to support adjustable inference levels. The graph below summarizes the results of OSWorld 2.1, which measures the PC performance of AI, with the horizontal axis showing the cost per task and the vertical axis showing the score. Claude Haiku 5.5 has higher performance per cost compared to GPT-6 Luna, and when the inference level is set to High, it can achieve a higher score than Claude Sonnet 5.5 Low at a lower cost.

 


 The third-party organization 

**, the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2**calculated the overall performance of the AI and scored '43,' a significant improvement from Claude Haiku 4.5's score of '17.'


 Anthropic suggests using Claude Haiku 5.5 in combination with Claude Opus 5.5 or Claude Sonnet 5.5. The following post includes a demo video demonstrating that combining Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Haiku 5.5 enables faster and lower-cost processing compared to using Claude Opus 5.5 alone.

 Haiku 5.5 is now available in the Claude Platform and Claude Code. On average, it costs around 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5.

 It pairs well with Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5 as a subagent. Use it for high-volume, cost-sensitive tasks like summaries, compactions, or database queries. https://t.co/H7XCCT5F19 pic.twitter.com/RB9xCWo4Db



 Claude Haiku 5.5 is available on all plans, including the free plan. It can also be used via API, with pricing varying depending on whether the prompt length is less than or equal to 100,000 tokens.
