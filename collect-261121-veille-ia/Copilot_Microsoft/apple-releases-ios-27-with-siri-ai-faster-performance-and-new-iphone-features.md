Apple has released iOS 27 for compatible iPhones, bringing Siri AI, major Apple Intelligence upgrades, faster system performance, improved Photos editing, Safari automation, new child safety controls, and several smaller changes across iPhone. The public release follows iOS 27 RC build 24A435, which Apple sent to developers on September 9.

iOS 27 is available as a free update for the iPhone 11 and later, along with the iPhone SE 2nd generation and newer. However, several of its biggest AI features require newer hardware, starting with the iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro Max.

## Siri AI is the biggest change in iOS 27

Siri AI is Apple’s redesigned assistant powered by the latest generation of Apple Intelligence. It supports more natural conversations and can maintain context across follow-up questions instead of treating every request separately.

Siri can also use information stored across apps to find relevant emails, photos, messages, notes, and other content. For example, you can ask Siri to locate an old vacation photo without knowing exactly when you took it, or find details buried inside an email.

The major Siri AI features include:

- Natural back-and-forth conversations with typed or spoken prompts
- Personal context from Mail, Messages, Photos, Notes, and other apps
- On-screen awareness for answering questions about what you are viewing
- Actions inside apps such as Messages, Music, Reminders, Calendar, and Photos
- Web-based answers using current information
- Write with Siri for drafting and improving text
- Siri mode inside Camera with Visual Intelligence
- A dedicated Siri app with conversation history

The new Siri app also syncs conversation history through iCloud, allowing a conversation started on one supported Apple device to continue on another.

Siri AI launches as a beta and initially supports English. Apple plans to add French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish in October.

## Apple Intelligence gets several new tools

Apple Intelligence now reaches deeper into Photos, Safari, Shortcuts, Image Playground, calls, and other parts of iOS.

| Feature | What it does | 
|---|---|
| Spatial Reframing | Changes the framing of a photo after capture | 
| Extend | Expands the edges of an existing image | 
| Enhanced Clean Up | Removes larger unwanted objects from photos | 
| Image Playground | Adds higher-quality images, including photorealistic styles | 
| Call Context | Shows useful information from your apps during business calls | 
| Safari Notify Me | Watches webpages for changes such as restocks or price drops | 
| Smart tab grouping | Automatically organizes Safari tabs by topic | 
| Shortcuts creation | Creates automations from a natural-language description | 

For example, Safari Notify Me can monitor a product page and alert you after a price change or restock, while Shortcuts can build multi-app automations from a written instruction rather than requiring every action to be added manually.

Some Apple Intelligence functions rely on Apple’s server models and are subject to usage limits.

## iOS 27 also makes the iPhone faster

Apple has also focused heavily on performance and system responsiveness this year.

According to Apple’s testing, iOS 27 delivers:

- Up to 30% faster app launches
- Up to 70% faster loading for newly captured photos
- Up to 80% faster AirDrop transfers
- Smoother switching between Wi-Fi and cellular connections
- Improved search ranking inside Mail
- CPU scheduling and other system-level performance improvements

Liquid Glass also receives another round of refinements. Apple has improved contrast and readability, sharpened app icons, and added a new slider that lets users adjust the interface between a clearer appearance and a more heavily tinted look.

## More new features in iOS 27

Several other changes are spread across Apple’s apps and services:

- iCloud Shared Albums now make it easier for Android and Windows users to participate, with full-resolution photo support.
- Health adds perimenopause and menopause support to Cycle Tracking.
- Maps improves Flyover using aerial imagery combined with Visual Intelligence models.
- AirPods settings gain Custom EQ controls for adjusting lows, mids, and highs.
- Home gains smarter video search and AI-generated activity summaries for supported cameras.
- GymKit comes directly to iPhone for compatible cardio equipment.
- Communication Safety expands protections against violent and graphic images and videos.
- Parents can set app schedules, time allowances, approved apps, and website permissions.

Apple has also added more accessibility tools, including improved VoiceOver image understanding and automatic captions for video.

## How to install iOS 27

Before installing, back up your iPhone and make sure you have a stable internet connection. Then:

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone.
2. Tap **General** .
3. Select **Software Update** .
4. Wait for iOS 27 to appear.
5. Tap **Update Now** or**Download and Install** .
6. Enter your passcode if requested and let the iPhone restart to complete installation.

iOS 27 is one of Apple’s largest iPhone software updates in recent years, particularly for users with Apple Intelligence-compatible hardware. Siri AI is the headline addition, while the Photos, Safari, performance, connectivity, Health, Home, and Liquid Glass changes extend the upgrade across the rest of the system.
