# iOS 27: 20 Hidden Features Worth Turning On First

- iOS 27’s useful changes extend beyond Siri AI, including a Liquid Glass appearance slider, automatic Safari tab groups, password repairs, Custom EQ for AirPods, and smoother network handoffs
- Several headline features require Apple Intelligence hardware, while performance, accessibility, safety, and interface improvements reach the wider compatibility list
- This guide explains where to find each feature, what it changes, and which settings are worth leaving alone

Siri AI owns the billboards, but iOS 27's best everyday improvements are scattered through Settings, Safari, Passwords, Photos, AirPods, accessibility, and parental controls. Some save time immediately; others are worth knowing before an alert appears at the least convenient moment.

These 20 features come from Apple's iOS 27 preview. Availability can vary by device, language, and region, and the intelligence items require compatible hardware.

## 1–5: Make the interface work for you

1. **Tune Liquid Glass.** A new appearance slider moves the interface from clearer glass to a more tinted, higher-contrast look. Choose readability over novelty.
2. **Use automatic Safari tab groups.** Safari can organize open tabs by topic, useful when one window has become a museum of unfinished research.
3. **Ask Safari to watch a page.** Notify Me can monitor supported pages for changes such as a restock or price movement.
4. **Search Mail by relevance.** A new ranking system pushes likely matches above simple chronological results.
5. **Try smoother network transitions.** iPhone can hand activity between Wi-Fi and cellular more gracefully when leaving home or moving through weak coverage.

## 6–10: Fix small annoyances

1. **Repair weak passwords.** Passwords can flag compromised credentials and, on supported sites, help update them with fewer steps.
2. **Adjust AirPods Custom EQ.** Compatible AirPods settings expose controls for lows, mids, and highs.
3. **Share full-resolution Shared Album photos.** Improved iCloud Shared Albums preserve more quality and make contribution easier for Android and Windows users through iCloud.com.
4. **Use translated video captions.** Accessibility captioning can generate synchronized subtitles and translate existing captions in supported languages.
5. **Connect GymKit from iPhone.** Compatible cardio machines can send live workout metrics directly to the phone.

## 11–15: Put Apple Intelligence to work

1. **Describe a Shortcut.** Explain an automation in plain language and let Shortcuts connect the actions.
2. **Pin Siri AI conversations.** The dedicated Siri app keeps a history and can continue a conversation across compatible devices.
3. **Change Siri's pace and expression.** Voice customization goes beyond choosing an accent.
4. **Use Suggestions in Messages and Mail.** Contextual buttons can turn a conversation into a reminder, note, event, or photo search.
5. **Try Call Context.** When contacting a business, iPhone can surface relevant information such as a confirmation code from Mail.

## 16–20: Photos, family, and the home

1. **Reframe a photo after capture.** Spatial Reframing can adjust composition on supported hardware.
2. **Extend an image.** Photos can generate believable content beyond the original frame.
3. **Approve websites in Messages.** Ask to Browse lets children request access and gives parents a preview before approving.
4. **Set Time Allowances by category.** Parents can define age-guided limits for games, social media, and entertainment rather than managing every app separately.
5. **Search HomeKit video by what happened.** Apple Intelligence can summarize related alerts and locate supported camera clips using natural descriptions.

## Three settings we would not rush to change

Do not disable security warnings because they are inconvenient, turn every notification into an intelligence summary before checking accuracy, or max out transparency if text becomes harder to read. The point is a calmer phone, not twenty toggles changed for sport.

Apple says app launches can be up to 30% faster, new Photos items up to 70% faster, and AirDrop transfers up to 80% faster in its testing. Real gains depend on hardware and conditions, but the focus on fundamentals is welcome.
