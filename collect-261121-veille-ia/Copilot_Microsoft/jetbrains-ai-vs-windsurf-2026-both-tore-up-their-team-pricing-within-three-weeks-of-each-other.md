We track pricing and features for 11 AI coding tools and re-check every claim against the vendor's own page. JetBrains AI and Windsurf (now Devin Desktop) are an odd pairing to begin with — a JetBrains-IDE completion assistant against an agentic coding tool from Cognition — but this month they share something unusual: both restructured how teams buy them, three weeks apart.

**JetBrains AI, 19 October 2026** — the second restructure in six weeks. The flat Personal/Commercial seat licence is gone entirely. Individuals now get a free tier (3 AI credits/mo) or paid Pro ($10/mo, 10 credits) and Ultimate ($30/mo, 35 credits). Organizations no longer buy named per-seat licences at all — they buy shared usage credits at API price, plus an optional "Air Governance" layer (Basic free, Business $20/seat/mo, Enterprise custom) for admin controls. Our comparison page already reflects this correctly — it's confirmed against jetbrains.com/store/ai/ and needed no correction for this piece.

**Windsurf (Devin Desktop), 12 November 2026** — this is the part our own comparison page doesn't say yet. Cognition relaunched the Devin Teams plan: Desktop access no longer requires a paid seat for every team member, the "quota seat" model is being retired within 30 days, the seat cap drops from 200 to 100, and Cognition added per-user usage tiers with an approval workflow, SSO via Okta/Entra/any SAML-OIDC, a self-serve BAA, and a startup credit program worth up to $65k. Free SWE-2 sessions (2 concurrent per user) also become permanent across Teams, Pro, and Max — not just a promo. (source) The headline price doesn't move — Teams is still $80/month base plus $40/seat — but the thing you'd actually be signing up for changed underneath that number, and the quota-seat retirement has roughly a 30-day shelf life from the announcement.

That combination is worth sitting with for a second: a reader comparing these two tools for a team purchase this week would get quoted JetBrains' new credit-pool model accurately, but Windsurf's outgoing seat structure — not the one Cognition is actively dismantling.

For individuals, nothing above changes what you'd pay: JetBrains AI Pro is still $10/mo, Windsurf Pro is still $20/mo. For a team buyer, treat the Windsurf Teams terms as in transition for the next month, and expect the quota-seat language to disappear from Cognition's own pricing page before it disappears from ours.
