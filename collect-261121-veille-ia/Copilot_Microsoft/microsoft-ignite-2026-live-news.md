					## Microsoft Perception: Security at every level of the cyber stackThe Microsoft cyber stack has three layers: signals, sensors and actuators; context; and agents, harnesses and models. With the latest threats, innovation is required at every level. That’s where **Microsoft Perception** comes in, which Satya is now announcing as generally available.Perception brings the top of the stack together. It orchestrates blue, red and green team agents through playbooks, working together in a closed loop to secure your systems. Perception is built to help security teams move at machine speed, drive outcomes rather than tasks and automate while staying in control. Users can set guardrails and sign off on high-impact actions, while automated triggers launch playbooks the moment an incident meets the criteria you set. To help drive deeper, interoperable visibility over how models and agents are deployed across enterprises, Satya is also announcing the AI Transparency Initiative. The Initiative is an industry effort, shaped with customers and partners, to establish a common, open approach to AI transparency, security and governance that works across models and providers.

					## Agent 365 Business Analytics: AI spend, the smart wayMany organizations are rightfully concerned about the cost of deploying AI tools. A recent study showed that AI spending can nearly quadruple as organizations move to enterprise-wide option, with 93% reporting they’ve exceeded their AI budgets. So fiscal responsibility and transparency is paramount. Satya is now talking about Agent 365, FinOps for AI. It helps organizations not only manage and control AI spend, but also understand the value those investments create. IT leaders need control through management, governance and security. Business leaders need visibility into spend, adoption and business outcomes. **Agent 365 Business Analytics** is a new, dedicated experience that helps business leaders understand their spend, drive business value and optimize ROI. Leaders can identify key cost drivers and assess value through measures such as success rate, user satisfaction and assisted hours. They can explore recommendations to guide investment decisions, ask questions with agentic Q&A, create custom reports and extend analysis into existing reporting tools and data lakes.**Agent 365 Cost Management** gives IT a central place to see and control advanced AI. Admins can understand what’s driving spend, set spending policies and get proactive alerts. These new capabilities now take IT beyond guardrails, helping them forecast and optimize spend so they can scale AI with confidence.
15. 
					## New Microsoft models for coding, cybersecurity and decision-makingThe next frontier of model building is about getting the most intelligence out of every model, for every task, at the best possible price. Microsoft is committed to building the best systems for turning intelligence into reliable, economical work, with customers staying in control of their models, costs and data. Satya is now sharing that Microsoft is launching new models that demonstrate this approach across three areas: coding, cybersecurity and decision-making. Each is purpose-built for the domain and designed to work with the harnesses (a.k.a. the orchestration layer), tools and products around it. The flagship family of agentic coding models is led by **MAI****Code 2** , which excels at complex, long horizon tasks, multi-file work and can reason across an entire repo.**MAI****Cyber 2 Flash** is our most capable security model, with 12 billion active parameters – delivering performance within a few points of larger cyber models at a fraction of the price.Finally, there’s the decision-making aspect. Agentic systems make many small decisions for every task they complete: which tool should handle this request? Is this ticket urgent? Did that step succeed? Sending every one of those to a frontier model is slow and expensive. **Microsoft-Decision-1.1** is a fast, cost-effective model built for the decisions that keep AI systems flowing: routing, classification, prioritization, verification and workflow control.
16. 
					## Microsoft IQ makes the most of organizational expertiseIn today’s fast-paced business climate, many companies have access to frontier models. But gaining true value from AI occurs when it benefits from the unique knowledge inside your organization, curated by the people who know your business best and shared across the entire company. Satya is now talking about **Microsoft IQ** , a new enterprise context management platform that allows users to build plugins that capture their know-how, context, skills and tools, creating their unique IQ and compounding their intelligence over time.It brings together Work IQ, Fabric IQ, Foundry IQ and Web IQ, in addition to over 350 third-party connectors to create an organization’s enterprise context. This gives AI a governed understanding of the organization, so you don’t have to explain how your business works every time you build an agent. In Microsoft IQ, we are also bringing together the capabilities from GitHub, Foundry and Fabric to build, run and continuously improve the apps and agents that put this intelligence to work. Improvements made for one business process become assets your organization can apply to the next update. That means the people who know their subject area the best are providing critical information to point agents to the right documents, models and sources. Think about a manufacturer who gives agents what they need for predictive maintenance and quality root-cause analysis. Or a healthcare provider who can steer prior authorization and policy guidance. In every case, one governed business plugin is reused across the agents, teams and platforms.

					## Microsoft, Salesforce announce Salesforce in Copilot, Teams and FabricSatya is now announcing Copilotforce, new integrations of Salesforce across Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft Teams and Microsoft Fabric. This brings Salesforce customer context, business processes and actions into the Microsoft tools people use every day. **Salesforce in Copilot** is a new plugin now available in preview. It features more than 38 prebuilt sales skills and connects customer context with the work happening across the business. From within Copilot, sellers can securely retrieve customer information and take supported business actions within their existing Salesforce permissions through authenticated Salesforce MCP access. Sellers can then put that context to work across Microsoft 365 with Work IQ, the intelligence layer powering Copilot.

Collaboration is at the core of the new Copilot, creating a connected system where people and AI work together with the context, choice and control organizations need to get more done. **Home** , where**Chat**  and**Cowork** come together, and**Office in Copilot** , which fully integrate**Word** ,**Excel** and**PowerPoint**  into Copilot, are now both generally available.
