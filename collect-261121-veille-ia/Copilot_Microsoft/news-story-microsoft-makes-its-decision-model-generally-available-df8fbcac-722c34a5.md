Microsoft makes its Decision model generally available
- Microsoft announced general availability of Decision-1.1 on November 17, 2026, now available in Microsoft Foundry, OpenRouter, and Vercel.
- More than 4,000 customers have used it since its public preview in October 2026 for routing and classification.
- It scores 68 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 — six points higher than its predecessor — and runs more than twice as fast at the same cost.
- The model adds multimodal support for images like photos and screenshots, in addition to text, for routing, classification, prioritization, verification, and workflow control.
- It outperforms Jev 1.14, Google’s Gemini Decision 1, and OpenAI’s GPT-6 Luna (Decisions) on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1.
- The update builds on Decision-1, which entered public preview in October 2026 and is used internally by Microsoft for routing, data labeling, personalization, and content filtering.
