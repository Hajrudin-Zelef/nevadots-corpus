# Cursor Pro vs Teams vs Usage Pricing on The X Subscription

Cursor Pro and the old Teams plan are closed to new customers. Compare X Subscription individual tiers with $30, $100 and $200 team seats, and budget third-party models at API list price on top.

Updated

In short

Cursor Pro and the old Teams plan are no longer sold to new customers. Since October 12, 2026, Cursor is bought through The X Subscription, which also covers Grok and X Premium benefits, so the decision is now between an individual tier and team seats, plus how much you will pay for third-party models outside the plan. Premium, at $30 a month, is the individual tier that replaces Cursor Pro.

## Individual tiers versus team seats

|  | Individual plan | Team seat | 
|---|---|---|
| Tiers and prices | Plus $8, Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200 a month | Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200 per user a month; Plus not available | 
| Weekly pool | One pool for the person, shared with their Grok use | One pool per seat; seats do not share | 
| Third-party models | API list price from the person's prepaid credit (Premium and up) | API list price from the team's shared prepaid balance, plus a $0.25 per 1M-token Cursor Token Rate | 
| Admin and security | None | SSO, admin controls, team-wide privacy settings, custom Chat retention, usage analytics | 
| Billing | Personal card | Centralized billing; seats can be added mid-cycle at a prorated price | 
| Extras | Cloud agents, Origin hosting and Bugbot from Premium | Same, plus a team marketplace for rules, skills and plugins | 

A team seat costs the same as the matching individual tier, so the team plan adds administration rather than a price premium. According to SpaceXAI, a new seat is cheaper than an older Cursor Teams seat and comes with three times its coding usage.

## When individual plans are enough

- One developer, or a few freelancers who each pay for their own tools.
- No requirement for SSO, enforced privacy settings or central invoices.
- Work that mostly uses Grok models or Composer, so third-party costs stay small.

Plus at $8 is the cheapest way into Cursor's agent, but it is limited to Grok models and has no cloud agents or Bugbot. Most professional developers start at Premium.

## When a team plan is worth it

- Several developers working on a commercial codebase, where offboarding, SSO and privacy settings need to be controlled centrally.
- A finance team that wants one invoice and a shared prepaid balance with spending caps.
- Mixed usage: heavy agent users can hold Super or Ultra seats while everyone else stays on Premium, because tiers can be mixed in one team.

Enterprise is the next step when the organization needs one usage pool shared by everyone, invoice or purchase-order billing, SCIM, audit logs, and repository, model and MCP access controls. Its price is set with sales.

## Where usage pricing adds to the bill

The subscription covers first-party usage up to the weekly pool. Two kinds of spend come on top:

1. **Third-party models** , such as Claude Sonnet 5.5 at $2 input and $10 output per million tokens, are paid from prepaid credit from the first request. On team plans each request also carries the $0.25 per million-token Cursor Token Rate. An Agent request that reads 40,000 tokens and writes 2,000 tokens costs about $0.10 on Sonnet 5.5 before that rate.
2. **First-party usage beyond the weekly pool** is also paid from prepaid credit until the pool refreshes.

A monthly spending cap limits both and can stop paid usage even when credit remains. Cursor usage limits explained covers the pool, top-ups and caps in detail.

## The older Cursor Pro, Pro+, Ultra and Teams plans

Existing subscribers keep these plans until they switch or cancel:

| Old plan | Price | How usage worked | 
|---|---|---|
| Pro | $20/month | Two monthly pools: Cursor models and third-party models at API prices | 
| Pro+ | $60/month | 3x the Pro Agent limits | 
| Ultra | $200/month | 20x the Pro Agent limits | 
| Teams Standard / Premium | $40 / $120 per user a month | Per-user included usage; Premium seats had 5x the Standard limits | 

On those plans, usage beyond the included amount is billed on demand in arrears. Switching to The X Subscription cancels the old plan with a prorated refund, ends its included third-party usage and cannot be undone. A team that relies heavily on Claude or GPT should price that usage at API list prices, plus the Cursor Token Rate, before switching. The X Subscription explained walks through the switch decision.

## Common questions

## What replaced Cursor Pro?

Premium on The X Subscription, at $30 a month. SpaceXAI says it carries the benefits of the older SuperGrok, Cursor Pro and X Premium+ plans, and it adds four times the Plus usage pool, cloud agents, Bugbot and third-party models at API list price.

## Is a Cursor team seat more expensive than an individual plan?

No. Team seats cost the same as the matching individual tier: $30 for Premium, $100 for Super and $200 for Ultra per user a month. The team plan adds SSO, admin controls, centralized billing and a shared prepaid balance.

## Does unused usage transfer between teammates?

No. Each seat on The X Subscription has its own weekly pool, and seats do not share allowances. Enterprise is the route with one pooled allowance across the organization.

## Do team plans pay extra for third-party models?

Yes. On team plans each third-party model request carries a Cursor Token Rate of $0.25 per million tokens on top of the API list price. Individual plans and first-party models such as Grok and Composer are exempt.

## Can I keep my Cursor Pro or Teams plan?

Yes. Existing plans continue until you switch or cancel. Switching cancels the old plan with a prorated refund, ends its included third-party usage and cannot be undone.

## When should a team move to Enterprise?

When it needs one usage pool shared by everyone, invoice or purchase-order billing, SCIM, audit logs, or repository, model and MCP access controls. Enterprise pricing is set with sales.
