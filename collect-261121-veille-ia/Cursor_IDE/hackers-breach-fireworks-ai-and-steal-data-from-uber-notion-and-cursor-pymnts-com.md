# Hackers Breach Fireworks AI and Steal Data From Uber, Notion and Cursor

Uber, online productivity startup Notion and artificial intelligence coding startup Cursor said they had data stolen during a hack of Fireworks AI, a company that lets developers access and use open-weight AI models, TechCrunch reported Monday (Nov. 16).
