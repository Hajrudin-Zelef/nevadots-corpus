Everyone has an opinion on how many data centers are being built. Far fewer people could tell you what is actually inside one.

That's a problem, because the answer to the second question is quickly becoming the answer to the first. Over the past year the market obsessed over **Power** (can you get electricity?) and **Planning** (will the town let you build?). Now, according to Morgan Stanley, the next debate is **Price**: what it costs to build the thing in the first place. And on that front the numbers are, well, moving in only one direction: the all-in cost of an average US data center is now **$49 billion per gigawatt**, according to BofA's latest annual "Who Makes the Data Center" primer, ***up $20 billion from just three years ago***.
