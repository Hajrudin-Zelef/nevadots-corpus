# SpaceXAI offers to buy 418 Mississippi homes, but sellers must drop noise lawsuit claims

● 24 days ago
SpaceXAI, through its subsidiary MZX Tech, is offering to buy 418 homes in Southaven, Mississippi, near the gas-turbine power plant that feeds its data centres. Sellers must give up their claims in a pending noise class action against the company, The Guardian reports.
A letter from Burch Realty Group says sellers will be unable to recover damages from the lawsuit. The offers are reportedly based on Zillow estimates, and at least 18 owners have agreed to sell.
Residents sued in June 2026, estimating that more than 10,000 people live in the affected area. The company denies the allegations, and a court conference on class certification is scheduled.
Renter Danielle Dillard was given 60 days to leave after her landlord took the buyout, with no moving costs offered. Mayor Darren Musselwhite called the programme "totally voluntary.
