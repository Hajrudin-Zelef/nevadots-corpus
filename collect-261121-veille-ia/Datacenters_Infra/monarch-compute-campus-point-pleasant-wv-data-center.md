## The NO₂ chart and notes

### What else could explain it

- Cities, highways, oil and gas fields and power plants in or near the box also emit NO<sub>2</sub> .
- Weather moves NO<sub>2</sub> from day to day (wind, sunlight, cloud), and it builds up in winter.

## Method

How this works: the Sentinel-5P satellite measures NO<sub>2</sub> in the air over every point on Earth once a day, around 1:30 p.m. local time, in pixels about 5.5 by 3.5 km. NO<sub>2</sub> comes from burning fuel (turbines, engines, traffic, power plants), so a rise over a site that open country does not share points to new combustion there. The site box is about 15 km across; the background box is open country about 58 km away.
