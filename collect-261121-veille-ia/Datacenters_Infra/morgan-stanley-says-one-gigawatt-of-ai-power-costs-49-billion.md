⚠ Regulatory Alert

        Florida's PSC voted 5-0 on November 3 to reject Duke Energy's data center tariff plan. Staff found it broke SB 484 — the state law that says data centers must bear their own power costs. Duke must refile before it can serve any new large-load clients.

       Florida isn't alone. In September, North Carolina AG Jeff Jackson pushed state rule-makers to create a new rate class for Duke Energy's data center clients. His point: home users shouldn't pay for data center power.
