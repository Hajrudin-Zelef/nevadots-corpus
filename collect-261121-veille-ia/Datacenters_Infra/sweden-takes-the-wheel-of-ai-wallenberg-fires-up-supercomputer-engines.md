# Sweden takes the wheel of AI: Wallenberg fires up supercomputer engines | List of hardware components | Cpu hardware examples | Cpu hardware list | Turtles AI

*22 August 2025*


**The Wallenberg Group, together with giants such as AstraZeneca, Ericsson, Saab, and SEB, has announced the creation of Sferical AI, a new company to develop an advanced AI infrastructure in Sweden. This project aims to boost the competitiveness of Swedish industry through a secure and integrated platform, supported by the computing power provided by NVIDIA.**

**Key Points:**

- Sferical AI is a joint venture between Wallenberg Investments, AstraZeneca, Ericsson, Saab, and SEB.
- The company will operate a sovereign AI supercomputer located in Linköping, powered by two NVIDIA DGX SuperPODs with 1,152 advanced GPUs.
- The project is the result of a collaboration with NVIDIA, which also includes the opening of an AI Technology Center in Sweden.
- Jenny Nordlöw has been appointed CEO of Sferical AI, with Anders Ynnerman as Executive Chairman.


In the heart of Sweden, in Linköping, a new entity destined to redefine the boundaries of AI will be born: Sferical AI. This joint initiative between Wallenberg Investments, AstraZeneca, Ericsson, Saab, and SEB represents a significant step towards the country’s technological self-sufficiency. The goal is to provide Swedish companies with an advanced, secure, and integrated AI infrastructure that can support the development and deployment of complex models.

At the heart of this ambitious project is a sovereign AI supercomputer, powered by two NVIDIA DGX SuperPODs equipped with 1,152 advanced GPUs. This computing power will enable participating companies to tackle complex challenges in various sectors, from healthcare to defense, from finance to industry. The choice of Linköping as its location is no coincidence: the city is already a recognized technology hub and offers a favorable environment for innovation.

The collaboration with NVIDIA, announced in May 2025, is not limited to the supply of hardware. The American company will also open an AI Technology Center in Sweden, designed to support training and AI skills development. This center will serve as a hub for Swedish companies and help consolidate Sweden’s position as a leader in the European AI ecosystem.

The appointment of Jenny Nordlöw as CEO of Sferical AI and Anders Ynnerman as Executive Chairman underscores the importance of experienced and visionary leadership. Both bring extensive experience in technology and science, essential elements to lead an initiative of this magnitude. Their joint vision aims to create a collaborative environment that fosters innovation and sustainable growth.

Sferical AI is not only a response to current technological challenges, but also a preparation for future opportunities. With the AI market in Sweden expected to grow 26.24% annually through 2031, the initiative is part of a journey of expansion and development. Investing in a sovereign AI infrastructure not only strengthens the competitiveness of Swedish companies but also ensures greater technological autonomy in an increasingly interconnected world dependent on advanced technologies.

*With Sferical AI, Sweden is preparing to chart new paths in the AI landscape, consolidating its role at the forefront of European technological innovation.*
