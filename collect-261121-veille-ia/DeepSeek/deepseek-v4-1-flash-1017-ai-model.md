# DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017

Open-weight MoE update to DeepSeek V4.1-Flash with 1M context, better agentic coding scores and shorter outputs.

## Model Overview

DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017 is an October 17, 2026 update to DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash. It is a sparse mixture-of-experts model with about 8B active parameters in prefill and 16B in decode, a 1M-token context and image-text input. DeepSeek reports gains over the September Flash on all 11 published benchmarks, including 77.0 on DeepSWE v1.1 and 42.6 on HLE without tools, with shorter outputs and a smaller KV cache. Weights are MIT-licensed on Hugging Face.
