Along the sidelines of the U.S.-Saudi Investment Forum, Amazon Web Services, Inc. (AWS<\/a>) and HUMAIN<\/a>, a Public Investment Fund (PIF<\/a>) company, announced their plans to provide, deploy, and manage up to 150,000 AI accelerators in a data center facility known as an \u201cAI Zone\u201d in Riyadh<\/a>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n As part of the expanded partnership, AWS will become HUMAIN\u2019s preferred AI<\/a> partner globally, and the two companies will collaborate to bring AI compute and services from Saudi Arabia<\/a> to customers worldwide.<\/p>\n\n\n\n The first-of-a-kind AI Zone in Saudi Arabia will support cutting-edge AI training and inference workloads with access to the latest NVIDIA GB300 AI infrastructure and AWS\u2019s Trainium AI chips.<\/p>\n<\/blockquote>\n\n\n\n The infrastructure will support a broad range of compute-intensive AI workloads, including model training and running inference for AI applications. This will enable customers to rapidly move from concept to production while responsibly leveraging NVIDIA<\/a> infrastructure and AI software seamlessly integrated with AWS infrastructure and services.<\/p>\n\n\n\n This industry-leading, accelerated computing infrastructure\u2014delivered with the security, scale, and reliability required to run AI workloads with confidence\u2014will establish Saudi Arabia\u2019s first AI Zone as one of the most modern and innovative in the world.<\/p>\n\n\n\n The AI Zone will support global enterprises and technology innovators and will serve both Saudi Arabia’s national AI needs and the rapidly growing global demand for compute. The availability of specialized AWS generative AI<\/a> services, including Amazon Bedrock, Amazon AgentCore, and Amazon SageMaker, will give customers immediate access to leading foundation models through a single platform. Amazon Bedrock removes the burden of infrastructure management, enabling customers to access foundation models without needing to select or manage the underlying compute infrastructure.<\/p>\n\n\n\n \u201cThe AI Zone represents the beginning of a multi-gigawatt journey for HUMAIN and AWS. From inception, this infrastructure has been engineered to serve both national priorities and the world\u2019s accelerating demand for AI compute,\u201d said Tareq Amin, CEO of HUMAIN.<\/p>\n<\/blockquote>\n\n\n\n \u201cWhat truly sets this partnership apart is the scale of our ambition and the innovation in how we work together. Through a groundbreaking commercial model and a shared commitment to global market expansion, we are creating an ecosystem that will shape the future of how AI ideas can be built, deployed, and scaled for the whole world,\u201d he continued.<\/p>\n\n\n\n To enhance the planned AI Zone\u2019s capabilities, HUMAIN will join the AWS Solution Provider Program, enabling customers to access AWS services through a unified platform to accelerate AI adoption across the region and internationally. This milestone advances the strategic partnership between the two companies and the joint plan announced earlier this year to invest more than USD 5 billion in AI infrastructure, AWS services, and AI training and talent development<\/a> in Saudi Arabia.<\/p>\n\n\n\n “This marks a pivotal moment in our commitment to our partnership with HUMAIN,” said Tanuja Randery, Managing Director, Europe, Middle East & Africa, AWS. “By combining HUMAIN’s local expertise and investment with AWS AI solutions\u2013including our advanced infrastructure, hardware partnerships with NVIDIA, the transformative AI platform Amazon Bedrock, and AI solutions for business users, including Amazon Quick Suite\u2013we’re establishing a world-class innovation hub that will serve customers across Saudi Arabia and around the globe.\u201d He added:<\/p>\n\n\n\n As an AWS Solution Provider, HUMAIN will empower organizations to unlock the full potential of generative AI and drive agentic transformation for businesses and government entities. Together, we’re accelerating digital transformation and fueling economic growth\u2014backed by a deep understanding of local needs and global-scale capabilities.<\/p>\n<\/blockquote>\n\n\n\n This AWS and HUMAIN collaboration is designed to accelerate AI adoption and advance the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia\u2019s goal to become a global AI leader.<\/p>\n\n\n\n Beyond infrastructure, AWS and HUMAIN will collaborate to accelerate AI adoption across the public and private sectors, develop advanced Arabic large language models, including \u201cALLAM,\u201d HUMAIN\u2019s Arabic-first large language model, and create a unified AI agent marketplace for government services.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
