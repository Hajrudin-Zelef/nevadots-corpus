## **Methodology.** How this data is collected.

- Updates
- Aggregated every hour. Days and weeks use UTC.
- Tokens
- Input, output, reasoning, and cached tokens for each request.
- Users and sessions
- Approximate counts of distinct users and OpenCode sessions.
- Cost
- Session cost is the average cost per OpenCode session. Token prices are list prices from the OpenCode model catalog.
- Retention
- The share of a model's users in one week who use it again the next week.
- Citation
- Cite OpenCode Data (opencode.ai/data) with the update time shown at the top of the page.
