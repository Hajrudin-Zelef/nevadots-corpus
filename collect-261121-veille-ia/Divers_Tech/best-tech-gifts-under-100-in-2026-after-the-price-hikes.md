Last year’s list of tech gifts under $100 is partly wrong this year, and not because the gadgets got worse. Memory chips got expensive, the makers passed it on, and some old standbys crossed the $100 line this year. Amazon’s Echo Dot Max launched at $99.99 and went to $119.99 when Amazon raised prices across its hardware line around August 21. Roku’s Streaming Stick Plus was announced at $39.99 and Roku’s store listed it at $59.99 on November 10, 2026; Roku raised prices across its sticks and boxes in late July. The 16GB Raspberry Pi 5 that launched at $120 went to $385 on November 2, 2026.

So this guide starts from the maker’s own list price, checked again on November 10, 2026, and only keeps what still fits. Seven gifts made it: the Apple AirTag 2 ($29), Roku Streaming Stick Plus ($59.99), Anker Nano power bank ($49.99), JBL Go 5 ($54.95), Sony DualSense controller ($74 in White), Fujifilm instax mini 13 ($89.95) and the Logitech MX Master 3S mouse ($99.99 list for the Bluetooth Edition). Sales often go lower.

## Under $30: Apple AirTag 2

The safest gift on this list, as long as the person has an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and an Apple Account; Apple says the new AirTag requires “a compatible iPhone with iOS 26 or later, or iPad with iPadOS 26 or later.” Apple launched the new AirTag on January 26, 2026 “for the same price as its predecessor: $29 for a single AirTag and $99 for a four-pack.” The upgrade is range: Apple says Precision Finding “guides users to their lost items from up to 50 percent farther away than the previous generation,” and the speaker is “50 percent louder than the previous generation,” so you can hear it from up to twice as far. It runs the same second-generation Ultra Wideband chip as the iPhone 17 lineup, and for the first time an Apple Watch Series 9 or later, or an Ultra 2 or later, with watchOS 26.2.1 can run Precision Finding to locate it, though Apple says availability varies by region.

One AirTag is a stocking stuffer. The four-pack at $99 is a proper present: keys, wallet, backpack, suitcase. Holders are extra: Apple says “AirTag accessories, including the AirTag FineWoven Key Ring, are sold separately.” If you want the long version of which tracker fits which bag, the Find My tracker guide covers wallet cards and luggage tags too.

**If they use Android:** buy a Tile Mate instead (Life360’s store now calls it simply “Tile”). Life360’s price is $24.99 for one and $79.99 for four, it has a 350-foot range and a battery Life360 rates for three years, and the app works on both iPhone and Android.  Life360’s checkout also offers a membership that “auto renews at $9.99/mo” after 30 free days.  AirTags need an iPhone or iPad to set up, so an Android household gets a paperweight.

## Under $60: Roku Streaming Stick Plus

This is the gift for the relative whose smart TV takes 40 seconds to open Netflix. The Streaming Stick Plus plugs into an HDMI port, can draw its power from the TV’s own USB port so there is no wall plug to find, and does 4K with HDR10+ on TVs that support it. The remote has TV power, volume and mute buttons, so on most TVs the old remote can go in a drawer.

Now the awkward part. Roku announced this stick at $39.99 in 2025. Roku’s store listed it at $59.99 on November 10, 2026, with a limited-time sale at $49.99. Roku raised prices across its sticks and boxes in late July, and a Roku executive, speaking on background, told The Desk the company made the tough decision to raise prices to address shortages of memory and other components caused by the AI rush. It is still the cheapest 4K stick Roku sells: the Streaming Stick 4K is now $79.99 and the HD-only Streaming Stick is $39.99, on sale at $24.99 on November 10. If the TV is 1080p, the $39.99 HD stick does the job for less.

## Under $60: Anker Nano Power Bank (10,000mAh, 30W)

The 2025 version of this guide recommended a 20,000mAh brick. This year’s pick is half the capacity and a much better gift, because the cable is built in. Nobody has to remember a cord. Anker lists the Nano at $49.99: 10,000mAh, a 30W USB-C cable that both charges the bank and charges the phone, plus a USB-C port and a USB-A port for more devices (24W in total when more than one is charging), at 7.58 ounces. 30W is enough to fast-charge a modern phone, and the little color screen shows the charging status. The built-in cable is USB-C, so it suits a phone with a USB-C port.

Why 10,000 and not 20,000? Weight. A 20,000mAh bank is a second phone in the bag. This one fits in a coat pocket and covers a long day away from an outlet.

## Under $60: JBL Go 5

The Go 5 is the speaker you buy for someone who does not own a speaker. JBL lists it at $54.95. It is the new model in this slot: in a release posted April 1, 2026, JBL announced “the next generation of two of its most popular portable speakers: JBL Xtreme 5 and JBL Go 5.” JBL calls it “IP68 waterproof, dust-proof, and drop-proof,” and rates the battery “for up to 8 hours, plus an extra 2 with Playtime Boost.”

Two of them pair for stereo: JBL says to “bump two Go 5 speakers together to link them up through AirTouch for true stereo sound,” and its Auracast feature links the Go 5 with other JBL speakers that support Auracast, so a second Go 5 next year is a natural follow-up gift. The new “ambient edge lighting” glows in a choice of themes. It comes in a pile of colors, which matters more for a present than any spec.

**To stay under $50:** JBL still sells the older Go 4 at $49.95.  It is rated IP67, dustproof and waterproof, and JBL rates its battery at “up to 7 hours of playtime plus 2 hours with Playtime Boost.”  The spec sheet lists Bluetooth 5.3 and a three-hour charge over USB-C.

## Under $100: Sony DualSense controller

Every PlayStation 5 household wants a second controller and nobody wants to buy their own. PlayStation Direct lists the DualSense at $74 in White, and Sony’s page says it is “Available now from $74.00,” so some colors cost more. Sony’s pitch is “haptic feedback and adaptive triggers,” which in plain terms means the controller shakes in detail and the triggers push back, plus a built-in microphone for online chat, which needs a PlayStation Network account.

The reason it belongs on a gift list rather than a PS5 accessory list: it works on more than a PS5. Sony says it connects by USB-C cable or Bluetooth to Windows PCs, Macs, and Android and iOS phones and tablets. The cable is not in the box: PlayStation Direct says “Cable not included,” and that “Haptic feedback and adaptive triggers may require USB connection,” so add a USB-C cable for a gamer without a PS5. A PC gamer or a phone gamer gets a better controller than most of what sells for the same money. Sony sells it in more than a dozen colors. On November 10, 2026 PlayStation Direct’s color list did not include Midnight Black, and the Amazon link below is Amazon.com’s own listing of the Midnight Black controller packed with a USB cable for PC, which covers the missing cable. ## Under $100: Fujifilm instax mini 13

The instant camera is the one gift on this list that gets opened and used at the party. With a $17.99 specialty pack like Fujifilm’s Pastel Galaxy, at its launch MSRP, the camera at its November 10 MSRP and one pack come to $107.94, and the film alone works out to about $1.80 a print, so treat this as the top of the range.

## Under $100: Logitech MX Master 3S

For anyone who spends eight hours a day at a computer, this is the gift that gets used more than any other on the list. Logitech lists the MX Master 3S Bluetooth Edition at $99.99, and on November 10, 2026 its store had it at $89.99, marked “10% off.” It has an 8,000 DPI sensor that tracks on glass, “Quiet Clicks” that Logitech says cut click noise by 90 percent compared with the older MX Master 3, and a metal electromagnetic scroll wheel that spins through 1,000 lines a second. The Bluetooth Edition has no receiver, so for a computer without Bluetooth, check that the version you buy includes Logitech’s Bolt receiver, and check its price; the original 3S did, per Tom’s Hardware’s review: “One Logi Bolt dongle that’s already pre-paired with your mouse comes in the box.” On Windows and macOS, the Logi Options+ app lets the buttons do different things in Photoshop, Excel and Chrome. It pairs with up to three computers and switches between them with a button, and Logitech rates the battery at up to 70 days per charge, per Tom’s Hardware’s review.

Full disclosure, because a gift guide should say it: there is a newer mouse. Its store still listed it at $119.99 on November 10, 2026. That is about $20 over this list’s line, and the haptics are a nice-to-have, not a reason to skip the 3S, which also has a 1,000-lines-a-second electromagnetic scroll wheel and fits the budget. ## What fell off the list this year

This section is new since the 2025 edition, and it matters because the same thing will keep happening through the holidays. The chips that store data in a gadget, DRAM and flash memory, got much more expensive in 2026 because AI data centers are competing for the same memory-chip factory capacity, and consumer-device makers have started passing the cost on. The site’s breakdown of the AI memory crunch covers the supply side; this is what it did to the gift list.

| Product | Then | Now (date read) | Who raised it, and why | 
|---|---|---|---|
| Amazon Echo Dot Max | $99.99 at launch | $119.99 (Nov 10) | Amazon, after its increase around August 21: “significant increases in memory and storage component costs” | 
| Amazon Echo Dot | $49.99 | $79.99 (Nov 10) | Same Amazon increase | 
| Roku Streaming Stick Plus (still on this list) | $39.99 at announcement | $59.99 list, on sale at $49.99 (Nov 10) | Roku; The Desk reported a late-July rise on its sticks and boxes | 
| Roku Ultra | $100 | $149.99 list, on a limited-time sale at $99.99 (Nov 10) | Same Roku increase | 
| Raspberry Pi 5, 8GB | $80 | $175 after April 2026, per CNX Software; $200 at US reseller Adafruit (Nov 10) | Raspberry Pi, December 2025, February 2026 and April 2026 rises | 
| Raspberry Pi 5, 16GB (never under $100; the extreme case) | $120 at launch | $385 announced November 2, 2026; the product page still said $305 (Nov 10) | Raspberry Pi, four rises since December 2025 | 

The Amazon, Roku and Adafruit prices in the “now” column were checked again on November 10, 2026. Amazon’s own listings showed the Echo Dot Max at $119.99 and the Echo Dot at $79.99 on November 10, and Roku’s store listed the Ultra at $149.99 with a limited-time sale at $99.99. The Echo Dot row comes from Fortune, which reported on August 21, 2026 that Amazon “overnight raised prices across its hardware product line,” moving the Echo Dot from “$49.99 to $79.99,” the Fire TV Stick 4K Max from “$59.99 to $84.99” and the 16GB Kindle from “$109.99 to $149.99,” and quoted Amazon’s statement that the industry is “facing significant increases in memory and storage component costs. After absorbing these increases for as long as we could, we recently adjusted pricing across our product lines.” Amazon’s launch announcement said the Echo Dot Max was “now available for $99.99”; TechTimes’ table of the new prices puts it at $119.99, a 20 percent increase. The basic Echo Dot is still under $100 at $79.99, but it now costs 60 percent more than its $49.99 price before the increase, so it lost its slot.

The Roku Ultra row comes from The Desk, which broke the story in July: Roku “adjusted the manufacturer’s suggested retail price (MSRP) of its Roku sticks and set-top boxes,” the Ultra is “now priced at $150, up from $100,” the basic Streaming Stick is “now priced at $40, up from $30,” and a Roku executive, speaking on background, told the site the company made the tough decision to raise prices to address shortages in computer memory and other components caused by the artificial intelligence rush. Roku confirmed the increases to Engadget. The Streaming Stick Plus on this list was announced at $39.99 in 2025 and Roku’s store showed it at $59.99 on November 10, 2026, with the Streaming Stick 4K at $79.99.

The Raspberry Pi rows are the starkest, and most of it comes from Raspberry Pi’s own blog and store page. The 16GB Pi 5 went on sale January 9, 2025 “priced at $120.” On December 1, 2025 the company raised it to $145, citing “the recent unprecedented rise in the cost of LPDDR4 memory.” On February 2, 2026 it added $60 more, to $205, writing that “price rises have accelerated as we enter 2026, and the cost of some parts has more than doubled over the last quarter,” and calling the situation “ultimately a temporary one” it looks forward to unwinding. On April 1, 2026 came a third rise, which The Register reported as “a $100 hike for the 16 GB Raspberry Pi 5.” On November 2, 2026 Raspberry Pi announced a fourth, effective the same day: “we are raising the price of the 16GB variant of Raspberry Pi 5 by $80, to $385.” Its product page still read “Raspberry Pi 5 16GB is available now for $305” on November 10, 2026. The 8GB board that cost $80 went to $95 in December and $125 in February by the same two posts. The April rise added $50, to $175, according to CNX Software’s table of the new prices. Raspberry Pi’s product page shows only a 16GB price, and US reseller Adafruit listed the 8GB board at $200 on November 10, 2026. A Pi is still a great gift for a tinkerer; just check the current price of the memory size you want before you assume it fits, because the boards with more memory are no longer sub-$100 gifts; even the 4GB board was $130 at Adafruit on November 10, 2026.

The list prices in the picks table are from the makers’ US store pages on November 10, 2026; the instax’s $89.95 is down from its $93.95 launch price. Read this list as correct on that date, and worth a 30-second price check the week you buy.

## A last-minute gift that arrives by email: Audible

If a box can’t arrive in time, an Audible gift membership doesn’t need to. Audible emails the recipient “on the day you choose,” or you can print the gift and hand it over. Each month of the gift adds one credit, good for any title in the app to keep, plus the Plus Catalog of audiobooks, podcasts and originals that comes with the membership.

| Gift length | Price on Amazon’s Audible gift page, November 10, 2026 | Credits | 
|---|---|---|
| 1 month | $15 | 1 | 
| 3 months | $45 | 3 | 
| 6 months | $90 | 6 | 
| 12 months | $150 (over this guide’s $100 line) | 12 | 

Before you buy one:

- **They need an Amazon.com account.** The gift can go to anyone who has one or creates one, and the claim code is redeemed on Audible.com, so check that it will work before sending it abroad.   Gift claim codes “do not expire.”
- **Buy it on the web.** Audible says gifting “is not currently available in the Audible app.”
- **It doesn’t renew.** Audible says gift memberships “do not auto-renew,” and the recipient keeps every book bought with a credit after the gift ends.
- **Credits expire.** Each gift credit expires 12 months after it’s issued, so the clock starts when the credits arrive, not when you buy.
- **Existing members get credits, not more time.** Someone already on Audible gets all the gift’s credits at once, and all of them expire 12 months later; they can cancel their own plan first to get the credits monthly instead. Members on the Standard plan get the credits but not the Plus Catalog, and someone on a free trial has to wait for it to end or cancel it before redeeming.
- **It can’t be undone once used.** A redeemed gift can’t be cancelled or refunded; an unredeemed one can be cancelled through Audible’s customer service.

## Four rules for buying tech gifts in 2026

**1. Ask which phone they have before you buy anything with an Apple logo.** AirTags need an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and an Apple Account.  None of the other six picks needs a particular phone, though the Anker’s built-in cable is USB-C, so it suits a phone with a USB-C port.       The Tile Mate is the Android answer for trackers, and the 3-in-1 charger in the stretch section is for Apple Watch owners.  

**2. Check the maker’s price the week you buy, not the price you remember.** The Echo Dot Max and the 8GB Raspberry Pi 5 crossed $100 in 2026, and Roku raised the prices of its sticks and boxes.     The reverse is also true: on November 10, 2026 Logitech’s store had the MX Master 3S Bluetooth Edition at $89.99 and Roku’s had the Streaming Stick Plus at $49.99, each ten dollars under list.  

**3. Compare any bundle with the plain item.** Before you buy a camera-plus-film kit or a controller-plus-cable pack, check what the pieces cost on their own at the maker’s store, and add film or a case separately if that comes out cheaper.

**4. Check the return window before you buy early.** Roku offers a “30-day money-back guarantee,” Logitech “Free returns within 30 days of your purchase,” PlayStation Direct “Free 30-day returns,” and Life360 and Anker each a 30-day money-back guarantee, so a gift bought in early November can be outside the window by December 25.      Belkin’s own store page for the UltraCharge Pro showed both a “30-Day money back guarantee” banner and “Not eligible for return,” so check before you buy it early.  On the Amazon links, check the return window shown for that seller on the listing before you order.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best tech gift under $100 in 2026?

For most people, the Apple AirTag 2 four-pack at $99, because it solves a problem everyone has and Apple kept the price flat at launch: “$29 for a single AirTag and $99 for a four-pack.” If the person uses Android, the Tile Mate covers the basics from $24.99.

### What is a good tech gift under $50?

The Anker Nano power bank at $49.99 and the older JBL Go 4 at $49.95 both sit just under the line at list price. The power bank is the practical one: 10,000mAh with the USB-C cable built in, so there is no cord to lose. The speaker is the fun one: waterproof to IP67 with a battery JBL rates at up to 7 hours, while the newer Go 5 is $54.95. The under-$50 section adds five more, from the $25.99 Anker Nano Charger Neo to the $39.99 Blink Mini 2K+.

### What tech gift can I get for under $30?

One AirTag 2 is $29 at Apple, and one Tile Mate is $24.99 at Life360. Both are stocking-stuffer sized and both fix the same daily problem.

### Why did tech gifts get more expensive in 2026?

Memory chips. Amazon told Fortune the industry is “facing significant increases in memory and storage component costs,” a Roku executive told The Desk on background that the increase was about shortages of computer memory and other components caused by the AI rush, and Raspberry Pi wrote that “the cost of some parts has more than doubled over the last quarter.” The steepest rise in this guide was the 16GB Raspberry Pi 5, which went from $120 to $385.

### Is the Echo Dot Max still under $100?

No. Amazon launched it at $99.99 and it went to $119.99 in Amazon’s increase around August 21, per TechTimes’ tally of the new prices; Amazon’s own listing still showed $119.99 on November 10, 2026. The basic Echo Dot is still under $100 at $79.99, up from $49.99.

### Should I buy the Logitech MX Master 3S or wait for a deal on the MX Master 4?

Buy the 3S if the budget is $100. Logitech launched the MX Master 4 at $119.99, still its store price on November 10, 2026, and lists the 3S Bluetooth Edition at $99.99. The newer mouse adds haptic feedback, an on-screen shortcut ring and what Logitech calls “2X stronger connectivity,” rather than a better sensor or scroll wheel.
