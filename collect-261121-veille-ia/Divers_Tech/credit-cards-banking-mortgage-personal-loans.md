Online Bill Payment can be used to pay a merchant bill or an individual person (domestic payees only). When you instruct Citibank to make an online bill payment, you authorize Citibank to withdraw the necessary funds from your Citibank account. You agree that you will instruct Citibank to make a payment only when a sufficient balance is or will be available in your account on the day you have scheduled your bill payment. If there are insufficient funds available in your designated account on the day a bill payment is scheduled to be made, Citibank will automatically try to complete it on the next two consecutive calendar days except for July 4, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day. If there are still insufficient funds on the third try, Citibank will cancel the bill payment and notify you.

You should not use Online Bill Payment to pay payees to whom you are obligated for tax payments, payments made pursuant to court orders (including court-ordered amounts for alimony or child support), fines, payments to loan sharks, gambling debts or payments otherwise prohibited by law.

Bill payments made as electronic funds transfer are processed each calendar day except for July 4, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Bill payments made by Official Check are mailed on Business Days only via USPS First-Class Mail. If the payment is made by Official Check, you should allow at least 7 business days for the payee to receive payment. Please note that checks are mailed without a postmark, as Citibank uses pre-printed envelopes with postal permit numbers for mailing. In addition, please be aware that many payees including the IRS and other government agencies do not accept electronic payments initiated from online banking websites.

It is your responsibility to set up and schedule bill payments with sufficient time for your payee to receive your payment on a timely basis. You understand and agree that Citibank reserves the right to impose a frequency or dollar limit on payment requests or to refuse to make any payment you have requested.

There are limits on the amount of money you can send or receive through Online Bill Payment as disclosed in your applicable account agreement.
