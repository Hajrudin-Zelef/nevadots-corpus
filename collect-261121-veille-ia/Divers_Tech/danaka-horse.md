📝 TL;DR 📝 Anthropic launched Claude Marketplace on September 23, 2026, a single hub inside Claude for browsing plugins, connectors, ready-built agents, and consulting partners, launching with more than 2,000 tools already listed. For anyone who uses Claude regularly, this turns "which tool should I connect" from a research project into a browsing decision made inside the same window you already work in.  🧠 Overview 🧠  Claude has supported plugins and connectors for a while, letting the assistant pull in outside tools and data on request. Anthropic's new Marketplace brings all of that, plus fully built agents and even outside consulting firms, into one searchable storefront inside Claude itself. It is Anthropic's clearest move yet toward treating Claude as a platform other companies build on and sell through, rather than a single chat product.  📜 The Announcement 📜  Anthropic announced Claude Marketplace on September 23, 2026. It has three sections: connectors and plugins from companies including Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Notion, and Salesforce, with more than 2,000 available at launch; ready-built agents and products from partners such as Cursor, Harvey, Lovable, and Snowflake; and a directory of consulting and systems integration partners, including Accenture, Boston Consulting Group, and Deloitte, for businesses that want implementation help.  Teams can browse and buy directly inside Claude, and in some cases can put existing committed Anthropic spend toward these purchases rather than paying separately. Two days later, on September 25, Anthropic also opened a developer portal so builders can submit their own plugins for review and track usage once listed.  ⚙️ How It Works ⚙️  • Three sections, one storefront - Connectors and plugins, ready-built agents and products, and consulting partners all sit inside the same Marketplace tab in Claude, instead of being scattered across separate directories.  • Over 2,000 connectors at launch - Tools from major software makers like Google, Microsoft, Notion, and Salesforce are already listed, alongside smaller specialist plugins built by outside developers.
