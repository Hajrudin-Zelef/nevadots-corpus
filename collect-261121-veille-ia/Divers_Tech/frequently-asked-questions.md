# Frequently Asked Questions

## I'm a university researcher. I hear that sometimes you make your source code available free to people like me. How do I get it?

Yes, it's true that we sometimes make the source code of our front ends available to researchers. We decide on a case-by-case basis, but if you are at a university and you are a professor or are working with one, it's pretty likely we would give you access. Please inquire at info@edg.com.
