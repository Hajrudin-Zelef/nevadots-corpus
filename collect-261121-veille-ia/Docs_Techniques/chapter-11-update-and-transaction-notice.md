We wanted to provide an update on the status of the Character’s Chapter 11 case.

On November 4, 2026, the Bankruptcy Court entered an order approving bidding procedures to govern Character’s sale process (the “Bidding Procedures Order”). The Bidding Procedures Order also approved a form of notice (the “Transaction Notice”) containing important information related to the Company’s ongoing sale process. A copy of the Transaction Notice is reproduced below.

For more information relating to the Chapter 11 case, or to obtain copies of motions, orders, including the Bidding Procedures Order, and other documents discussed in the Transaction Notice online, you can visit the website administered by the Company’s claims and noticing agent, Stretto, Inc.: https://cases.stretto.com/chartech/.

IN THE UNITED STATES BANKRUPTCY COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF DELAWARE

In re: CHARACTER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. Case No. 26-11603 (BLS)

 Ref. Docket Nos. 15, 17 & 106__NOTICE OF PROPOSED SALE OF ASSETS, STALKING HORSE TRANSACTION AGREEMENT, BIDDING PROCEDURES, AUCTION, AND SALE HEARING__ 

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that the above-captioned debtor and debtor-in-possession (the “__Debtor__”) filed a voluntary petition for relief under chapter 11 of title 11 of the United States Code, as amended (the “__Bankruptcy Code__”), on October 12, 2026, in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware (the “__Bankruptcy Court__”). The Debtor is seeking to sell, license or dispose of (the “__Sale__”) all or substantially all of its assets (the “__Assets__”) free and clear of all liens, claims, interests, and encumbrances to the fullest extent authorized by section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code. 

PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that in connection with the Sale, the Court has approved the Debtor’s designation as a stalking horse agreement, the Stalking Horse Transaction Agreement, dated as of October 13, 2026 (the “__Stalking Horse Transaction Agreement__”) for the sale or license of certain assets (the “__Disney Target Assets__”) with Disney Worldwide Services, Inc. (“__Disney__”), free and clear of all liens, claims, interests and encumbrances, subject to the Debtor’s acceptance of higher or otherwise better offers in accordance with the Bidding Procedures. 

PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that by order, dated November 4, 2026 [Docket No. 106] (the “__Bidding Procedures Order__”), the Bankruptcy Court approved certain relief requested in the related motion [Docket No. 15] (the “__Bidding Procedures Motion__”), and certain procedures that govern the Sale (the “__Bidding Procedures__”). The Bidding Procedures also contain a process for designation of other Additional Stalking Horse Purchasers and related asset purchase agreements for Assets other than the Disney Target Assets, subject to the Debtor’s acceptance of higher or otherwise better offers in accordance with the Bidding Procedures. 

Copies of the Bidding Procedures Motion, the Bidding Procedures Order, the Bidding Procedures, and the Stalking Horse Transaction Agreement are available for download at **https://cases.stretto.com/chartech** (the “__Case Website__”) from the Debtor’s claims and noticing agent, Stretto, Inc. (“__Stretto__”). Fay (efay@wsgr.com) and Shane M. Reil (sreil@wsgr.com).**

- Following service of a notice of Auction results, parties may raise objections solely with respect to the conduct of the Auction, the Winning Bidder (only if not a Stalking Horse Purchaser), modifications to any Stalking Horse Agreement, and adequate assurance of future performance by a Winning Bidder other than any Stalking Horse Purchaser (each such objection, a “__Supplemental Sale Objection__ ”) at the Sale Hearing.

- transferring the Disney Target Assets free and clear of all liens, claims, interests, and encumbrances to Disney,
- assuming the TSA pursuant to sections 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy Code,
- finding that the Debtor has demonstrated good, sufficient, and sound business purposes to promptly consummate the Transaction, including assumption of the TSA, that doing so is an appropriate exercise of the Debtor’s business judgment and is in the best interests of the Debtor, its estate, its creditors, and other parties in interest, and that certain special committees of the Debtor’s board of directors (the “__Board__ ”), the members of which are each disinterested with respect to the Transaction, led the strategic direction of the marketing process and all negotiations with Disney, and have recommended the Board approve the Transaction, including the Stalking Horse Transaction Agreement, the Exclusivity and Prepayment Agreement, the TSA (including assumption thereof, and in connection with such, the assumption of the Exclusivity and Prepayment Agreement),
- finding that the Debtor, consistent with its fiduciary duties, conducted a robust and extensive marketing process overseen by the Debtor’s special committees both before and after the commencement of this Chapter 11 Case, which was substantively and procedurally fair to all parties and all potential bidders, and afforded a full, fair, and reasonable opportunity for any interested party to make a higher or otherwise better offer to purchase the Disney Target Assets,

**PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE THAT FAILURE TO ABIDE BY THE BIDDING PROCEDURES, THE BIDDING PROCEDURES ORDER OR ANY OTHER APPLICABLE ORDER OF THE COURT ENTERED IN THIS CHAPTER 11 CASE MAY RESULT IN THE REJECTION OF YOUR BID AND YOUR DISQUALIFICATION FROM PARTICIPATING IN THE BIDDING FOR AND AUCTION OF ANY OF THE DEBTOR’S ASSETS.**
