Samsung has officially opened the **One UI 9.5 Beta program** for Galaxy S26 series smartphones across Germany, India, Korea, Poland, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Eligible users in these six markets can apply immediately through the Samsung Members app to test the incremental platform upgrade ahead of its stable release.

The One UI 9.5 update focuses heavily on granular privacy controls, professional camera ergonomics, desktop multitasking in Samsung DeX, and expanded personal wellness tracking in Samsung Health. Alongside dozens of system refinements. This includes native App Lock, simultaneous fingerprint and face authentication, and built-in Office document viewing in My Files. The update also retires Audio brief and Moments from the Now brief panel.

Key Takeaways: What’s New in One UI 9.5 Beta


**Initial Beta Availability:** Open today to Galaxy S26 series owners in Germany, India, Korea, Poland, the UK, and the US via the Samsung Members application.
**Native App Lock & Ultra Privacy Display:** Locks individual apps behind biometrics or PIN/pattern credentials while concealing their notifications and widgets, paired with automatic Privacy Display triggers on Ultra models.
**Simultaneous Biometric Verification:** Enables fingerprint and face recognition to evaluate concurrently without requiring an extra tap to initiate facial scanning.
**Overhauled Camera & Pro Video Modes:** Consolidates default camera quick controls onto a single screen without scrolling and introduces custom buttons, presets, focal-length readouts, and upgraded histograms for Pro modes.
**Samsung Health Expansion:** Debuts Mindful Note journaling, global Medications tracking, single-photo AI meal logging, and a 3-day Sleep Coaching requirement.
**Enhanced Samsung DeX Windowing:** Adds four-corner window snapping, 2-to-4 app auto-split layouts, and full-screen maximized windows with status-bar title controls.
**Deprecated Features:** Removes Audio brief and Moments from the Now brief experience upon upgrading.

## Privacy and Security: Native App Lock and Concurrent Biometrics

Privacy receives the most substantial structural upgrade in One UI 9.5 with the arrival of a system-level **App Lock**. Users can now restrict access to individual applications using biometric authentication or their primary screen lock credentials. When an application is locked, One UI automatically masks its notification contents and hides its home screen widgets, preventing bystanders from reading sensitive messages or previews even when the handset is unlocked.

On Galaxy S26 Ultra hardware, Samsung ties this security layer directly into **Privacy Display**. The screen-shielding feature can now engage automatically whenever a user opens locked apps, enters Secret mode in Samsung Browser, views private albums in Gallery, or accesses locked notes in Samsung Notes. Automatic Privacy Display protection also extends to the notification panel and Recents multitasking previews for any application configured with privacy triggers.

Authentication speed also improves through **simultaneous fingerprint and face recognition**. When both biometric methods are registered on a compatible prompt, the system evaluates facial geometry and the ultrasonic fingerprint sensor at the exact same time, eliminating the extra tap previously required to trigger face unlock. Samsung has also redesigned the fingerprint and face registration setup screens for a cleaner onboarding flow.

## Camera Overhaul: Single-Screen Controls and Custom Pro Presets

One UI 9.5 reworks the stock Camera interface to reduce menu navigation during time-sensitive shots. In the default photo and video viewfinders, quick controls are now arranged onto a single unified screen so every toggle remains visible without horizontal scrolling, paired with a simplified bottom shutter cluster.

For creators shooting in **Pro and Pro video modes** (available on models supporting full Pro modes, excluding Pro Lite and Pro Video Lite variants), Samsung introduces deeper hardware customization:

- **Custom Buttons and Presets:** Photographers and videographers can save tailored exposure, white balance, and focus presets alongside assignable quick-access buttons for recurring lighting conditions.
- **Focal Length Telemetry:** Lens switches and zoom levels are now displayed directly as optical focal lengths.
- **Upgraded Monitoring Tools:** Manual adjustment sliders have been refined alongside higher-precision histograms and real-time audio level meters.

## Samsung Health: Mindful Note, Global Medications, and Photo Meal Logging

Samsung Health gains several daily tracking tools alongside customizable dashboard layouts. The new **Mindful Note** module (not supported on Galaxy Watch) offers a structured journaling space supporting both free-form writing and guided reflection prompts. Entries synchronize directly with Mood check-in data to visualize emotional trends over time.

Medication management also expands globally with the **Medications** tracker. Users can log prescriptions, vitamins, and supplements, configure custom dosage schedules, and track daily adherence from a unified dashboard. Nutrition tracking becomes faster as well: users can snap a single photograph of a meal before eating to automatically extract ingredient breakdowns, nutritional profiles, and calorie estimates without manually searching food databases.

Sleep Coaching lowers its barrier to entry, allowing users to initiate personalized sleep guidance using just three days of sleep data recorded within the preceding two weeks. Finally, users can drag and drop cards across the Activity, Sleep, Vitals, Mindfulness, and Nutrition tabs to prioritize their most-viewed health metrics.

## Samsung DeX, My Files, and Lock Screen Personalization

Desktop productivity in **Samsung DeX** becomes noticeably more flexible in One UI 9.5. Maximizing an application window now expands the canvas across the entire display by merging window controls into the top status bar and extending the app’s background color behind the bottom taskbar. Multitasking window management also gains **four-corner quadrant snapping**—complementing existing left, right, top, and bottom edges—as well as an **Auto split** layout option capable of arranging active applications into two, three, or four simultaneous split-screen panes.

Within **My Files**, Samsung eliminates the need for third-party office suites for basic document viewing by adding native reader support for **DOCX** and **PPTX** files alongside existing PDF and TXT compatibility. Storage management tools in My Files also allow users to clean up uncategorized items and active files grouped under the “Other” storage category.

Lock screen and notification customization expand across three visual areas:

- **Live Effect Wallpapers:** Applies multi-layer depth parallax to lock screen wallpapers, shifting foreground and background elements dynamically as the phone tilts.
- **Seconds Clock Timeout:** Lets users choose how long a seconds-enabled lock screen clock remains visible, offering timeout intervals of 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, 5 minutes, or 10 minutes.
- **Now Bar Sports Animations:** Expands celebratory goal and victory visual effects to additional sports leagues, downloadable directly inside Now bar settings.

## Accessibility, Digital Wellbeing, and System Refinements

Accessibility tools become faster to trigger one-handed through a persistent **floating accessibility shortcut** equipped with clear visual on/off state indicators. For users with connected hearing aids, One UI 9.5 adds direct volume sliders inside the notification panel alongside granular audio routing toggles that let users exclude ringtones, alarms, or notification chimes from streaming to their hearing devices.

Digital Wellbeing introduces **Pause before app launch**, an intentional friction setting that enforces a 10-second countdown before opening selected applications or entire app categories. Elsewhere in the operating system, editing the Quick panel is streamlined with a built-in search bar for locating toggles by app or control name, while the sound picker adds downloadable archival versions of Samsung’s signature *Over the Horizon* ringtone.

## One UI 9.5 Full Changelog

The One UI 9.5 beta program is open today to Galaxy S26 series users in select markets, including Germany, India, Korea, Poland, the UK, and the U.S.


**Camera**

Improved camera controls and zoom bar

Quick controls are now laid out on one screen, so every option is visible without scrolling. The layout of the bottom shutter area has also been improved, providing a cleaner and simpler look.

Enhanced Pro and Pro video modes

You can now create custom buttons and presets, see lens and zoom levels as focal length, fine-tune manual sliders, and monitor your shot with an improved histogram and audio meter.

**Lock screen personalization**

Live effect for wallpapers

Add depth to your Lock screen wallpaper. As you tilt your device, elements in the image shift at different depths.

More customizable Lock screen clock

You can choose how long the clock with seconds appears on your Lock screen. Choose a timeout of 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, 5 minutes, or 10 minutes from the settings in the upper-right corner.

Sports animations in Now bar

Animation effects are now available for more sports. Celebrate your favorite team’s goals and victories with special visual effects. You can download these effects in Now bar settings.

**Digital Wellbeing**

Pause before app launch

Turn on Pause before app launch to add a 10-second pause before a selected app or app category opens. The pause helps you reconsider whether you really need to use the app.

**Privacy and security**

App lock

Lock individual apps to keep them private. Locked apps require biometrics or your screen lock (PIN, pattern, or password) to open, and their widgets and notification content are hidden, even when someone has access to your unlocked phone.

Privacy display for secure app features (Ultra model only)

Privacy display can now turn on automatically for secure features, including Locked apps, Secret mode in Samsung Browser, private albums in Gallery, and locked notes in Samsung Notes. It also protects notifications panel and Recents previews from any app set to turn on Privacy display automatically.

Fingerprint and face recognition at the same time

Fingerprint and face recognition now work at the same time if you’ve registered both and the feature you’re using supports both verification types. You no longer need an extra tap to use face verification. Just look at the screen or touch the fingerprint sensor to verify your identity.

Redesigned biometric registration

Fingerprint and face registration screens have been redesigned with a cleaner, more refined look.

**My Files**

Improved document support

You can now open PPTX and DOCX files directly in My Files without installing Microsoft Office or other apps. Document Reader already supports PDF and TXT files.

Easier storage cleanup

When you manage storage in My Files, you now have the option to delete uncategorized and files that are in use in the Other category.

**Samsung DeX**

Optimized window maximizing

Maximizing a window now fills the entire screen, window controls are now integrated with the status bar at the top of the screen, and the apps background color is visible behind the taskbar at the bottom, providing a more immersive experience.

Auto split and more snap options

You can now snap windows to all four corners in addition to the top, bottom, left, and right. You can also arrange your running apps into 2, 3, or 4 split-screen windows at once.

**Samsung Health**

Enhanced sleep coaching

You can now start sleep coaching with just 3 days of data from the past two weeks. Check your overall sleep status and get personalized coaching tailored to your sleep patterns and goals.

Scan meals and log them instantly

Take a single photo of a meal before you eat it and get nutrition details, ingredients, and calorie estimates right away. No need to search for and enter every food you ate.

Mindful notes

Clear your mind and record your thoughts with free writing and guided prompts. Your notes sync seamlessly with Mood check-in to reveal your mood trends at a glance.

Track your medications worldwide The Medications tracker is now available globally. You can enter your medications, set personalized reminders, and track your daily intake all in one place.

Reorder cards

You can now change the order of cards in the Activity, Sleep, Vitals, Mindfulness, and Nutrition tabs. Just drag and drop the cards you use the most to make them easier to find.

**Accessibility**

Intuitive floating accessibility shortcuts

Access your accessibility features from a floating button that’s always available, without the need to select additional options. Visual on and off indicators make it easy to see which features are available.

Improved hearing aid controls

Adjust your connected hearing aids’ volume from the notification panel. You can also choose which sounds from your phone reach your ears by opting out of notifications or ringtones and alarm.

**Even more improvements**

Search for Quick panel controls

When editing the Quick panel, you can search by app name or control name to find the controls you want quickly without scrolling through the full list.

Downloadable ringtones

Download past versions of Over the Horizon, Samsung’s signature sound, along with a curated selection of ringtones from past and present Samsung devices. Set any of them as your ringtone or use them for alarms.

**Other changes**

Audio brief and Moments no longer available

Audio brief and Moments will no longer be available in Now brief after you upgrade to One UI 9.5.
