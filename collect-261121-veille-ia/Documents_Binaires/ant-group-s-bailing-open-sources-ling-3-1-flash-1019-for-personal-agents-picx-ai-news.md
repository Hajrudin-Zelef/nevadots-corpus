On October 28, 2026, Ant Group's Bailing open-sourced Ling-3.1-flash-1019, an updated model for personal-agent tasks, along with its base model Ling-3.1-flash-base. The new version scores 44 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2, up 3 points from the previous version, and shows improvements across benchmarks including AA-Briefcase, GDPval-AA, AutomationBench-AA, CritPt, and SciCode. The release is linked to the Lingguang 2.0 upgrade, which positions the assistant as a proactive personal AI that can manage long-running tasks.
