# GLM-5.3-Flash: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

GLM-5.3-Flash is above average in intelligence and well priced when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also notably slow per task and somewhat verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

GLM-5.3-Flash scores 28 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it above average among comparable models (median: 23). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 124M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 101M.

Pricing for GLM-5.3-Flash is $0.15 per 1M input tokens (competitively priced, median: $0.53) and $0.50 per 1M output tokens (competitively priced, median: $2.10). On average, it costs $0.58 per task to evaluate GLM-5.3-Flash on the Intelligence Index.

At 41.4 minutes per Intelligence Index task, GLM-5.3-Flash is notably slow compared with the median of 15.0 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
