## How the tiers work

These are Crazyrouter selection tiers for Zhipu AI, not the vendor's official ratings. Compare within the same category and generation; newer or more expensive does not automatically mean a better fit.

Classification reviewed

Categories follow output task: text, image, video, embeddings and audio/music are compared separately; price is a cross-check, not a capability score.

Flagship: capability, complex tasks, quality or control come first; sharing a tier does not imply equal capability.

Mainstream: balances capability, latency and cost for production; speed and balanced variants are classified by use, not price alone.

Value: cost, latency or throughput come first; lower cost does not imply better quality on every task.

Coding, reasoning, speed and specialist capabilities are descriptive tags; output modalities are assessed separately, with video and audio prices read alongside specs and duration.
