# ADNOC to deploy OpenAI Frontier agents across processing, refining and trading decisions

11/3/2026 12:00:00 PM

        
ADNOC has signed a multi-year partnership with OpenAI and G42 that makes it the first energy company to deploy OpenAI Frontier, a platform for connecting and coordinating AI agents across complex business operations. The partners will jointly develop a bespoke decision-intelligence capability that combines OpenAI's latest frontier models with ADNOC's proprietary industrial data and expertise. It will run on G42's sovereign AI infrastructure in the UAE.

For the downstream business, the more relevant of the two initial applications spans ADNOC's integrated value chain from production and processing through refining, logistics, sales and trading. According to ADNOC, decisions across those segments currently tend to rest on different systems, data and priorities. Under the new setup, interconnected AI agents will continuously forecast crude oil supply, demand, prices and margins, predict the effects of those shifts before they happen, and weigh the commercial and operational impact of each decision as one integrated process. The company frames the goal as valuing every barrel while it is still in the ground and capturing the highest value from each barrel it produces, processes and sells.

Human experts will keep oversight of consequential decisions, and all solutions will operate under ADNOC's control so that proprietary data and critical infrastructure stay protected. The agreement also covers cyber defence: OpenAI's Daybreak Blue initiative will help ADNOC security teams identify vulnerabilities and protect critical energy infrastructure.

The second initial application sits upstream, merging subsurface, facilities and economic studies into single full-field development master plans. ADNOC positions Frontier as the layer that links the intelligence built by its existing tools, including the ENERGYai subsurface system, so that operational, capital and commercial decisions are taken together rather than in sequence.

"This is industrial AI at scale, pioneered here in Abu Dhabi," said Sultan Ahmed Al Jaber, ADNOC managing director and group CEO. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said the partnership will help ADNOC "make better decisions across the energy value chain."

Beyond the first use cases, the partners plan a shared base of industrial knowledge intended to preserve institutional expertise and speed future AI applications across ADNOC's operations, along with co-developed AI solutions and workforce training. The deal builds on ADNOC's rollout of ChatGPT Enterprise to more than 40,000 employees, where adoption exceeds 97%. The company counts more than 200 AI applications and 70 robotics use cases deployed or under development.
