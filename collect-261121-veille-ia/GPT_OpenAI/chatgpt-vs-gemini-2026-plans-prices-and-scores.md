**Quick answer:** Pick Gemini if your work lives in Gmail, Docs and Drive, you want storage with your AI plan, or you need a published 1 million token window: it scores 94 of 100 on our seven printed rules. Pick ChatGPT if you want the assistant more US adults use (44% against 24% in Pew's 2026 survey), a plugin directory on every plan and Office-file work in one app: it scores 88.

Whether you search ChatGPT vs Gemini or Gemini vs ChatGPT, the starting point is the same: both assistants are free to start, both have a plan near $20 a month, and both now make images, run deep research and talk back by voice. The differences that decide the choice are documented ones: price steps, storage, published limits, add-ons and privacy settings. This page puts them side by side with the official page for each fact, checked on 22 October 2026.

## Where ChatGPT is stronger, and where Gemini is

ChatGPT's documented strengths are reach, a plugin directory on every plan, file work across Office and Google formats, and a training opt-out that keeps your chat history. Gemini's are price steps, storage, a published context window, video on paid plans and personal custom apps. The score counts only some of these, so read both lists.

### ChatGPT's documented strengths

- **Reach.** OpenAI says "More than 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week" (21 October 2026) [1]. In Pew's February 2026 survey, 44% of US adults said they ever use ChatGPT, against 24% for Gemini [3]. A tool your colleagues already use is easier to share prompts and habits around.
- **A plugin directory on every plan.** "The plugin directory is available across ChatGPT plans"; plugins bundle connected apps such as Slack with skills for a task [11]. Pro users can also connect custom MCP servers with read/fetch permissions [12].
- **Files in your formats.** ChatGPT Work "can create or edit documents, spreadsheets, presentations, reports, and analyses", supports native Google Docs, Sheets and Slides [10], and ChatGPT for Excel and Google Sheets is available to Free, Go, Plus and Pro users [22].
- **Opt out and keep your history.** With "Improve the model for everyone" off, new conversations aren't used for training but "can still appear in your chat history" [14].
- **A coding agent on every plan.** Codex is included on Free and above; Plus adds Codex on the web, in the CLI, in the IDE extension and on iOS [5].

### Gemini's documented strengths

- **Cheaper steps and storage.** Google AI Plus is $4.99 with 400 GB; AI Pro is $19.99 with 5 TB of storage [4].
- **A published context window.** 32K tokens without a plan, 128K on AI Plus, 1 million on AI Pro and Ultra [6].
- **Video generation from $4.99.** AI Plus and AI Pro list video generation; AI Pro's FAQ mentions "a limited trial of Veo 3.1 Lite" [4].
- **Gemini inside Google's apps.** AI Plus lists "Gemini in Gmail, Vids, and more" and AI Pro "Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Vids, and more" [4].
- **Personal custom apps.** A personal Google account can connect a custom MCP server, if you are 18 or over and in the US [13].
- **A student year.** Eligible US college students can claim Google AI Pro free for a year [17].

## ChatGPT vs Gemini for research

Gemini documents Deep Research for every signed-in user 18 and over, including without a plan; OpenAI lists "Deep Research tools (where available)" on ChatGPT Plus and web search on Free. For long reports the practical difference is access on the free plan.

Google describes Deep Research as "in-depth and real-time research on almost any subject": Gemini drafts a plan you can edit, uses Google Search as a source by default, can add Gmail, Drive, uploaded files or NotebookLM notebooks, and "usually takes about 5-10 minutes" to write the report, which you can export to Google Docs [18]. **What is Deep Research?**Deep Research is an assistant mode that plans a multi-step investigation, searches many sources on its own, and writes a cited report instead of a single chat answer. Gemini and ChatGPT both use the name; Gemini's version takes about 5 to 10 minutes and can be exported to Google Docs.

## ChatGPT vs Gemini context window and usage limits

Gemini publishes its context window per plan: 32K tokens without a plan, 128K on AI Plus and 1 million on AI Pro and Ultra. OpenAI publishes no per-plan figure on a page that opens without logging in; the ChatGPT Go page says only "a larger context window" than Free.

**What is a context window?**A context window is the amount of text, measured in tokens, that an AI model can take into account at once: your messages, its replies and any files you upload. When a conversation or document exceeds it, the assistant can no longer see the earliest parts. For Gemini, 100 tokens is about 60 to 80 English words.

## Google apps vs ChatGPT plugins

ChatGPT's add-on layer is a plugin directory open on every plan, custom MCP servers with read/fetch permissions on Pro, and full MCP for Business and Enterprise/Edu workspaces. Gemini's is connected apps on every plan, plus custom MCP apps that a personal Google account can add if the user is 18 or over, in the US, with Keep Activity on.

OpenAI: "The plugin directory is available across ChatGPT plans"; installing an individual plugin "depends on your plan, workspace, role, region" [11]. "Custom MCP server connections and full MCP support are available for ChatGPT Business and Enterprise" and Edu, and "Pro users can connect custom MCP servers with read/fetch permissions" [12].

Google: connected apps are ticked for every plan on the limits page [6]. For custom apps you need a personal account (not work or school), must "Be 18 or over and in the US", and Keep Activity must be on: "Custom apps are unavailable when this setting is off." Both score 4 on rule A5, for different reasons: Gemini lets an individual connect their own server without a paid plan, but only with activity saved and only in the US; ChatGPT offers ready-made plugins on every plan and custom servers from Pro (from $100 a month), read/fetch only outside business plans.

## ChatGPT vs Gemini privacy: Keep Activity vs Improve the model

Both assistants let you stop your chats being used for training and both have a temporary chat that is never used for training. ChatGPT's switch is "Improve the model for everyone"; Gemini's is Keep Activity. The retention periods differ: 72 hours for Gemini's temporary chats, up to 30 days for ChatGPT's.

| Setting | ChatGPT | Gemini | 
|---|---|---|
| **Training opt-out** | Turn off "Improve the model for everyone" in Settings, Data controls; new conversations "won't be used to train OpenAI models" [14] | Turn off Keep Activity; future chats "won't be used to train our AI models" unless you send feedback [15] | 
| **History after opting out** | Chats "can still appear in your chat history" [14] | Chats with Keep Activity off are kept with your account for 72 hours [15] | 
| **Temporary chat** | Not used to improve models; "may be retained for up to 30 days for safety purposes" [14] | "Temporary chats are not used to train Google's AI models"; kept for 72 hours [15] | 
| **Default history** | Saved chats stay until you delete them; the opt-out "does not delete or hide saved chats" [14] | Auto-deleted after 18 months by default; can be set to 3 or 36 months, or indefinite [15] | 
| **Human review** | Feedback (thumbs up or down) may let the whole conversation be used for training [14] | Some chats are reviewed by people; reviewed chats are kept up to 3 years and aren't deleted with your activity [15] | 
| **Business plans** | Business, Enterprise and Edu content not used for training by default [14] | Workspace: customer data is not used for training "without customer's prior permission or instruction" [23] | 

**What is Keep Activity?**Keep Activity is the Gemini Apps setting that saves your chats to your Google account. While it is on, chats may be used to improve Google's AI and are auto-deleted after 18 months by default. Turning it off stops future chats being used for training, keeps them for 72 hours only, and disables custom apps.

**What is a temporary chat?**A temporary chat is a conversation that does not appear in your history and is not used to train the vendor's models. ChatGPT's temporary chats may be kept up to 30 days for safety; Gemini's are kept with your account for 72 hours.

Business plans are not scored; rule A6 reads only the consumer settings. Google updated its Gemini Apps Privacy Hub on 19 October 2026 and OpenAI its data-controls article within hours of our check, so re-check both pages before you rely on a period. ## Frequently asked questions

- Is Gemini better than ChatGPT?
- On documented facts, Gemini scores 94 and ChatGPT 88 of 100 on our seven printed rules, checked on 22 October 2026. Gemini leads only on a published 1 million token window; the two tie on free plan, price, tools, add-ons, privacy controls and surfaces. Answer quality is not scored, so test both on your own tasks.
- Is Gemini free?
- Yes. Gemini works without a paid plan once you sign in with a Google account. The free tier includes Deep Research, image generation and editing, Canvas, connected apps and Gemini Live, with a 32K token context window and 15 GB of Google storage. Usage limits refresh every 5 hours until you reach a weekly limit.
- How much is Google AI Pro compared with ChatGPT Plus?
- Google AI Pro costs $19.99 a month in the US and ChatGPT Plus $20 a month, billed monthly, so a year costs $239.88 versus $240.00. AI Pro includes 5 TB of storage and a published 1 million token window; Plus adds Codex on the web, CLI, IDE and iOS to the plugin directory. Prices checked on 22 October 2026.
- What is Google AI Plus?
- Google AI Plus is Google's lowest paid AI plan, $4.99 a month in the US on 22 October 2026. It gives twice the free usage in Gemini, a 128K token context window, video generation, Gemini in Gmail and Vids, and 400 GB of storage. Its nearest ChatGPT equivalent is ChatGPT Go at $8 a month.
- Which is better for image generation, ChatGPT or Gemini?
- Both document image generation on their free plans and higher image limits on paid plans, and neither publishes a quality measure. Gemini also documents video generation from Google AI Plus at $4.99 a month, while the ChatGPT Plus page lists images but no video. Try both on the same prompt and compare the results yourself.
- Which is better for studying, ChatGPT or Gemini?
- For US college students Gemini has the documented advantage: Google AI Pro free for a year for eligible students 18 and over who redeem by 31 December 2026, plus a published 1 million token window for long readings. ChatGPT's free plan covers web search, uploads and data analysis. Follow your school's rules on AI use.
- Is Gemini Pro free for students?
- Google offers eligible US college students aged 18 and over a free year of Google AI Pro if they redeem by 31 December 2026. A payment method is required at sign-up, eligibility is checked each year, and the plan then renews at $19.99 a month unless cancelled. Students in other countries should check Google's page for their country.
- How big is Gemini's context window?
- Google publishes three sizes: 32K tokens without a plan, 128K tokens on Google AI Plus, and 1 million tokens on Google AI Pro and Ultra. For Gemini models, 100 tokens is about 60 to 80 English words, so 1 million tokens holds roughly 600,000 to 800,000 words. OpenAI publishes no ChatGPT figure on an open page.
- Does Gemini use my chats to train its models?
- It can while Keep Activity is on. With Keep Activity off, Google says future chats won't be used to train its AI models unless you send feedback, and they are kept for 72 hours. Temporary chats are never used for training. Chats reviewed by people are kept up to three years, even if you delete your activity.
- Can ChatGPT or Gemini make a presentation?
- Yes. Gemini's Canvas can create slides and export them to Google Slides or as a PDF, and ChatGPT Work can create presentations, including native Google Slides when that app is connected. For a designed deck with sources on figure slides, use a dedicated presentation maker and check every number before you present.
