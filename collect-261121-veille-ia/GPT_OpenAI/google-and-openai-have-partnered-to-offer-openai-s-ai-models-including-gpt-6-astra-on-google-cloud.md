# Google and OpenAI have partnered to offer OpenAI's AI models, including 'GPT-6 Astra,' on Google Cloud.


 OpenAI has announced that it will provide its latest AI models through Google Cloud's AI model delivery platform, ' 

**OpenAI models are now available on Google Cloud | OpenAI**

**https://openai.com/ja-JP/index/openai-models-google-cloud/**

**Google Cloud brings OpenAI models to customers | Google Cloud Blog**

**https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/google-cloud-brings-openai-models-to-customers?hl=en**

Google Cloud's Model Garden is a service that allows users to access AI models developed by various companies, including Google Gemini. With over 200 models available, developers can choose the model that best suits their needs, performance requirements, and budget.

This partnership will add three OpenAI models to Model Garden: 'GPT-6 Astra,' 'GPT-6.1 Sol,' and 'GPT-6 Luna.' Companies can purchase these models through the Google Cloud Marketplace, and the usage fees will be included in their existing Google Cloud billing. Furthermore, companies that have committed to a certain amount of spending on Google Cloud can also apply the usage fees for OpenAI models to their contractual spending. A key feature is that users can choose the AI model that best suits their needs without having to contract with a different provider for each model.


 OpenAI models run on Google Cloud's dedicated infrastructure, and user input such as prompts and output results are not used to train the underlying models. Furthermore, because it leverages Google Cloud's access control and audit logging mechanisms, it becomes easier for companies handling sensitive information to manage their AI models. Future plans include supporting 'zero data retention' (where no data is retained) and 'confidential computing' (where data is protected during processing).

 In addition, integration with OpenAI's programming support tool, 'Codex,' will be enabled. This will allow developers to connect their local Codex CLI or desktop version Codex workflows to the Google Cloud platform and execute inference processing using OpenAI models. In the future, OpenAI models will also be available in Google's AI development environment, 'Google Antigravity,' and Gemini-related enterprise services.

 According to Google Cloud, the three models are currently available as a limited preview to select enterprise customers, and OpenAI plans to make them generally available within the next few weeks.

 


 Greg Brockmann, co-founder and president of OpenAI, commented, 'Google Cloud is the leading platform for developers and companies working to build AI agents, develop software, and use AI in their operations. This partnership is an important step in enabling our customers to use our most powerful models and Codex in the environments they are already developing in. We will grow the agent ecosystem together and further expand the options our customers have for using frontier AI.'

 Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian also stated, 'We are focused on providing our customers with a wide range of options, including OpenAI models. By enabling our customers to choose the model that best suits their specific business needs, we help them optimize quality and reduce costs.'

 In addition, OpenAI has stated in this announcement that Microsoft will continue to be its primary cloud partner.
