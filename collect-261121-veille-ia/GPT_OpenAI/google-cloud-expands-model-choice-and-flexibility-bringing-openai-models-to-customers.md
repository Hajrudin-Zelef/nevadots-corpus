## 01 What happened

Google Cloud on October 21, 2026 announced it is launching three OpenAI models in Model Garden.

## 02 Key details

- Google Cloud is the only hyperscaler to host OpenAI models alongside Gemini and Claude in Model Garden on a unified enterprise platform.
- GPT-6 Astra, Sol and Luna are rolling out in private preview to select enterprise customers with API access.
- Customers can dynamically select models without architectural lock-in, with usage billed on a single Google Cloud invoice.

## 03 Why it matters

Google Cloud integrates OpenAI models into its enterprise platform, giving customers unified access to GPT, Gemini, and Claude with consolidated billing.

## 04 Who it matters to

Enterprise AI customers, AI implementation leaders and AI developers.
