# GPT-6.1 Terra: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

GPT-6.1 Terra (Max) is above average in intelligence and well priced when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also slower than average per task and somewhat verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

GPT-6.1 Terra (Max) scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it above average among comparable models (median: 44). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 99.4M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 65.4M.

Pricing for GPT-6.1 Terra (Max) is $1.00 per 1M input tokens (competitively priced, median: $2.00) and $5.00 per 1M output tokens (moderately priced, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $0.96 per task to evaluate GPT-6.1 Terra (Max) on the Intelligence Index.

At 16.3 minutes per Intelligence Index task, GPT-6.1 Terra (Max) is slower than average compared with the median of 12.7 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
