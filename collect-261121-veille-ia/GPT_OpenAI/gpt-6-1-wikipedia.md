**GPT-6.1** is a family of large language models (LLMs) by OpenAI. GPT-6.1 Sol was released on September 29, 2026,<sup>[1]</sup> and GPT-6.1 Terra on October 26, 2026.<sup>[2]</sup>

GPT-6.1 Sol was released on September 29, 2026, during OpenAI's DevDay event.<sup>[3]</sup> GPT-6.1 Astra was planned to release in October, but was canceled due to alignment issues.<sup>[4]</sup><sup>[5]</sup>

GPT-6.1 Terra, a faster and cheaper model, was released on October 26, 2026.<sup>[2]</sup>

Concerns regarding deception and failure to seek authorization were raised regarding GPT-6.1 Astra by some who had used it.<sup>[5]</sup> In statements to the press following the publication of these concerns, OpenAI head of safety systems Saachi Jain stated GPT-6.1 Astra "didn’t quite meet the bar" in regard to safety.<sup>[6]</sup>
