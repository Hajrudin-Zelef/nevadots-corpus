Starting today, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol are available in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users.

Pro 500 subscribers can use Ultrafast with either model. For the first time, we’re also bringing Ultrafast to Pro 200 subscribers, starting with GPT-6.2 Sol.

Developers can access gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol in the OpenAI API, with Ultrafast available.

### GPT-6.2 Astra

A frontier model built for your hardest projects. It sets new highs on evals across professional work and scientific research at less than a third of the cost per task of GPT‑6 Astra. You’ll see the difference in things like slideshows, websites, and 3D designs.

On Agents’ Last Exam, 6.2 Astra at xhigh scores 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra at max, at less than half the cost per task.

## 
GPT-6.2 Astra is our most capable model ever for cyber. It jumps 22 points over GPT-6 Astra on ExploitBench Internal Port.

Due to increased capabilities, we’re launching GPT‑6.2 Astra and Sol with tighter safeguards for higher-risk cybersecurity assistance. We are working to bring these models to Daybreak so approved defenders can access them with reduced refusals.

 
### GPT-6.2 Sol

An efficient and versatile model for everyday work. It powers through multistep agentic workflows, writes clearly and naturally, and is intuitive to collaborate with. Many OpenAI employees use GPT-6.2 Sol as their go-to model across technical and non-technical work.

On FrontierCode 2.0, Sol nearly matches Astra on merge-ready changes to real codebases at about one-fifth the cost.
