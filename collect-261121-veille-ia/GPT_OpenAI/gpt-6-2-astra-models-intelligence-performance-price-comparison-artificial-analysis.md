# GPT-6.2 Astra: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

GPT-6.2 Astra (Max) is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but particularly expensive when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also slower than average per task and somewhat verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

GPT-6.2 Astra (Max) scores 62 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 44). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 65.8M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 65.4M.

Pricing for GPT-6.2 Astra (Max) is $8.00 per 1M input tokens (expensive, median: $2.00) and $40.00 per 1M output tokens (expensive, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $4.00 per task to evaluate GPT-6.2 Astra (Max) on the Intelligence Index.

At 14.2 minutes per Intelligence Index task, GPT-6.2 Astra (Max) is slower than average compared with the median of 12.7 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
