OpenAI has announced two new AI models, GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, with significant improvements in coding, scientific research, professional work, and computer use. The company claims that the new models can deliver better results while using fewer tokens, allowing users to complete more tasks within their existing usage limits.
Back in September, OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra, which delivered a generational improvement in AI capabilities. However, models from other labs soon caught up. Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 surpassed Astra in several coding benchmarks, while Google's Gemini 4 Carbon delivered competitive performance at a significantly lower price.
With GPT-6.2 Astra, OpenAI is once again trying to take the lead in AI performance while also addressing the growing importance of cost efficiency.
On Agents' Last Exam, which evaluates AI models on complex, multistep professional workflows, GPT-6.2 Astra scored 5.2% higher than GPT-6 Astra at less than one-third the cost. It also outperformed Claude Fable 5.2 by 5.5 percentage points while costing just 5% as much per task. On Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, GPT-6.2 Astra scored 9% higher than GPT-6 Astra at less than one-third the cost.
Beyond benchmarks, OpenAI claims that GPT-6.2 Astra will be more useful for professional work as the model can now create better-designed PowerPoint presentations, Excel spreadsheets, and documents that closely follow existing templates and industry conventions. GPT-6.2 Astra also comes with improved computer-use and visual reasoning capabilities.
For software developers, the company claims that GPT-6.2 Astra writes cleaner code, makes better architectural decisions, and provides more focused testing to reduce the number of iterations required to complete complex coding tasks.
While GPT-6.2 Astra is targeted toward complex work, GPT-6.2 Sol is the model for everyday work. On the FrontierCode 2.0 coding benchmark, GPT-6.2 Sol comes within just 0.5 percentage points of GPT-6.2 Astra while costing approximately one-fifth as much per task. On OpenAI's own LifeVal benchmark, Sol outperformed GPT-6 Astra at approximately one-seventh the cost.
OpenAI is reducing GPT-6.2 Astra's standard API input and output token prices by 20% compared to GPT-6 Astra. Cached input tokens are now 60% cheaper. GPT-6.2 Sol retains the same pricing as GPT-6.1 Sol.
Both models are available starting today to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu subscribers through ChatGPT Work and Codex. Pro 500 subscribers can access Ultrafast mode with either model, while Pro 200 subscribers can now use Ultrafast with GPT-6.2 Sol.
