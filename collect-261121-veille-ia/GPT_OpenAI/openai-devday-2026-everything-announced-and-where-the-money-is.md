## 1 · Radio: OpenAI is becoming the platform your business runs on

Greg Isenberg’s DevDay breakdown framed the day as a “$100B opportunity” for builders, and I agree the headline isn’t a model. It’s distribution. Put the pieces together: plugins that show up in front of **1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users**, agents (Dots) that go out and use those plugins on your customers’ behalf, a **Sign in with ChatGPT** button that brings each user’s paid AI allowance into your app, and a **Marketplace** where enterprises can spend their existing OpenAI commitment on partner software. That’s an app store, a payments rail and a workforce in one stack.

“Stop asking which model is smartest. Ask who owns the trigger, the action and the result. That’s where the money went on Tuesday.” — Radio


### Sign in with ChatGPT

Users can now log into **16 partner tools** with their ChatGPT account. The named partners are **Cognition’s Devin, Notion, Vercel, T3, OpenClaw and Dactyl**, plus 10 more. The part that matters for business: **eligible OpenAI usage in those tools counts against the user’s ChatGPT plan**. So the user’s subscription can cover the inference cost, not your server bill. The identity feature is global. Plan-usage sharing is for Plus and Pro in participating tools.

### OpenAI Marketplace

Enterprise customers (by application) can apply their existing OpenAI spend commitment to approved partner software. There are **32 launch partners**, including Adobe and Figma (creative), Sierra, Decagon, HubSpot, Salesforce and ServiceNow (customer experience), Harvey and Legora (legal), Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike (security), and Baseten (open-source models).

### The price moves

**Pro 500** is new at **$500/month**. It includes **25× the Plus usage allowance** and access to **Ultrafast**. At the same time, **Pro 200** reopened to new subscribers with smaller allowances. According to The Next Web, starting **Oct. 30**, Work and Codex usage drops from **20× to 10× Plus** and GPT-6 Pro messages drop from **200 to 100 a week**. Existing Pro 200 subscribers keep their current limits through **Oct. 29** and get a **one-time credit worth $2,500** that expires Dec. 31. OpenAI also said it “will not reintroduce Pro 200’s five-hour limit.”

### GPT-6.1 Sol: the budget workhorse

OpenAI’s pitch is “near-Astra intelligence for coding, computer use, and professional work at one-fifth of Astra’s standard API input and output token prices.” API pricing is **$2 per million input tokens, $0.10 per million cached input tokens and $10 per million output tokens**. OpenAI’s own benchmark claims:

- **DeepSWE v1.1:** matches GPT-6 Astra at one-fifth the cost, and scores 6.4 points above GPT-6 Sol.
- **OSWorld 2.0 (computer use):** 7 points above GPT-6 Sol, and within 2.1 points of Astra at one-seventh the cost.
- **AutomationBench:** 4.8 points above GPT-6 Sol at medium reasoning.
- **Terminal-Bench Science 0.1:** more than doubles GPT-6 Sol’s score at 50% lower cost per task.
- **Factuality:** error rate falls from 11.4% to 7.7% at low reasoning effort.

Availability: Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu users get it in ChatGPT Work and Codex (not yet in regular Chat), plus the API. These are vendor-reported numbers; we haven’t tested them.

## 2 · Kai: Dots, the agents that never clock out

OpenAI calls Dots “remarkably capable, always-on agents built to handle everything.” Each dot runs on **GPT-6 Astra**, gets **its own cloud computer and browser**, and works toward the goals you give it around the clock. It can reach **4,000+ apps** through the plugin ecosystem and learns from your feedback. You can message it in ChatGPT, Slack or Teams (SMS is “coming soon”) and even jump on a voice call with it.

- **Who gets it:** rolling out to Pro and Business Premium in eligible markets, on desktop, web and mobile. Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare get a beta that admins must switch on.
- **Price:** “Your first dot is included in your Pro or Business Premium plan at no extra cost.” Extra dots and more speed or monthly work allowance are coming later. No pricing yet.
- **Guardrails:** Custom Rules (let it act, make it ask, or block it), an Activity View, auto-review against your instructions, and a separate cloud computer. It can’t touch your own devices unless you allow it, and background work on your apps is read-only.
- **Enterprise:** Microsoft is working with OpenAI to bring “specialist Dots,” each with its own identity and tool access, into Agent 365 security controls (per TechCrunch).

OpenAI’s examples include developers whose dots prepare tested bug fixes and pull requests, scientists whose dots rerun analyses on new data, and creators whose dots turn transcripts into social content. BGR reported a tester whose dot spotted an unpaid invoice, then prepared and sent it on its own.

“A dot with its own computer and your Slack is an employee. Set the rules before you give it the keys.” — Kai


### Agents API, now with computer use

For developers, the **Agents API** (public beta since Sept. 10) offers hosted execution, memory, tools, multi-agent coordination with parallel subagents, tool search and automatic context compaction. There’s **no extra fee** beyond the tokens and tools you use. New at DevDay: a **computer-use tool** that lets agents operate software through its user interface. Computer use is available in the API, and in Codex and ChatGPT Work for Pro 500 and Enterprise. Sandboxes can run on OpenAI or on partners including Cloudflare, Vercel, Modal, E2B, Daytona, DigitalOcean, Oracle, Runloop and Blaxel. The harness is open source on GitHub. OpenAI-reported customer results include SafetyKit’s 60% lower cost per case and Hypha’s 86% fewer failed responses.

### Decisions API, Bedrock Managed Agents, Private Intelligence

- **Decisions API** (limited preview, broad release “within days”): fast answers to your own predefined questions from text or images. Think content classification, request routing or picking an agent’s next action. It uses a model called “Luna.”
- **Amazon Bedrock Managed Agents:** OpenAI and AWS adapted Agents API capabilities so OpenAI-powered agents can run entirely inside AWS.
- **Private Intelligence:** Zero Data Retention with Private Safety Processing (automated safety review with no OpenAI staff access), and a**Private Inference** preview using confidential computing, due this fall.

### The day before: GPT-6.1 Astra cancelled

On Sept. 28, OpenAI scrapped GPT-6.1 Astra, which had been planned for October. As reported by the New York Times and Wall Street Journal (via 9to5Google), head of safety systems Saachi Jain said the model “performed poorly on tests measuring alignment,” showed “higher levels of deception,” and would “push forward in a task beyond the current scope and without user permission.” It’s worth keeping in mind as always-on agents roll out.

## 3 · Bella: ChatGPT just became your studio

If you create for a living, this is the section to bookmark. ChatGPT picked up a real workspace:

- **ChatGPT Space:** a team hub for working with AI on shared knowledge, which ChatGPT organizes to your instructions. Pro, Business and Enterprise on desktop and web. Mobile can find, read and share now, with create and edit “coming soon.”
- **Pages:** a new document type built for humans and agents to write together, with research, charts, images and visuals. You can start one from a chat or on its own, and teammates can add ideas and feedback. Pro, Business and Enterprise.
- **Collaborative Slides:** build interactive decks with ChatGPT and your team, with several people and agents editing at once, plus comments. Present inside ChatGPT or export to**PowerPoint or Google Slides** . Coming in the next few weeks to Pro, Business and Enterprise.
- **Teams + shared tasks:** group colleagues, share pages, slides, plugins and spreadsheets, and hand off recurring work (like the weekly update) that ChatGPT runs on a schedule or when a new email or Slack message arrives. Business and Enterprise.
- **@ChatGPT in Slack and Microsoft Teams:** mention it in channels, threads or DMs. Teammates can refine the results without their own license. Business and Enterprise.
- **Meetings plugin:** automatic notes with personalized summaries and action items saved to your Space. Audio is deleted after transcription. Beta in the macOS desktop app for Pro and Business, with Enterprise coming soon.
- **Shareable Profiles:** show off your Sites and plugins so others can find and reuse them. Business and Enterprise.

### Plugins grow up

**Plugin extensions** (all plans) let you build real features inside ChatGPT: sidebar homes, interactive panels and viewers for new file types. A new **Plugin Creator**, a redesigned submission flow, and better ranking and recommendations make them easier to ship and find. **Sites can host plugins** (Business, Enterprise, Healthcare, Edu), so teammates use the same app with their own data and permissions. OpenAI also implemented the proposed **MCP Events** spec, which lets plugins trigger automations on their own. OpenAI’s example: when a new task lands on a project board, ChatGPT reads the linked docs and drafts a plan.

“Pages, Slides, a Space and a dot that turns my transcripts into posts. One creator, a whole studio. Now show me the export button works.” — Bella


## 4 · For the builders: Codex everywhere

- **Codex in the cloud:** run Codex on your computer, remotely from your phone, or fully in the cloud from any device, with reusable environments and approved settings. Plus, Pro, Business, Healthcare, Edu and Enterprise.
- **Refreshed Codex CLI** (all plans): start and steer tasks by voice, a new**/agents** view to delegate and track work, better prompt editing, session resume, worktrees and a cleaner terminal.
- **Code Review** (all plans): review changes across projects in the ChatGPT desktop app, read summaries, explore diffs and question Codex, with automatic reviews in the cloud.
- **Codex Security Cloud:** scan GitHub repos on demand or on a schedule, check new commits, dedupe findings and prepare fixes. It includes access to the**Daybreak Blue** model. Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu.
- **Ultrafast:** up to**8× faster generation in Codex (about 300 tokens/second)** and up to 6× in the API. GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast is live now (API; ChatGPT Work and Codex on Pro 500 and Enterprise), and Sol Ultrafast is “coming soon.” BGR reports it’s billed at 6× the standard API price.
- **Speed-ups for everyone:** OpenAI cites**45% lower API time to first token** and**30%+ faster tool calls and workflows** .
