# OpenAI’s release of GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol delivers the #2 model in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and most of the Cost per Task Pareto frontier


GPT-6.2 Astra is priced at $8/$40, 20% below GPT-6 Astra, and cached input drops to $0.40 from $1.00 per million tokens. GPT-6.2 Sol pricing is unchanged from GPT-6.1 Sol, at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens.

**Key takeaways:**

**➤ Second in Intelligence Index:** OpenAI now trails only Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.2 in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. GPT-6.2 Astra jumps 5 points from GPT-6 Astra, overtaking Gemini 4 Carbon (60) and Claude Opus 5.5 (59) at their top effort settings. GPT-6.2 Sol gains 2 points at 57, placing it on par with GPT-6 Astra at ~15% of the Cost per Task.

**➤ Occupies most of cost and output token frontiers:** OpenAI models now occupy most of the Cost per Task Pareto frontier, sharing the frontier with only Anthropic and Meta. OpenAI also dominates the Output Token per Task Pareto Frontier, with Claude Fable 5.2 being the only model from a non-OpenAI lab appearing on the frontier.

**➤ Strengths in science and knowledge:** OpenAI performs strongly in both of our key science-focused evaluations, leading Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, covering agentic scientific research workflows, and placing second only to Claude Fable 5.2 in CritPt v2 (hard subset), covering physics reasoning. GPT-6.2 Astra performs below only Claude Fable 5.2 in factual knowledge as measured by AA-Omniscience v1.1 Accuracy, and in HLE-Diamond, a reasoning and knowledge benchmark.

**➤ Leading in 3D and robotics:** GPT-6.2 Astra leads AA-3D at 1318, with Claude Fable 5.2 at 1271 and GPT-6.2 Sol at 1259. This benchmark measures how well models build interactive 3D experiences, and is rated by human graders. GPT-6.2 also leads AA-Robotics v0.1, our research preview measuring how well language models can understand, plan, and act in the real world. GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol scored 79 and 78 respectively, ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at 75. Our team is hard at work adding more difficult tasks, as OpenAI and Anthropic models can now perform the majority of basic tasks in this initial research preview.

**➤ No improvement in Coding Agent Index:** GPT-6.2 Astra is flat with GPT-6 Astra, and GPT-6.2 Sol loses 3 points relative to GPT-6.1 Sol in the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (Codex harness at max effort). For Sol, the drop is driven by a 14-point drop in SWE-Atlas-QnA-AA, where the model is more likely than its predecessor to omit key facts in its responses. Cost efficiency however does place several effort levels of GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on the Pareto frontier for Cost per Task.
