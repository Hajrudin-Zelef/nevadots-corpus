Adnoc and G42 will deploy OpenAI Frontier on UAE sovereign AI infrastructure, with separate deals spanning investment analysis and government adoption.

**What's going on here?**

OpenAI has landed a cluster of new Abu Dhabi partnerships – with Adnoc, G42, and Mubadala – to roll out its latest “OpenAI Frontier” models across energy operations, investment analysis, and public-sector use in the UAE.

**What does this mean?**

The centerpiece is a multi-year deal where Adnoc, the UAE’s state-owned oil company, will deploy OpenAI Frontier across its operations, but run the models on G42’s sovereign AI infrastructure inside the country. That matters because keeping computing and data in-country can make it easier to meet data-residency rules and the governance checks that regulated sectors require, especially when human oversight is built into key deci..

y decisions. Adnoc says the tools will plug into its existing AI systems, including ENERGYai, and start with planning and forecasting tasks like “field master planning” and crude-price and demand scenarios. Separately, Mubadala Investment, a state-backed investment firm, is partnering with OpenAI and G42 unit Inception42 to use AI for investment research and risk assessment. OpenAI and Inception42 also signed a five-year agreement to broaden frontier-AI adoption across government, enterprise, and education.

**Why should I care?**

**For markets:***Adnoc’s OpenAI Frontier rollout will run on G42’s sovereign infrastructure.*

For big organizations, the toughest part of adopting cutting-edge AI is often less about model quality and more about control: where the data sits, who can access it, and how decisions get audited. By running these models on a local, state-aligned stack, G42 positions itself as the default “plumbing” for frontier AI in sensitive UAE workflows, from energy operations to public services. And once those workflows are built – including the human-in-the-loop review steps – switching to a different provider can be slow and expensive. That dynamic can make deployment platforms and infrastructure providers, like G42 and Inception42, disproportionately important beneficiaries alongside the model maker.
