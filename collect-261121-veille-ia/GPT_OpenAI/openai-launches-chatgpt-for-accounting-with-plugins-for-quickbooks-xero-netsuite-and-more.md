The new accounting plugin targets invoices, reconciliations and month-end close, with humans still signing off on final entries

OpenAI wants to do your bookkeeping. On November 18, 2026, the company launched “ChatGPT for Accounting,” a package built around a dedicated plugin for finance teams.

## What OpenAI actually shipped

The centerpiece is the Accounting plugin, version 1.0.0. The launch was announced through the official @ChatGPT account.

The plugin links ChatGPT to accounting software that firms already use. Instead of copying numbers between windows, users can ask the assistant to handle the shuffling.

OpenAI is targeting five core jobs. They are invoice processing, bank reconciliation, month-end reports, data entry and audit preparation.

One important guardrail is built in. Final entries still require human oversight, and data changes need confirmation from a person before they stick.

## Eight partners, one sidebar

The Accounting plugin does not arrive alone. It ships alongside eight partner plugin integrations from financial software vendors.

Named partners include Intuit QuickBooks, Xero, Oracle NetSuite, BILL, FreshBooks, Rillet and Campfire.

Access runs through ChatGPT Work and Business plans. Users find the tools in a sidebar menu, so they sit next to the chat rather than in a separate app.

## Part of a bigger vertical push

ChatGPT for Accounting is not a one-off experiment. It follows the earlier launch of “ChatGPT for Sales,” another industry-specific package.

It also builds on OpenAI’s prior work in finance. In September 2026, the company introduced a package focused on financial services.
