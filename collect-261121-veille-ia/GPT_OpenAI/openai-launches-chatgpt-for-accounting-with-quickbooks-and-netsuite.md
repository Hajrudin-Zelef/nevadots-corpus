OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Accounting on 18 November 2026 at openai.com/business/solutions/accounting, extending its vertical workspace suite one day after ChatGPT for Sales. The release combines a first-party OpenAI Accounting plugin with partner connectors for Intuit QuickBooks, Xero, Oracle NetSuite (plus NetSuite QuickStart), BILL, FreshBooks, Rillet and Campfire, while ChatGPT computer use steps in for legacy or specialist accounting apps without a native plugin.

## Invoice coding, bank reconciliation, workpaper checks and reusable close skills

The Accounting plugin structures four recurring finance workflows across email, uploaded spreadsheets and connected ledgers: preparing draft journal entries and customer invoices from emails and receipts with suggested account codes while holding unclear items for review; gathering statements and payment records for bank reconciliation; comparing workpapers against supporting documents to flag duplicates, missing fields or unexplained variances; and saving monthly closing instructions as reusable skills.

Workspace administrators govern which accounting connectors are enabled, individual users sign in with their existing app permissions, every draft entry waits for human sign-off before posting, and ChatGPT Business and Enterprise data is excluded from model training by default. OpenAI has not announced API or commercial pricing.
