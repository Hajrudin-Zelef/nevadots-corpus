OpenAI has introduced GPT-6 Astra, a new model the company said is rolling out to a limited set of organizations starting today.

OpenAI said the model will become available over the coming days to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users, as well as through the OpenAI API and AWS.

The launch comes less than two months after OpenAI released the GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna model family. OpenAI had slowed frontier model development in August after one of its models hacked AI platform Hugging Face.

OpenAI said GPT-6 Astra is built for agentic and computer-use tasks, including software engineering, cybersecurity, science and other professional work. A company demo showed the model handling tasks such as 3D modeling, building slideshows and ordering food while coding a game.

OpenAI said Astra can manage multi-step workflows across a computer and browser, maintain its original instructions and make decisions with what the company described as strong visual judgment.

OpenAI said Astra scored 98.6% on ARC-AGI-3, a benchmark used to measure how well AI systems solve unfamiliar problems. VentureBeat noted that benchmark comparisons carry caveats because systems are not always configured the same way and features such as persistent memory can affect results.

OpenAI said Astra scored 57.7% on Terminal Bench 4.0, a coding benchmark, and 59.3% on Agent’s Last Exam, which measures agentic capabilities. The company said both results were higher than GPT-5.6 Sol.

OpenAI also highlighted Astra’s cybersecurity performance. The company said the model achieved a perfect score on ExploitBench, up from 78.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol.

On SRE-Bench, which measures a model’s ability to reverse engineer software binaries without raw source code, OpenAI said Astra solved 88.0% of tasks on the first attempt and 99.2% within four attempts. GPT-5.6 Sol solved 55.9% and 68.7%, respectively, on the same benchmark.

OpenAI said it added alignment features aimed at improving safety, including stronger compliance with templates and more transparent responses. The company also said Astra was built not to comply with advanced cybersecurity tasks and includes stronger protections against jailbreak attempts and misuse monitoring.

For developers using the OpenAI API, Astra costs $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens.

“What you actually want, and I think the market is starting to really wake up to, is the price per task,” OpenAI President Greg Brockman told VentureBeat. “It’s just about: can you get the thing done for an appropriate cost at appropriate speed?”
