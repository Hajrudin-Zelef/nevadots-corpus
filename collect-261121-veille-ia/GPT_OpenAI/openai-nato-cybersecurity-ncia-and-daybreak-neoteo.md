OpenAI announced on Nov. 17, 2026, that it is working with NATO’s Communications and Information Agency (NCIA) to provide NATO core institutions access to advanced cybersecurity models through its Daybreak program for authorized defensive work. OpenAI described the arrangement.

## What cybersecurity work does OpenAI describe?

OpenAI lists software review, investigation of suspicious activity, vulnerability validation, and developing and testing fixes as tasks for the work.

## What does the 300-billion-token figure measure?

OpenAI reports that the NATO work used up to **300 billion raw input and output tokens per month**. That figure describes reported model usage, not a count of vulnerabilities found or a measure of security outcomes.

The token total describes usage; the task list defines the defensive work OpenAI says the access supports.

## Where did Daybreak feature in a separate exercise?

Crossed Swords 2026 ran Nov. 2–6 in Tallinn, Estonia, according to Euronews. OpenAI said participants in a training stream used Daybreak in the exercise’s technical environment to build, deploy, and evaluate AI agents for cyber operations. The training emphasized human oversight.
