# OpenAI releases 'GPT-6.2 Astra' and 'GPT-6.2 Sol,' improving document creation and PC operation, and enabling the processing of complex tasks with fewer tokens.


OpenAI has announced two new AI models, 'GPT-6.2 Astra' and 'GPT-6.2 Sol.' GPT-6.2 Astra is the top-of-the-line model suitable for complex tasks, programming, and scientific research, while GPT-6.2 Sol is designed to efficiently handle everyday tasks. Both models are said to deliver high performance with fewer output tokens than previous models, and also offer improved accuracy in document creation and PC operation.

 **Introducing GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol | OpenAI** 

In September 2026, OpenAI released the 'GPT-6 Astra,' which excels at PC operation and programming, followed by the 'GPT-6.1 Sol,' which prioritizes a balance between processing performance and cost. The GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol are successors to both models and have been improved to allow for even more efficient work with multiple applications and the creation of specialized documents.

**'GPT-6 Astra' appears, boasting exceptional PC operation capabilities, enabling tasks like 'creating 3D models in Blender and turning them into walkable spaces in UE5,' and achieving 99.9% accuracy with ARC-AGI-3 - GIGAZINE**

When entrusting tasks to AI, in addition to the accuracy of the answers, it's also important to consider how much revision the completed documents will require. Traditional AI models could generate text and programs, but human intervention was often necessary to ensure they conformed to specified formats or correctly completed multiple processes.

GPT-6.2 Astra is designed for professional work in fields such as legal, finance, and sales, and allows users to create slides, spreadsheets, and documents that conform to industry practices. OpenAI's presentation included an example of creating new slides that align with existing ones.


 The slide created with GPT-6 Astra (left) matches the color scheme, but fails to reproduce the other design elements and formatting. On the other hand, GPT-6.2 Astra (right) was able to reproduce the design fairly well.

 


 In the 'Agents' Last Exam,' a performance test that simulates multiple processes similar to actual business operations, GPT-6.2 Astra scored 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra. Furthermore, the cost per task in the same test was reduced to less than one-third of the previous cost.

 


Performance has also improved in scientific research, with GPT-6.2 Astra outperforming GPT-6 Astra by 9 percentage points in 'Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1,' a benchmark that tests AI performance on scientific research tasks. OpenAI positions GPT-6.2 Astra as its top-performing model for scientists.

 


Meanwhile, in Artificial Analysis's own evaluation of AI performance, GPT-6.2 Astra ranked second in the 'Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index,' which combines multiple performance tests, behind Anthropic's 'Claude Fable 5.2.' In the 'Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index,' which evaluates coding ability, GPT-6.2 Sol performed similarly to GPT-6 Astra, but scored 3 points lower than GPT-6.1 Sol.

 Furthermore, GPT-6.2 Astra has enhanced capabilities for operating applications on a PC. While the previous GPT-6 Astra could create 3D models and operate software, GPT-6.2 Astra allows for designs that take into account the connections and operation of components.

 In a demo released by OpenAI, GPT-6.2 Astra creates a 3D model of a car, writes a program to calculate the movement of the tires and suspension, and animates the car's movement during driving in Blender. The intended use is to check the impact of design changes before creating a prototype.

 


 Website and game creation capabilities have also improved. GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol can create controllable 3D content from text-based instructions, and examples of their creations, such as a playable racing game and a Colosseum that can be viewed from any angle, have been released.

On the other hand, GPT-6.2 Sol was developed to efficiently handle everyday tasks. It has improved capabilities in handling complex instructions and writing compared to GPT-6.1 Sol, and in 'FrontierCode 2.0,' a measure of programming performance, it achieved a score only 0.5 points lower than GPT-6.2 Astra at about one-fifth the cost.

The GPT-6.2 Sol is said to perform well on everyday tasks such as finding a suitable home after moving and creating schedules that fit family circumstances. In OpenAI's evaluation test 'LifeVal,' it achieved a higher score than the GPT-6 Astra at approximately one-seventh the cost.


Plotly, a developer of data analysis tools, also tested the performance of GPT-6.2 Sol. In a 43-question test on data analysis, they answered 40 questions correctly, achieving a 93% accuracy rate. While the previous GPT-6.1 Sol answered all 43 questions correctly, the overall test cost decreased from $2.61 (approximately 418 yen) for GPT-6.1 Sol to $1.22 (approximately 195 yen) for GPT-6.2 Sol. The number of input tokens also decreased from 7.8 million to 4.6 million, indicating an improvement in processing efficiency while the accuracy rate slightly decreased.

 


 With improved processing efficiency, GPT-6.2 Astra's fees have been reduced by 20% for input and output compared to the previous GPT-6 Astra, and by 60% for cached input, which reuses previously processed information. As a result, input is now $8 (approximately 1,280 yen) per million tokens, output is $40 (approximately 6,400 yen), and cached input is $0.40 (approximately 64 yen).

 The fees for GPT-6.2 Sol remain the same as GPT-6.1 Sol, with inputs at $2 (approximately 320 yen) per million tokens, outputs at $10 (approximately 1600 yen), and cached inputs at $0.10 (approximately 16 yen).

 On the other hand, both models have also improved their cybersecurity capabilities, so OpenAI is strengthening restrictions on requests that could lead to misuse and enhancing monitoring of their operation. GPT-6.2 Astra has also received high praise for its ability to accelerate AI research, and OpenAI has strengthened monitoring of the research environment and introduced a mechanism to stop AI output when necessary.

 GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol are now available for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users on ChatGPT Work and Codex, and can also be accessed via API. However, there are no plans for their availability on regular ChatGPT at the time of writing.
