OpenAI has released two new models, GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol. Astra is its new frontier model for the hardest projects, and Sol is a faster, cheaper model for everyday work. OpenAI says both deliver better results with fewer output tokens, so subscribers can get more done within their usage limits.

Both models are available starting today to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex. Enterprise and Edu users get access once their workspace admin enables it. Developers can use gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol through the OpenAI API.

## What GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol Improve

OpenAI says it trained GPT-6.2 Astra to create clearer slides, spreadsheets and documents that follow the templates users provide and the conventions of fields like law, sales, finance and accounting. The company says the writing is more direct, with fewer generic takeaways and less filler. Astra also writes cleaner code and builds better frontends for websites, games and 3D design.

OpenAI shared these benchmark results:

- on Agents' Last Exam, which tests multistep professional work, Astra scored 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra at less than a third of the cost
- on Terminal-Bench-Science, Astra scored 9 percentage points higher than GPT-6 Astra, and OpenAI calls it the best model in the world for scientists
- on GDPval v2, Astra improved 6.2 percentage points over GPT-6 Astra while using 22 percent fewer output tokens
- on FrontierCode 2.0, Sol came within 0.5 points of Astra at about one fifth of the cost
- on LifeVal, which tests everyday tasks like planning meals or shortlisting kindergartens, Sol outscored GPT-6 Astra at about one seventh of the cost

Benchmark results come from OpenAI and its partners, so independent testing will show how the models perform in daily use.

## GPT-6.2 Pricing and Ultrafast Mode

Astra is cheaper per task than GPT-6 Astra. Standard API prices per million tokens are:

1. GPT-6.2 Astra: $8 for input, $0.40 for cached input and $40 for output, 20 percent lower than GPT-6 Astra with a 60 percent discount on cached input.
2. GPT-6.2 Sol: $2 for input, $0.10 for cached input and $10 for output, the same as GPT-6.1 Sol.

Pro 500 subscribers can use the faster Ultrafast mode with both models. For the first time, Ultrafast also comes to Pro 200 subscribers, starting with GPT-6.2 Sol. In the API, Ultrafast is available for both models at launch.

OpenAI rates both models at the Critical level for cybersecurity and the High level for biological and chemical capabilities under its Preparedness Framework, and says it added protections and tighter restrictions on dual use requests. GPT-6.2 Astra is also the first model the company has released that meets its High threshold for AI self improvement, which measures how much a model can speed up AI research.

OpenAI also reports one weakness. In two alignment tests, the models were more likely to leave out obstacles that blocked their work, such as a missing file. The company says it added instructions in Codex that substantially reduce this and that it is still investigating the behavior.
