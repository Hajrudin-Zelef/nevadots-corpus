OpenAI signed four agreements with Abu Dhabi-based entities spanning the energy, government, and investment sectors on Monday, according to a statement from the Abu Dhabi Media Office, deepening the AI company’s ties to the emirate as it seeks billions of dollars in financing from the region’s state-backed investors.

## Four Agreements Across Energy, Government, and Investment

__said__.

In the energy sector, state oil company ADNOC, AI firm G42 through its subsidiary Inception42, and OpenAI signed an agreement that will make ADNOC the first energy company to deploy OpenAI’s frontier technology, integrating AI capabilities across its operational, investment, and commercial decision-making. The deployment will run on OpenAI’s GPT models using in-country inference infrastructure in the UAE.

In government, Abu Dhabi launched what it called the world’s first AI-native Machine-Readable Government initiative with OpenAI, translating government policies into machine-readable form so AI systems can assist officials with regulatory compliance tasks such as permits and certificates. Abu Dhabi officials said the first pilot is already running with the Abu Dhabi Agriculture and Food Safety Authority on food import reviews, with government officials retaining oversight of final decisions.

In investment, sovereign wealth fund Mubadala signed an agreement with Inception42 and OpenAI to apply AI to investment analysis, opportunity identification, and risk assessment. A separate five-year agreement between Inception42 and OpenAI covers AI adoption across government, enterprise, and education sectors more broadly.

“Abu Dhabi and the UAE are setting a bold, global standard for AI integration across both the public and private sectors to make sure the benefits of AI reach everyone,” Altman said in a statement. “We are proud to support the UAE’s ambition, anchored in a shared commitment to safety, accessibility and responsible innovation.”

## Agreements Land Amid a Major Funding Push

The signings land as OpenAI works to close a new funding round of up to $30 billion at a valuation of roughly $1.25 trillion, with UAE-based investors playing a lead role. Bloomberg has reported that several UAE funds, including Abu Dhabi-based MGX, are discussing a syndicate that could contribute up to $10 billion toward the round, with BlackRock also reportedly in discussions to participate. Reuters has separately reported that OpenAI is working to secure commitments before rival Anthropic’s expected stock market listing in mid-November, since both companies draw on an overlapping pool of investors.

MGX has backed prior funding rounds for both OpenAI and Anthropic. Monday’s agreements extend a relationship between OpenAI and Abu Dhabi that includes the UAE’s Stargate data center project, OpenAI’s first such buildout outside the United States.
