**SALEM, Ore. —** Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield has filed a lawsuit seeking approximately $2.035 billion in damages against OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT.

The lawsuit was filed Tuesday in Multnomah County Circuit Court. Rayfield alleges the company endangered children and misled parents about the safety of its artificial intelligence chatbot.

The legal action also marks the launch of the Oregon Department of Justice’s new Keeping Kids Safe Initiative.

The lawsuit represents a significant escalation in Oregon’s efforts to address concerns involving artificial intelligence, online safety and the potential effects of emerging technology on children.

The allegations have not been established in court.
