## What happened

Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield sued OpenAI, the maker of ChatGPT, seeking $2.035 billion. He claims the company put young users at risk and deceived families about the chatbot's safety. The state also asks a court to order working safeguards for minors.

## Why it matters

The filing joins other state legal actions that say the chatbot is habit-forming and can expose minors to harmful content.
