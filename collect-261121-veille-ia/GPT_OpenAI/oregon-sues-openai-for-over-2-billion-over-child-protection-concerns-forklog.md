# Oregon Sues OpenAI for Over $2 Billion Over Child Protection Concerns

Oregon sues OpenAI for $2 billion over child safety.

Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI for $2.035 billion. The authorities accused the ChatGPT developer of insufficient protection of minors and misleading users about the chatbot’s safety. According to the prosecution, the service’s features may contribute to emotional dependency in children. The lawsuit also demands changes to the company’s practices.
