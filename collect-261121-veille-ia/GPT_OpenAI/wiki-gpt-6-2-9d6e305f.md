GPT-6.2 is a family of large language models (LLMs) by OpenAI. GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol were released on November 17, 2026,[1] both of which are exclusively available to paid users.[2]
Upon release, both GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol received mixed reviews from power users, drawing criticism for frequent mistakes and negligible real-world improvements over their predecessor models.[3]
