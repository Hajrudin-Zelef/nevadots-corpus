RETRIEVED. Google DeepMind blog, 11 November 2026, by Adam Gayoso, Natasha Latysheva, Pushmeet Kohli and Ziga Avsec. A conditional DNA-sequence model that 'predicts how DNA sequences and their variations impact specific human cell types', predicting one condition (tissue, cell type or state) at a time with that condition as an input at each stage; a cell encoder lets scientists build a condition from their own single-cell data, and the model can be conditioned on new single-cell data WITHOUT RETRAINING. TRAINING DATA: the page gives two figures - 'more than 100 million individual cell profiles across 68 tissues' in the introduction and 'over 107 million individual cell profiles' in the body, unreconciled. READOUTS: gene expression, histone modification and chromatin accessibility, plus DNA METHYLATION as a new addition; cross-modality imputation from one measurement to unmeasured properties. RESULTS: nearly twice as many expression-changing variants identified as the previous state of the art; on the TenK10K dataset (28 primary immune cell types) causal variants found at 30% against 16%, and directional gene effects right 43% against 23%, compared with the original AlphaGenome fine-tuned on single-cell data and other specialised tools; reproduced a published finding that a variant drives inflammatory bowel disease by overactivating ETS2 in gut macrophages; separated vaccine-activated from resting T cells in a COVID-19 mRNA vaccine study it had not seen. AVAILABILITY: non-commercial research now via the AlphaGenome API, private preview for commercial research, Colab tutorials, 'AlphaGenome 2 Skills' for Google Antigravity 'coming soon'. LIMITS, in the page's own words: research use only, '[has] not been validated or approved for clinical or diagnostic purposes'. THE PAGE DOES NOT SAY whether weights are released.

# DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a conditional DNA-sequence model trained on over 107 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues that nearly doubles detection of expression-changing variants and reaches 30% causal-variant recall against 16% on TenK10K - API-gated, no weights, research use only

The architectural change is that predictions are made one condition at a time - a tissue, cell type or state supplied as an input at every stage - with a cell encoder that lets a researcher build a condition out of their own single-cell data and condition the model on cell states it never saw, without retraining. DNA methylation joins expression, histone modification and chromatin accessibility as a readout, and the model will impute unmeasured properties from one measurement. The numbers DeepMind publishes: nearly twice as many expression-changing variants identified as the previous state of the art; on TenK10K, across 28 primary immune cell types, causal variants found at 30% against 16% and the direction of the gene effect right 43% against 23%; a published inflammatory-bowel-disease finding reproduced, with a variant overactivating ETS2 in gut macrophages. Two honest limits are on the page: it is research use only and 'not been validated or approved for clinical or diagnostic purposes', and the training-scale figure appears as both 'more than 100 million' and 'over 107 million' cell profiles without reconciliation. Access is non-commercial research via API now with commercial research in private preview, and the page does not say whether weights are released - which is the quiet part: the most capable public model of how human cells read DNA is gated, and 43% directional accuracy is a research instrument rather than a discovery engine.
