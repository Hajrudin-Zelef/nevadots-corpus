# Gemini 3.8 Live: *What It Does and How to Use It*

Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar adds near real-time video presence to Gemini's live dialogue, available in Gemini Enterprise with 97-language support.

**Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar is a feature that adds near real-time visual presence to Gemini's live dialogue models, letting an AI agent listen, see, and speak with a dynamic visual persona.** It pairs low-latency streaming video with speech, including lip-syncing and natural expressions. Google DeepMind announced it on September 24, 2026, and it is available in Gemini Enterprise.

## How it works

Live Avatar couples Gemini's live dialogue abilities with low-latency streaming video. The model processes visual and audio inputs at the same time, then responds with expressive audio and video. Google DeepMind ties this to how people talk: we listen, look, speak and use facial expressions, and Live Avatar brings those abilities to enterprise agents. Google DeepMind describes the result as an experience that "listens, sees, and speaks with a dynamic visual persona."

Google DeepMind says the feature is backed by Gemini's advanced reasoning. A key part is asynchronous tool calling. The avatar can start tool calls and retrieve data behind the scenes, and the conversation does not stop. In the hotel check-in example from the announcement, the avatar calls tools in the background while the dialogue continues uninterrupted. That means the agent can handle complex tasks without pausing the conversation.

The avatar also handles multiple languages. It uses native multilingual speech-to-speech synchronization, adapting lip-sync and expressions as it moves across 97 languages mid-conversation. Google DeepMind states that video fidelity does not degrade and visual drift does not appear during these transitions.

Organizations can pick from a library of preset avatars. Google DeepMind also says the feature supports a wide range of characters, and each one has its own look, voice and expressive presence. Organizations that need a specific look can customize avatars. From a high-quality reference image, developers can generate a fully animated, responsive avatar that keeps the reference likeness, brand styling, or character identity. Custom avatar creation is currently available only through enterprise allowlisting.

The announcement includes short videos that show the preset characters, the hotel check-in and a mid-conversation language switch. Gemini 3.8 Live itself launched the week before, and Live Avatar adds the video layer on top of it.

Google DeepMind says it built Live Avatar with strict safeguards meant to respect identity and keep AI-generated content transparent. Every output carries a SynthID watermark. This imperceptible watermark is woven into the audio and video, which helps keep AI-generated content detectable. Google DeepMind says this supports its aim to reduce misinformation and misattribution.

- **Custom avatars:** built from a reference image, currently available only through enterprise allowlisting.
- **Transparency:** output carries a SynthID watermark.

Google DeepMind describes the audience as enterprises that want to make their virtual offerings more interactive.

## What you can use it for

- **Customer service.** Google DeepMind names customer service as a use case, where the avatar can interact with users in a more natural way.
- **Interactive walkthroughs.** The announcement also lists interactive walkthroughs as a way to make digital exchanges richer and more accessible.
- **Hotel check-in.** In Google DeepMind's example, the avatar checks in a hotel guest and calls tools in the background while the dialogue continues.
- **Multilingual support.** Because the avatar transitions across 97 languages, it can serve users who switch languages during a conversation.
- **Branded characters.** Organizations can build avatars that match their brand styling or a specific character identity.
