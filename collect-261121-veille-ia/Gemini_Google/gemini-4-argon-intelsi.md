Gemini 4 Argon is Google DeepMind's flagship model, positioned for long software engineering tasks, enterprise knowledge work and cyber defense. Google announced it on September 30, 2026, for cyber defenders in its Fairwind Program, then made it generally available on October 13, 2026, for paid Gemini API customers and Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

**What's new (November 11, 2026):** Gemini 4 Carbon, built on Argon, became generally available on November 10 for paid Gemini API customers and Google AI Ultra subscribers, at Argon's per token price. Google says Argon remains the better choice for most software engineering and knowledge work because it is faster and uses fewer tokens per turn, while Carbon is for the hardest reasoning jobs. Argon is still the Gemini 4 model included with Google AI Pro. See our Gemini 4 Carbon explainer.

## Pricing details

Argon is on the paid API tier only, not the free tier. Its introductory price is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with implicit cache reads at $0.10, through November 30, 2026. From December 1 the price doubles to $4, $20 and $0.20. Batch and Flex tiers cost half the standard rate. Explicit caching is not supported.

## Where to use Gemini 4 Argon

- **Gemini app and AI Mode in Search:** for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app.
- **Developers:** the paid Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Android Studio and Google Antigravity. In AI Studio, Argon is available on the Google AI Pro and Ultra plans (Ultra has the highest quota) but not on the Free plan.
- **Cyber defenders:** a build without cyber guardrails through the Fairwind Program.

## Features

Argon supports low, medium and high thinking levels, search grounding, code execution, function calling and computer use in preview. It does not generate images and does not support the Live API.

## How it compares

Google reports strong results on long software engineering and video understanding, and the independent Artificial Analysis index placed Argon level with GPT-6 Astra. At the introductory price it costs the same per token as GPT-6.1 Sol.
