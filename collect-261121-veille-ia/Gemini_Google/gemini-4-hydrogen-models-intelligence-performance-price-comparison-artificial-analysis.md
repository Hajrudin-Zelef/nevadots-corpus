# Gemini 4 Hydrogen: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Xhigh) is above average in intelligence and reasonably priced when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also slower than average per task and very verbose. The model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Xhigh) scores 54 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it above average among comparable models (median: 44). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 304M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 65.4M.

Pricing for Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Xhigh) is $1.50 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $2.00) and $7.50 per 1M output tokens (moderately priced, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $3.16 per task to evaluate Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Xhigh) on the Intelligence Index.

At 15.7 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Xhigh) is slower than average compared with the median of 12.8 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
