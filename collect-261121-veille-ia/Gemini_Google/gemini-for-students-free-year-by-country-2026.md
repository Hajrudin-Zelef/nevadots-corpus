## Gemini for students: who gets which plan

Google launched its new student offer on Aug 19, 2026: “one year of a Google AI plan free of charge for eligible college students around the world”[7]. The word “plan” matters. Students in the US get **Google AI Pro**. Students in over 140 markets, including the UK and Australia, get **Google AI Plus**, a smaller plan with 2x instead of 4x higher usage limits and 400 GB instead of 5 TB of storage[7][2]. There is no student offer in Canada right now[5][4].

Many guides still say “Gemini Pro free for students” for everyone. That was closer to true in 2025, when Google One offered a Google AI Pro student trial[6]. Today the plan you get depends on the country of your university, as the offer terms put it[5].

## How to claim it, and who can’t

You claim the offer at one.google.com/ai-student: sign in, pick the student offer, verify through SheerID, then finish the sign-up with a payment method[6]. Four things decide whether it works:

**Personal Google Account**Use your own account. The offer is not available on your school-issued Workspace for Education account[5].

**SheerID**You verify your student status through SheerID, using a valid school email address. Claim with the same Google Account you used when SheerID opened[6].

One more detail for people who had the 2025 offer: if your old AI Pro student trial has expired, you can claim the new one[1][2]. If it is still running, Google says you may get a limited-time discount on regular plans when it ends[6].

### Is there a Gemini student discount after the free year?

Outside the US, Canada, Albania, Bolivia, Hong Kong, Macau and Tunisia, Google’s help center lists two more student offers: a discounted Google AI Pro plan “while you remain a student, for up to 4 consecutive years”, and Google AI Pro bundled with YouTube Premium[6]. The help page shows no price; you see the offers after you sign in. For US students, the pages we read mention no discount after the free year: AI Pro renews at $19.99 a month[1].

## What to do before your free year ends

The free year is easy to start and easy to forget. The offer terms describe what happens on day 366, so we turned them into a short plan[5]:

1. **Redeem the offerby Dec 31, 2026** Your 12 months start on the day you redeem, not on Jan 1. If you start another Google One student offer while you are on a trial, the current one ends and the new one starts[5][6].
2. **Put the end date in your calendarthe day you redeem** Add a reminder two weeks before the 12 months end. Google says it will notify you before the change, but the reminder is yours[6].
3. **Re-verify when Google asksabout once a year** The student pages say: “Eligibility must be verified each year to maintain access.”[1][2]
4. **Clean up your storageone month before the end** If you used more than the free storage during the offer and then cancel, you cannot store anything new (in Drive, Gmail, Photos) until you delete files or buy a plan[5]. Move big lecture videos and old backups first.
5. **Save what you needbefore you decide** Download the study notes, summaries and research reports you want to keep into your own files, so you are not tied to one subscription during your final semester.
6. **Cancel or keep itbefore the 12 months end** If you do nothing, your card is charged the regular monthly price. If you cancel during the free year, your membership may stay active until it ends. Upgrading from the AI Plus trial to a paid plan means you cannot get the trial again[5][6].

## Before you upload your thesis draft to Gemini

Unpublished research, interview data and your own drafts are worth a quick look at Google’s privacy settings first:

**Keep Activity on**Human reviewers, including trained reviewers from service providers, may read some chats. Google: “Please don’t enter confidential information that you wouldn’t want a reviewer to see.”[24]

**How long chats stay**Gemini activity is auto-deleted after 18 months by default (you can pick 3 or 36 months, or keep it until you delete it). Chats that were reviewed are kept for up to three years, even if you delete your activity[24].

**Voice and Live**Google says your audio and Gemini Live videos and screenshares aren’t used to improve Google services by default[24].

**Unpublished work**For a draft or data you don’t want reviewed, turn Keep Activity off or use a temporary chat. Those chats are kept for 72 hours and, without feedback, not used to improve Google’s AI models[24].

Also check your course’s rules on AI use. Some programs ask you to declare which tools you used and for what.

## Gemini for students: FAQ

## Is Gemini free for students?

Gemini itself has a free plan for everyone, and it already includes Deep Research and Gemini Live. On top of that, Google gives eligible college students 18+ one paid plan free for 12 months: Google AI Pro in the US, Google AI Plus in over 140 markets outside the US. You must redeem by December 31, 2026, verify through SheerID and add a payment method.

## Is the Gemini student offer Google AI Pro or AI Plus?

It depends on where your university is. Students in the US get Google AI Pro (then $19.99 a month). Students in the UK, Australia, Ireland, New Zealand, India, the Philippines and most other markets get Google AI Plus (then £4.49, A$7.99 or the local price). Google’s offer terms say the plan depends on the country of your higher education institution.

## Can students in Canada get Gemini for free?

Not right now. Google’s offer terms exclude Canada, along with Albania, Bolivia, Hong Kong, Macau and Tunisia. Google’s Canadian student page says the previous student offer ended on December 9, 2025. The regular free Gemini plan still works in Canada.

## Is there a Gemini student discount after the free year?

In the US, not on the pages we read: after 12 months, AI Pro renews at $19.99 a month. Outside the US (except Canada, Albania, Bolivia, Hong Kong, Macau and Tunisia), Google’s help center also lists a discounted Google AI Pro student plan for up to 4 consecutive years and a Google AI Pro bundle with YouTube Premium. Google does not show the price on that page; you see the offers after you sign in at one.google.com/ai-student.

## What happens when the free year ends?

Your card is charged the standard monthly price for your plan, unless you cancel before the offer period ends (or your local law requires your consent first). If you cancel during the free year, Google says your membership may stay active until the end of it. If your Google storage is over the free limit by then, you cannot save anything new until you delete files or pay.

## Can I use Gemini to write my thesis?

Gemini is strong for understanding readings, quizzing yourself and getting feedback on your own text. For a thesis you also need verified academic sources, a page number for each citation and your required citation style. Google’s student and plan pages we read do not mention page numbers or citation styles, and Google’s own footnote says “Check responses.” Check your course’s AI policy either way; you stay the author.

## Is ChatGPT also free for students?

Until October 31, 2026, OpenAI offers students at eligible U.S. degree-granting institutions four free months of ChatGPT Plus (then $20 a month, verified through SheerID). After that date, check OpenAI’s help center for a new offer. Gemini’s offer runs longer (12 months) and is available in many more countries.
