# Gemini free for one year for university students, how to claim it

##### Google is giving university students a free year of Gemini. In the US it is AI Pro, elsewhere AI Plus with 400 GB. Redeem by December 31, 2026.

Google is giving away twelve months of the **Google AI Plus** plan to adult university students in over 140 countries, the UK included, with the Gemini app, 400 GB of storage and video generation. The plan is worth 4.99 dollars a month and must be activated by **December 31, 2026**. In the United States the offer is richer (a year of Google AI Pro worth 19.99 dollars a month), but for students outside the US the free deal runs through Google One and a student status check. This guide covers what's actually included, who can claim it and the steps to activate it without paying anything in the first year.

Inside the free window you get access to Gemini Omni with video creation, usage limits doubled compared with the base plan, and 400 GB of storage shared across Drive, Gmail and Photos.

The useful part for studying isn't just the model's power, it's the surrounding features. There are also podcast-style audio summaries and Canvas, the workspace where Gemini writes, revises and shows interactive visualizations.

One point trips up a lot of people. The gift changes depending on the country, and the difference isn't cosmetic. Students in the United States get **Google AI Pro**, a higher tier plan with 5 TB of storage and quadrupled usage limits. Students in the UK and most of the rest of the world get Google AI Plus instead, a full free year but with a smaller allowance. The table below compares the two plans.

The core condition is simple. You must be an enrolled university student and a legal adult. Sources report an indicative range between 18 and 24 years old, but the constraint that really matters is a different one. At activation Google asks you to prove your student status, and it does so through **SheerID**, the external service that verifies enrollment by cross-checking data with your university.

Two more things are needed. A valid payment method registered with Google, which isn't charged during the free year but is required for automatic renewal, and willingness to redo the verification every year. Eligibility isn't permanent, it has to be confirmed each time, so anyone who graduates or changes status loses the discounted rate.

The offer is open to both new users and anyone who had already tried AI Pro in 2025 and saw that period expire. In practice, having used a previous trial doesn't rule you out. A handful of markets remain excluded where the deal changes or isn't active, including Bolivia, Albania, Canada, Macao, Hong Kong and Tunisia, while the United States gets AI Pro instead of AI Plus.

The process takes just a few minutes and runs entirely through Google One. Here are the steps in the right order.

Go to one.google.com/ai-student or start from the official Gemini for Students page. It's the single entry point for activation, don't look for the offer inside the Gemini app.

Use the Google account you want to link to the subscription. A personal account is better than one issued by your university, because some features and bundles aren't available on school accounts managed by the institution.

When prompted, complete the SheerID verification by entering your university and enrollment details. If automatic verification fails, the system may ask for a document proving current enrollment, such as a certificate or an institutional email.

Add a valid card. There will be no charge during the 12 free months, but activation won't complete without a payment method.

Confirm the activation. From this point Gemini runs with the limits and features of the AI Plus plan, and the 400 GB of storage appears in your Google One account. Mark the expiry date on your calendar, you'll need it to decide whether to renew or cancel.

Once the gift ends, the plan doesn't turn itself off. Unless you cancel, it renews automatically at full price. For most of the world that means **$4.99/month** for Google AI Plus, in the United States $19.99/month for Google AI Pro. You can cancel at any time from Google One settings, and cancelling before the expiry date avoids any charge.

If after twelve months AI has become part of your routine, $4.99/month is a modest amount. If you've barely used it, the expiry date is the right moment to stop, no guilt and no cost.

## #### 1. What's the difference between Google AI Plus and Google AI Pro?


**AI Plus is the mid-tier plan, AI Pro is the higher one.** Students in the UK and in over 140 countries get AI Plus, with 400 GB of storage and double usage limits. Students in the United States get AI Pro, with 5 TB of storage, quadruple limits and Gemini built into Gmail and Docs. Both last a free year.

## #### 2. Do you really need to enter a credit card?


**Yes, a valid payment method is mandatory.** Nothing is charged during the twelve free months, but without a card the activation won't complete and automatic renewal can't start. You can remove the card or cancel before the expiry date to avoid paying.

## #### 3. What happens when the free year ends?


**The plan renews automatically at full price.** For most of the world that's $4.99/month for AI Plus, in the United States $19.99/month for AI Pro. If you cancel before the expiry date from Google One settings, you pay nothing and still keep the benefits until the last day of the free period.

## #### 4. Can I activate it if I'd already used the AI Pro trial in 2025?


**Yes.** The offer is valid for both new users and anyone who tried AI Pro in 2025 and saw that period expire. Having used a previous trial doesn't rule you out of the current free year.

## #### 5. Which countries is it not available in?


**The AI Plus offer covers over 140 markets but with a few exceptions.** Bolivia, Albania, Canada, Macao, Hong Kong and Tunisia are excluded. The United States doesn't get AI Plus but the higher tier AI Pro instead. Verification runs through SheerID on Google One.

## #### 6. Is a university account fine or do I need a personal one?


**A personal Google account is better.** Some features and bundles, such as the YouTube Premium discount available in some markets, don't work on school accounts managed by the university. Linking a personal account avoids blocks during activation.
