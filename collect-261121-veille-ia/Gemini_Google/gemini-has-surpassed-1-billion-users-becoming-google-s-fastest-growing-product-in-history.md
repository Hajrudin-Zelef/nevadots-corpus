# Gemini has surpassed 1 billion users, becoming Google's fastest-growing product in history.


 Google CEO Sundar Pichai has announced that its AI, Gemini, has reached 1 billion monthly active users. Gemini is integrated into almost all Google services, including Google Search and Gmail.

 1B+ people are now using @Geminiapp every month to spark new ideas and get things done. It's our fastest growing product ever, and our 14th to hit the 1B-user mark.

 Kudos to @JoshWoodward & the entire Gemini team, and thank you to everyone who has been on this journey with us -… pic.twitter.com/sSWrULX4Qn



 Gemini is an AI that debuted in 2023, and in addition to chat support, it leverages features such as image processing and speech recognition to integrate with numerous Google services.

 **Gemini, a multimodal AI that can process text, voice, and images simultaneously and engage in more natural interactions than humans, surpassing GPT-4 performance, has been released - GIGAZINE**


 According to CEO Pichai, Gemini is the 14th Google product to reach the milestone of '1 billion users,' and is the fastest-growing in terms of growth potential.

 Josh Woodward, Google's vice president in charge of Gemini, has provided further details.

 First, 63% of users speak directly to Gemini, and the percentage of users who use Gemini solely through voice communication is also increasing.

 🗣️Voice is transforming daily interactions

 + 63% of users now talk directly to Gemini, including more “voice only” users

 + Busy parents are 43% more likely to use voice for everyday tasks

 + Rolling out 60+ new regional dialects soon, can't wait for you to try this!



 'Gemini Live,' which allows users to interact with Gemini in real time while displaying surrounding video, is proving popular among DIY enthusiasts and students. 38% of requests from schools include attachments, indicating that students' use of AI extends beyond simple text input.

 📷Gemini in the physical world

 + 1 out of 5 Gemini Live interactions go beyond voice

 + People are using live camera feeds and screen sharing for real-time problem-solving

 + Really popular with DIY'ers and students



 More than 150 million images are generated every day, and Gemini is reportedly a favorite among small and medium-sized businesses for creating marketing materials.

 🖼️ Small businesses love Gemini's creative features

 + Gemini now generates 150M+ images every day

 + Gemini lets you do images, video, and audio in one place

 + SMBs love Gemini for making marketing materials



 iOS has over 100 million active users, and macOS power users reportedly use prompts about twice as often as those on other platforms.

 🍎Apple users are turning to Gemini

 + Over 100M+ active users on iOS

 + macOS power users prompt ~2x more frequently than other surfaces



 Most recently, it has been revealed that they are developing a new model of Gemini, called 'Gemini 4'.

 **Google announces 'Gemini 3.6 Flash,' 'Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite,' and 'Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber,' and is also developing Gemini 4 - GIGAZINE**
