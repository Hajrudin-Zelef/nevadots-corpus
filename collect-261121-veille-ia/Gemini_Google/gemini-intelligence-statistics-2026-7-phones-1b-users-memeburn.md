Google’s Gemini app crossed **1 billion monthly users** in August 2026, yet the full Gemini Intelligence experience runs on just **seven phones**. That gap matters because Google is now replacing Google Assistant with Gemini on Android, so the AI on your next phone depends on which tier of Gemini it can actually run. Here’s what the latest data says about supported devices, hardware rules, real-world usage, rival assistants and what Google’s AI costs.

## **What Is Gemini Intelligence?**

### **Definition in Plain Language**

**Gemini Intelligence** is Google’s name for the most advanced set of Gemini features on Android phones. Regular Gemini, for example, answers questions and generates text or images. By contrast, Gemini Intelligence can carry out multi-step tasks inside other apps, fill in forms using your personal info, and run some AI directly on the phone through **Gemini Nano**, Google’s small on-device AI model. In other words, the processing happens on your phone’s chip instead of Google’s servers.

Google announced Gemini Intelligence at The Android Show: I/O Edition on **May 11, 2026**. At first, the feature list included task automation, Chrome’s Auto Browse and smarter Autofill. It also added **Rambler**, a Gboard voice-typing tool that cleans up casual speech, as well as **Create My Widget**, which builds home screen widgets from a text prompt.

### **The 2026 Rollout Timeline**

**Table 2: Gemini Intelligence and Gemini on Android, key dates**

Of these dates, the last one is the change most Android owners will notice. Google began the Assistant-to-Gemini switch on **September 4, 2026**, about six months later than its original March 2026 target. Once your phone switches, though, you can’t go back. As a result, the move ends almost a decade of Google Assistant, which launched in 2016.

## **Which Phones Support Gemini Intelligence?**

### **The Seven Phones Running Gemini Nano 4**

Then, in late August 2026, Google quietly updated its developer documentation. Since that update, seven phones support Gemini Nano 4, the newest version of Google’s on-device model.

**Table 3: Phones that support Gemini Nano 4 (as of August 28, 2026)**

Even within that list, however, features differ. For example, only the Pixel 11 phones currently support Rambler and Create My Widget, although Samsung is reportedly working on its own version of the widget tool.

From our perspective, therefore, the cheapest way into full Gemini Intelligence in October 2026 is the **$899 Pixel 11**. That’s because every Samsung option is a foldable that starts above $1,100.

### **Hardware Requirements: 12GB RAM and Six Years of Updates**

Google’s android.com page lists eight requirements a phone must meet before it can get Gemini Intelligence. For buyers, the most important ones are:

- **12GB or more RAM** (RAM is the short-term memory your phone uses to run apps)
- **Gemini Nano v3 or newer** running through Android’s AI Core system
- A **qualified flagship chip**
- **5 Android version upgrades** and**6 years** of quarterly security updates
- Ongoing quality checks, including crash-rate targets in 2026 and stricter enforcement in 2027

Taken together, these rules work as a quality gate. Google isn’t just asking for powerful hardware; it’s also asking phone makers to commit to years of support before their devices get its best AI.

In reality, Gemini isn’t one product with one set of requirements. Instead, it’s a ladder, and each step needs more memory.

### **What Happened to the Galaxy S26 and Pixel 10?**

Above all, this is the biggest open question in the data. In May 2026, Google said Gemini Intelligence would reach the Galaxy S26 and Pixel 10 **“this summer.”** However, by August 27, 9to5Google reported it was still unclear whether either phone would get it. In addition, neither phone appears on the Gemini Nano 4 list.

Still, owners aren’t left with nothing. Gemini’s earlier task automation beta was available at launch on both phones in February 2026. Meanwhile, Google says the full features will arrive **“in waves,”** so we’ll update this section once it confirms a date.

## **How Many People Use Gemini on Their Phones?**

### **Gemini App Growth Timeline, 2024–2026**

Google doesn’t publish separate numbers for Gemini Intelligence. However, it regularly shares user counts for the Gemini app, and that’s where the phone story shows up most clearly.


 Clearly, the growth is steep, especially over the past year. By our calculation, the app grew about **2.5x** between May 2025 and August 2026, which is why Google calls Gemini *“the fastest-growing product in Google’s history.”* Engagement is rising too, since Sundar Pichai said in July 2026 that **daily active users tripled** in a year.

### **Why “950 Million” and “1 Billion” Aren’t the Same Metric**

If you look closely at Table 5, you’ll notice the labels change. Google used **monthly active users (MAU)** in its July earnings remarks, but **monthly users** in its August blog post. Because those terms may not measure the same thing, we don’t treat the jump from 950 million to 1 billion in three weeks as precise growth. In fact, we ran into the same issue in our ChatGPT statistics research: a big number can be accurate and still mislead you if you don’t know what it counts.

### **Gemini on iPhone: 100 Million Users and the Siri Deal**

Gemini isn’t just an Android story, though. According to Google, the Gemini app has **100 million+ active users on iOS**. So if you divide that by 1 billion monthly users, iPhone owners make up **at least 10%** of Gemini’s audience, by our calculation. We call it a minimum because Google used different labels for the two figures.

On top of that, there’s a bigger iPhone link. In April 2026, Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian confirmed that Gemini-based models will power Apple Intelligence features, including *“a more personalized Siri coming later this year.”* Therefore, Gemini technology now sits behind the default assistant on both major phone platforms, even when the Gemini brand isn’t visible.

## **How People Use Gemini on Smartphones**

### **Voice, Camera and Gemini Live**

Phones also change how people talk to AI. According to Google’s August 2026 data:

- **63%** of Gemini users talk to it directly by voice
- In particular, busy parents are **43%** more likely to use voice for everyday tasks
- **1 in 5** Gemini Live conversations go beyond voice, using the live camera or screen sharing
- Gemini also creates **150 million+ images** every day

For context, Gemini Live is the real-time conversation mode, where you can point your camera at something and ask about it. Overall, the 1-in-5 figure suggests visual help is becoming a regular habit rather than a novelty.

### **Task Automation: From a Handful of Apps to 40+**

Above all, task automation is Gemini Intelligence’s headline feature. Specifically, it lets Gemini open apps and complete steps for you, such as building a shopping cart or booking a table, and then asks you to approve the final step.

At Galaxy Unpacked on July 22, Google said this feature grew from a handful of apps to more than 40. Similarly, the supported tasks moved beyond the early beta’s rideshares and takeout to shopping, dinner reservations, travel, and event tickets.

That said, the fine print matters. Specifically, Google’s footnotes limit task automation to the **US and South Korea**, to select apps and devices, and to users **18 and older**. Similarly, on the Pixel 11, Android Central notes that agentic task handling is **US-only**. So most of the world’s Gemini users can’t use the flagship feature yet.

### **On-Device AI: Scam Detection and Gemma**

Not all of Gemini’s phone AI is visible. In fact, some of it runs quietly in the background, on the device:

- Android’s scam protections handle **over 10 billion suspected malicious calls and messages** every month, combining blocked calls and warnings. According to Google, a Gemini on-device model powers Scam Detection’s on-device analysis. Keep in mind, though, that the 10 billion figure covers all of Android’s protections, not only Gemini.
- In addition, Google’s **Gemma** models, open models built to run on local devices, have been downloaded**900 million+** times. Gemma 4 alone reached**300 million** downloads after its April 2026 launch, and our open-weight AI model statistics show how these models compare.
- Finally, Google claims the Pixel 11’s **Tensor G6** chip delivers a**50%** boost in on-device AI performance through its TPU (tensor processing unit), a part of the chip built for AI math.

### **What Samsung’s Galaxy AI Data Shows**

Samsung’s phones are Gemini’s biggest Android showcase, so Samsung’s own data gives a hint of which phone AI features people actually use. For example, in March 2026, Samsung said 431 million people have Galaxy AI on their phones. Meanwhile, in Colombia, Samsung reported:

- **Circle to Search** , a Google feature built on its AI search, had usage close to**70%**
- Photo Assist and Generative Edit topped **20%**
- Instant Slow-mo passed **14%**
- Writing Assist sat near **7%** , while Call Assist was near**3%**

We noticed a clear pattern here: visual and search tools win, whereas writing and calling tools lag. These are Samsung’s own numbers for one country, though, so they’re a signal rather than a global benchmark.

## **Gemini vs ChatGPT and Other AI Assistants**

### **US Survey Data by Age**

Gemini is built into billions of Android phones, but surveys show ChatGPT still leads in the US. When Pew Research surveyed US adults in February 2026, it found that 24% have used Gemini, compared with 44% for ChatGPT.

**Table 6: Share of US adults who have ever used each chatbot**

One detail stands out in particular. Specifically, Gemini’s strongest group is adults aged **30–49 (34%)**, not the youngest group. ChatGPT, by contrast, peaks with 18-to-29-year-olds. Pew also notes that not every gap in its chart is statistically significant.


 Likewise, a separate Ipsos poll from March 2026 measured past-week use: **31%** for ChatGPT and **21%** for Gemini. Both surveys were taken before Gemini passed 1 billion users, so the gap may have narrowed since then.

### **Mobile App Share**

App data, on the other hand, tells a faster-moving story. For example, Apptopia tracked daily active users across the top seven AI chatbot apps between August 2025 and February 2026:

- **Worldwide:** Gemini’s share rose from**9% to 25%** , while ChatGPT’s fell from 73% to 57%
- **US:** Gemini rose from**13% to 25%** , while ChatGPT dropped from 57% to 42%


 As a result, Apptopia called Gemini *“the clear number two globally.”* ChatGPT still leads, but the gap on phones is shrinking quickly.

### **Reader Polls vs Representative Surveys**

You’ll also see very different numbers in tech-site polls. In June 2026, more than 2,000 Android Authority readers voted on their favorite assistant, and 71.5% picked Gemini. That’s an enthusiast audience of Android fans, so it doesn’t represent the general public. Instead, we include it to show how much your source changes the story, not as a market share figure.

## **The Business Side: Pixel, Samsung and AI Subscriptions**

### **Pixel Shipments Grow While the Market Shrinks**

Google’s own phones are growing even though the overall market is shrinking. For instance, Counterpoint data shows:

- **Q1 2026:** Pixel shipments rose**14%** while the global market fell 6%
- **Q2 2026:** Pixel shipments rose**16%** while the market fell**11%** , its weakest second quarter since 2013

Even so, Pixel still sits outside the top five brands. By comparison, Samsung held 24% of the market in Q2 and Apple held 20%. Meanwhile, the Pixel 11 launched at **899–****1,299**, $100 more than last year, with **12GB of RAM as standard** and **7 years** of updates.

### **Samsung’s 800 Million Gemini Devices Target**

Samsung is also Gemini’s largest distribution partner. In January 2026, co-CEO TM Roh told Reuters Samsung would double Gemini-powered mobile devices to 800 million by the end of 2026, up from about 400 million in 2025.

When you put that next to Table 3, the scale of the gap becomes obvious. On one hand, Samsung’s Gemini footprint is measured in hundreds of millions of devices. On the other hand, its Gemini Nano 4 lineup is just three foldables, a category that makes up only about 2% of smartphone shipments. More broadly, Counterpoint expects GenAI smartphones to reach **45%** of global shipments in 2026. Most of those phones run some AI, but not Google’s top tier.

### **What Google’s AI Plans Cost**

Generally, Gemini’s basic features are free. However, the heavier features sit behind Google’s AI subscriptions, which Google reorganized at I/O 2026.

**Table 7: Google AI subscription plans (US, 2026)**

New phones come with free trials, but they’re getting shorter. For instance, Galaxy Z8 buyers get a **six-month Google AI Pro** trial on select models. Likewise, for the Pixel 11 Pro, ChannelNews reports Google cut the trial from **12 months to six**.

Reports also disagree on which plan Pixel 11 buyers get. PhoneArena calls it AI Plus *“valued at $119.94.”* By our math, though, six months of AI Pro at $19.99 equals exactly **$119.94**, while six months of AI Plus would be just $47.94. So the bundle is most likely AI Pro, but check Google’s store page before you buy.

## **Where Gemini Intelligence Is Available, and Where It Isn’t**

Ultimately, availability is the quiet limit on all these numbers. As of October 2026, here’s where things stand:

- **Task automation:** US and South Korea only, users 18+
- **Pixel 11 agentic tasks:** US only
- **Gemini assistant:** about 164 countries, as reported in May 2026
- **Scam Detection for calls (Pixel):** 12 countries, including the US, UK, India and Japan
- **Gemini-powered Messages protections:** latest flagships in the US, Canada and the UK

In short, Gemini reaches a global audience, but Gemini Intelligence’s most advanced features reach a narrow slice of devices in a handful of markets. So if you live outside the US, a new Pixel 11 or Galaxy Z Fold8 won’t give you everything Google demos on stage, at least for now.

## **What the 2026 Data Tells Us**

Three things stand out when we put the numbers side by side.

First, **reach and capability have split apart.** Gemini’s app audience passed 1 billion, but the version Google markets as Gemini Intelligence runs on seven phones. For most Android owners, then, “Gemini” in 2026 means the assistant and the app, not the agent that books your dinner.

Second, **hardware rules now decide who gets the best AI.** The 12GB RAM floor and six-year update promise favor expensive flagships at a time when memory costs are pushing phone prices up. Consequently, Gemini Intelligence could stay a premium feature for longer than Google’s marketing suggests.

Third, **Gemini is closing the gap with ChatGPT faster on phones than in surveys.** Apptopia’s app data shows Gemini’s share of chatbot daily users nearly tripling in six months, while early-2026 US surveys still show ChatGPT well ahead. Google’s built-in distribution on Android, plus Gemini’s role behind Siri, also gives it an advantage no standalone chatbot has. ## **FAQs**

### **What is Gemini Intelligence?**

Gemini Intelligence is Google’s most advanced set of Gemini features for Android, and it includes multi-step task automation across apps and Gemini-powered Autofill, as well as Rambler voice typing and Create My Widget. Google first announced it on May 11, 2026.

### **Which phones support Gemini Intelligence?**

As of August 2026, seven phones run Gemini Nano 4: the Pixel 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL and 11 Pro Fold, plus the Galaxy Z Flip8, Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra. In addition, Google requires 12GB+ RAM and six years of security updates.

### **Will the Galaxy S26 and Pixel 10 get Gemini Intelligence?**

Google said in May 2026 that they would, “this summer.” By late August, however, it was still unconfirmed, and neither phone appears on the Gemini Nano 4 list. Both already have Gemini’s earlier task automation beta.

### **How many people use Gemini?**

Google says the Gemini app passed 1 billion monthly users in August 2026, including more than 100 million active users on iOS. Even so, it hasn’t shared separate user numbers for Gemini Intelligence.

### **Is Gemini more popular than ChatGPT?**

Not yet in the US. In fact, Pew found 24% of US adults have used Gemini, compared with 44% for ChatGPT (February 2026). On phones, though, Apptopia data shows Gemini’s share of chatbot daily users rose to 25% globally by February 2026.

### **Does Gemini Intelligence cost money?**

The core features come with supported phones, but heavier AI use sits behind Google’s AI plans, which cost $7.99 (Plus), $19.99 (Pro) or $99.99 and up (Ultra) per month in the US. New Pixel 11 and Galaxy Z8 phones also include a six-month trial.

### **Is Google Assistant going away?**

Yes, on phones, since Google started replacing Google Assistant with Gemini on Android on September 4, 2026. After the switch, you can’t undo it, although phones that can’t run Gemini keep Assistant for now.

### **Where is Gemini Intelligence available?**

For now, task automation is limited to the US and South Korea for users 18 and older. Similarly, agentic tasks on the Pixel 11 are US-only. Other countries get some Gemini Intelligence features, but not task automation.

## **Methodology**

We built this stat-camp from data published between October 2024 and October 2026, with priority on figures from the last six months. Our source ranking put company disclosures first (Google blog posts, Alphabet earnings remarks, android.com and Samsung Newsroom), followed by research firms and survey organizations (Pew Research, Ipsos, Apptopia, Counterpoint and Epoch AI), and then established tech publications.

In addition, each figure carries a label: company disclosure, survey, third-party estimate or our calculation. When companies used different metrics, such as *“monthly active users”* versus *“monthly users,”* we flagged it rather than treating the numbers as directly comparable. We also excluded figures we couldn’t trace to a source, including widely shared Circle to Search device counts. Because Google hasn’t published usage numbers specific to Gemini Intelligence, we don’t estimate any. Finally, we checked all data on October 13, 2026.
