Google is turning Gemini Live from a chatty voice assistant into an actual productivity tool. The company today rolled out a batch of **Gemini Live integrations** that let you compose emails, search your inbox, and manage Workspace tasks entirely by voice. Instead of just talking with an AI, you can now get real work done without touching your keyboard.

The update focuses on three of the most common desk tasks: writing emails, digging through your inbox, and juggling calendars and documents. Google built the integrations directly into the Gemini Live voice interface, so you never have to leave the conversation to switch apps. If you’ve used Gemini Live mainly for brainstorming or casual Q&A, this update changes what it’s actually good for.

Here’s a full breakdown of what’s new, how each integration works, and how to start using it today.

## What’s New: Gemini Live Gets a Productivity Overhaul in 2026

Google announced this expansion as part of a broader push to make Gemini Live useful for daily work, not just conversation. The company added support for **Spark**, its AI-assisted email composer, along with deeper Gmail access and hooks into other Google Workspace apps.

The timing matters. Voice assistants have historically been great at answering trivia or setting timers, but weak at handling multi-step tasks like drafting a professional email. Google’s update targets that gap directly.

A few things stand out about this rollout:

- Multiple integrations launched together rather than trickling out one at a time
- The focus is entirely on actionable output, not just information retrieval
- Everything happens inside the existing Gemini Live voice session, with no app switching required

This is a meaningful shift in how Google positions Gemini Live. It’s no longer just a talking companion. It’s becoming a hands-free control layer for your entire Google account.

## Spark Integration: Compose and Manage Emails Without Stopping

The headline feature is **Spark integration**, which lets you draft entire emails using only your voice. You describe what you want to say, and Gemini Live turns it into a properly formatted message, complete with a subject line and appropriate tone.

What makes this different from basic dictation is the editing layer. You can ask Gemini Live to make the email shorter, more formal, or friendlier, and it revises the draft in real time without restarting the conversation. That back-and-forth mimics how you’d actually work with a human assistant.

Once you’re happy with the draft, you get a clean handoff between voice and text. The email lands in your Gmail drafts folder, fully editable, so you can make final tweaks before hitting send. This matters if you’re walking, driving, or otherwise unable to type but still need to get a message out quickly.

### Why This Beats Basic Voice-to-Text

Standard dictation tools transcribe your words literally, typos and all. Spark integration understands intent instead. You can say “tell my landlord the sink is still leaking and ask when someone can come fix it,” and Gemini Live builds a coherent, polite email around that request rather than transcribing it word for word.

## Gmail Integration: Access Your Inbox in Conversation

Beyond composing new messages, **Gmail integration** lets you query your existing inbox hands-free. You can ask Gemini Live to summarize unread messages, pull up a specific email thread, or check whether someone already replied to you.

This is useful in situations where opening your phone and scrolling isn’t practical. Think cooking, commuting, or sitting in a meeting where you need a quick answer without visibly checking your phone.

Some of the things you can ask Gemini Live to do with your inbox:

- Summarize everything you received today from a specific sender
- Find an email containing a particular keyword or attachment
- Read back the most recent message in a conversation thread
- Check if a flight confirmation or receipt has arrived

Google says the goal is to reduce the friction of email management, not replace the Gmail app entirely. You’ll still open the app for detailed review or bulk actions, but for quick lookups, voice now works.

## Beyond Email: Other Productivity Integrations Explained

Email isn’t the only target. Google also expanded Gemini Live’s reach into other core Workspace apps, giving it a broader role as a voice-driven productivity hub.

The current set of integrations includes:

- **Google Calendar** : check upcoming events, ask about scheduling conflicts, or add new appointments by voice
- **Google Docs** : dictate notes into a new document or ask Gemini Live to summarize an existing one
- **Google Tasks** : add to-do items or check what’s due without opening a separate app
- **Gmail and Spark** : compose, search, and manage email as described above

Each integration follows the same design principle: stay inside the conversation, get the task done, and hand off a clean result you can review later. Calendar requests, for example, don’t just confirm verbally, they actually create the event so it’s waiting for you when you check your phone.

This app-by-app rollout suggests Google is building toward a future where Gemini Live functions as a full voice layer over Workspace, similar to how a human assistant might manage your inbox and calendar together.

## How to Use Gemini Live’s New Features: A Practical Walkthrough

Getting started with these integrations doesn’t require much setup. Here’s a simple walkthrough to try them out.

**Open Gemini Live**on your phone through the Gemini app or your assigned shortcut.

**Start a voice session**by tapping the microphone icon and speaking naturally.

**Ask for an email draft**, for example: “Draft an email to my team about moving Friday’s meeting to 3pm.”

**Review the spoken draft**and ask for edits if needed, such as “make it shorter” or “add a line thanking everyone for flexibility.”

**Confirm and send**, or ask Gemini Live to save it as a draft in Gmail for later review.

**Try inbox queries**by asking things like “Do I have any emails from my accountant this week?”

**Test calendar and task requests**with phrases like “What’s on my calendar tomorrow?” or “Add ‘renew car registration’ to my tasks.”

A few practical tips make this smoother:

- Speak in complete sentences rather than fragments; Gemini Live handles natural phrasing better than short commands
- Always review email drafts before sending, especially for anything sensitive or professional
- Use follow-up requests to refine results instead of starting a new session each time

On compatibility, these features work on devices that already support Gemini Live, which generally means recent Android phones and the Gemini app on iOS. Some integrations may require you to be signed into the Google account tied to your Gmail and Workspace apps.

## Why This Matters: The Shift Toward Conversational Productivity in 2026

This update reflects a bigger trend in how people expect to interact with their phones. Typing is slow. Switching between five apps to draft an email, check a calendar, and add a task is even slower. Voice assistants that can actually complete multi-step tasks close that gap.

Google isn’t alone in this race. Competing AI assistants from other tech companies have pushed similar productivity angles, betting that whoever nails hands-free task completion first wins a chunk of daily usage. By tying Gemini Live directly into Gmail and Workspace, Google is leaning on its existing ecosystem advantage rather than building a standalone assistant from scratch.

The long-term implication is a shift in how “productivity app” gets defined. Instead of opening separate tools for email, calendar, and notes, users may increasingly just talk to one assistant that reaches into all of them. Whether that becomes the default habit depends on how reliable these integrations prove to be in daily use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I compose an email using Gemini Live and Spark?

Open a Gemini Live session and describe the email you want, including the recipient and key points. Gemini Live drafts it using Spark, and you can ask for edits like a shorter tone or added details before it saves to your Gmail drafts.

### Can Gemini Live search through my entire Gmail inbox by voice?

Yes, you can ask Gemini Live to find specific emails, summarize recent messages, or check for replies from a particular sender. It’s designed for quick lookups rather than deep inbox management, so complex searches may still require opening the Gmail app.

### What other Google apps integrate with Gemini Live besides Gmail and Spark?

Gemini Live also connects with Google Calendar for scheduling, Google Docs for dictation and summaries, and Google Tasks for managing to-do items, all accessible through the same voice conversation.

### Do I need a Google Workspace account to use these integrations?

A standard personal Google account with Gmail generally works for the core email and calendar features. Some advanced capabilities may roll out first to Google One AI subscribers or Workspace accounts before reaching everyone.

### Is Gemini Live available on all Android and iOS devices with these features?

Gemini Live works on most recent Android phones and through the Gemini app on iOS, but the new integrations are rolling out gradually. If you don’t see a feature yet, check for app updates or try again in the coming weeks.
