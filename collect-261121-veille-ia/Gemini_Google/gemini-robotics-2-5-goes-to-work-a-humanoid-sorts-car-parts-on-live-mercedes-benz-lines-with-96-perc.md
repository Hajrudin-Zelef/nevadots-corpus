**📅 November 10, 2026 • Google DeepMind**

Per Google, Gemini Robotics 2.5 was announced on November 10, 2026 as the company's most capable vision-language-action (VLA) model to date. In a proof-of-concept on active Mercedes-Benz production lines in Kecskemet, Hungary and Sindelfingen, Germany, the Apptronik Apollo 2 humanoid performed automotive kitting with a 96 percent success rate, a figure The Decoder corroborates in its coverage of the pilot. Kitting means sorting individual metal parts from bulk bins into sequencing containers in the exact order needed for each vehicle on the line.

The system runs as a pair of models: the Gemini Robotics ER embodied-reasoning model tracks the workflow, while the Gemini Robotics 2.5 actions model controls hands and arms. Google evaluated the model across more than 200 distinct tasks, such as serving food with tongs, tying a bow on a gift box and pipetting liquid. Compared to previous baselines, the success rate is 30 to 100 percent higher depending on task type, and tasks complete 20 to 30 percent faster. The platform natively works with multiple robot types, including Apptronik Apollo 2 with the five-finger SharpaWave hand that has 22 degrees of freedom, and the Franka Duo platform. Per Apptronik, quoted in the Google post, the models improved dramatically in a few weeks of post-training, and the gains carried over to Mercedes-Benz production with very little drop-off.

Safety numbers are part of the release. Per Google, inference-time reasoning guidance boosted safe task completion around obstacles by 33 percent and around nearby people by 43 percent. Google also released ASIMOV-Jailbreak, an open-source benchmark on Hugging Face with hundreds of prompts that disguise unsafe requests: Gemini 4 Argon achieves 99.5 percent jailbreak resistance versus 82 percent for the previous baseline, Gemini Robotics ER 2. Ars Technica corroborates both the release and the open benchmark. Another announced use is simulation: Gemini 4 Argon acted as a simulation engine for robotics training, generating thousands of varied simulated practice runs from a single real camera scene and building a realistic digital twin. Per Google, Gemini Robotics 2.5 is rolling out to Google robotics partners.

**How it works in practice.** For manufacturers, the Mercedes-Benz pilots put a humanoid on live lines doing a task where part order matters for each vehicle, and the reported 96 percent success rate with 20 to 30 percent faster completion sets the baseline for future deployment comparisons. For robotics teams, the ER plus actions split, native support for SharpaWave and Franka Duo hardware, and simulation runs generated by Gemini 4 Argon define a concrete integration path. For safety review, the ASIMOV-Jailbreak dataset is downloadable on Hugging Face, so prompt safety can be measured against a public benchmark rather than an internal one.
