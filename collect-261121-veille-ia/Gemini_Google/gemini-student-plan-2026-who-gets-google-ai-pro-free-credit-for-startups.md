## Who qualifies: the fine print in Google's terms

The offer terms list five requirements. You must be **18 or older**, be **enrolled at a higher education institution** in a supported country, **verify your student status through SheerID**, use a **personal Google Account**, and have a **Google Payments account with a qualifying form of payment** at sign-up. High school students do not qualify; the offer is for college and university students.

Several groups are excluded even if they are students. You do not qualify if you are part of a Google One family group, an enterprise purchaser, a discounted Pixel bundle subscriber, or if you already have a Google One subscription through a third party such as Google Fi. Supervised Google Accounts are out, and the offer does not work on your school-issued Workspace for Education account. If you are in a family group, you have to leave it before you claim.

Two details from Google's student page are worth knowing. Students whose free AI Pro trial from 2025 has expired can claim again. And eligibility "must be verified each year to maintain access", so expect SheerID to ask again rather than treating this as a permanent student rate.

## Gemini for Education: what your school may already give you

Separate from the personal offer, Google includes **Gemini for Education** (the Gemini app and Gemini Notebook) in every Google Workspace for Education edition at no extra cost, according to Google for Education. If your university runs on Google Workspace and has the app turned on, you can use Gemini through your school login today. Google says data in that version is not human-reviewed or used to train its models.

Schools can also buy a paid add-on, Google AI Pro for Education, for higher limits and premium features. Whether you have it depends entirely on what your school pays for. Ask your IT help desk.

The practical answer for most students is to use both: the school version for coursework where privacy matters, and the free personal AI Pro or AI Plus year for everything else, since the personal offer is the one with the extra storage and it stays with you after graduation (until the year runs out).

## The fine print

For a prototype, a Gemini API key from Google AI Studio is free with no card and a rate-limited free tier on most current Flash models. For a company, the **Google for Startups Cloud Program** gives startups from Seed to Series A $200,000 in Google Cloud credits, or up to $350,000 for AI-first startups, according to cloud.google.com/startup. The credits are Google Cloud credits, and Google lists Gemini models as part of the AI stack it pitches to startups in the program.

If you are pre-funded, Google lists a separate pre-funded track on the same page. Our Google for Startups listing covers eligibility and how to apply, and the Gemini API pricing guide shows what the credits are worth in tokens. Microsoft and OpenAI run their own startup programs; see Microsoft for Startups and OpenAI for Startups if you are comparing.
