The Google Assistant handover, and why 4th September matters

The largest single change to Gemini's reach is not a feature. It is a default.

4 Sep 2026Assistant removal begins on phones, tablets, Wear OS and Android Auto

Google has told users that "Google Assistant on your mobile device is being discontinued, and Gemini, our next-generation AI-powered assistant, is now the Assistant experience on Android", with removal beginning .

The scope is phones, tablets, Wear OS and Android Auto. Cars with Google built-in keep the classic Assistant past that date.

Gemini itself is "now available globally" on those cars, per Google's 20th Oct post. The removal schedule is unchanged.

On Nest and Home hardware the replacement is a separately branded product, Gemini for Home, which launched in October 2025.

Google first signalled this in March 2025. It pushed the date once already, from the end of 2025 into 2026.

So treat the date as firm but not immovable.

The reason it belongs on a marketing news page is reach. Every figure in the statistics section describes people who chose to use Gemini.

After 4th Sep, a large share of Android users will encounter it without choosing anything. That is the same distribution argument Statcounter's CEO makes.

What to watch: whether the next user disclosure separates people who opted in from people who were moved. The second thing to watch is whether referral share moves in the months after the handover.

Neither is knowable yet, and this page will not guess.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode?

Gemini is Google's assistant: an app, a website, and from September 2026 the assistant slot on Android. AI Overviews are the AI summaries at the top of a Google Search results page.

AI Mode is the conversational search experience inside Google Search.

All three run on Gemini models, but only the first is the assistant. They also have different publisher controls, which is why we track them on separate pages.

How many people use Gemini?

Google said the Gemini app passed 1 billion monthly users on 11th Aug 2026. It called it the fastest-growing product in its history.

The series ran 400 million in May 2025 and 650 million that October. It reached 750 million in February 2026 and 900 million in May.

Google has never defined what it counts as a monthly active user for the app. So the numbers are consistent self-reporting rather than audited figures.

How do I stop Gemini using my content?

Disallow the Google-Extended token in your robots.txt. Per Google's crawler documentation, that governs whether your crawled content trains future Gemini models.

It also governs whether that content can be used to ground answers in the Gemini app.

It does not run a crawler of its own. It is applied as a usage restriction on content Google has already crawled.

Does blocking Google-Extended hurt my Google Search rankings?

No, and Google states this directly: Google-Extended "does not impact a site's inclusion in Google Search nor is it used as a ranking signal in Google Search".

It also does not remove you from AI Overviews or AI Mode. Those run on ordinary Search indexing rather than on this token.

So blocking it is a decision about Gemini specifically, not a trade-off against Search visibility.

One caveat arrived in April 2026. A SIGIR study found that sites blocking the token are cited less often in AI Overviews.

That is an association rather than a demonstrated cause, and it concerns citation rather than eligibility.

Check it against your own citation data before drawing a conclusion.

Does Gemini link out to websites?

Sometimes. Google says a Sources button appears "when sources are available", either below the response or inline, and that not every response includes one.

A link is guaranteed in one case: when Gemini quotes a large amount of text from a webpage. Google also shows "related links", which its own documentation says are "not necessarily sources used to generate the response".

Is Gemini replacing Google Assistant?

Yes. Google has confirmed that Google Assistant on mobile is being discontinued from 4th Sep 2026, with Gemini described as "now the Assistant experience on Android".

It covers phones, tablets, Wear OS and Android Auto. Cars with Google built-in keep classic Assistant beyond that date.

Google's 20th Oct 2026 post says Gemini is now available on those cars too.

Nest and Home devices move to a separate product called Gemini for Home.

What does Gemini cost, and do paid plans get better models?

The app is free. Google AI Plus is $4.99 a month and AI Pro $19.99.

AI Ultra is either $99.99 or $199.99 depending on the usage tier.
