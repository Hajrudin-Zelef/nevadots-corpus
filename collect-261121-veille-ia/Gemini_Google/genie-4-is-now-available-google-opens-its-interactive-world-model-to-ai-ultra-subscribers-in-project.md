Google has opened Genie 4, its most realistic interactive world model so far, to paying users. On 18 November 2026 Google DeepMind and Google Labs began rolling out a new Project Genie experience to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers, with Genie 4, an agent that helps build worlds, upgrades to Genie 3 and a community gallery for sharing and remixing worlds.

## Quick Take

1. **Who gets it:** eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers from today; Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist.
2. **Genie 4:** edit a running world with new prompts mid-session, with real-time native audio for the first time in Project Genie.
3. **Genie 3 upgrades:** sessions of up to 3 minutes per generation (up from 60 seconds), world chaining, 360-degree panorama grounding and real-time in-world events.
4. **Project Genie agent:** turns a conversation into a world, using Google’s multimodal models for the look and a matching background score.
5. **Gallery:** curated and community worlds that others can explore and remix.

## In plain English

Genie does not make a video you watch. It makes a small world you can move through, like a video game generated on the fly. With this update you can type “make it rain” or “add a dragon” while you are inside the world, hear sound that fits the scene, and publish what you made so others can play with it.

## What happened

In a blog post, Diego Rivas of Google DeepMind and Pavan Kumar of Google Labs said early testers wanted more control over what happens inside worlds and easier ways to build and share them. The new Project Genie experience “brings together our most advanced world models alongside our Gemini models,” they wrote, letting people generate, step inside and shape worlds from the browser.

Genie 4 was first presented in October as a research preview for trusted testers; our earlier report covers the Genie 4 model and its limits. This is the first time Ultra subscribers can use it directly.

## What’s in it

- **Mid-session prompting:** shift the weather, drop in a character or redirect the action while navigating.
- **Native audio:** ambient soundscapes to character dialogue, adapting as the scene unfolds.
- **Genie 3 in the model picker:** for longer runs or worlds seeded from panorama images.
- **Gemini in the session:** it observes what is on screen, suggests where to go next or triggers events automatically.
- **Creator showcases:** “Villa Giallo” with creative technologist Will aka BLVCKL!GHT and “The Life of Ants” with filmmaker Anand Gandhi.
- **Safety:** all generated video and audio carries SynthID watermarking, and inputs and outputs go through safety screening.

## Why it matters

**For creators**, world models move from demo clips to a tool with remixing and publishing, closer to a game engine than a video generator.

**For developers and game studios**, prompt-driven worlds with sound could speed up prototyping and previsualisation.

**For Google**, Project Genie becomes another reason to pay for its top AI subscription tier.

## What’s still open

- Google says both Genie models remain in the frontier research space, with open challenges in camera latency, complex physical interactions, real-time audio precision and long-term scene permanence.
- When Google AI Pro members or other countries get access.
- Whether developers get Genie 4 through the Gemini API beyond early-access partners.
