# Google announces Gemini Robotics 2.5, an AI for robots.


 Google DeepMind announced Gemini Robotics 2.5, an AI model for robotics, on November 10, 2026. It is designed to learn faster in the real world and is adapted to handle a wide range of situations, from daily life to factory work.

 **Gemini Robotics 2.5: Accelerating Physical Intelligence — Google DeepMind**

 

The Gemini Robotics series is an AI designed to determine robot actions. Gemini Robotics 2, released in July 2026, is designed as a VLA (Visual, Language, and Behavioral) model, enabling it to determine actions based on camera images and verbal instructions. Google DeepMind explains that the same model can control a variety of robots of different shapes and sizes, from desktop robotic arms to full-body humanoid robots.

**Google DeepMind announces 'Gemini Robotics 2,' an AI model for robots, enabling full-body control from walking to fine motor skills with both hands - GIGAZINE**

Gemini Robotics 2.5 further improves both the range of skills robots can learn and the speed at which they learn. This results in improved performance in terms of 'dexterity,' enabling them to perform more tasks with greater efficiency than ever before; 'reliability,' based on field testing conducted on Mercedes-Benz production lines; and 'safety,' which includes improved physical safety maneuverability and the introduction of new benchmarks for testing against adversarial escapes specific to robots.

Gemini Robotics 2.5 expands the model's capabilities in terms of task breadth, performance, and complexity. The model was evaluated on 200 different tasks, including plating food with tongs, tying ribbons around gift boxes, and pipetting liquids. These tasks combine many skills, such as advanced tool manipulation, handling complex deformable objects, and delicate object handling, to approach human-level dexterity.  In terms of safety, Gemini Robotics 2.5 uses guidance based on inference time to avoid physical hazards in real time. Google DeepMind's evaluation showed that the safety classification feature improved the safe task completion rate around obstacles by 33% and the safe task completion rate around nearby people by 43%.
