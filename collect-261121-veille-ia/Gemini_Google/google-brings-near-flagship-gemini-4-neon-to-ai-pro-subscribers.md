Google has released Gemini 4 Neon, bringing a new AI model with performance approaching its flagship Gemini 4 Carbon to AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

Neon sits between Carbon and the lighter Gemini 4 Hydrogen, targeting coding, research and complex analytical tasks. It supports a one-million-token context window and accepts text, images, audio, video and documents.

The model scored 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, compared with 59 for Carbon, and ranked first on the Coding Agent Index, according to Google. The results reflect specific benchmark conditions and do not establish that Neon outperforms Carbon across all tasks.

Google says Neon costs 32% less per task than Carbon in the Artificial Analysis evaluation at its default high reasoning setting. API pricing is $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens.

The company also introduced Gemini 4 Neon for cyber, a version developed to identify, validate and patch software vulnerabilities. Google reported a 90.5% score on SEC-bench Pro, with initial access restricted to approved security researchers through its Fairwind Program.

Neon is available in the Gemini app for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and through Google AI Studio, the Gemini API and Google's enterprise tools.
