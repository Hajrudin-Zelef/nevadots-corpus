Google DeepMind has officially unveiled Genie 4, its world artificial intelligence (AI) model that introduces real-time, interactive world simulation with native audio and realism. The system allows users to generate and actively navigate dynamic environments, steering scenes on the fly while the model simultaneously calculates physics, character behavior, and soundscapes. Alphabet and Google CEO Sundar Pichai highlighted the milestone, calling Genie 4 âour most realistic and interactive world model.â

Excited to share Genie 4 today our most realistic and interactive world model:- steer dynamic environments in real time, shaping events, camera angles, and actions- generate native audio matched to the world, from realistic background sounds to characters who can converse in real time!- simulate photorealistic scenes and real-world physics- create more coherent multi-step events through reasoning from GeminiWe've started to roll out as a research preview to trusted testers. Coming soon to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and early access partners via the Gemini API and AI Studio.
## Genie 4 is built on Gemini Intelligence

Genie 4 is not a standalone graphics engine; it directly integrates the multimodal reasoning capabilities of Googleâs Gemini family:

Gemini Omni: Anchors each simulation in authentic real-world appearances, physical dynamics and visual continuity.

Gemini reasoning models: Maintain scene logic, ensuring events unfold with cause-and-effect coherence over time.

Gemini 3.8 Flash: Operates as an active agent in the loop. As Genie 4 renders the world, Gemini 3.8 Flash tracks incoming frames, breaks broad goals into sequential steps, verifies that actions succeed, and adapts plans instantly whenever the scene shifts or a user intervenes.

## Google lists four core breakthroughs in Genie 4

Google DeepMind detailed four primary technical advancements that define the new world model:

Real-time scene direction
Users can explore simulated environments using standard keyboard controls for navigation and camera positioning, while using plain text prompts to alter the world in real time. They can change the weather, adjust camera angles, or introduce new characters and objects.

Advanced physics and motion
Google says that Genie 4 delivers a significant leap in physical accuracy, accurately rendering complex interactions such as aerodynamics, collisions, and environmental resistance. Character animation sees an equally sharp upgrade: digital figures move naturally, interact organically with their surroundings, and respond immediately to real-time steering.

Native audio generation
As the first real-time world model to generate synchronised audio natively, Genie 4 produces sound directly alongside visual frames, Google says. This includes contextual ambient soundscapes, such as wind through trees or breaking ocean waves as well as background music.

Crucially, the model handles interactive, real-time spoken dialogue. Characters converse, react, and respond on the fly to user prompts and unfolding environmental events.

Complex multi-step simulation
By pairing world generation with advanced AI reasoning, Genie 4 can simulate multi-stage events, allowing developers to see how artificial agents navigate complex settings before deploying them into real-world scenarios.
