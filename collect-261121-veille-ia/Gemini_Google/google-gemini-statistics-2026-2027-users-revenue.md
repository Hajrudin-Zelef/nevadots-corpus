## Google Gemini Statistics 2026-2027

**By Axis Intelligence Research**

**Co-author:** Sarah Mitchell *|* **Last updated:** November 12, 2026 | License: CC BY 4.0

Gemini’s app passed one billion monthly users in August 2026, Alphabet reports, after 400 million at I/O 2025. Independent survey data is more cautious: 28.7% of internet users aged 16+ used Gemini in the past month in Q2 2026, against 34.7% for ChatGPT, a Gemini–ChatGPT Reach Parity Index (GRPI) of 82.7.

## Quick Answer

How many people use Gemini? Alphabet reports more than 1 billion monthly Gemini app users as of August 2026, a company-reported threshold that does not say which surfaces are counted. GWI’s survey puts worldwide past-month reach at 28.7% of internet users aged 16+, versus 34.7% for ChatGPT.

## Key Findings

- Alphabet reports more than 1 billion monthly Gemini app users in August 2026, up from 400 million at I/O 2025.
- Net adds slowed from 43.9 million users a month before I/O 2026 to 23.8 million in the next interval, on Alphabet’s own thresholds.
- Gemini reaches 82.7% as many internet users as ChatGPT worldwide (GRPI 82.7) and leads ChatGPT in only four of 53 markets.
- Alphabet publishes no Gemini revenue or profit line; Q3 2026 capex equalled 121.5% of operating cash flow while Cloud margin hit 36.2%.
- API list prices double on December 1, 2026 for Gemini 4 Argon and on January 1, 2027 for Gemini 3.8 Flash.

### How fast is the user base growing?

Between the disclosed thresholds, Axis converts each interval into net adds per month: (end MAU − start MAU) ÷ (days ÷ 30.4375).

| Interval | Net adds (million users per month) | 
|---|---|
| I/O 2025 to Q3 2025 call | 47.0 | 
| Q3 2025 call to Q4 2025 call | 31.1 | 
| Q4 2025 call to I/O 2026 | 43.9 | 
| I/O 2026 to Q2 2026 call | 23.8 | 

From the Q3 2025 call to the August 11, 2026 announcement, the average was 37.2 million a month. The 400-to-900 million move between the two I/O keynotes is a 2.25× multiple in a year.

Two cautions on this table. Every input is a lower bound, so each interval’s pace is approximate. And the final interval (950 million to “1 billion+”) is excluded on purpose: both figures are rounded thresholds, and the resulting pace would be an artifact of rounding, not a measurement. Alphabet also says Gemini app daily active users tripled over the year to Q2 2026, but it does not publish a daily count.

### Which Gemini surfaces are counted?

Google’s August 11 announcement calls the milestone “more than 1 billion monthly users” but its body text does not define “monthly users” or list the surfaces counted. The same post reports 100 million+ active iOS users, 150 million+ images generated per day, and that 63% of users talk to Gemini directly. Around the app, Alphabet reports separate counts for AI Overviews (2.5 billion+ monthly users at I/O 2026) and AI Mode (over one billion monthly active users, Q2 2026 call). These are different products; Axis does not add them to the app figure.

## Gemini vs ChatGPT: who has more users?

The headline comparison people search for mixes units. Alphabet’s one billion is a monthly count. OpenAI’s most recent figure is 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users, announced at DevDay on September 29, 2026. A weekly user count and a monthly user count measure different things, so dividing one by the other is not a valid parity ratio.

Axis therefore anchors the comparison on one survey that measures both products the same way: GWI’s Q2 2026 question on past-month use, published in DataReportal’s Digital 2027 Global Overview Report (Simon Kemp, October 21, 2026). Worldwide, 34.7% of internet users aged 16+ used ChatGPT in the past month and 28.7% used Gemini, a gap of 6.0 percentage points.

ChatGPT’s own reach climbed from 18.5% in Q2 2024 to 34.7% in Q2 2026, a 16.2-point rise in two years. This edition of the report gives Gemini’s country and age-gender reach for Q2 2026 only, with no quarterly series, so Axis does not claim a Gemini trend from GWI.

One comparability note: GWI removed Russia from its survey from Q1 2026, so these worldwide values do not line up with earlier editions.

### What is the Gemini–ChatGPT Reach Parity Index (GRPI)?

The **Gemini–ChatGPT Reach Parity Index (GRPI)** is an Axis Intelligence Research metric that scores how close Gemini’s past-month reach is to ChatGPT’s inside the same population.

**GRPI = 100 × (Gemini past-month reach ÷ ChatGPT past-month reach)**

Inputs: GWI Q2 2026 past-month reach, internet users aged 16+, from the DataReportal report. GRPI of 100 means equal reach; below 100 means ChatGPT reaches more of that population. It replaces the GERI composite previously published on this page, which relied on fixed ceilings rather than a head-to-head ratio.

- **Worldwide GRPI: 82.7** (28.7 ÷ 34.7).
- **Median across 53 markets: 63.4.** The world value sits above the country median; India (97.8) and Brazil (84.3), both large markets, score well above it.
- **Markets at or above 100: four.** Morocco (126.2), Hong Kong (118.7), Thailand (109.7) and Mexico (103.4).
- **Markets at 97 or higher: nine.** Turkey (98.8), India (97.8), Taiwan (97.6), Japan (97.5) and Indonesia (97.0) join the four above.
- **United States: 68.8.** The United Kingdom scores 53.0, Germany 54.1 and France 55.3.
- **Lowest five:** Denmark (33.3), Sweden (35.7), Norway (35.9), New Zealand (37.5) and Croatia (37.9).

For country-level Gemini reach on its own, the five highest GWI values are Turkey (51.1%), Brazil (50.9%), Morocco (49.2%), India (48.6%) and Israel (46.4%). The United States stands at 22.5% for Gemini against 32.7% for ChatGPT.

### Does Android explain where Gemini wins?

The obvious hypothesis is that Gemini does better where Android dominates. Axis tested it with Statcounter’s July 2026 Android share of mobile page requests (via DataReportal) against GRPI. Fifty-one markets appear in both series; China and Serbia are missing from the Android chart.

| Relationship (51 markets) | Pearson r | Variance explained | 
|---|---|---|
| Android share vs Gemini reach | 0.654 | 42.7% | 
| Android share vs ChatGPT reach | 0.573 | 32.8% | 
| Android share vs GRPI | 0.346 | 12.0% | 

Android share tracks AI adoption in general (it correlates with both products’ reach), and only weakly tracks Gemini’s position relative to ChatGPT. Grouped by Android share, the median GRPI is 75.9 in the 14 markets at 75% or more, 68.1 in the 22 markets between 50% and 75%, and 50.3 in the 15 markets below 50%. Hong Kong, with Android at 47.85% of mobile page requests, posts the second-highest GRPI at 118.7. Distribution on Android helps; it does not explain parity.

## Why do Gemini user numbers differ by source?

Four measurement lenses give four different pictures. Axis keeps them separate rather than averaging them.

**App lens (Similarweb, standalone mobile apps).** On an index where August 2024 equals 100, the Gemini app stood at 2,478.4 in August 2026 and the ChatGPT app at 363.9, a 6.81× difference in growth multiples. This is a growth index, not a user count, and it excludes China and Gemini’s OS integrations. Gemini’s quarterly index growth fell from 42.3% (February to May 2026) to 4.6% (May to August 2026), below ChatGPT’s 5.9% in the same latest quarter.

**The lens gap.** From May 2025 to May 2026, Similarweb’s standalone-app index for Gemini rose 6.39×, while Alphabet’s reported monthly users rose 2.25× (400 to 900 million). The 2.84× difference is consistent with the standalone app taking a growing share of Alphabet’s count, but Alphabet does not publish the split, so that remains an inference, not a measurement.

**Web lens (Similarweb, June–August 2026 monthly averages).**

| Metric | chatgpt.com | gemini.google.com | 
|---|---|---|
| Visits per month | 5.49 billion | 2.72 billion | 
| Unique visitors per month | 487 million | 310 million | 
| Time per visit | 6m 29s | 7m 00s | 
| Pages per visit | 4.28 | 4.17 | 
| Share of top-10,000 sites’ traffic | 1.29% | 0.64% | 
| Global rank by visits | 5 | 15 | 
| Visits per unique visitor (Axis) | 11.27 | 8.77 | 
| Minutes per unique visitor per month (Axis) | 73.1 | 61.4 | 

Gemini’s website draws 63.7% of ChatGPT’s unique visitors but 49.5% of its visits: fewer return trips, longer sessions. Its monthly web visits equal 3.2% of google.com’s 86.2 billion.

**Referral lens (Statcounter).** In July 2026, ChatGPT accounted for 77.89% of page-view referrals from the tracked AI platforms and Gemini for 9.93% (DataReportal, Statcounter data). Scaling by Similarweb’s unique-visitor ratio, Gemini sends about 0.20 times the referral traffic per web visitor that ChatGPT does. The two datasets differ in provider and month (July versus August), so treat that ratio as an order of magnitude. For publishers, this is the figure that matters; for the wider picture see AI search statistics.

Statcounter’s live AI chatbot page for October 2026 shows a different picture: Gemini at 28.86% worldwide against 51.58% for ChatGPT, and in the United States 35.42% against 37.10%. Gemini’s worldwide figure is 2.91× its July value. Statcounter’s own twelve-month chart shows the break: ChatGPT’s line runs high and flat from November 2025 to September 2026, then falls to 51.58% in October while Gemini and Perplexity both rise. A one-month move of that size across all providers fits a change in what the series counts better than a market shift, but the page documents no change. Axis therefore treats the October values as a separate series, reports the step as unexplained, and builds no index on it.

**Search-interest lens (Google Trends, July 2025–June 2026).** “ChatGPT” ranked seventh among top searches with an index of 45 (+70%); “Gemini” ranked twentieth with an index of 20 (+450%). “Google Gemini” and “Gemini AI” each grew about 500%. Gemini’s search interest is 44.4% of ChatGPT’s, well below its 82.7 GRPI: more people use Gemini than search for it by name. That is consistent with reach through Google surfaces, though Axis has no direct measurement of how users arrive.

## How does Gemini make money?

Alphabet does not report Gemini revenue, so any single “Gemini revenue” number is an outside estimate. What Alphabet does disclose points to four channels, each with a different disclosure.

1. **Consumer subscriptions.** Google’s subscriptions page lists AI Plus at $4.99 a month (2× the free usage limits, 400 GB storage), AI Pro at $19.99 (4× Free, 5 TB), and AI Ultra from $99.99 (5× Pro, from 20 TB) and $199.99 (20× Pro). Prices are US list prices on November 12, 2026 and vary by country. On the Q3 2026 call, Alphabet called Google One its fastest-growing subscription product, driven by demand for AI plans. The enclosing Subscriptions, Platforms & Devices line grew 11% to $14.3 billion, but it also holds YouTube and devices, so it is not a Gemini number. Paid subscriptions across all consumer services passed 325 million at the Q4 2025 call.
2. **API and Cloud.** First-party model APIs process nearly 32 billion tokens a minute (see below). Google Cloud revenue grew 103% to $30.7 billion in Q3 2026, and the contracted backlog stands at $561 billion.
3. **Enterprise seats.** Gemini Enterprise had more than 8 million paid seats at more than 2,800 companies at the Q4 2025 call. At Q3 2026, Alphabet said it had sold more than 100 million seats for knowledge work across all its AI services, and that nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 use Gemini Enterprise.
4. **Advertising.** Alphabet says it is scaling ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode, and that click-through rates on sponsored and organic product list items are already similar in AI Mode. No figure is given. For the ad side of this, see Google Ads statistics.

For group-level financials, see Alphabet stock statistics and Google statistics.

## Is Gemini profitable for Google?

Not in any way Alphabet lets outsiders measure. The company reports Cloud segment profit and consolidated cash flow, and Gemini sits across both. The table shows what the transcripts disclose, plus two Axis calculations.

| Quarter | Cloud revenue | Cloud growth | Cloud op. income | Cloud margin | Backlog | Capex | Free cash flow | Capex ÷ operating cash flow (Axis) | 
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q3 2025 | $15.2B | 34% | $3.6B | 23.7% | $155B | $24B | +$24.5B | 49.5% | 
| Q4 2025 | $17.7B | 48% | $5.3B | 30.1% | $240B | n/r | +$24.6B | n/r | 
| Q2 2026 | $24.8B | 82% | $8.8B | 35.6% | $514B | $44.9B | −$5.9B | 115.1% | 
| Q3 2026 | $30.7B | 103% | $11.1B | 36.2% | $561B | $51.4B | −$9.1B | 121.5% | 

*n/r: not retrieved. Quarters shown are those with a retrieved transcript. Operating cash flow is reconstructed as free cash flow plus capex; for Q3 2026 it reproduces the $42.3 billion in Alphabet’s Form 10-Q. Backlog is not like-for-like across the table: in Q1 2026 Alphabet began including contracts with an original term of one year or less, so the Q3 and Q4 2025 values use the earlier definition.*

Reading the table: Cloud margin rose 12.5 points in four quarters, while capex went from about half of operating cash flow to above all of it. The 10-Q confirms the Q3 figures to the million: Cloud revenue of $30,709 million (up 102.6% from $15,157 million, which Alphabet rounds to 103%) and operating income of $11,121 million against $3,594 million. Over nine months, capex reached $132.0 billion against $63.6 billion a year earlier (2.07×), 103.8% of nine-month operating cash flow of $127.1 billion, which leaves nine-month free cash flow at negative $4.8 billion. Trailing-twelve-month free cash flow was $19.7 billion at Q3 2026. Alphabet says Gemini serving unit costs fell 78% over 2025, which supports the margin trend but is not a Gemini profit figure. Full-year 2026 capex guidance is $195–205 billion, raised on the Q2 call from $180–190 billion and maintained on the Q3 call; its midpoint is 2.19× FY2025 capex of $91.4 billion. Subtracting the nine-month actual of $132.0 billion implies fourth-quarter 2026 capex of $63.0 to $73.0 billion if guidance is met.

The 10-Q puts total revenue backlog at $567.7 billion, of which $560.8 billion is Google Cloud. Alphabet expects “just over 55%” of the total to be recognized over 24 months, a floor of $312.2 billion. The total includes $16.8 billion (3.0%) tied to a multi-year agreement with Crux AI, an unconsolidated joint venture that Alphabet identifies as a related party.

That is at most 4.57× the October 2025 level in a year (Alphabet says “more than four times”). Alphabet states +40% on the prior quarter; computing from the rounded 22 and 32 gives 45.5%, and “nearly 32” explains the gap.

Axis also relates throughput to total volume. At the same dates, direct API traffic converts to 23.3% (Q3 2025: 7 billion tokens a minute against 1.3 quadrillion tokens a month across Google) and 25.7% (I/O 2026: about 19 billion against 3.2 quadrillion) of monthly tokens. The share barely moves while total volume more than doubles, so API and non-API usage are scaling at similar rates. Both inputs come from the same event; the 30-day month is an Axis assumption.

Customers consuming at least 100 billion tokens in twelve months rose from over 2,000 (Q2 2026) to over 2,600 (Q3 2026).

## How does Gemini use your data?

Google’s Gemini Apps Privacy Hub says the default auto-delete period is 18 months (options of 3 or 36 months, or off), chats reviewed by humans can be kept up to three years, and Temporary Chats and chats with Activity off are retained for 72 hours and not used to train Google’s AI models. With Activity on, activity is used to improve services, including training generative AI models.

## Methodology

**Source hierarchy.** Company statements come from Alphabet’s earnings call transcripts and Form 10-Q, Google’s own posts and Google’s pricing and privacy pages, and are labelled company-reported. Survey, panel and traffic data (GWI, Similarweb, Statcounter, Google Trends) are labelled estimates and come from DataReportal’s Digital 2027 report or Statcounter directly. Every figure sits in the CSV with its source document, URL, retrieval date and an `axis_calculated` flag.

**What a threshold means.** “Over 650 million” is a floor. Axis never treats a floor as a point estimate, which is why net-add rates are shown as approximations and the 950-million-to-1-billion interval is excluded.

**GRPI.** Computed for the 53 markets where both Gemini and ChatGPT reach appear in the GWI charts; country values were transcribed from the report’s charts and double-checked. The worldwide GRPI uses GWI’s own worldwide values. The Android analysis uses the 51 markets present in both the GWI and Statcounter series.

**Scope notes.** GWI covers internet users aged 16+ and excludes Russia from Q1 2026 onward. Similarweb app data excludes China and Gemini’s OS integrations. Statcounter measures page-view referrals, not users. Alphabet counts may include surfaces its announcements do not itemize. None of these sources measures the same population, which is why Axis reports lenses side by side.

**Rounding.** Q3 2025 and Q3 2026 Cloud revenue and operating income, nine-month capex and cash flow, and backlog come from the Form 10-Q, to the million. Other quarters are as spoken on the calls (to $0.1 billion), so Axis ratios computed from them can differ from Alphabet’s stated percentages by a point.

**Not independently verified.** OpenAI’s weekly-user figure is reported as the company states it: the page blocked automated retrieval, and its text was confirmed through the search listing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many people use Gemini each month?

Alphabet reports more than 1 billion monthly users of the Gemini app as of August 11, 2026, repeated as “one billion users” on the Q3 2026 call. It is a company-reported floor, and the announcement does not define which surfaces count. Daily active users tripled over the year to Q2 2026; no daily count is published.

### Does Gemini have more users than ChatGPT?

Not by the most comparable measure. GWI’s Q2 2026 survey finds 28.7% of internet users aged 16+ used Gemini in the past month against 34.7% for ChatGPT, a GRPI of 82.7. Gemini leads in four of 53 markets: Morocco, Hong Kong, Thailand and Mexico. OpenAI’s 1.2 billion is weekly, Alphabet’s 1 billion monthly.

### Does Google report how much money Gemini makes?

No. Alphabet publishes no Gemini revenue line. It discloses Cloud revenue ($30.7 billion in Q3 2026, up 103%), growth in Google One driven by AI plans, Gemini Enterprise seat counts, and a qualitative update on ads in AI Mode. Any single “Gemini revenue” figure is an outside estimate.

### Is Gemini profitable for Google?

Alphabet does not say. The disclosed proxies point two ways: Cloud operating margin rose from 23.7% to 36.2% in a year, and Gemini serving unit costs fell 78% in 2025. Yet capex reached 121.5% of operating cash flow in Q3 2026, and free cash flow was negative $9.1 billion.

### Is Gemini’s user growth slowing?

On the available evidence, yes, modestly. Net adds fell from 43.9 million a month (Q4 2025 call to I/O 2026) to 23.8 million (I/O 2026 to Q2 2026 call), and Similarweb’s standalone app index grew 4.6% in the latest quarter, versus 42.3% the quarter before. Alphabet’s figures are rounded thresholds, so the pace is approximate.

### Why do Gemini user numbers differ between sources?

Each source counts something different. Alphabet counts monthly users across surfaces it does not itemize; Similarweb counts standalone app and web traffic; GWI surveys internet users; Statcounter counts page-view referrals. Between May 2025 and May 2026, Similarweb’s Gemini app index grew 6.39× while Alphabet’s reported users grew 2.25×.

### Which countries use Gemini the most?

By GWI’s Q2 2026 past-month reach, Turkey leads at 51.1%, followed by Brazil (50.9%), Morocco (49.2%), India (48.6%) and Israel (46.4%). Relative to ChatGPT, Morocco (GRPI 126.2) and Hong Kong (118.7) rank highest. In the United States, Gemini reaches 22.5% of internet users.

### Does Gemini train on my chats?

With Gemini Apps Activity on, Google says activity is used to improve its services, including training generative AI models. Temporary Chats and chats with Activity off are kept for 72 hours and are not used for training. Default auto-delete is 18 months. Details and controls are in our Gemini safety analysis.

### Will Gemini API prices change in 2027?

Yes. Standard and Batch prices for gemini-4-argon double on December 1, 2026 (input $2 to $4, output $10 to $20 per million tokens), and prices for gemini-3.8-flash double on January 1, 2027 (input $0.75 to $1.50, output $3.75 to $7.50). Alphabet also expects 2027 capex to increase significantly.
