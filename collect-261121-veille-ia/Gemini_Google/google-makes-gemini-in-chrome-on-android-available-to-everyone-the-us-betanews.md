Google’s drive to get more and more people using its AI-powered assistant Gemini is stepping up a gear. The company has announced that it is making the tool available in the Android version of Chrome for everyone in the US.

The expansion of availability is being billed as a way to not only enhance the web browser, but also to make life easier and faster for users. With free and paid-for subscription tiers for Gemini, just what you get access to will depend on whether you are willing to pay, and how much.

Announcing the widening of Gemini’s available in its mobile web browser, Google says: “Gemini in Chrome, your browsing assistant, is now available to all Android users in the U.S., making it easier than ever to explore the web on your phone”.

The company goes on to say:

Gemini in Chrome summarizes long articles, answers questions about web pages, and connects with your favorite Google apps — including Calendar and Keep — without making you switch between tabs. It even lets you customize and generate images on the go with Nano Banana.

AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers on Android in the U.S. can now also take advantage of Gemini in Chrome’s agentic capabilities with auto browse. Auto browse can handle tedious everyday tasks for you, helping you do things like book parking for an event, update a recurring online order, or organize travel for your next vacation.

These features are built to be secure by design, and our models are trained to detect known threats, such as prompt injection. And for added control, auto browse is designed to ask for confirmation before completing some sensitive tasks.

You can access Gemini in Chrome by tapping the Gemini icon or the three-dot menu on the top bar of your Android device.
