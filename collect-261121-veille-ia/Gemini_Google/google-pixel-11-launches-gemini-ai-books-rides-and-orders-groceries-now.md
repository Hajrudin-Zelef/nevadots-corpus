It is the formal launch of Gemini Intelligence — and specifically its agentic capabilities that make this the most significant shift in how Pixel phones are positioned since the original Pixel launched a decade ago. Google's official Pixel 11 blog

Agentic AI refers to a system that does not merely answer questions or generate text, but takes autonomous, multi-step actions in the real world on a user's behalf. Gemini Intelligence on the Pixel 11 can order groceries, book rides, manage reservations, and dial businesses to complete tasks across third-party apps — including apps that have no native Gemini integration built in. Gboard Rambler, launching alongside the phones, filters filler words ("um," "ah") and restructures natural speech into clean formatted text in real time. ### Tensor G6: How Google's First 2nm Chip Works — and Where It Falls Short

The Tensor G6 is built on TSMC's N2 manufacturing process — the commercial 2nm node that uses Gate-All-Around (GAA) transistors for the first time on any major Android smartphone chip. In a conventional FinFET transistor (the architecture used through 5nm, and in many 3nm designs), the gate material wraps around three sides of the conducting silicon channel. GAA surrounds the channel on all four sides, tightening electrostatic control, reducing current leakage, and enabling faster switching without proportional increases in power draw. TechTimes on Tensor G6

The practical result: a 7-core ARM C1 CPU layout — one C1-Ultra prime core at 4.11GHz, four C1-Pro performance cores at 3.38GHz, and two C1-Pro efficiency cores at 2.65GHz — delivering 15% faster app loading and 25% faster web browsing over the Tensor G5. Tensor G6 official specifications The chip arrives approximately one month ahead of Apple's A20 Pro — expected in the iPhone 18 Pro in September — in reaching the 2nm node. TechTimes on 2nm chip timing

The "Santafe" TPU gets 50% more compute and twice the memory bandwidth compared to the Tensor G5's TPU — translating to on-device AI tasks running up to 3.5 times faster while consuming up to 3.5 times less energy. Tensor G6 TPU performance The Tensor G6 also pairs with a MediaTek M90 modem, ending Google's longstanding dependency on Samsung's Exynos modems and potentially addressing the persistent signal and battery-drain complaints that have followed the Tensor line since its introduction. A new Titan M3 security chip adds quantum-safe boot protection — Google's first chip with post-quantum cryptography built in at the hardware level. Titan M3 and MediaTek modem

One acknowledged limitation: the GPU, a PowerVR C-Series unit, does not represent the same generational leap as the CPU and TPU. Pre-launch analysis from multiple outlets flagged this, and Google's own emphasis on AI processing efficiency over peak graphics performance is consistent with its hardware philosophy — but buyers who use their phones heavily for graphics-intensive gaming should note the GPU trajectory.

### What is agentic AI, and how does Gemini Intelligence use it on the Pixel 11?

Agentic AI refers to systems that take autonomous, multi-step actions — not just answer questions. Gemini Intelligence on the Pixel 11 can navigate third-party app interfaces without those apps having any Gemini integration built in, using screen-parsing to interact with them directly. Confirmed actions include ordering groceries, booking rides, managing reservations, and dialing businesses. Google requires a confirmation tap before execution. ### What is HiLight on the Pixel 11 Pro, and which phones get it?

HiLight is a ring of ambient LEDs built around the flash in the Pro models' edge-to-edge Camera Bar. It replaces the temperature sensor that appeared on the Pixel 8 Pro through Pixel 10 Pro. HiLight pulses with customizable patterns to indicate Gemini AI activity (listening, thinking, responding) and glows in user-assigned colors when favorite contacts call, even when the phone is face-down. It is exclusive to the Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold — the base Pixel 11 does not have it. HiLight on Pixel 11 Pro models

### Does the Pixel Watch 5 run Gemini AI on the watch itself, without needing your phone?

Yes — but with a tradeoff. The Pixel Watch 5's RAM has been doubled to 3GB specifically to run Gemini Nano locally. Offline Gemini commands for timers, alarms, and workouts work without a phone connection. The catch is that the Pixel Watch 5 uses the Snapdragon W5 Gen 2 Accelerated chip — a tuned version of the same 4nm architecture in the Watch 4 — which lacks a dedicated neural processing unit (NPU). Samsung's Galaxy Watch 9 uses Qualcomm's Wear Elite chip, which has a dedicated Hexagon NPU for more efficient on-device AI. Gemini on the Pixel Watch 5 runs on general-purpose CPU cores, which is less power-efficient for AI workloads, though Google argues the Watch 5's battery life advantage over the Wear Elite-powered watch offsets this. ### Does the Pixel Tag actually support UWB precision finding, like AirTag?

Yes — UWB is confirmed on the Pixel Tag. This is the key upgrade over many third-party Find Hub trackers (Chipolo, Pebblebee, and others), which use only Bluetooth crowd-sourced location and cannot guide you within inches of a lost item. UWB precision finding gives directional guidance to within centimeters (a few inches). The Pixel Tag is $29 single or $99 four-pack — matching AirTag pricing — but does not ship until November 11.
