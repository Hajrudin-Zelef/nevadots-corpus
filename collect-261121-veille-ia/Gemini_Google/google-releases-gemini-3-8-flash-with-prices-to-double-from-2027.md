# Google releases 'Gemini 3.8 Flash,' with prices to double from 2027.


 On September 2, 2026, Google announced the AI model 'Gemini 3.8 Flash' and the cybersecurity-focused 'Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber.' Gemini 3.8 Flash is an enhanced model with improved software development capabilities, AI agent performance, and multi-stage inference capabilities, while Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is a security-focused model that assists with everything from vulnerability detection to remediation.

 **Introducing Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.8 Flash Cyber**

 Google released Gemini 3.6 Flash on July 21, 2026, and Gemini 3.7 Flash on August 13, 2026, making Gemini 3.8 Flash the third update in just six weeks. Google is continuously improving the Flash series, which is fast, relatively low-cost, and capable of handling large amounts of processing, to support long-running AI agents and coding applications.

 **Google releases 'Gemini 3.7 Flash,' offering performance comparable to Claude Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.6 Terra, and available at half the price of Gemini 3.6 Flash until the end of the year - GIGAZINE**


 When AI agents handle complex tasks such as software development, the process doesn't always end with a single response. It requires repeatedly writing and executing code, checking the results, and making corrections. Since running high-performance models for extended periods increases computational costs, it's crucial to enhance multi-stage inference capabilities while keeping processing speed and cost down.

 Gemini 3.8 Flash utilizes an 'agent loop,' a mechanism where the AI iterates through processing, reviewing results, making additional decisions, and calling external tools. According to Google, Gemini 3.8 Flash now performs more inference steps than before for complex problems, improving multi-stage inference performance in software development and specialized fields. However, setting a high level of inference may increase the number of tokens consumed.

 The image below compares the benchmark results of each AI model, including Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.7 Flash. In performance evaluation, Gemini 3.8 Flash scored 73.7% in 'DeepSWE v1.1,' which measures long-term software development capabilities, an improvement over Gemini 3.7 Flash's score of 65.3%. It also scored 54.9% in HLE-Verified, which deals with difficult problems in science, humanities, and specialized fields. Google explains that Gemini 3.8 Flash delivers performance close to that of some high-priced, cutting-edge models.

 


 The API is priced at $0.75 (approximately 119 yen) per million input tokens and $3.75 (approximately 595 yen) per million output tokens, the same price as Gemini 3.7 Flash. However, from January 1, 2027, the price will increase to $1.50 (approximately 238 yen) for inputs and $7.50 (approximately 1190 yen) for outputs. Developers who prioritize processing efficiency can set the inference amount lower, and Gemini 3.7 Flash will continue to be available.

 Released alongside Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is specifically designed for autonomously finding vulnerabilities, creating and testing fix code. Google explains that it prioritized vulnerability fixing capabilities over offensive capabilities such as exploiting vulnerabilities. It achieved an 86.2% success rate in CyberGym, which evaluates vulnerability detection capabilities, and a success rate exceeding 70% in Google's internal evaluation across 20 programming languages. It achieved a 47.2% success rate in the CWE-Bench vulnerability fix benchmark.

 


 Google has also begun using Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber internally, and reports that in evaluations using actual Chrome vulnerabilities, it generated 2.6 times more corrective code than larger, major commercial models. Furthermore, Google Cloud's vulnerability research team reportedly found a critical vulnerability in less than two hours that would normally take several months to investigate and discover.

 However, because advanced cybersecurity capabilities can also be used for attacks, the use of Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is limited to trusted defenders such as government cybersecurity agencies, critical infrastructure operators, and software maintenance personnel through Google's newly established 'Fairwind Program.' As of the time of writing, more than 650 partners worldwide are participating in the Fairwind Program, and organizations using the software are required to adhere to operational standards such as limiting users to internal security personnel and implementing multi-factor authentication.

 The standard version of Gemini 3.8 Flash is available via the Google AI Studio API, as well as with Google Antigravity, Android Studio, and Stitch. For Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra users, it is also available through the Gemini app, Google Search's AI mode, and Google Sheets. Google also plans to expand the Fairwind Program to meet the needs of its partners, developing the program while balancing the scope of cyber defense capabilities with security.

 On the same day that Gemini 3.8 Flash was announced, Meta also announced 'Muse Spark 1.3,' and some performance evaluations have reported that it outperforms Gemini 3.8 Flash.
