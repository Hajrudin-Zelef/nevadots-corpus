# Google Replaces Gemini Robotics ER 2 With Gemini 4 Hydrogen in Robotics Work

Google published a robotics write-up for Gemini 4 Hydrogen on Tuesday and released a Robotics plugin for its Antigravity coding environment. The company says Hydrogen, added to the Gemini 4 line on November 12, takes over the role of Gemini Robotics ER 2, released by DeepMind in July. Google’s comparison reports an average of 84.8 for Hydrogen versus 75.5 for ER 2 across 15 tests; Hydrogen leads on 13, is roughly level on multi-pointing, and trails on steerable pointing.

Hydrogen brings an agentic workflow—writing and running robot-control code from a plain-language goal—to a lower-cost model tier. Google describes the model as planning a task from a goal and camera view, executing control code, and adjusting until the job is complete. VP and head of robotics at Google DeepMind Carolina Parada said a robot arm can be connected over USB in 10 minutes, and that the team built a snack-sorting conveyor demonstration in about an hour. Google also reports a score of 58 on the AA-Robotics benchmark and describes Hydrogen as similar to Argon at a lower cost per token.

Google has not said whether it plans a standalone ER 3 or whether partners using ER 2 in their own systems should switch to Hydrogen. The published comparison does not provide underlying scores for multi-view correspondence or 3D trajectory reasoning, areas Google says Hydrogen handles better. The October AA-Robotics leaderboard did not yet list Hydrogen.
