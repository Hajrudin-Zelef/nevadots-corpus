# Gemini 3.7 Flash

Gemini 3.7 Flash is Google's most capable Flash model for complex coding, agentic workflows, multimodal reasoning, and knowledge work. Based on Gemini 3.6 Flash, it adds algorithmic improvements to its reasoning foundation, customizable low, medium, and high thinking levels, native computer use, a 1M-token context window, and 64K-token output.
