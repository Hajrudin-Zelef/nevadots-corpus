# Google's Missing Flagship: Inside the Sudden Rush to Ship Gemini 4

For most of a year, Google has not shipped a flagship AI model. On 23 September 2026, Google DeepMind's new chief Koray Kavukcuoglu said on the record that Gemini 4 has entered early post-training, and that the intention is to get it out as soon as possible, well before the end of the year. This deep dive unpacks what was actually said, how the flagship tier came to sit empty, and what shipping an early post-training output really means.
