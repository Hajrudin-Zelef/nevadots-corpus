Google began rolling out its November 2026 Pixel Drop on November 10, bringing its Gemini Intelligence features to Pixel 10 phones, adding a tool that builds custom home screen widgets from a text description, and opening sales of its first Bluetooth item tracker, Pixel Tag, on November 11 at $29.99. Google said in its announcement that the updates “start rolling out today and will continue over the next several weeks,” so not every Pixel owner will see them right away.

The drop covers phones going back to the Pixel 6a for some features, while the biggest additions are limited to the Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 series, and a few are restricted by country or language.

**Quick answer:** The November Pixel Drop adds Gemini Intelligence (including the Rambler dictation tool) to Pixel 10 phones, custom widgets on Pixel 10 and 11, new icon styles and Pixel search for Pixel 6a and newer, and an upgraded Call Screen for Pixel 11 owners in the US. Pixel Tag goes on sale November 11 for $29.99, or $99.99 for a four-pack. The rollout is gradual, so check Settings > System > Software updates over the next few weeks.

## What Google announced

In a post on Google’s official blog dated November 10, Pixel product marketing manager Mayra Fajardo listed the following changes.

- **Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10:** Google said the Pixel 10 series now gets Rambler, which strips filler words like “um” out of dictated speech and formats it as finished text, along with proactive suggestions and multi-step tasks. Google’s footnote says it requires a Pixel 10 (not the Pixel 10a) or newer, is limited to users 18 and older, and is available only in select countries and languages. Multi-step tasks such as ordering takeout or booking a ride are listed as available in Japan, the UK and India, and Google did not list the US for that feature.
- **Custom widgets:** Owners of the Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, 10 Pro Fold and the Pixel 11 series can describe a widget, such as a trip countdown or a spending tracker, and the phone builds it. Google said the feature is available “in select markets.”
- **Call Screen upgrade:** On the Pixel 11 series in the US, Gemini now asks unknown callers why they are calling in a more conversational way and shows a live transcript until you pick up or end the call.
- **Icons and search:** New custom icon styles and an expanded Pixel search, which finds photos, emails, messages, calendar events and settings, are available on the Pixel 6a and newer in all regions, according to Google’s footnotes.
- **HiLight for Messages:** On the Pixel 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL and 11 Pro Fold, the HiLight light now glows for Google Messages from favorite contacts when the phone is face down.
- **Pixel Watch:** Step-by-step workouts are rolling out to Pixel Watch 3 and newer, and automatic watch unlock is due “in the next few weeks” for Pixel Watch 3, 4 and 5 paired with supported Pixel Pro phones in regions that allow UWB.

## Pixel Tag price and availability

Pixel Tag is Google’s first item tracker, and Google said it goes on sale November 11 at $29.99 for one or $99.99 for a four-pack. According to Google, the tag is slim enough to fit in a wallet, has a battery that lasts “over a year,” and uses the Find Hub network, which Google says includes more than 1 billion Android devices, to locate lost items, with ultra-wideband (UWB) guidance for finding them nearby. Google’s footnote says it works with Android 9 phones across all regions and languages. Google’s post did not list specific retailers.

## Google Fi changes

Google also said that, starting November 10, Google Fi on eligible Pixel phones can switch automatically between two 5G networks and Wi-Fi hotspots. Its footnotes say this needs a Pixel 9 or newer with Fi active on an eSIM. Google is also offering existing Pixel owners who don’t use Fi 50% off select unlimited plans for 12 months, and that offer ends December 1, 2026.

## What this means for Pixel owners

For most people in the US, the practical changes are the new icon styles and Pixel search on any Pixel 6a or newer, custom widgets on the Pixel 10 and 11, and the smarter Call Screen on the Pixel 11. Pixel 10 owners get Rambler and other Gemini Intelligence features where Google has enabled them, but Google did not list the US for multi-step tasks, so not every feature is available here yet.

Because the rollout is staged, the update may not appear right away. You can check for it under Settings > System > Software updates, and Google’s Android update help page covers the steps.
