Chinese technology company Kuaishou has released Kling Image 4, an updated generative-image model that it says can produce native 8K images and maintain visual consistency across sets of up to 10 images.

The model is available through Kling AI’s Creative Studio and application programming interface, according to the company’s Nov. 18 announcement. The release adds to Kuaishou’s push to commercialize generative artificial intelligence tools for creators, advertisers and enterprise users in China and overseas.

Kuaishou said Kling Image 4 uses a unified autoregressive architecture for image understanding and generation. The company claims this approach improves instruction following and the rendering of text, small objects and spatial relationships in complex scenes. It also said the model can accept prompts of as many as 2,000 characters.

The release supports multi-image generation, allowing users to create sets of as many as 10 images while retaining elements such as characters, visual style and layout. Kuaishou highlighted potential uses in e-commerce product displays, advertising materials, web design and long-form visual content.

The company said native 8K output is intended to reduce the need for a separate upscaling step when images are used in detailed commercial layouts. It also positioned the longer prompt limit as useful for instructions that specify several objects, text elements or design constraints within one scene. Kuaishou did not provide comparative tests showing how these features perform against rival models.

The company did not publish independent benchmark results, detailed evaluation methodology or market-by-market pricing in the announcement. Claims about realism, precision, consistency and instruction adherence therefore remain vendor assertions rather than independently verified performance findings.

Kling AI has become a significant part of Kuaishou’s artificial intelligence business. Earlier in November, the company said Kling AI completed a $2.9 billion financing that valued the business at about $28 billion. That transaction was led by Kuaishou with participation from other investors, according to the company.

Kuaishou has expanded Kling from video generation into a broader set of image and creative tools as competition intensifies among Chinese and international model developers. The company has not provided a country-by-country list of Kling Image 4 availability, but said the model is live in the Kling Creative Studio and through its API.

Users can select Kling Image 4 in the studio for text-to-image and image-based workflows. Kuaishou said the API is intended for businesses that want to integrate the model into production systems, while access terms and costs depend on the company’s current service plans.
