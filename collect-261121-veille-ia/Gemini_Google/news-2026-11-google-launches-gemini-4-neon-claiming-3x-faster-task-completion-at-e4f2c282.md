Google launches Gemini 4 Neon, claiming 3x faster task completion at 60% of Carbon's cost
Google has introduced Gemini 4 Neon, a new model positioned between flagship Gemini 4 Carbon and the more affordable Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Designed for complex coding, analytical reasoning, and multi-step workflows, Neon can reportedly complete tasks three times faster than Carbon at 60% of the cost per task. While Carbon remains Google's most capable model overall, the company says Neon outperforms it in certain tasks, although these claims have not yet been independently verified.
Neon scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and 65.4% on CursorBench 4.1. It supports text, images, audio, and video, with a context window of up to one million tokens for handling large documents and codebases.
API pricing starts at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, currently higher than Carbon's promotional rates of $2 and $10, available until November 30. However, Google claims Neon is more cost-efficient when measured by completed tasks rather than token pricing. The model is available to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, as well as through the paid Gemini API, Google Antigravity, and Google's business offerings.
