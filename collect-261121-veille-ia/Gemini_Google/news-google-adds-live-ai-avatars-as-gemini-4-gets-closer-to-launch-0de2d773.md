Tech giant Google GOOGL -1.26% ▼ has launched Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar, which is a new AI model that gives conversational AI a real-time visual character that can listen and respond with speech. The release comes just one day after Google introduced two Gemini 3.8 text-to-speech models. More importantly, Google DeepMind chief Koray Kavukcuoglu said this week that the flagship model, Gemini 4, is getting close to release. That could be important after Google shares fell roughly 20% from their recent highs as investors worried about delays to Gemini 3.5 and stronger competition from other AI companies.
Interestingly, Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar combines near-real-time video generation with speech. As a result, companies can create an AI character that appears on screen and responds as someone talks to it.
Google says that the avatar can match its mouth movements with speech and use natural facial expressions. It can also handle conversations where the user and AI take turns speaking without long pauses.
That could make the technology useful for customer service. Companies could also use it for interactive product demonstrations or other situations where a visual AI assistant may feel more natural than text or voice alone.
Google Is Releasing New Gemini Models Ahead of Gemini 4
The Live Avatar release adds to a busy week for Google’s AI team. On Wednesday, the company released Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS.
Flash TTS lets users create custom character voices from simple text descriptions. Flash-Lite focuses more on lower-cost audio production for jobs such as dubbing and voice agents. Google says that the two models rank first and second on Hume AI’s Overall Quality Index.
Now attention is turning toward Gemini 4. Kavukcuoglu said that Gemini 4 has entered post-training, which is the stage where Google improves how the model responds and adds safety protections before release. He said that Google wants to release an early version as soon as possible.
For now, Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar is available through Gemini Enterprise.
