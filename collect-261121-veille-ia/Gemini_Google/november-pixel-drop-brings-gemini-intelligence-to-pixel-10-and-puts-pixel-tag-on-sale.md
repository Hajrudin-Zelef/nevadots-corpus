Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop starts rolling out today, and the biggest news is for anyone still carrying a Pixel 10. Gemini Intelligence is coming to last year’s phones, bringing the same features Pixel 11 owners already have. If you’re weighing an upgrade instead, our guide to the best Pixel phones is a good place to start.

That means Rambler, automations, and personalized suggestions on the Google Pixel 10. Rambler is my personal favorite; I’ve loved using it on the Pixel 11 and Googlebooks. With Rambler, you talk the way you normally would, and it strips out the filler words and formats your thoughts into clean text. Google says Rambler works in all regions and languages on Pixel 10, while the rest of Gemini Intelligence is limited to select regions and languages.

Automations let you hand Gemini routine jobs in approved apps, like ordering groceries, booking a ride, getting takeout, or making a reservation. In the US, Gemini can now place calls for you from your own phone number on Pixel 10 and Pixel 11. It can make or change a reservation, ask a store to hold an item, or check inventory.

## Pixel 11 owners get the new Gemini Call Screen

Pixel 11 owners in the US also get Gemini Call Screen. It turns manual Call Screen into an assistant that screens calls on demand and hands you the details you need to decide what to do next.

Pixel Tag, which Google first showed off at Made by Google in August, is now officially available to buy. It costs $29, or $99 for a four-pack, and taps into the Find Hub network of over 1 billion Android devices. It works with any phone running Android 9 or later.

Create My Widget lets you describe the widget you want in plain language, or pick from Google’s suggestions, on Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 phones. It’s coming to Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5 later. Custom Icons gets 10 new styles, now built on Google’s Nano Banana model, for Pixel 6a and newer. Pixel 11 Pro owners also get HiLight support for Google Messages, so the light glows when a favorite contact texts you while the phone is face down.

On the Pixel Watch side, step-by-step workouts are rolling out now for Pixel Watch 3 and newer. You get guidance for each exercise, can log reps and weights, and track live metrics on your watch or phone. Phone-to-Watch Unlock and notification summaries are listed as coming soon.

The update is rolling out to all supported Pixel phones, tablets, and watches starting today, so it may take a few days to reach yours. Compared to the September Pixel Drop, this one is a much bigger deal for Pixel 10 owners.

## November 2026 Pixel Drop FAQ

### When does the November 2026 Pixel Drop come out?

Google started rolling out the November Pixel Drop on November 10, 2026. It goes out in stages, so it can take a few days to reach your phone or watch.

### How do I get the November Pixel Drop on my Pixel?

Open Settings, tap System, then Software updates, and check for a system update. If it isn’t there yet, check back over the next few days.

### Which Pixel phones get the November Pixel Drop?

Every currently supported Pixel phone, tablet, and Pixel Watch gets the update. Some features depend on your model, though. Gemini Intelligence is for Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 phones, while Custom Icons, Transit Mode, and Unit Conversion are available on Pixel 6a and newer.

### Is Gemini Intelligence coming to the Pixel 10?

Yes. The Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold all get Gemini Intelligence in this update. Rambler works in all regions and languages, while automations and personalized suggestions are limited to select regions and languages.

### Does the Pixel 9 get Gemini Intelligence?

Not in this update. Google lists only Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 phones for Gemini Intelligence in the November Pixel Drop.

### What is Rambler on Pixel?

Rambler turns the way you naturally talk into clean text. It removes filler words and formats your thoughts, so you can speak a message or note and get something you’d actually send.

### How much does the Google Pixel Tag cost?

Pixel Tag costs $29 for one, or $99 for a four-pack. It works with phones running Android 9 or later and uses Google’s Find Hub network to locate your stuff.

### What else is new in the November Pixel Drop?

Transit Mode now turns on automatically based on your phone’s sensors, and the Calculator adds Unit Conversion with live exchange rates. Pixel Search can find apps, settings, and personal content with natural language. Pixel 10 also gets Extreme Charging Mode, and a new Family Hub in Settings puts family tools in one place.
