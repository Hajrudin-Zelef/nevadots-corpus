# November Pixel Drop Brings Gemini Intelligence to Pixel 10, Pixel Tag Goes on Sale

Google's November Pixel Drop and Android 17 QPR2 started rolling out on 10 November 2026, bringing Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series and the $29.99 Pixel Tag.

Google started rolling out the **November Pixel Drop** on **10 November 2026**, together with the stable Android 17 QPR2 release. The update is free for supported Pixel phones from the Pixel 6a onward, and its biggest addition brings Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series. Google is also putting its new Pixel Tag tracker on sale from 11 November at **$29.99**.

It follows the delay we reported in Google's Pixel November update delay. Google says the rollout happens in stages over the next several weeks, so not every phone will see it on day one.

## What's New in the November Pixel Drop

Pixel 10 owners get the most out of this one. Gemini Intelligence, which Google first showed on the Pixel 11 series, now runs on the Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL and 10 Pro Fold. The Pixel 10a is not included.

- **Rambler:** voice typing that strips out filler words and tidies your speech into clean text before you send it.
- **Multi-step tasks:** Gemini can handle jobs like grocery orders, rides, takeout and restaurant bookings in supported apps. Google lists Japan, the UK and India among the first markets.
- **Create My Widget:** build a custom home screen widget, such as a trip countdown or a spending tracker, by describing it. This works on Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 phones.
- **Calls on your behalf:** in the US, Gemini can phone a business from your number to change a reservation or check stock.

Older phones are not left out. Custom icon styles and Pixel search reach the Pixel 6a and newer. Pixel 11 Pro owners also get HiLight support for Google Messages, so the back glows when a favourite contact texts while the phone lies face down.

## Android 17 QPR2 Arrives Early

According to 9to5Google, Android 17 QPR2 landed about a month earlier than the usual quarterly pattern. It adds a redesigned long-press home screen menu, an accent colour slider, new Quick Settings tiles for audio streams and voice broadcast, and finer control over status bar icons. The supported list runs from the Pixel 6a and Pixel 7 up to the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, which means the original Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro miss out.

## Pixel Tag Price and Availability

- **USA:** $29.99 for one Pixel Tag, or $99.99 for a four-pack, from 11 November 2026.

The tag is slim enough for a wallet, lasts over a year on its battery according to Google, and uses the Find Hub network. It works with any Android phone running Android 9 or later.

## How It Compares With Earlier Drops

Unlike most drops, this one hands a year-old flagship the headline AI suite. Until now, Gemini Intelligence was a reason to buy a Pixel 11. Pixel 10 buyers now get almost the same assistant, minus a few Pixel 11 extras like the Gemini-powered Call Screen.

## Our Opinion

This is a generous update, and it makes the Pixel 10 a smarter buy right now, especially if you can find one discounted below the Pixel 11. Rambler alone is worth having, because it works in all regions and languages, unlike the task automations. The catch is regional: many of the best Gemini features are limited to a few countries and to English, so buyers in other markets should check before getting excited. Pixel 6 and 6 Pro owners have reached the end of the feature road. The $29.99 Pixel Tag is fairly priced too, though rivals have sold trackers at this price for years.
