# Oracle Diversifies AI Chips: Google TPU, Cerebras and SambaNova Join Nvidia on OCI

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure adds Google TPU, Cerebras and SambaNova chips to its AI offering while expanding its Nvidia partnership, a first for TPUs outside Google.

Oracle announced a major overhaul of its AI computing lineup on October 27, 2026, opening Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) to several chipmakers that compete with Nvidia, while simultaneously deepening its partnership with the semiconductor giant. This dual announcement illustrates a broader trend in the AI cloud sector: diversifying chip supply without breaking ties with the market leader.
