# November Pixel Drop Brings Gemini Intelligence to Pixel 10

Pixel 10 phones now get Rambler, Create My Widget and Gemini task automation, while the $29.99 Pixel Tag goes on sale. Here is who gets what, and when.

The November Pixel Drop is rolling out now, and its biggest change is for Pixel 10 owners: their phones are getting Gemini Intelligence, Google's top tier of on-device AI features, including the Rambler voice typing tool and Create My Widget. Google announced the update on November 10, 2026, and its Pixel Tag tracker goes on sale on November 11 for $29.99, or $99.99 for a four-pack.

The rollout starts with Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL and Pixel 10 Pro Fold, and continues over the next several weeks. The cheaper Pixel 10a is left out of the headline features again, so check your exact model before you go looking for the new menus.

## What Is Gemini Intelligence, and Why Does It Matter for Pixel 10?

Gemini Intelligence is Google's label for Android phones that can run its most capable Gemini features, the ones that act across your apps instead of only answering questions. Until now, it lived on the Pixel 11 series and, since this summer, on Samsung's Galaxy S26, S26+, S26 Ultra, Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Fold8 and Z Flip8. Google's Android blog post says Pixel 10 now joins that list.

Motorola is next. The motorola signature 27 is its first Gemini Intelligence phone, with US preorders opening on November 19. We covered the motorola signature 27 price when it was announced.

## What Does Rambler Do?

Rambler is a new voice typing mode in Gboard, Google's keyboard. Regular dictation writes down every "um" and every time you correct yourself. Rambler drops the filler words and turns what you said into a clean, formatted message that you can review and send.

In practice, this is the feature most Pixel 10 owners will actually notice, because it works anywhere you can type. It is also coming to the motorola signature 27.

## What Else Can Gemini Do on a Pixel Now?

Task automation lets Gemini handle chores like ordering groceries, booking a ride, getting takeout or finding a restaurant table. With this drop, it expands to the UK, India and Japan. It is still a beta with daily limits, and you confirm the final step yourself.

Proactive suggestions are the other half. If someone texts asking when you will arrive, your Pixel can offer to share your ETA with one tap. Google says this needs a Pixel 10 or newer (not the 10a), works for users 18 and older in select countries, and is coming to the Pixel Watch 4.

Conversation summaries, which condense long chats, now also work in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Hindi and Traditional Chinese.

## How Does Create My Widget Work?

Create My Widget lets you build a home screen widget by describing it in plain words. Google's examples include a monthly spending tracker, a game day widget in your team's colors and a trip countdown that pulls flight and reservation details from Gmail and Calendar. You do not need to write any code.

The feature first appeared on Google's Googlebook laptops. It now comes to the Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 series in select markets, with Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5 support coming soon.

## What Do Pixel 11 and Older Pixels Get?

On the Pixel 11 series in the US, Call Screen gets a Gemini upgrade. Gemini now talks to unknown callers in a more conversational way and shows a live transcript, so you can decide when to pick up. On the Pixel 11 Pro models, HiLight, the light on the back of the phone, now glows for Google Messages from favorite contacts too.

Older phones are not ignored. Pixel 6a and newer get new custom icon styles, and Pixel search, which finds photos, emails, messages, calendar events and settings from one search bar, now works on Pixel 6 and newer. 9to5Google reports the drop also adds Gemini calls on your behalf in the US on Pixel 10 and newer, plus Extreme Battery Charging on the Pixel 10 series.

## How Much Is Pixel Tag?

Pixel Tag, Google's first item tracker, costs $29.99 for one or $99.99 for four, priced in line with Apple's AirTag. It uses UWB (ultra-wideband, a short-range radio for precise direction finding) and the Find Hub network of more than 1 billion Android devices. The battery lasts over a year, and the tag works with any phone running Android 9 or newer, not just Pixels. Our Pixel Tag launch story has the full specs.

On eligible Pixels, the drop also turns on the Google Fi super network, which switches between two US 5G carriers. Pixel Watch owners get automatic unlock with a nearby phone in the coming weeks and step-by-step workouts on Pixel Watch 3 and newer.

## Is the November Pixel Drop Worth Waiting For?

In our view, this is one of the more meaningful Pixel Drops in a while, because it brings much of the Pixel 11's AI layer to a phone that is a year old. The catch is the fine print. Many features are limited by region, language or model, and the Pixel 10a misses the most interesting ones again.

If you own a Pixel 10, update this month and try Rambler first. If you own a Pixel 10a or an older Pixel, expect smaller upgrades like icons and search, and the $29.99 Pixel Tag is the only part that works on any recent Android phone.
