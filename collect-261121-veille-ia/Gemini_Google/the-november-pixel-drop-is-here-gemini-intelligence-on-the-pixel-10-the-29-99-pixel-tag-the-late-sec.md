## The security patch finally landed

Google published the November Pixel bulletin on Tuesday, November 10, with security patch level 2026-11-05, about a week later than usual. It covers every supported Pixel, Pixel 6a and newer, including the Pixel Tablet and Fold. The update is Android 17 QPR2, with 33 Pixel bug fixes on top of the security fixes. That includes the fixes for the Android bugs I covered on Monday, like the "SMS of Death." (Google, 9to5Google)

The build is CP4A.261105.040.A1 for the Pixel 6a through Pixel 10a, and versions ending in D1 and B2 for the Pixel 11 series. Google says the rollout continues "over the next several weeks," so it may not show up for you today. (Android Authority, Google)

## Pixel 10: Gemini Intelligence arrives

Gemini Intelligence, Google's bundle of AI features that launched on the Pixel 11 this summer, now comes to the Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL and 10 Pro Fold. Not the Pixel 10a, and not the Pixel 9 or older. You need the Gemini app set up, you have to be 18 or older, and it's limited to some countries and languages. (Google)

- **Rambler,** a voice typing mode in Gboard. You talk the way you normally do, and it writes clean text in real time, dropping the "ums" and false starts. It's dictation, not a voice memo app.
- **Multi-step tasks.** Gemini can work through apps for you to order groceries, book a ride, get takeout or make a reservation. It's now in the UK, India and Japan as well.
- **Create My Widget.** Describe a home screen widget in words and it builds one. Rolling out over the next few weeks.
- **Device Assistance.** Ask it to change or fix a setting instead of digging through menus.
- **Proactive suggestions,** like offering to share your arrival time with one tap.

## Every Pixel 6a and newer

- **Transit Mode turns on by itself** when your phone's sensors detect you're on a train or bus.
- **Calculator gets unit conversion** with live currency exchange rates.
- **Ten new icon styles,** 16 in all.
- **Pixel Search** expands to older phones.
- **Conversation summaries** add German, French, Spanish, Italian, Hindi and Traditional Chinese.

Some extras are Pixel 11 only, like Gemini Call Screen in the US. On the watch side, Pixel Watch Unlock (unlock your Pixel 8 or newer with a Pixel Watch 3, 4 or 5) is coming "in the next few weeks." (Google, Android Authority)

## The Pixel Tag: $29.99, on sale today

Google's first item tracker goes on sale Wednesday, November 11: $29.99 for one or $99.99 for a four-pack. Google says the battery lasts over a year. It finds your stuff using Find Hub, the network of over a billion Android phones (it used to be called Find My Device), and uses ultra-wideband to point you to it when you're close. Google says it works with any phone running Android 9 or later. (Google)

Google hasn't said which countries get it at launch, and says nothing about iPhones. If you have an iPhone, an AirTag is still the obvious choice. If you're on Android and have been using a Tile or a cheap Bluetooth tag, this is the one built into your phone.

## What to do

- **Check for the update:** Settings, System, Software updates. If nothing shows up, check again over the next few days.
- **Install it even if you don't care about the features,** since this is also the November security patch.
- **On a Pixel 10?** Open the Gemini app after updating to turn on the new features, and try Rambler in any text box.
- **Galaxy owners:** this doesn't change anything for you. Samsung's November patch is on its own schedule.

No big bugs had been reported in the first few hours. I'll note anything serious in a future post.
