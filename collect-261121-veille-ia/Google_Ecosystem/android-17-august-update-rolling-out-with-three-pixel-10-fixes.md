Google is rolling out the August 2026 security patch for Android 17 on the Pixel 8, 8 Pro, 8a, 9, 9 Pro, 9 Pro XL, 9 Pro Fold, 9a, Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, and Pixel 10a.

There are no security issues resolved in the Android 17 August patch. The dedicated security bulletin for Google devices lists one additional security fix. In addition to the Global version, there’s a variant for Rogers in Canada (Pixel 9+).

**Global**

- Pixel 8: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 8 Pro: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 8a: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 9: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 9 Pro: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 9 Pro XL: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 9 Pro Fold: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 9a: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 10: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 10 Pro: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 10 Pro XL: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 10 Pro Fold: CP2A.260805.005
- Pixel 10a: CP2A.260805.005

Use the following device key to interpret the Pixel’s Android 17 August update changelog:

**[1] Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold:, Pixel 10a*
