Decemberâs expected Pixel update has landed in November. Google began rolling out Android 17 QPR2 on November 10, 2026, bringing customization controls and security fixes to supported devices roughly a month ahead of schedule.

The update gives Pixel owners more control over system colors, notification icons, and wallpaper appearance. But not everything previewed during beta testing made the release, and eligibility for Android 17 QPR2 does not guarantee access to every feature in Googleâs accompanying November Pixel Drop.

## Pixel 6a through Pixel 11: Who gets QPR2

Googleâs November Pixel update announcement confirms that Android 17 QPR2 is rolling out to 23 supported phones and tablets across six numbered Pixel generations:

| Pixel series | Eligible devices | 
|---|---|
| Pixel 6 | Pixel 6a | 
| Pixel 7 | Pixel 7, 7 Pro, 7a | 
| Pixel 8 | Pixel 8, 8 Pro, 8a |

| Pixel 11 | Pixel 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, 11 Pro Fold |

The original Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro are excluded, although the Pixel 6a remains eligible.

Google is distributing the update in phases over approximately one week, depending on device and carrier. Owners can check manually under **Settings > System > System update > Check for update**.

Quarterly Platform Releases (QPRs) deliver features and fixes between major Android versions. They are part of Googleâs broader update cycle, explained in TechRepublicâs Android cheat sheet, which also covers security patches and manufacturer-specific updates.

## New color, wallpaper, and status bar controls

The most visible addition is a custom accent-color slider under **Wallpaper & style > Color**. Users can select a specific hue rather than relying on preset palettes. Four intensity options â Neutral, Soft, Bright, and Bold â provide further control over the interfaceâs appearance.

Compatible wallpapers can also switch between light and dark variants to match the system theme. Status bar customization now lets users display all notification icons, priority notifications only, or none.

The new **Settings > System > Status bar** menu also brings mute, vibration, and battery-percentage indicators into one place. Vibration and battery-percentage controls were already available elsewhere in Pixel settings. Quick Settings gains a brightness override for apps that attempt to control display brightness.

One anticipated feature did not arrive. The Quick Settings layout editor tested in QPR2 Beta 3, which allowed users to rearrange the brightness slider, tiles, and media controls, is absent from the stable release.

Beyond customization, Googleâs November Pixel security bulletin documents fixes for critical-severity vulnerabilities. The November 5, 2026, patch level addresses the applicable Android and Pixel issues. Google also fixed problems involving unexpected theme-color changes, notification readability, fingerprint recognition, and connectivity.

The broader Android security update addresses three critical remote code execution flaws. Android 17 QPR2 also adds protections against call-forwarding fraud, restricting certain automated commands and requiring user confirmation for affected dialer codes.

The rollout may take several days to reach every eligible Pixel, but owners can check for the update manually in Settings. For organizations managing multiple devices, verifying installed security patch levels provides a more reliable deployment check than waiting for update notifications.
