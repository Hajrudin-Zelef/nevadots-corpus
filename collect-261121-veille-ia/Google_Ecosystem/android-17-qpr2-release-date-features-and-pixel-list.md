# Android 17 QPR2: Release Date, New Features and Supported Pixels

**Android 17 QPR2** is the second quarterly update for Android 17, and Google released it to Pixel phones on 10 November 2026, about a month earlier than the December date most people expected. It is available for the Pixel 6a and newer, but not for the Pixel 6 or Pixel 6 Pro. Below you will find what QPR2 is, the key dates, what changed, and how to install it without losing data.

## What Is Android 17 QPR2?

QPR stands for Quarterly Platform Release. After a major Android version comes out, Google adds fixes and features every few months through QPRs. Google delivers them to Pixel phones as part of its Pixel Drops and also publishes them to the Android Open Source Project (AOSP).

- **It is still Android 17.** QPR2 is not Android 18. Your phone will still show Android 17 as the version.
- **It comes after QPR1.** Android 17 launched on 16 June 2026, QPR1 followed on 15 September, and QPR2 is the third stable release of the Android 17 cycle.
- **It includes a small developer update.** QPR2 has a minor SDK release for app developers. Google’s API difference report for it is labelled 37.2, after Android 17’s API level 37.
- **It is Pixel-first.** Samsung, Xiaomi and other brands build their own software on Android 17 and decide for themselves which later changes to include.

## Android 17 QPR2 Release Date and Key Dates

When QPR2 Beta 1 arrived in July, reports described it as the release for the fourth quarter (December). Instead, the stable build arrived on 10 November 2026 together with the November Pixel Drop and the November Pixel security bulletin (patch level 2026-11-05).

Google rolls updates out in stages, so the stable build should reach phones over roughly a week, not all on day one.

## What’s New in Android 17 QPR2

These changes arrived with QPR2 and the November 2026 Pixel Drop. Some depend on your Pixel model.

### Look and feel

- **Any accent colour:** in**Wallpaper & style** >**Color** , a new slider lets you pick any colour instead of only the presets. You can also choose a Neutral, Soft, Bright or Bold style.
- **Custom icon styles:** more home screen icon styles, on the Pixel 6a and newer.
- **New home screen menu:** the long-press menu on the home screen has a revised design.

### Status bar, Quick Settings and battery

- **Status bar control:** in**Settings** >**System** , choose which notification icons appear (All, Priority or None) and turn the Mute, Vibrate and Battery percentage icons on or off.
- **New Quick Settings tiles:** Audio streams, and Voice broadcast, which streams your voice to nearby Bluetooth Auracast listeners.
- **Battery:** the battery icon in Quick Settings gets an outline while charging or in Battery Saver, and battery settings move to**Settings** >**Battery & charging** .

### Privacy and security

- **Protection from call-forwarding scams:** scammers sometimes trick people into dialling a call-forwarding code such as`*21#` , which sends their calls to the scammer’s number. In QPR2, when you dial a call-forwarding code yourself, Android shows a confirmation screen first. Normal apps can no longer run these codes in the background. Other USSD codes, such as mobile money transfers and balance checks, work as before.
- **Private Compute Core log:** under**Security & privacy** >**Privacy dashboard** >**Data logging** .
- **Agents dashboard:** under**Security & privacy** >**Privacy controls** >**Agents** .
- **App installs from outside the store:** Android Authority reports a refreshed permission screen when you install apps from other sources. Google’s separate sideloading rules are explained in our developer verification guide.

### Other changes

- **HiLight on Pixel 11 Pro models:** the Pixel 11 Pro, Pro XL and Pro Fold light can now also blink for messages from favourite contacts. Set it in**Settings** >**System** >**HiLight** .
- **Notification intelligence:** one place for notification summaries, the notification organizer and notification cooldown.
- **For developers:** messages an app sends to itself (self-broadcasts) are delivered more efficiently, so apps can no longer use them to keep running in the background.

One planned change is not in this release: rearranging the brightness slider and media player in Quick Settings.

## Which Pixel Phones Get Android 17 QPR2?

Google lists these devices for Android 17 QPR2:

- Pixel 6a
- Pixel 7, Pixel 7 Pro, Pixel 7a
- Pixel Tablet, Pixel Fold
- Pixel 8, Pixel 8 Pro, Pixel 8a
- Pixel 9, Pixel 9 Pro, Pixel 9 Pro XL, Pixel 9 Pro Fold, Pixel 9a
- Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, Pixel 10a
- Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, Pixel 11 Pro Fold

### Why the Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro are missing

Google promises the Pixel 6 series five years of updates from launch. Android Authority reported in July that this window closes for the Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro in October 2026, so Android 17 QPR1 was their last platform update. The Pixel 6a launched later and is still supported.

### Samsung, Xiaomi and other brands

Other brands do not ship an update called “QPR2”. Samsung ships One UI versions and Xiaomi ships HyperOS versions on their own schedules, so check your brand’s update notes. See our Android 17 release date and phones guide for each brand’s main Android 17 update.

## How to Install Android 17 QPR2

### Over the air (recommended)

1. Back up your phone. A normal update keeps your data, but a backup protects you if something goes wrong.
2. Connect to Wi-Fi and charge to at least 75%.
3. Open **Settings** >**System** >**Software updates** (on some Pixels it is called**System update** ) and tap to check for an update.
4. Download and install, then restart when asked. Pixels install updates in the background, and the new version starts after the restart.

### If you are in the Android Beta program

Phones on QPR2 Beta 7.1 get a small update to the final build. If you want to leave the beta without losing data, opt out on the Android Beta for Pixel page after that final QPR2 build is installed and before you install any QPR3 beta.

**Warning:** if you already installed a QPR3 beta, opting out sends a downgrade update that wipes all data on the phone. Back up first, or stay on the beta until QPR3 is stable.

### Manual install (advanced)

- **Full OTA image:** Google publishes full OTA images for Pixel phones. Google says applying one has the same effect as a factory image, but it does not wipe data or need an unlocked bootloader. It is useful when an over-the-air update keeps failing.
- **Factory image:** Google warns that installing a factory image erases all data, and it needs an unlocked bootloader, which makes the phone less secure. Use it only to repair a phone, and relock the bootloader afterwards.

For the commands involved, see our fastboot commands list and our older guide on flashing Pixel factory images.

## Troubleshooting

- **No update yet:** wait a few days and check again. If a download stops, a Pixel tries again by itself over the next few days.
- **Battery drains faster after updating:** Google says this is common for a short time while the phone optimises the new software.
- **Phone keeps restarting after the update:** follow our boot loop fixes, which start with a forced restart and safe mode, before you try anything that erases data.

## FAQ

### When was Android 17 QPR2 released?

Google started rolling it out to Pixel phones on 10 November 2026, with the November Pixel Drop. Earlier reports had expected it in December.

### Will my Pixel 6 or Pixel 6 Pro get QPR2?

No. Google’s QPR2 list starts with the Pixel 6a. The Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro stopped at Android 17 QPR1.

### Does installing QPR2 delete my data?

No, not through a normal over-the-air update or a full OTA image. Data is erased if you flash a factory image, or if you leave the beta program while your phone runs a beta build newer than the stable release.

### Is Android 17 QPR2 the same as Android 18?

No. QPR2 is a quarterly update to Android 17. The next one is Android 17 QPR3, which has been in beta since 2 October 2026. 9to5Google expects the stable release in March 2027.

### Will Samsung or Xiaomi phones get Android 17 QPR2?

Not under that name. QPR2 is Google’s Pixel release. Samsung and Xiaomi deliver Android 17 changes through their own One UI and HyperOS updates, and we have not seen an announcement from either brand tied to QPR2.
