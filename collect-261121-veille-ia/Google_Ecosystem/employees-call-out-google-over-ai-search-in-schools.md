The Alphabet Workers Union voiced concern over Google requiring its AI tools be bundled with Search.

Google continues to draw fire for its handling of AI features in its Classroom services for students. Earlier this month, Google set out a policy that would essentially require schools to have some AI features enabled in order for their students to use the company's Search tools. The argument from the business was that "Search and AI are deeply integrated" to the point that a blanket ban on AI at a school would make Search unavailable to its students. The claim seems to dismiss Google's decades of operation before it even launched Gemini and other public-facing AI tools. It's a controversial enough move that some of Google's own employees have spoken out opposing the decision.
