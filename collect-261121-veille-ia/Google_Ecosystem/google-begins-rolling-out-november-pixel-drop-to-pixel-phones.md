Jules Park, Nov 20, 2026

Google began its November Pixel Drop rollout on Tuesday, pushing Gemini Intelligence to Pixel 10 phones and launching a new Pixel Tag tracker. 9to5Google reported that the update arrives a month early alongside Android 17 QPR2, while Gadgetry reported that Pixel Tag costs $29.99 for one or $99.99 for a four-pack and goes on sale November 11. The tag joins Google's Find Hub network, which spans more than 1 billion Android devices, and uses ultra-wideband radio to give visual direction and distance guidance when the tag is nearby. Google says the tag is thin enough to slide into a wallet and its battery is rated for more than a year.

Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10 now includes Rambler for voice typing. According to Google, Rambler can remove filler words such as "um" and format thoughts into clear, polished text. Across the four Pixel 10 models, Gemini app automation can handle grocery orders, rides, meal delivery, marketplace purchases, and reservation bookings. Proactive Gemini Actions can send groceries straight to a Google Keep list from an ongoing conversation. Google also said proactive suggestions may provide an estimated arrival time or suggest a place to eat, and will appear on Pixel Watch 4 soon. The multi-step requests have reached users in Japan, the UK, and India.

Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 phones receive Create My Widget, a generative UI tool that builds custom home-screen widgets from natural language requests. Users can ask for a sports widget or an event countdown. For Pixel 11, Gemini Call Screen replaces Manual Call Screen and acts as an "intelligent, proactive assistant that screens incoming inquiries on demand." It speaks to callers in a more conversational way while a transcript appears on screen. On Pixel 10 and newer in the US, Gemini can make calls on your behalf. Beta testing started in September for tasks like changing reservations, putting items on hold, and checking inventory.

The update also spreads several features across older Pixel phones. Pixel Search is now available on Pixel 6 and newer, letting people search across apps, web, settings, photos, emails, messages, and calendar entries from the app grid search bar. Ten new custom icon styles arrive with higher-quality icons powered by Nano Banana and work on Pixel 6a and newer. HiLight now supports Google Messages notifications on Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. HiLight lights up when the phone is face down and a favorite contact sends a message. Extreme Battery Charging is available on Pixel 10 series, and Pixel VIPs gains Live Location Sharing, which pulls live location and battery data from Find Hub into contact cards and home-screen widgets on Pixel 6a and newer. Transit Mode can now trigger automatically based on device sensors. Pixel 9 and newer phones can use Notification Summaries in Germany, Canada, France, Spain, Italy, India, and Taiwan. Notification Organizer now supports German and Japanese.

Google Fi's new super network went live on Tuesday for supported Pixel phones. Google said it automatically hops between two nationwide 5G carriers and many protected Wi-Fi networks to give the fastest, most extensive coverage. Pixel owners who are not already on Google Fi can take 50% off select unlimited plans for 12 months, and the deal ends December 1, 2026. Automatic unlock for Pixel Watch is coming within a few weeks. As long as the watch sits on your wrist and the paired phone has been unlocked, the watch will skip its PIN or pattern. Guided workouts have started rolling out to Pixel Watch 3 and later models. They give instructions, record repetitions and weight, and log metrics from either the watch or a phone. A routine can come from Google Health Coach or a plan built in the Google Health app. More updates are expected over the next few weeks.

Jetstream reported on Tuesday that Google's November 2026 software update covers 23 Pixel models, but the Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro are no longer on the eligible devices list. The update includes the Android security patch level, 32 bug fixes and improvements, and the rollout of November's Pixel Drop features. Build numbers are CP4A.261105.040.A1 for Pixel 6a through Pixel 10a and CP4A.261105.040.D1 for Pixel 11 series models. Google is releasing the update over the air in phases through next week; users can check by going to Settings > System > System update.
