# Google Labs Launches Stitch Loop to Automate Continuous Product Improvement

Image: Google · Source

Google Labs introduces Stitch Loop, an AI-driven product agent that connects to codebases and live metrics to continuously optimize software by identifying issues, suggesting fixes, and submitting pull requests for user approval.

Google Labs has unveiled Stitch Loop, an innovative product agent designed to help lean development teams maintain and improve software products continuously after launch. Stitch Loop integrates directly with a team’s codebase and live product metrics, such as logs, user behavior, and feedback, to identify bugs, security vulnerabilities, latency issues, and accessibility improvements around the clock.

The system works by running evergreen “loops” that focus on specific goals defined by the team, including built-in modules for cybersecurity, latency optimization, and accessibility enhancement. It surfaces actionable suggestions grounded in real product evidence, offers multiple solution options with tradeoff analyses, and then builds the selected changes in an isolated cloud sandbox. For each approved update, Stitch Loop opens a pull request in the team’s GitHub repository containing detailed contextual information such as screenshots and videos.

Importantly, all changes require manual review and approval, ensuring teams retain full control over their code. While automated agents typically stop contributing once code merges, Stitch Loop continues monitoring live data to evaluate the outcome of each change and refine its recommendations in subsequent iterations. It keeps working until the defined targets are met and continues vigilance thereafter to maintain quality.

In addition to its standalone capabilities, Stitch Loop can integrate with other coding agents and existing team workflows, including Slack, enabling seamless adoption and enhancement of continuous product optimization processes. Users can create custom loops tailored to their unique product goals beyond the default options.

Google Labs is opening a global waitlist for Stitch Loop at stitch.google.com/loop/waitlist, prioritizing Google AI Ultra users. This launch represents a progressive step toward Google's ambition to empower lean teams with sophisticated agent-based tools that support the full product lifecycle—from ideation through continuous post-launch improvement, ultimately increasing software reliability and user satisfaction while freeing teams to focus on new feature development.
