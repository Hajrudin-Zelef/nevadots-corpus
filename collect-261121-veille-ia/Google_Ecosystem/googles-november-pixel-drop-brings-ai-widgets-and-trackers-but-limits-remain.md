Google’s November Pixel Drop expands Gemini Intelligence features to Pixel 10 devices, introduces AI-generated home screen widgets, and launches the Pixel Tag tracker. While the update adds capabilities like voice cleanup and multi-step task automation, it excludes the budget Pixel 10a and restricts certain features to specific regions and languages.

## Key points

- The November Pixel Drop is rolling out to Pixel 10 and 11 series devices, excluding the Pixel 10a.
- Create My Widget allows users to generate custom home screen widgets via natural language prompts, supported in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and India.
- Gemini Intelligence features, including Rambler for voice typing and multi-step task automation, are now available on Pixel 10 devices.
- The Pixel Tag tracker, priced at $29.99 for a single unit, is available for purchase starting November 11, 2026.
- Manual Call Screen and Pixel Solver are exclusive to the Pixel 11 series, while UK users lack Gemini Connected Apps integration for widgets.

## Background

This update follows recent expansions of Google’s AI assistant to car systems and the introduction of Extreme Charging for Pixel 10 devices. The November Pixel Drop continues Google’s strategy of differentiating its flagship hardware through exclusive AI capabilities, building on previous updates that introduced basic custom icons and security patches.

## How outlets are covering it

Android Authority emphasizes the limitations of Create My Widget, noting that while it supports personal data via Google Workspace, UK users miss out on Gemini Connected Apps integration. Ars Technica highlights the narrow use cases for AI-generated widgets, restricting them to search data and select Google account information. PCMag focuses on the practical benefits of Gemini making calls on behalf of users, such as restaurant reservations, but notes the feature is strictly US-only and English-only at launch. All sources agree that the Pixel 10a is excluded from these new AI features, a point reiterated in the background archive regarding previous updates.

## Why it matters

The November Pixel Drop marks a significant shift in how users interact with their home screens, moving from static app widgets to AI-generated, personalized interfaces. The introduction of the Pixel Tag also signals Google’s direct entry into the consumer tracking hardware market, challenging Apple’s AirTag. However, the regional and device-specific limitations highlight the ongoing fragmentation of AI features across different markets and hardware tiers.

## What to watch

Google is expected to continue rolling out the November Pixel Drop over the next several weeks. Future updates may expand Create My Widget to Pixel Watch devices and potentially broaden language and regional support. Users should monitor their device settings for the update, as rollout is phased. Additionally, Google may introduce further Gemini Intelligence features to older Pixel models in subsequent quarterly drops.
