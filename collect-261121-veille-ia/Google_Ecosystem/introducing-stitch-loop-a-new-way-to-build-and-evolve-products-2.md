As part of a new direction for Stitch, we’re introducing a new experiment, Stitch Loop: a long-running product agent that connects to your codebase and live metrics to continuously improve what you’ve shipped.

We are introducing Stitch Loop, a product agent that connects to your codebase and live metrics to continuously optimize your software. It identifies issues, suggests fixes, and opens pull requests to help your team maintain product quality around the clock. You can join the waitlist today at stitch.google.com/loop/waitlist to get started with these automated tools for security, latency, and accessibility.

We are introducing Stitch Loop, a new tool from Google Labs that helps you maintain and improve your software after it launches. It connects to your code and live data to find bugs, fix security issues, and boost performance around the clock. You simply review the suggested changes before they go live. This helps your team focus on building new features while the agent handles the ongoing work of keeping your product running well.

Coding agents let you build and ship new features fast, but their job ends the moment your pull request merges. Once code hits production, the real work starts: patching vulnerabilities, speeding up checkout, and diagnosing signup drop-offs buried in noisy feedback. For lean teams, balancing post-launch maintenance with continuous improvement is a constant challenge, yet it’s also what drives some of the world’s most successful products.
