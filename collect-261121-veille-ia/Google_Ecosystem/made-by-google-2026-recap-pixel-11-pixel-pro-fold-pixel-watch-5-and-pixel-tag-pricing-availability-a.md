Pre-orders are open today, August 12, with most of the lineup shipping August 20.

Below, we've rounded up every device, price, and feature Google announced on stage, followed by our live coverage from the Made by Google event in New York.

Beyond the phones themselves, Google leaned hard into on-device AI and a few smaller hardware refreshes at the show. Here's a quick rundown six things:

- The Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and Pixel 11 Pro XL get a thinner, less wobbly camera bar as part of their redesign. All three run on Google's new Tensor G6 chip, with the base Pixel 11 also picking up a brighter M16 OLED display and Qi2 25W wireless charging.
- The Pixel 11 Pro models pack incredible new camera features and Google's new HiLite, an LED that provides color-coded visual alerts when your phone is resting face down.
- The Pixel 11 Pro Fold is slimmer, has a less visible crease, and has a new hinge that Google stressed tested with more than 80 million open-close tests.
- The Google Pixel Watch 5 packs improved performance plus new Health and Fitbit AI features, including blood pressure and insulin resistance trend tracking and a Smart Wake alarm. It ships August 20 alongside the phones.
- Google also unveiled the new Pixel Tag, its long-awaited answer to the AirTag. It's the only one you'll have to wait for, as it's not shipping until November 11.
- On the software side, Gemini Nano got faster, bringing up to 25% quicker browsing and a new Magic Capture camera tool to the Tensor G6 chip. There's also a new sign-to-text feature that allows people who are deaf or hard of hearing to use ASL to text, translate, and interact with gemini.

## Carrier and pre-order deals

Beyond Google's own pricing, carriers and retailers are already running early promotions on the new lineup.

- T-Mobile is offering the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and 11 Pro XL for free with a new line, or up to $1,300 in trade-in credit.
- Verizon is offering up to $1,100 in trade-in credit for new lines (or $900 for existing customers). The credit is paid out over 36 months though, so if you switch from Verizon early, you'll forfeit your remaining credit.
- Google Fi is knocking $450 off the Pixel 11 Pro for existing subscribers, without a long-term contract.
- Best Buy and Amazon are each tossing in a $200 gift card with unlocked Pixel 11 Pro preorders, or $350 on the Pro Fold.
