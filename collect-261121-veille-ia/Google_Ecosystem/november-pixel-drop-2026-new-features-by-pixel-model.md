## What is in the November 2026 Pixel Drop?

**It’s a feature update plus the monthly security patch.** Google’s announcement leads with three things: Gemini Intelligence arriving on the Pixel 10 series, AI-built custom widgets, and the launch of Pixel Tag. Around those sit smaller upgrades that reach much older phones.

Here are the headline additions, with the devices Google lists in its own footnotes:

| Feature | What it does | Which devices | 
|---|---|---|
| **Gemini Intelligence** (Rambler, proactive suggestions, multi-step tasks) | Cleans up voice typing, suggests actions like sharing your ETA, lets Gemini order groceries or book rides in allowlisted apps | Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, 10 Pro Fold (not 10a) and Pixel 11 series; select countries and languages, 18+ | 
| **Create custom widgets** | Describe a widget in plain words (a spending tracker, a trip countdown) and Gemini builds it | Pixel 10 (not 10a) and Pixel 11 series, select markets; Pixel Watch 4 and 5 “coming soon” | 
| **New custom icon styles** | More AI-generated styles applied across all home screen icons | Pixel 6a and newer, all regions | 
| **Pixel search** | Natural-language search across photos, email, messages, calendar and settings | Pixel 6a and newer per Google’s footnote | 
| **Gemini Call Screen** | Gemini talks to unknown callers more conversationally and shows a live transcript | Pixel 11 series, US only | 
| **HiLight for Google Messages** | The rear light glows for messages from favorite contacts when the phone is face down | Pixel 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, 11 Pro Fold | 
| **Conversation summaries** | Summaries of long chats, now in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Hindi and Traditional Chinese | Not itemized in Google’s post | 
| **Pixel Tag** | Item tracker on the Find Hub network, with UWB direction-finding up close | Works with Android 9 or later phones; $29.99 or $99.99 for four | 

*Device lists come from the numbered footnotes in Google’s announcement.*

One wrinkle on Pixel search: the body of Google’s post says “Pixel 6 and newer,” but the footnote says **Pixel 6a phones and newer**. The Pixel 6 and 6 Pro left Google’s support window in October, and they aren’t on this month’s Pixel update list, so don’t expect it there.

## Which new features do older Pixels get?

**Pixel 6a through Pixel 9 owners get the “everyone” features, not the Gemini Intelligence bundle.** According to Android Authority and Tech Advisor, these reach the Pixel 6a and newer in all regions:

- **10 new AI-generated icon styles** , now built on Google’s Nano Banana image model
- **Pixel search** from the app drawer
- **Unit Conversion in Calculator** , with inline math and live exchange rates
- **Transit Mode 2.0** , which can now switch itself on from the phone’s sensors instead of needing a manual toggle
- **Live location and battery in Pixel VIPs** , pulled from Find Hub into contact cards and widgets
- **Family Hub** , a single page in Settings for Google’s family-management tools

Those are small, but they are real changes on phones that are three or four years old. Tech Advisor also notes that **Pixel 10a misses the Pixel 10 extras**, even though it gets the update itself.

## What do Pixel 10 owners get from Gemini Intelligence?

**The Pixel 10 series gets most of what launched with the Pixel 11.** Google’s post lists three parts:

- **Rambler:** speak naturally, and it strips out filler like “um” and formats the text before you send it. Android Authority says Rambler works in all regions and languages, unlike some other parts.
- **Proactive suggestions:** for example, a one-tap ETA share when someone asks when you’ll arrive. Google’s footnote says this needs a Pixel 10 (not 10a) or newer, the Gemini app, setup, and age 18+, in select countries.
- **Multi-step tasks:** Gemini can handle groceries, rides, takeout and restaurant reservations inside allowlisted apps. Google says this is now available in Japan, the UK and India.

Android Authority adds two more Pixel 10 items: **Gemini making calls for you** with your own number (US, English) and **Extreme Charging Mode**, which first appeared on the Pixel 11.

Our take: these features ask Gemini to act inside your apps and messages, so check what each one can access when you turn it on. Google itself tells users to “check responses.”

## What’s exclusive to the Pixel 11 series?

**Gemini Call Screen, Pixel Solver and HiLight for Messages.** Google says Call Screen on the Pixel 11 now lets Gemini question unknown callers “in a much more conversational manner” with a live transcript, US only. Android Authority reports **Pixel Solver**, a generative AI math helper in Calculator, is Pixel 11-only. HiLight for Google Messages is limited to the 11 Pro models.

If unknown calls are your main headache and you don’t have a Pixel 11, the free fixes in our guide to stopping spam calls on Android still apply to every phone.

## How much is Pixel Tag?

**$29.99 for one, or $99.99 for a four-pack, on sale from November 11, 2026.** Google says the tag is slim enough for a wallet, the battery “lasts over a year,” and it uses the Find Hub network of more than 1 billion Android devices. Up close, UWB gives direction and distance guidance. Google’s footnote says it works with Android 9 phones in all regions and languages, so you don’t need a Pixel to use it.

## Is the security patch included, and should I install it?

**Yes, and yes.** Google published the Pixel Update Bulletin for November 2026 on November 10. It brings supported Pixels to the **2026-11-05** security patch level, which covers the November Android bulletin plus **166 Pixel-specific vulnerabilities** in our count of Google’s tables (**43 Critical, 87 High, 36 Moderate**). Google doesn’t flag any of them as exploited. Android Authority reports the build is **CP4A.261105.040.A1** on most models and that this release is **Android 17 QPR2**.

The full fix list, build numbers and Pixel 6 status are in our Pixel November update guide. The Android-wide fixes are covered in our Android November 2026 security update explainer.

## How do I get the November Pixel Drop?

**Open Settings > System > Software updates and tap to check.** The rollout is staged, so it may not show up on day one.

1. Back up first if you haven’t recently. Our guide to backing up an Android phone covers the free options.
2. Charge to at least half and connect to Wi-Fi.
3. Go to **Settings > System > Software updates** and install when offered.
4. After the restart, check **Settings > About phone > Android version** . The security update date should read**November 5, 2026** .
5. Update your apps in the Play Store. Google rolls some features out separately from the system update, so a few may appear days later.

Google’s announcement says updates “start rolling out today and will continue over the next several weeks.” Android Authority reports the system update itself should reach phones in phases over about a week.

## FAQ

### When did the November 2026 Pixel Drop come out?

Google announced it on November 10, 2026, and the rollout started the same day. Google says it will continue over the next several weeks, so some phones get it later than others.

### Which Pixel phones get the November Pixel Drop?

Every Pixel Google still supports, from the Pixel 6a up to the Pixel 11 series, including the Pixel Fold and Pixel Tablet. The Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro are not on November’s Pixel update list because their support ended in October 2026. Many features are limited to newer models.

### Does the Pixel 10a get Gemini Intelligence?

No. Google’s footnotes exclude the Pixel 10a from Gemini Intelligence and custom widgets. The 10a still gets the update, the security patch and the features listed for Pixel 6a and newer.

### What is Rambler on Pixel?

Rambler is a voice-typing feature that removes filler words like “um” and formats what you say into clean text before you send it. With the November Pixel Drop, it comes to the Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL and 10 Pro Fold.

### How much does Pixel Tag cost?

Google lists Pixel Tag at $29.99 for one or $99.99 for a four-pack, available from November 11, 2026. It works with Android phones running Android 9 or later.

### Is the November Pixel Drop the same as the November security update?

They arrive in the same system update. The Drop is the feature side; the security side is Google’s November 2026 Pixel Update Bulletin, which brings phones to the 2026-11-05 patch level. Android Authority reports this release is Android 17 QPR2.

### Why don’t I see the new features after updating?

Google stages the rollout over several weeks, and many features are limited by model, country or language. Some also depend on app updates, so update your apps in the Play Store and check again in a few days.
