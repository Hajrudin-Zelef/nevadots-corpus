Galaxy S26 owners in the US can now sign up for the One UI 9.5 beta through the Samsung Members app. Samsung opened the program on November 18 for the Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra. The same beta is also live in the UK, Germany, Poland, South Korea and India.

Samsung’s newsroom post frames the program as “giving users an early look at One UI 9.5 ahead of its official debut”. According to Android Police, One UI 9.5 is still built on Android 17. That makes it a point update to One UI 9, not the jump to Android 18. The launch pattern is unusual too. Android Police notes that Samsung usually starts betas in the US, UK and Korea and adds India and Germany later. This time, all six countries opened on the same day.

If you have been following the build since it first turned up on Samsung’s servers, here is who can join, how to do it, and what is new.

## Which Galaxy S26 Models and Countries Get the One UI 9.5 Beta

Samsung’s announcement names only the Galaxy S26 series:

- **Galaxy S26**
- **Galaxy S26+**
- **Galaxy S26 Ultra**

The beta is open in these six markets:

- **Americas:** United States
- **Europe:** United Kingdom, Germany, Poland
- **Asia:** South Korea, India

No other Galaxy phone or tablet appears in Samsung’s release. Lists circulating online of every device eligible for One UI 9.5 are projections, not Samsung’s word. For now, there is no official timeline for older models like the Galaxy S25 series, which only recently got stable One UI 9.

### What about Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile models?

This is the open question for US buyers. Samsung’s release does not say whether carrier-locked S26 units from Verizon, AT&T or T-Mobile are included, or only unlocked phones. The early coverage from other outlets does not settle it either. So carrier eligibility is not confirmed in either direction.

The practical check is simple. Open Samsung Members on your phone and see whether the One UI 9.5 beta banner appears. For the latest carrier-side update news, see our coverage of the Galaxy S26 November patch on Verizon.

## Before You Join: Read This First

Your Galaxy S26 is probably your daily phone, so weigh the risks before enrolling. Android Police warns that a first beta can be buggy. Installing the beta does not wipe your data. Rolling back to the stable release, however, does wipe the phone completely. Beta slots are also limited, so not everyone who applies is guaranteed a place.

Back up your photos, chats and app data before you start. That way a rollback costs you time rather than files.

## How to Join the One UI 9.5 Beta

1. Open the **Samsung Members** app on your Galaxy S26, S26+ or S26 Ultra.
2. Tap the **One UI 9.5 beta** banner.
3. Complete the on-screen registration steps.
4. Go to **Settings > Software update** and download the beta.

GSMArena reports that the first beta build ends in ZKN and carries the November 2026 security patch. It weighs about 3.6GB, though the size varies by model and region. Android Police, citing early user reports, says a second update of about 540MB shows up after the first install and restart. Keep enough free storage for both.

## What’s New in One UI 9.5

### Camera: Pro mode controls and a zoom bar

Pro and Pro video modes get customizable controls and presets. The default camera gets improved controls and a zoom bar. Samsung’s footnote says the enhanced Pro modes apply only to models that support Pro and Pro video. Phones with the Pro Lite and Pro Video Lite modes are excluded.

### Samsung Health: journaling and medication tracking

Mindful Note adds a journal inside Samsung Health, with free writing or guided prompts. Medications lets you add prescriptions, vitamins and supplements, set reminders and track whether you took them. Samsung says availability may vary by market and that the feature does not give medical advice. It also does not work on Galaxy Watch.

### Accessibility: floating shortcut and hearing-aid controls

A floating accessibility shortcut can sit on screen for quick access. Hearing-aid users get controls in the notification panel. From there you can adjust volume and choose which sounds go to the hearing aids.

### App Lock for individual apps

You can now lock individual apps behind biometrics or your screen lock. While an app is locked, its notification content and widgets stay hidden. That matters if others use or glance at your phone, since a notification preview can reveal as much as the app itself.

## When Will Stable One UI 9.5 Arrive?

Samsung has not announced a stable release date. Android Police expects it around January or February, probably debuting on the Galaxy S27 series first. GSMArena also says the S27 might get it first. Treat both as expectations, not Samsung’s plan. Meanwhile, the stable One UI 9 rollout to older Galaxy phones is still in progress.
