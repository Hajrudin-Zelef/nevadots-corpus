*Editor’s note (13 November): Since we published this article, early adopters have reported multiple issues with the November Pixel Drop. As a result, we’d recommend holding off on installing it for now.*

Pixel owners are in for a treat, as the new November Pixel Drop update has arrived.

Every phone that Google still supports is included (dating back to the Pixel 6a), but there are specific benefits on the Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL and 10 Pro Fold. Sadly, the mid-range Pixel 10a misses out.

Most notably, Google has confirmed that all of last year’s flagships are getting Gemini Intelligence, the umbrella term for its advanced, agentic AI features. Until now, these have been exclusive to 2026 Pixel and Galaxy flagships, while the new Motorola Signature 27 is also confirmed to get the upgrade.

This includes task automation, where Gemini can carry out multi-step tasks on your behalf, such as booking restaurants and trips, ordering items online or hailing rides (stopping before it’s paid or finalised). It also gets you Proactive Suggestions, which deliver smart reply suggestions and seamless sharing of information between apps, and the ability to generate your own widgets.

Henry Burrell / Foundry

But arguably the highlight is Rambler, an advanced speech-to-text feature built directly into Gboard, the default keyboard on Pixel. It removes filler words, reflects any corrections you make and can even switch languages within the same recording.

All of these features are already available on Pixel 11 handsets, though the task automation feature was previously exclusive to the US and South Korea. It’s now been rolled out to the UK, India and Japan.

## Pixel 11 gets two new features

Don’t worry, Pixel 11 owners — you’re not being left out.

The November Pixel Drop also brings two upgrades to Google’s latest generation of flagships. Call Screen, where Gemini can answer an unknown caller on your behalf and ask why they’re calling, will now do so “in a much more conversational manner”. This should reduce the chances of a genuine caller hanging up when they hear the AI-generated voice.

Henry Burrell / Foundry

And HiLight, the much-derided glowing notification light on the back of Pixel 11 Pro phones, now supports texts as well as calls. Favourite contacts can now be assigned a colour, ensuring you never miss a text. Third-party apps are still not supported.

## Every supported Pixel phone gets new features

However, provided your Pixel phone is still getting software updates from Google, the November Pixel Drop is worth installing on paper. If you have a Pixel 6a or later (anything released since May 2022), you’ll get several new features:

- 10 new AI-generated icon styles for home screen apps
- Boosted cross-app search functionality on all models
- Transit Mode with automatic train detection
- Motion Assist to reduce travel sickness
- Unit conversions in Calculator (the AI-powered Ask math tool remains exclusive to the Pixel 11 series)
- Dedicated Family Hub in Settings
- VIP contacts can now display their live location and phone battery percentage

As you can see, it’s a very comprehensive update! While based on Android 17’s QPR2 update, devices running Android 16 will be able to get the new features with a single update — no need to get an earlier version of Android 17 first.

The November Pixel Drop began rolling out on 10 November, but Google warns that the rollout will “continue over the coming weeks in phases”.

With a September Pixel Drop and another key update last month, Google is on a roll with its updates for Pixel phones.
