Samsung has released its August 2026 security patch for Galaxy devices, addressing 56 vulnerabilities, including 8 critical and 30 high-severity issues. Notable CVEs include CVE-2026-25289 and CVE-2026-24084, both published on August 4, 2026. The patch also includes 18 One UI-specific improvements. Meanwhile, Google has rolled out its August update for Pixel phones, fixing a high-severity vulnerability related to the video processing unit. This update affects Pixel 10-series devices and includes several bug fixes. Both updates emphasize the importance of timely patching to mitigate security risks.
