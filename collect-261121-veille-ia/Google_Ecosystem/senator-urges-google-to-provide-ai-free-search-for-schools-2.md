A US Senator on Monday asked Alphabet Inc. to let schools block artificial intelligence without losing access to Google search, adding to mounting concern about how the tech giant has brought AI to schoolchildren.

In a letter to Alphabet Chief Executive Officer Sundar Pichai, Senator Ed Markey, a Democrat from Massachusetts, urged Google to abandon policy changes it has announced for its search product. Last week, the tech giant said that it would turn AI tools, including its search engine, off by default for K-12 student accounts.
