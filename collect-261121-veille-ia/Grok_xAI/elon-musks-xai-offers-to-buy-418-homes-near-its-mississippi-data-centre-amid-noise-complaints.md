## Why is xAI offering to buy homes?

The proposed purchases are focused on Colonial Hills, a residential neighbourhood in Southaven. According to The Guardian, a real estate agent said the company had a target of buying 418 homes. Five homes had reportedly been purchased since October 9. The offers come as xAI expands its AI computing operations around Memphis, Tennessee, and Southaven. The company has been using gas turbines to generate electricity for its data centres.
The reported terms include a release of claims connected to the residents’ legal action. Mississippi Today, which reported on the buyout offers earlier this month, said a letter sent to some homeowners offered to purchase their property in exchange for, among other things, releasing claims linked to a class-action lawsuit. The company has denied the allegations against it and has said it needs property for business purposes. Southaven Mayor Darren Musselwhite has also said the purchases could provide housing for employees and form part of a broader effort to address community concerns.

## Residents complain of sleepless nights

The central dispute concerns noise and vibrations from the gas turbines. Three residents filed a lawsuit in June, alleging that the sound was persistent and had affected their ability to live comfortably in their homes. A separate report by CBS News described residents hearing a constant, aircraft-like roar, including late at night. One homeowner told the broadcaster that noise could be heard from his backyard after 10 pm.
The complaints have added to wider concerns about the environmental impact of the turbines. The National Association for the Advancement of Colored People has also brought legal action over alleged air pollution, arguing that the plant’s operations violate federal clean-air requirements. These are allegations being contested through legal proceedings, not final findings against the company. xAI has said it is trying to be a good neighbour. The company has reportedly installed sound barriers and adjusted power output in response to complaints.
