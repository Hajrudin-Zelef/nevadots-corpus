# Grok 4.7 AI model launched for coding, knowledge work with improved performance

SpaceXAI has introduced Grok 4.7, its latest AI model designed for coding and complex knowledge tasks. The model offers enhanced performance, competitive pricing, and robust safety features, positioning it as a strong tool for developers and engineering teams.

## Highlights

- Grok 4.7 launched by SpaceXAI for coding and knowledge work.
- Offers competitive pricing at $2 input, $6 output per million tokens.
- Features enhanced safeguards, leading in jailbreak resistance and biosafety.
- Available via API, Cursor, Grok Build, and major cloud platforms.
