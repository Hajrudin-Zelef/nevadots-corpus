# Grok Voice 3.0, a voice-activated AI, has been released, topping benchmarks with performance surpassing OpenAI and Google models.


 SpaceXAI announced its voice-to-voice AI model, 'Grok Voice 3.0,' on October 14, 2026. With industry-leading low latency and low cost, SpaceXAI claims it is 'the world's most intelligent voice-to-voice model.'

 **Introducing Grok Voice 3.0 | SpaceXSI**

 SpaceXAI's voice AI model, 'Grok Voice,' is an AI model that can interact with users via voice. ' **Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0,** ' released in July 2026, is a significant improvement over the previous model in terms of voice inference ability, conversational ability, and reliability when using tools, achieving the highest score or fastest results among its comparison targets. It is characterized by being far smarter than other voice dialogue models and minimizing latency because it infers queries while speaking.

 The latest model of Grok Voice, Grok Voice 3.0, was announced on October 14, 2026. Grok Voice supports millions of conversations per day at Tesla and SpaceXAI, and according to SpaceXAI, Grok Voice 3.0 has set a new record for customer support call resolution time.

 In our proprietary tests using Artificial Analysis, the following scores, from top to bottom, represent 'Overall Voice-Related Quality Metrics,' 'Voice Inference,' 'Human Preference,' 'Task Success Rate,' and 'Conversational Dynamics.' Each score is an improvement over the previous model, Grok Voice 2.0, and surpasses GPT-Live-1 (Astra, Medium) and Gemini 3.8 Live (Ext. Thinking, High) in all categories.

 


 Furthermore, in 'τ-Voice,' which measures how well agents can handle support calls with various accents, background noise, and interruptions, Grok Voice 3.0 scored 71.6%. While performing at the same level as GPT-Live-1 (Astra, medium), it had 40% lower latency, and SpaceXAI states that it 'opens new ground in the balance between agent intelligence and responsiveness.'

 


 In the ' **Speech to Speech Index** ,' which analyzes and compiles data from Artificial Analysis's voice-to-voice models, Grok Voice 3.0 currently ranks first with a score of 86.9. It also achieved third place overall in human preference assessments of live voice conversations with the model hidden, first place overall in task success rate, and second place overall in performance benchmarks.

 Furthermore, in the ' **Big Bench Audio** ' benchmark, which measures the inference capabilities of voice dialogue AI, Grok Voice 3.0 recorded an average time of '0.81 seconds' to the first audio, a shorter response time than the 1.34 seconds of GPT-Live-1 (Astra, medium) and the 1.35 seconds of Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking (High), which are voice AI models with comparable performance. At the time of writing, Grok Voice 3.0 and Grok Voice 2.0 are the only models in the top 5 of the Speech to Speech Index to have recorded a response time of less than 1 second.

 Grok Voice 3.0 is used for hands-free requests in Tesla vehicles and in Starlink's call center, and will be available via the Voice Agent API from October 14, 2026. The API fee is $0.08 per minute (approximately 12 yen). Since this is the same price as Grok Voice 2.0, agents using grok-voice-latest will be automatically switched to Grok Voice 3.0 without any changes required.
