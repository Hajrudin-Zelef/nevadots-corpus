## Update (November 18, 2026, 13:00 UTC): The Verge frames halt as H200 reversal after White House approval fight

The Verge reported November 18, 2026 that interim CEO **Colette Kress** said on the earnings call **licensed Hopper data center products**, including the **H200** line, are **no longer shipping to China** and are **excluded from guidance** — despite **White House approval** last December for those exports and **Jensen Huang's** years of lobbying for that lane.

The piece adds **Reuters-reported context** explainx.ai has **not** independently verified in a filing: roughly **600,000 H200 chips** sat in **Hong Kong** after **Beijing withheld large-scale import approval**, with China seeking to **reduce dependence on U.S. silicon**. Readers should keep the **November 17 newsroom statement** as primary for **Q4 zero China data-center compute outlook**; treat The Verge's narrative as **political and supply-chain color** around the same call.

## Update (November 18, 2026, 07:30 UTC): Kress lifts FY2028 growth outlook to 75% on supply confidence

Kress framed the raise as **stronger demand plus additional supply** Nvidia can deliver — **not** a return of **China data-center compute** into the model (the **Q4 outlook still assumes zero** on that line).
