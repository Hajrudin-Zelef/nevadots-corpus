Sellers must sign a release that bars them from recovering damages in a class action over noise from the Southaven plant.

Elon Musk’s xAI, through its subsidiary MZX Tech, is offering to buy hundreds of homes near its data center power plant in Southaven, Mississippi, and 18 homeowners have reportedly agreed to sell, The Verge reported, citing The Guardian.

The offers require sellers to sign a separate release agreement that would bar them from recovering damages in a federal class action over noise from the plant, according to a letter obtained by the Mississippi Free Press. “After executing the Release Agreement, you will be unable to recover any damages or other relief from the … lawsuit,” the letter says.

## Class action covers more than 10,000 residents

Three residents filed the suit against xAI and MZX Tech in June, alleging persistent noise and vibration from the plant’s gas turbines harm sleep, quality of life and property values. The complaint says more than 10,000 class members live in the affected area. MZX denies the allegations.
