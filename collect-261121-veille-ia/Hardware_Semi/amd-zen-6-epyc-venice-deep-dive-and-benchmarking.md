# AMD Zen 6 EPYC ‘Venice’ deep dive and benchmarking

Tom's Hardware · By Jake Roach ·

## The Brief · written from 4 sources

- AMD has announced availability of EPYC 9006 "Venice" series Socket SP7 processors, with systems beginning to ship from OEM/ODM partners on 2026-11-10.
- Phoronix published benchmarks of the 256-core EPYC 9996, including a dual-socket configuration with 512 cores / 1024 threads.
- Phoronix also tested the EPYC 9686F, a 96-core Zen 6 part with clocks up to 5.3GHz.
- AMD also announced the EPYC Embedded 9006 Series SP7 processors.

### Why it matters

Venice is the first server CPU family built on the Zen 6 architecture, so developers and operators planning server capacity or benchmarking workloads now have shipping hardware to evaluate.

### Who’s affected

- Server and data center operators evaluating SP7 platforms
- Developers running highly parallel workloads on x86 servers
