StepFun Step 5 Preview: A Price-Disrupting Agentic Model

Chinese AI company StepFun (阶跃星辰) unveiled its Step 5 Preview API on September 20. The model has 600 billion total parameters but uses a sparse mixture-of-experts architecture that activates only 27 billion parameters per token when generating answers. It reads up to 1 million tokens of context at once and accepts both text and images.

Pricing is $1 per million input tokens, $0.05 on a cache hit, and $2.7 per million output tokens. Its Artificial Analysis intelligence score is 44, on par with Moonshot's Kimi K3 Max, and one-seventh the cost of GPT-5.6 Sol. The company said it plans to release the model weights on October 15.

The competitive focus in China's AI industry is shifting from model scale to agentic commercialization. StepFun is targeting coding, financial analysis, and long-running agentic tasks.

Zhipu GLM-5.3-FlashX: Commercial Inference on 100,000 Domestic Accelerators

Zhipu (智谱) released GLM-5.3-FlashX on September 18. It generates 200 tokens per second. The model is identical to the existing GLM-5.3-Flash, but inference configuration changes running on 100,000 domestically made accelerators raised its speed by 3.2 times.

The model has 320 billion total parameters with 18 billion active, and accepts 1 million tokens of context. An AI agent (Infra Agent) handled the optimization work. Once engineers set a goal, the agent diagnosed root causes, proposed kernel patches and stack modifications, and applied them after testing.

This is a real-world case of running a top-tier inference service entirely on domestic accelerators without Nvidia GPUs.

(September 18, 2026, Pandaily, SCMP)

AI Semiconductors and Computing Infrastructure

CXMT Begins Mass Production of Fifth-Generation DRAM

CXMT (长鑫存储) announced mass production of its fifth-generation DRAM platform, the G5, at the Hefei World Manufacturing Convention on September 20. Without EUV equipment, it used quadruple patterning to shrink the active-area half-pitch to 11.95 nanometers. The capacitor aspect ratio is 45:1, and cell height is 6,762 nanometers.

The number of dies per wafer rose more than 50 percent compared to the previous generation. Two types of 24Gb LPDDR5X have entered mass production: a 496-ball package for smartphones and a 245-ball package for mobile devices.

According to Counterpoint, CXMT's global DRAM revenue share in the second quarter reached 10 percent, ranking fourth behind Samsung (38-39 percent), SK Hynix (25 percent), and Micron (23 percent). The combined share of these three companies fell below 90 percent for the first time in a decade.

CXMT has also started risk production of HBM3E, targeting mass production in 2027.

(September 21, 2026, TechNode, SCMP, Seoul Economic Daily, XenoSpectrum)

Huawei Unveils Ascend 960 Supernode

Huawei unveiled the Ascend 960 supernode at Huawei Connect 2026 in Shanghai on September 17, becoming the first in the industry to incorporate Near-Packaged Optics (NPO) optical engines.

The Ascend 960DT training card delivers 2 PFLOPS at FP8 and 4 PFLOPS at FP4. The inference-oriented Ascend 960PR delivers 8 PFLOPS at FP4. High-bandwidth memory is 288GB with 9.6TB/s access bandwidth. A single supernode can scale up to 4,096 chips.

The 960DT is now scheduled for the first quarter of 2027, three quarters ahead of its original timeline. Huawei also outlined a one-chip-per-year roadmap, with the Ascend 970 planned for 2028 and the Ascend 980 for 2029.

Huawei said more than 1,000 sets of Ascend 950 supernodes have already been deployed. It also unveiled the Peerium architecture, aimed at treating one million processors as a single computer, though performance validation has not yet been presented.

(September 17, 2026, Huawei, 21st Century Business Herald, WCCFTech)

Telecom, Networking, and Edge AI

Huawei's Stellar AI Network 2.0 and HiONE Optical Engine

At Connect 2026, Huawei unveiled Stellar AI Network Solution 2.0, built around its self-designed Ascend A5/A6 chips inside the SF9300 series switches. A two-tier, multi-plane architecture eliminates the core layer, cutting deployment costs by 30 percent and reducing end-to-end latency from 20 microseconds to 11 microseconds.

HiONE is the industry's first NPO optical engine to reach mass production. Its 36-channel, 3.2T optical engine delivers 7.2Tbps. It replaces 48,000 conventional pluggable optical modules with 5,500 units, cutting power consumption by 550kW.

Huawei also introduced the concept of an Agent Communication Network (ACN), which handles identity verification, session management, and guardrail blocking (a 95 percent block rate) between agents at the network level. The company said it maintains 95 percent efficiency even over 1,000-kilometer distances on WAN links.

Huawei's vertical-integration strategy, tying together AI computing infrastructure and networking, is taking shape.

(September 17-18, 2026, Huawei, PR Newswire, Telecompaper)

AI Safety Governance Framework 3.0 Released

This version is the first to formally codify risks from autonomous AI agent behavior. It classifies risks into five categories: planning errors, privilege escalation, memory tampering, identity spoofing, and tool misuse. Refusing shutdown or concealing capabilities is also classified as a risk.

While earlier frameworks focused on controlling model outputs, version 3.0 shifts its weight toward controlling agents' actions in the physical world, extending its regulatory scope to embodied intelligence and computing infrastructure.

US-China Tech Rivalry

Bessent and He Lifeng Hold Eight-Hour Working-Level Talks

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng held roughly eight hours of talks at JPMorgan's New York headquarters on September 20. US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer also participated.

The US proposed a mutual notification system for major, national-security-level AI incidents. Bessent said moving from opacity to transparency between the world's number one and number two AI powers matters. Both sides agreed to establish an AI dialogue channel and form a working group.

Export controls on advanced AI chips and semiconductor equipment were kept off the negotiating table. Greer stated flatly that export controls are not part of this proposal. There was no progress on rare earths or critical minerals either.

The tariff truce expires November 10, and whether it will be extended is expected to be decided at the summit. A Trump-Xi summit is scheduled for September 24 at the White House in Washington. Business figures from BYD, CATL, and Xiaomi are expected to accompany Xi Jinping.

Pentagon AI Misinformation Incident: Chinese Ship Intercept Halted at the Last Moment

CNN reported exclusively on September 18 that during this spring's Iran conflict, an analyst at US Special Operations Command Pacific asked an AI chatbot about the cargo manifest of a Chinese vessel sailing through the Middle East. The chatbot mixed open-source information with classified signals intelligence and concluded the cargo included nuclear weapons components. The analyst used AI to generate a standard-format intelligence report and distributed it.

The military immediately moved toward an interdiction operation. Armed personnel prepared to board, and aircraft took off. Just before the operation, another analyst reviewed the report again and confirmed it was entirely false, and the operation was halted.

The incident showed that an AI-generated intelligence report could nearly trigger military action. It is also part of the backdrop for discussions of an incident-notification system in the US-China AI safety dialogue.

(September 18, 2026, CNN, The Defense Post, Rolling Stone)
