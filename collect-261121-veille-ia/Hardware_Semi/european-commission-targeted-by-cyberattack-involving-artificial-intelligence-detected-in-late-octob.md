*19/11/26 (Agence Europe)* – “*I can confirm that the Commission has recently taken necessary steps in response to a detected cyber campaign*”, said Thomas Regnier, one of the institution’s spokespersons, on Thursday 19 November, confirming a report by *Politico*. He added that the incident, which involved an AI tool, had been detected on 22 October. Seeking to reassure, the spokesperson said that, at this stage, there was no evidence of “*a data breach affecting Commission staff as a whole*”. As for how the attack was carried out, it reportedly involved a “*publicly available*” AI tool, *ARTEX*, according to *Politico*. It is a next-generation penetration-testing tool repurposed from its original use. The spokesperson did not comment on the perpetrators of the cyberattack or on its scale, which is “*very difficult to determine (...) while the investigation is still ongoing*”. *(LD)*
