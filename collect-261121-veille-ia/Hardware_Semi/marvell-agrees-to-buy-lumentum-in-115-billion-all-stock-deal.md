# Marvell Agrees to Buy Lumentum in $115 Billion All-Stock Deal

Marvell Technology MRVL agreed to buy Lumentum LITE, which makes lasers and optical components for AI data centers, in an all-stock deal valued at $115 billion including debt. Lumentum shares fell 2.93% and Marvell shares fell 0.96% in premarket.

Lumentum shareholders will receive 4.5 Marvell shares for each share they own, valued at $1,132, a 23.9% premium to Lumentum's unaffected close on October 28, and will own about 33% of the combined company. The deal carries an equity value of $102.54 billion and is expected to close in the first half of 2028. Marvell expects more than $1 billion in annual run-rate cost synergies and said the deal should add to non-GAAP EPS in its first year after closing.

CEO Matt Murphy said AI infrastructure is shifting toward optical connectivity, an opportunity that comes along once in a generation. Marvell said the deal expands the combined company's addressable market to $550 billion. Separately, Lumentum expects fiscal second-quarter revenue of $1.58 billion to $1.63 billion, above the $1.48 billion LSEG estimate. Nvidia NVDA invested $2 billion in each company earlier this year.
