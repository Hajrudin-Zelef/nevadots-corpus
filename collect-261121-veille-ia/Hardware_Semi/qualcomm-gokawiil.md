The Bluetooth SIG has officially adopted a High Data Throughput (HDT) specification that raises the maximum physical-layer data rate for Bluetooth Low Energy from 2Mbps to 7.5Mbps, exceeding even Bluetooth Enhanced Data Rate's 3Mbps ceiling. The SIG says the extra bandwidth could support uses like extended reality, spatial computing, AI-enabled devices, and standardized high-resolution or lossless audio. Qualcomm's FastConnect 8800 chipset already claims HDT support, though it was built against a draft version of the spec, and the feature requires entirely new radio hardware in both phones and accessories.

Metalenz, the maker of flat metasurface camera lenses, has acquired PxE, a company whose HoloCoder technology patterns microscopic structures directly onto image sensors to capture light data that conventional cameras discard. Terms of the deal were not disclosed. Metalenz CEO Rob Devlin says PxE's technology isn't yet on the market but could integrate with Metalenz's existing supply chain and imaging pipeline.

A startup cofounded by Franck De Girolami has unveiled Kora, a standalone Linux-based desktop operating system designed to let AI models access a user's files and context directly at the machine level. Kora runs on x86-64 PCs today and will come to Qualcomm Snapdragon PCs next year, with compatibility layers for Windows apps, native productivity apps, an SDK for developers, and a gaming layer called GameFrame derived from Playtron's GameOS.

Following a market slide tied to voter backlash against local data center projects in the midterm elections, executives from Arm, OpenAI and Qualcomm pushed back on fears of a lasting hit to AI infrastructure spending. Arm's Rene Haas called the sell-off reaction short-term thinking, OpenAI's Sarah Friar pledged continued investment and community engagement including a promise not to raise electricity bills, and Qualcomm's Cristiano Amon said data centers are only a small part of his company's business.

SpaceX has outlined plans to make Starlink Mobile a major US mobile carrier, partnering with Qualcomm to deploy cellular technology across tens of thousands of 5G sites starting in 2027. Reports indicate SpaceX is also in talks to acquire Boost Mobile and could tap roughly 80,000 existing cell sites nationwide to expand its network.

BMW announced plans to extend its navigation-guided, partially automated driving system beyond highways into urban environments, launching in Germany in 2027 before expanding to other markets. The Level 2 system will assist with lane changes, turns, yielding, and roundabouts in cities, though drivers must keep hands on the wheel and remain ready to take control, unlike the hands-free highway mode.

Qualcomm Technologies commissioned a report surveying more than 12,000 people across the U.S., U.K., Germany, India, Brazil, and China about personal AI and intelligent wearables. The survey found respondents are less interested in standalone devices and more drawn to ecosystems of connected tools—smartwatches, smart glasses, hearables, rings, pins, and pendants—working together around an AI agent that understands the user's context.

Google and XREAL have released XREAL AURA, described as the first wired XR glasses built on Android XR. The glasses weigh under 95 grams and offload processing to a connected compute puck containing the battery and Qualcomm's new Snapdragon Reality Elite platform, offering a 70-degree field of view and Gemini AI integration across real-world and app content.
