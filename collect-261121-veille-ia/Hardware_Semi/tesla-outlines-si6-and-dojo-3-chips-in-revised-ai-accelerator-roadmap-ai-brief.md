# Tesla outlines SI6 and Dojo 3 chips in revised AI accelerator roadmap

Tesla outlines a revised accelerator roadmap that includes its SI6 inference chip and Dojo 3 training chip, reports Tom's Hardware. The company is also replacing artificial intelligence branding with super intelligence and renaming its accelerators under SI labels.

Dojo 3, also called D3, will combine multiple chiplets, advanced packaging and stacked high bandwidth memory. The design differs from Dojo D1, which used wafer scale packaging and relied on on chip SRAM. Tesla has not disclosed D3's compute performance, memory capacity, manufacturing process or launch schedule.

The roadmap places SI6 in 2029 to 2030 and lists 80 times the compute performance and nine times the memory of SI4. SI4 is Tesla's current vehicle inference processor and the baseline for its Full Self Driving system, but the company does not publish its absolute compute performance.
