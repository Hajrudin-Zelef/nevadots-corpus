## Executive Summary

- •Tesla disclosed a revamped silicon roadmap that rebrands its AI inference chips to "SI" (Super Intelligence) and revives its in-house Dojo training line with Dojo 3.
- •Tesla expects the 2nm-class SI6 inference chip, due in 2029–2030 under a Samsung Foundry deal through 2033, to deliver 80X the compute of SI4 (HW4) and 144 GB of memory.
- •Dojo 3 shifts from wafer-scale SRAM to 2.5D/3D chiplets with stacked HBM and space optimization, though Tesla has not disclosed its compute specs, process node, or release schedule.

Ditching Dojo D1's custom wafer-scale SRAM for stacked HBM chiplets is a quiet admission that fighting industry memory standards wasn't worth the capacity limits, while SI6's 80X target in 2029–2030 trails the ~50 PFLOPS Nvidia's Rubin ships today.
