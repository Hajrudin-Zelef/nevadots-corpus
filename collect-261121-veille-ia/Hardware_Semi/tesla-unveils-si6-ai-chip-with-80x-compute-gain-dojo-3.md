## TL;DR

Tesla has revealed a sweeping AI chip roadmap under a new "Super Intelligence" brand, promising 80X compute gains by 2029-2030 and a revived Dojo training chip designed for space deployment.

## What happened

- Tesla disclosed its **SI (Super Intelligence) accelerator roadmap** , renaming all existing chips from HW/AI to SI monikers, with Elon Musk also rebranding SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI.
- The **SI6 inference chip** (6th generation) targets 17,280 to 46,080 TOPS, an 80X compute and 9X memory improvement over the current SI4 (HW4), with a target arrival of 2029 to 2030.
- SI5, the next step, is already **nearing production** on TSMC 3nm or Samsung 2nm, with an estimated 40X gain over SI4 and up to 96 GB of memory.
- **Dojo D3** is back: after Musk cancelled the wafer-scale Dojo project in August 2025 and declared exclusive Nvidia use in August 2026, Tesla is now developing a chiplet-based D3 with 2.5D and 3D packaging plus stacked HBM, abandoning the SRAM-centric system-on-wafer design.
- D3 is explicitly **optimized for space, training, and scaling** , strongly implying radiation-hardened SKUs for orbital or extraterrestrial data centers.

## Why it matters

- **Nvidia's Rubin GPU ships today** at 50 NVFP4 PFLOPS for inference; SI6 targets a comparable 17 to 46 PFLOPS range but arrives 3 to 4 years later, keeping Tesla dependent on third-party silicon in the near term.
- The **Dojo reversal is strategically significant** : two separate cancellations followed by a redesigned revival signals Tesla still wants training independence, even if the timeline and architecture have shifted dramatically.
- Switching Dojo D3 to **stacked HBM from on-chip SRAM** aligns Tesla with industry-standard memory scaling but trades away the ultra-high bandwidth advantage that made the original Dojo concept distinctive.
- The **space-optimized D3** directly supports SpaceX and xAI ambitions for orbital compute, a market with no established AI chip incumbent and significant first-mover value.
- Rebranding to "Super Intelligence" is not cosmetic: it signals a **product positioning shift** aimed at differentiating Tesla silicon from commodity AI accelerators as the inference market commoditizes.

## What to watch next

- **SI5 production launch** : its ramp on TSMC 3nm or Samsung 2nm will be the first real proof point for Tesla's in-house chip ambitions and a signal of foundry partnership health.
- **D3 compute and schedule disclosure** : Tesla has revealed architecture intent but no performance numbers, process node, or timeline, making the next roadmap update critical for assessing whether D3 is real or aspirational.
- **Nvidia dependency duration** : if Tesla ships SI5 at scale before 2028, it reduces reliance on Nvidia hardware that Musk committed to for both terrestrial and extraterrestrial data centers as recently as August 2026.
