# What is a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)?

A forward deployed engineer (FDE) is a senior software engineer whom their employer — a software vendor, a model provider or an IT services firm — places at a client to turn a business need into an AI system running in production. Over several months, the FDE diagnoses, builds, deploys, measures adoption and feeds what they learn back to the product team.

## Origin: Palantir and the Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE)

Palantir popularised the role and, as of 6 November 2026, still recruits for it under the title Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE). The engineer works at the client for a set period, alongside the client’s teams, and configures the employer’s platform (Foundry, Gotham) for that client’s problems alone. The English Wikipedia article gives the same definition: a customer-facing software engineer, employed by the company that makes the product, who deploys that product at the client.

The model inverts the classic software vendor’s. A product engineer builds one capability for every customer; a forward deployed engineer builds several for a single customer, and what they learn in the field flows back into the product.

## Variants: AI FDE, FDSE, Forward Deployed Engineering

-  **AI FDE** : a forward deployed engineer who specialises in generative AI and agents (in
        French,*FDE IA* ). Vendors hire under the plain title Forward Deployed Engineer. The term is
        ambiguous: Palantir also gives the name AI FDE to a product, a conversational agent that drives Foundry,
        which its documentation, read on 6 November 2026, defines as*“an interactive agent that operates
        Foundry for you”* .
-  **FDSE** : Forward Deployed Software Engineer, the historical title, still in use at Palantir.
-  **Forward Deployed Engineering** : the practice or the organisation, as opposed to the
        person. AWS and Microsoft use this form.
-  **FDEs at Anthropic** : since October 2026, Anthropic also abbreviates “Frontier Deployed
        Engineers” to FDEs. On an Anthropic page, the acronym alone can mean the role or the badge.

## Forward or Frontier: two FDEs at Anthropic

On 2 October 2026, Anthropic announced the Claude Frontier Academy: $100 million to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027. Its first programme, the Frontier Deployed Engineer residency, starts with a four-day in-person intensive (Claude Resident Engineer badge), followed by twelve weeks leading a real Claude deployment inside the engineer’s own organisation (Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer badge, the first ones expected in early 2027). The first cohorts, in San Francisco, New York and London, bring together engineers from Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and Novo Nordisk.

Entry is by nomination only. As of 6 November 2026, the residency is in early access with a small group of customers and partners chosen by Anthropic. An organisation asks its Anthropic account team, or a partner its Partner Account Manager, whether it is eligible: Anthropic publishes no eligibility criteria, neither for customers nor for Claude Partner Network partners.

The role keeps its name. As of 6 November 2026, Anthropic is hiring Forward Deployed Engineers in Paris, Munich and London. Forward names a role: the engineer a vendor sends to the client. Frontier names a badge: the title Anthropic awards to an engineer who works at one of its customers or partners and has completed the residency.

## Partner FDE programmes: AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft

AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft opened the model to their partners between June and October 2026; Anthropic chose a services firm and a badge programme.

-  **AWS Partner-Led Forward Deployed Engineering** (30 June 2026): AWS extends to its most
        strategic partners the $1 billion investment behind its own FDE organisation,*“not as a
        certification, not as a training program”* . On 1 October 2026, AWS added three capability pathways
        (Ground, Orchestrate, Prove) and two credentials, FDE Validated and FDE Advanced. They are badges, not
        certifications, and each requires an active AWS certification: Associate or Professional for the first,
        AWS Certified Generative AI Developer - Professional for the second.
-  **Google Cloud Partner Forward Deployed Engineer** : Google engineers assigned to a strategic
        partner to*“co-build with a strategic AI partner’s own FDE teams”* . As of 6 November 2026, the
        open posting is titled “Partner Forward Deployed Engineer, Strategic Partners”, in Sydney and Melbourne.
-  **Microsoft Frontier Company** (2 July 2026): $2.5 billion and 6,000 industry experts and
        engineers embedded with customers, led by Rodrigo Kede Lima, with Accenture, Capgemini, EY, KPMG and PwC
        as FDE partners. Judson Althoff writes that the initiative*“goes beyond what has been labeled as
        Forward Deployed Engineering”* . Despite the word “Frontier”, it has no link with Anthropic’s badge.
-  **Anthropic** : on 4 May 2026, Anthropic, Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman and Goldman
        Sachs announced an AI services firm for mid-sized companies, starting with those in the three investors’
        portfolios; Goldman Sachs presents it as a way to widen their access to forward-deployed engineers. The
        announcement gives no investment figure. For partners, Anthropic works through the Claude Frontier
        Academy and the Partner Account Manager.

## What an FDE does, and with what

An FDE spends several days a week on site, over cycles of several months. They diagnose the need with the people who run the process and those who decide; code the architecture, integrations, data pipelines, agents and observability; see the system through to adoption and a measured return; then pass the patterns back to the product team.

- **Software engineering** : Python or TypeScript in production, tests, CI/CD, code review.
- **Cloud** : GCP, AWS or Azure, infrastructure as code, security, FinOps.
- **Generative AI** : foundation models, RAG, evaluation, cost and latency.
- **Agents** : MCP, orchestration, guardrails, traceability.
- **Client posture** : dealing with executives, business sense, autonomy, mobility.

## FDE, consultant, sales engineer, solution engineer

A consultant delivers a diagnosis, a recommendation or a master plan, and the client executes it. An FDE delivers a system that runs, and is judged on that system. A sales engineer or solution engineer works during the sales cycle: they demonstrate the product and size the solution. Once the contract is signed, the FDE takes over, settles in at the client for months and codes the integration with the client’s systems. An engineer on a staff-augmentation contract carries out, inside the client’s team, work the client specifies; the FDE owns the result, from scoping to adoption.

## FAQ

- What is the difference between an FDE and a consultant?
- A consultant delivers a diagnosis, a recommendation or a plan, and the client carries it out afterwards, with or without them. A forward deployed engineer joins the client’s teams, writes the code, puts the system into production and stays until it is adopted. They are measured on the software in service and on its actual use.
- Are Forward Deployed Engineer and Frontier Deployed Engineer the same job?
- No. Forward names a role: the engineer a software vendor or an IT services firm sends to the client. Frontier names a badge: the one Anthropic awards, through the Claude Frontier Academy announced on 2 October 2026, to a customer or partner engineer who has completed its twelve-week residency. Anthropic still hires Forward Deployed Engineers.
- What does AI FDE mean?
- AI FDE, like the French form FDE IA, refers to a forward deployed engineer who specialises in generative AI and agents: they install models, RAG pipelines and agents in the client’s systems and take them into production. Vendors hire under the plain title Forward Deployed Engineer. At Palantir, AI FDE is also the name of a product, an agent that operates Foundry.
- What is an FDSE at Palantir?
- FDSE stands for Forward Deployed Software Engineer, the title Palantir still uses in its job postings as of 6 November 2026. The engineer works at the client, alongside its teams, and configures Foundry or Gotham for that client’s problems, with the code reviews and production oversight of any engineering role.
- Can a partner have its FDEs recognised by AWS or Anthropic?
- At AWS, since 1 October 2026, two credentials, FDE Validated and FDE Advanced, recognise the Ground, Orchestrate and Prove pathways; each requires an active AWS certification. At Anthropic, the Claude Frontier Academy residency is by nomination, with no published criteria: an organisation asks its account team or its Partner Account Manager whether it is eligible.
