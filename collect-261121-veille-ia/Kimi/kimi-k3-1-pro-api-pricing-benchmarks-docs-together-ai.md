## About model

Kimi K3.1 Pro is the first model in Moonshot AI's K3.1 series, which Moonshot describes as its most capable 3T-class open-weight model to date. It is a post-trained version of Kimi K3, trained with substantially larger post-training compute through joint reinforcement learning across hundreds of downstream domains, with Moonshot reporting consistent gains in every domain. The model covers the full range of coding work, from quick fixes in real codebases to full-project builds and long-horizon engineering, and brings stronger composition and polish to front-end pages, Three.js scenes, and games built with Unity, Unreal Engine, and Godot. It infers shape, proportions, and spatial relationships from a text brief, sketch, or reference image to produce usable 3D models, and handles complete financial workflows with targeted improvements to factual accuracy. The model supports a 1M-token context window. Available on Together AI.
