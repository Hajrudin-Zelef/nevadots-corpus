# Discord Rewrote Its App in Rust and Used 70% Less RAM. Electron Still Isn’t the Villain.

Discord has a new desktop app, and it’s not built on Electron.

Windows Latest got a look at it this week. It’s written in Rust, it shows up in Task Manager as “Discord Phoenix,” and with two accounts signed in at once it was sitting at 290 MB of RAM.

The current Electron client, the author says, usually idles at 1 to 2 GB on their machines.

In a cold-start clip on two Windows 11 ThinkPads, the Rust client’s window appeared about a second after the double-click and loaded the friends list while the Electron one was still on its “Starting” screen.

Discord’s CTO, Stanislav Vishnevskiy, put it like this. “Clicking around feels instant, I can’t go back.”
