Microsoft said on Nov. 17, 2026 that Microsoft-Decision-1.1 is generally available in Foundry and through OpenRouter and Vercel. The company said it is a Gemma 4 26B mixture-of-experts model that accepts images and is more than twice as fast as Microsoft-Decision-1, with Foundry input at $0.042 per million tokens on Global Standard, $0.046 in the US data zone, and $0.05 in the EU and APAC data zones, and with output tokens free. Microsoft said more than 4,000 customers have used it since a public preview, and its Nov. 18 Foundry post reports 78.8 percent average accuracy on 102 company benchmarks.
