Nvidia’s Vera Rubin NVL72 systems are operating in Azure data centers, with access to virtual machines powered by the systems expanding during Microsoft Ignite, extending preview access to the AI infrastructure.

Nvidia described the systems as supporting demanding AI inference, agent applications and large-scale model training and inference.
