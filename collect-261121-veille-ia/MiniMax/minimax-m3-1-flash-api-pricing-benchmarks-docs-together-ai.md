## About model

MiniMax M3.1 Flash is a native multimodal Mixture-of-Experts model from MiniMax for coding, agentic workflows, computer use, and cybersecurity. With roughly 428B total parameters and 23B active per token, it accepts text, image, and video input across a context window of up to 1M tokens. Building on MiniMax M3, it improves both task performance and inference efficiency: MiniMax Sparse Attention now runs throughout the model, quantization-aware training covers the routed experts and attention paths to reduce memory footprint, and a DSpark speculative decoding head ships with the release. The model carries complex, multi-step professional workflows through to completion, understands real codebases, fixes bugs, and builds features, and reasons over images and charts to produce polished frontends and presentations. Released under the MiniMax Community License. Available on Together AI.
