# MiniMax-M3.1-Flash: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

MiniMax-M3.1-Flash is amongst the leading models in intelligence and well priced when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also slower than average per task and very verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

MiniMax-M3.1-Flash scores 29 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 23). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 161M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 101M.

Pricing for MiniMax-M3.1-Flash is $0.15 per 1M input tokens (competitively priced, median: $0.53) and $0.60 per 1M output tokens (competitively priced, median: $2.10). On average, it costs $0.49 per task to evaluate MiniMax-M3.1-Flash on the Intelligence Index.

At 19.3 minutes per Intelligence Index task, MiniMax-M3.1-Flash is slower than average compared with the median of 15.0 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
