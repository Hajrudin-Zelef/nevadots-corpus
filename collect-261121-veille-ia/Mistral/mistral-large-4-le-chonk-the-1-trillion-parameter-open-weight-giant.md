# Mistral Large 4 Le Chonk: The 1-Trillion Parameter Open-Weight Giant

- Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk) is a 1.05-trillion parameter multimodal model with open weights.
- It features a 1-million-token context window and excels in cybersecurity, finance, and coding.
- Trained on 3,800-4,000 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in European data centers for AI sovereignty.
- Offers three reasoning modes (low, medium, high) to balance performance and inference costs.

The landscape of open-weight artificial intelligence has shifted with the arrival of Mistral Large 4, internally dubbed **Le Chonk**. This is not merely an incremental update but a massive leap in scale and capability, positioning the French AI lab as a primary challenger to both closed-source giants and the dominant Chinese open-weight models. By releasing the weights of a 1.05-trillion parameter model, Mistral is providing enterprises with a tool that rivals the most sophisticated frontier models while maintaining the flexibility of self-hosting.

## A trillion parameters for sovereign control

At the heart of Le Chonk is a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture. While the total parameter count reaches 1.05 trillion, the model is designed for efficiency, activating only 49 to 52 billion parameters per inference. This balance allows the model to maintain a massive knowledge base without requiring impossible compute resources for every single query. The technical ambition is matched by the infrastructure; Mistral trained the model from scratch using between 3,800 and 4,000 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs located within its own European data centers.

This commitment to European infrastructure is a strategic move toward Sovereign AI. By controlling the entire lineage from inception to release, Mistral ensures that organizations can deploy the model within their own firewalls. This eliminates the risk of provider-level refusals—a critical issue in cybersecurity where a closed-model provider might block a legitimate vulnerability research request—and prevents sensitive corporate data from ever leaving the company's private cloud.

## Breaking benchmarks in coding and cybersecurity

Mistral Large 4 was engineered with a specific focus on agentic behavior—the ability of an AI to plan, research, and execute complex tasks autonomously. The results are evident in the benchmarks. On the Terminal-Bench 4.0, which evaluates agentic coding within a terminal environment, the model climbed from 28% to 41% following three weeks of additional reinforcement learning. This puts Le Chonk on par with top-tier competitors like the Chinese GLM-5.3, though Mistral claims it achieves these results while using 47% fewer tokens per task on average.

The model's defensive security capabilities are equally potent. It scores 82% on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index and 93% on Cybench. These capabilities extend to log inspection, code auditing, and the identification of newly published exploits. Mistral emphasizes that providing open weights for such a powerful cyber-tool is essential for incident response, as losing access to a capability mid-incident due to a third-party API outage could represent a catastrophic security risk for an enterprise.

## Multimodality and the million-token window

Le Chonk is natively multimodal, integrating a 1.6-billion-parameter vision encoder that allows it to process images and text simultaneously. This capability is particularly relevant for industrial applications. For instance, the model can analyze manufacturing blueprints, process scanned invoices, or interpret product catalogs with high precision. This visual grounding allows it to surpass even some frontier closed models in specific spatial and visual tasks.

Complementing the vision capabilities is a massive 1-million-token context window. This is currently the largest window available in an open-weight model, enabling the AI to ingest entire codebases, lengthy legal archives, or massive financial reports in a single prompt. For businesses, this means the model can maintain coherence across thousands of pages of documentation without the need for complex external retrieval systems.

## Optimizing costs through reasoning modes

To address the inherent cost of running a trillion-parameter model, Mistral has introduced three distinct reasoning modes: low, medium, and high. Instead of requiring developers to engineer complex prompts to trigger deeper thinking, users can simply select the mode that matches the task's complexity.

Open source is essential to ensure state-of-the-art AI does not end up concentrated in the hands of a few actors, stated co-founder and CEO Arthur Mensch.


This tiered approach allows a company to use the low-reasoning mode for quick, everyday tasks to save on token costs, while reserving the high-reasoning mode for deep work on extremely complex problems. This flexibility is a key selling point for SMBs who want frontier-level performance without the unpredictable costs associated with closed-API subscriptions.

## Vertical performance in finance and law

Beyond coding, Mistral Large 4 demonstrates significant strength in highly regulated professional sectors. In the financial domain, it scored 67% on the Finch benchmark, which tests enterprise finance and accounting workflows. This makes it capable of detecting financial anomalies and generating accounting reports at an enterprise grade. In the legal sector, it achieved a 15% task-pass rate on Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark, proving its utility in analyzing complex contracts and legal documentation.

The model's linguistic versatility further supports its global application, having been trained on over 160 languages, including every official language of the European Union. This ensures that the model is not just a tool for English-speaking markets but a truly global asset for multinational corporations operating across diverse regulatory and linguistic landscapes.

## Strategic implications for US and UK enterprises

For businesses in the USA and UK, the release of Mistral Large 4 introduces a critical alternative to the current duopoly of US-based closed models and the rise of Chinese open-weight models. In the US, where data privacy concerns often clash with the convenience of cloud AI, the ability to download weights and run Le Chonk on local hardware provides a path to compliance with strict internal data governance policies.

In the UK, where the regulatory approach to AI has leaned toward a more pro-innovation, sector-specific framework, Le Chonk offers a way for firms in the City of London to automate financial and legal workflows without sending sensitive client data to external servers. The model's efficiency—specifically its ability to rival the GLM family while using fewer tokens—means that UK and US firms can scale their AI agentic workflows with lower operational overhead. By providing a Western-developed model that competes with the scale of Chinese LLMs, Mistral gives global enterprises a strategic hedge, ensuring they are not dependent on a single geopolitical region for their core intelligence infrastructure.

## FAQ

## What is the total parameter count of Mistral Large 4?

Mistral Large 4, also known as Le Chonk, has 1.05 trillion total parameters, with 49 to 52 billion active parameters per inference.

## How does Le Chonk differ from previous Mistral models?

It is natively multimodal (text and images), features a massive 1-million-token context window, and offers significantly improved performance in agentic coding and cybersecurity.

## Can companies run Mistral Large 4 on their own servers?

Yes, because Mistral has released the open weights, organizations can deploy the model on their own hardware and within their own firewalls.

## What are the three reasoning modes available in ML4?

The model offers low, medium, and high reasoning modes, allowing users to balance the depth of the AI's analysis with the cost of token usage.
