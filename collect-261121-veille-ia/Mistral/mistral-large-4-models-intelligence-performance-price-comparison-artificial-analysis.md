# Mistral Large 4: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

Mistral Large 4 (High) is above average in intelligence and reasonably priced when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also notably slow per task and somewhat verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Mistral Large 4 (High) scores 26 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it above average among comparable models (median: 23). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 107M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 101M.

Pricing for Mistral Large 4 (High) is $0.70 per 1M input tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $0.53) and $2.10 per 1M output tokens (moderately priced, median: $2.10). On average, it costs $2.06 per task to evaluate Mistral Large 4 (High) on the Intelligence Index.

At 23.8 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Mistral Large 4 (High) is notably slow compared with the median of 15.0 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
