# Mistral Large 4 Preview: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

Mistral Large 4 Preview is among the least intelligent models, but well priced when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also notably slow per task and very verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 524k tokens context window.

Mistral Large 4 Preview scores 25 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it at the lower end among comparable models (median: 44). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 148M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 65.4M.

Pricing for Mistral Large 4 Preview is $0.70 per 1M input tokens (competitively priced, median: $2.00) and $2.10 per 1M output tokens (competitively priced, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $4.21 per task to evaluate Mistral Large 4 Preview on the Intelligence Index.

At 40.2 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Mistral Large 4 Preview is notably slow compared with the median of 12.8 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
