## FAQ

## What is the newest AI model?

The live tracker at the top of this page is rebuilt every day from the OpenRouter models API, so it always names the newest models available to developers, with their exact first-availability dates. The month sections below carry the same releases in chronological order.

## What are the latest GPT and Claude models?

OpenAI's latest flagship line is GPT-5.6 — the Sol, Luna and Terra tracks launched in July 2026, with Luna becoming the ChatGPT free-tier default in August. Anthropic's latest is the Claude 5 family — Opus 5, Fable 5, Mythos 5, and Sonnet 5 — which succeeded the Claude 4 line (Opus 4/4.1/4.5/4.8, Sonnet 4/4.5, Haiku 4.5).

## How often do new AI models release?

Major labs ship a new flagship roughly every 6–12 months, with point upgrades every few weeks to months. In practice a significant model now lands almost every month: 2025 alone saw 25+ notable releases across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, DeepSeek, Alibaba, Mistral, and xAI.

## What is a frontier model?

A frontier model is one at or near the state of the art, pushing the boundary of capability in reasoning, coding, or multimodality. Current examples include Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.1, and Gemini 3 Pro. Frontier models are typically closed-weights; open-weights alternatives like DeepSeek, Qwen, and Llama usually follow within months.
