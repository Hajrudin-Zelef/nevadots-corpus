### Microsoft Azure

Azure is Microsoft's cloud platform. In the July 29, 2026 release, Satya Nadella said Azure revenue surpassed $100 billion for the first time and was up 41%. The financial statements do not print a separate Azure revenue line. Intelligent Cloud, a wider segment, had quarterly revenue of $39.3 billion, up 32%.
