Ant Group’s Ling team has open-sourced Ling-3.1-flash-1019, which scored 44 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2, up from 41 for the previous Ling-3.1-flash version. The team also released Ling-3.1-flash-base, allowing developers to continue training it. The latest model received further post-training optimizations for reasoning and agentic workflows, with gains across several benchmarks: AA-Briefcase rose from approximately 1,400 to 1,524 Elo, AutomationBench-AA increased from 62% to 68%, CritPt climbed from 18% to 26%, and SciCode advanced from 54% to 57%. Both models are available on Hugging Face and ModelScope.
