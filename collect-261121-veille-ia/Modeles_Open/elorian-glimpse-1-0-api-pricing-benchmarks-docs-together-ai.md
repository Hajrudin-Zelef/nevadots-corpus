## About model

Glimpse is a research preview from Elorian, which describes it as a new category of visual thinking model: a system that reasons about the physical world from what it sees. It follows objects in motion and across changing viewpoints, understands how parts fit together, and connects an observation to what happens next. Rather than reasoning only in text, Glimpse brings visual representations into the reasoning process itself, revisiting and refining them as its thinking unfolds: it looks closely, questions its first impression, and returns to the visual evidence before it answers. As an agent, it is trained end to end to use multimodal tools that write code, transform images, and watch videos, and it can coordinate subagents that exchange text, visual artifacts, and internal representations. Post-trained on an open-weights base model. Available on Together AI.


Native

- **Grounded Visual Reasoning:** Reasons in visual space, zooming into regions and returning to the visual evidence to check its interpretation before answering
- **Motion & Physical Understanding:** Follows objects through motion and viewpoint changes, and connects an observation to its likely physical consequence
- **Multimodal Agent Tools:** Trained end to end to use tools that write code, transform images, and watch videos, and to coordinate subagents on a shared problem
- **Production-Ready Infrastructure:** 99.9% SLA, available on serverless and dedicated infrastructure
