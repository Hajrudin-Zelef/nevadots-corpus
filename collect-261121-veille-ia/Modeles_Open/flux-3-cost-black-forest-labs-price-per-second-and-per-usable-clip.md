## FAQ

### How much does FLUX 3 cost per second?

On Black Forest Labs' API and Playground (checked Nov 3, 2026 ~9:10pm CT on docs.bfl.ai and bfl.ai/pricing), 1 credit = $0.01 and text-to-video and image-to-video cost the same: Draft (HD) $0.06/sec, HD $0.15, FHD $0.24, QHD $0.28, UHD $0.41. Audio is included with no extra charge. Clips go up to 20 seconds; partial seconds round up. FHD/QHD/UHD are the same generation finished by an upsampler and billed at the delivered class. help.bfl.ai still shows older $0.17/$0.29 HD/FHD rates — ignore those; docs + bfl.ai win.

### How much does a 30-second FLUX 3 video cost?

One 30-second try costs $1.80 on Draft HD, $4.50 on HD, $7.20 on FHD, $8.40 on QHD and $12.30 on UHD. With 2 retries (3 attempts) a usable 30-second clip is $5.40, $13.50, $21.60, $25.20 and $36.90. Each generation is capped at 20 seconds, so 30 seconds takes at least two generations.

### What is the cheapest FLUX 3 rate?

FLUX 3 Draft at HD: $0.06 per second, $1.80 per 30-second try and $5.40 per usable 30-second clip (#51= of the 237 rates ClipCost ranks). Full HD (non-draft) is $0.15/sec and $13.50 usable (#147=).

### Does FLUX 3 include audio?

Yes. docs.bfl.ai and bfl.ai/pricing both list native audio included with no extra charge for FLUX 3 text-to-video, image-to-video and video-to-video.

### Is FLUX 3 cheaper on Black Forest Labs or inside Luma?

Since Luma cut Flux 3 to 50 / 80 credits per second (HD / FHD; re-verified Nov 10, 2026 on lumalabs.ai/pricing), Luma is cheaper on Plus annual credits ($0.0025/credit): HD $0.125/sec ($11.25 usable 30s, #130) vs native BFL HD $0.15/sec ($13.50, #147=), and FHD $0.20/sec ($18.00, #171=) vs native $0.24/sec ($21.60, #183=). Pick BFL direct for Draft, QHD/UHD or if you do not want a Luma subscription; pick Luma if you already buy Plus credits and use them up.

### How much does FLUX 3 video-to-video cost?

Video-to-video (continuation, extend, edit, restyle) is dearer than text/image-to-video: HD $0.21/sec, FHD $0.27, QHD $0.31, UHD $0.45, and Draft $0.12 (docs only — bfl.ai shows an em dash for v2v draft). A usable 30s at v2v HD would be $18.90. ClipCost does not rank v2v as a clip-generation rate.
