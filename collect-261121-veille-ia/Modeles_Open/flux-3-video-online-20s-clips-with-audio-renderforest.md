FLUX 3 is Black Forest Labs' multimodal model family, announced on July 23, 2026: one model trained across video, audio, images and actions. FLUX 3 Video generates clips with native audio and is the model in Renderforest. FLUX 3 Max is fal's post-trained variant of FLUX 3 Video, tuned for prompt adherence and lower latency. FLUX 3 Image and FLUX 3 Image Klein (5.6B parameters, open weights) make images. FLUX 3 Action predicts robot actions with industrial partners. FLUX 3 Dev, an open-weights version of the backbone, has been announced without a date. FLUX 3 is not Flux.ai, the circuit-board design tool, and it is the first FLUX that makes video at all: FLUX.1 and FLUX.2 are image models.

FLUX 3 reads a prompt as a shot list. Lead with the subject and the action, then the camera (“handheld”, “slow push-in”, “one continuous shot”), the light, and the sound: name what should be heard (“rain on a tin roof, no music”). Put spoken lines in quotes and say the language (“she says in German: ‘Wir haben geöffnet.’”). Put on-screen text in quotes too. For several shots, describe each cut in order. With start and end frames, describe the motion between them, not the frames themselves. Keep negatives in plain words inside the prompt. Draft the idea on Renderforest Fast, then run the keeper on FLUX 3.
