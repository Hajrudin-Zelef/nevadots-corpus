    **#263** ▲35  ❝0 2026-11-01 

  Order-Only On-Policy Distillation (O³PD) revisits on-policy distillation, where a student matches the teacher's token probabilities on its own generations. Those probabilities mix which tokens the teacher prefers with how confident it is; the latter reflects the teacher's capacity and calibration, which a smaller student may not reproduce, and since temperature changes the values but not the ordering, OPD is temperature-sensitive. At each student prefix, O³PD trains the student to reproduce the order of the teacher's top-K tokens without fitting probability values, making the objective invariant to teacher temperature. Experiments span six teacher-student pairs.

  Calpha aims at one general model that folds proteins and complexes, understands protein sequence and structure, and lets its language reasoning influence the structures it predicts. This report covers the first step, in which an LLM outputs single-chain protein structures.   TabBioMed is a large-scale benchmark of 94 curated public biomedical tabular datasets spanning electronic health records, drug discovery, genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, single-cell omics, and systems biology. Evaluation on structured biomedical data has remained fragmented, especially for datasets with high dimensionality, large sample size, missingness, and class imbalance. TabBioMed unifies these datasets through a standardized, dataset-aware preprocessing and evaluation framework, enabling reproducible comparison across classification, regression, and multi-target tasks. The authors benchmark 23 models across classical baselines, gradient-boosted trees, neural tabular architectures, and tabular foundation models. Foundation models deliver the strongest family-level predictive performance, but their gains are regime-dependent: tree-based models and tuned multilayer perceptrons remain highly competitive, often dominating under inference-time or training-cost constraints. Overall, biomedical tabular learning is strongly context-dependent, with optimal model choice depending on data regime, task type, and computational budget. The authors release curated datasets and splits, preprocessing code, baselines, and results.
