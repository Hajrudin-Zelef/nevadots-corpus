    **#504** ▲12  ❝0 2026-11-03 

  FlowTranslator studies in-image machine translation (IIMT): replacing text in an image with its translation while preserving the surrounding visual context. Existing task-specific models perform convincingly but need large amounts of paired before/after-translation images. The authors argue that the multilingual text generation of text-to-image (T2I) models is now practically usable and can be transferred to IIMT, and present a training-free framework that uses an OCR model and an LLM to produce a translation prompt, which is injected into a diffusion-based T2I model. To make the translated text accurate and natural, they add two trajectory controls: Region Coupling Release (RCR), which partially decorrelates target-branch noise inside the text region early in the trajectory, and Coupling Expansion (CE).     **#521** ▲10  ❝0 2026-11-02 

  The paper proposes the SIMPLE law for LLM inference energy: generation is billed per token, but tokens are not equally expensive, since each token enters the KV-cache and raises the cost of later ones, so energy grows non-linearly along a sequence. The most accurate existing model is a fit rather than a law and needs per-LLM fitting. The authors derive closed-form compute and memory-access complexity in terms of architectural hyperparameters (MHA and GQA), compare analytical models of increasing granularity, and obtain SIMPLE, a four-parameter law separating prefill from decode and splitting decode into KV-cache cost and next-token computation. It is evaluated on traces of 13 dense LLMs from six families on NVIDIA H100 GPUs. The abstract is truncated in our store; accuracy figures follow the original paper.
