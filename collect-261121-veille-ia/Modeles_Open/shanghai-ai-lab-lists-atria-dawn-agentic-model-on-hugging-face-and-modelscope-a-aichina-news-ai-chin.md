# Shanghai AI Lab Lists Atria Dawn Agentic Model on Hugging Face and ModelScope

Shanghai AI Lab has published model pages for Atria Dawn, an agentic model built on a 744B-parameter MoE GLM-5.2 foundation, on Hugging Face and ModelScope, with full and FP8 releases and a 512K context listed.

Shanghai AI Lab has listed Atria Dawn, a new agentic model, on Hugging Face and ModelScope. The model card describes Atria Dawn as a "new-generation agentic model developed by the Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory," built on the 744B-parameter MoE GLM-5.2 foundation model and aimed at research and engineering scenarios that require continuous environmental understanding, tool use and multi-step task completion.

## What is new

The card states that Atria Dawn is designed to carry open-ended problems toward "executable, verifiable, and reproducible" results, combining task objectives with environmental feedback across problem analysis, solution design, tool use, code implementation, experiment execution, result analysis and failure recovery. It groups its agentic work into four areas: Discovery (evidence retrieval, deep research and turning research questions into executable experimental plans), Creation (software, interactive applications, games, data visualisations and machine learning systems), Delivery (reports, presentations and other structured deliverables) and Cybersecurity (analysing security issues, validating vulnerabilities, applying fixes and re-validating in authorised environments).

The model downloads table lists four entries: Atria Dawn (instruct model, full release, 512K context), Atria Dawn FP8 (FP8-quantised instruct model, full release, 512K context), Atria Dawn Preview (instruct model, 256K context) and Atria Dawn Preview FP8 (FP8-quantised instruct model, 256K context). The card also names Atria-Dawn as the model value to use in API requests.

## Benchmarks

The lab reports that it evaluated Atria Dawn and Atria Dawn Preview across search, coding, tool use, productivity and security benchmarks. The card's table shows Atria Dawn at 54.7 on AutomationBench, 86.8 on CyberGym and 67.8 on SkillsBench; the table is cut off after a Workspace entry.

## Availability and pricing

The card shows a Licence MIT badge and links to a LICENSE file, and it lists model pages on Hugging Face and ModelScope. It does not state API pricing. The evidence does not show whether access is gated.
