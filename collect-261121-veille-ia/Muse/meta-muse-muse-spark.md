**Update 2026-10-14 (launch Oct 12, 2026; Meta newsroom via TechCrunch and launch-day trackers):** Meta released **Muse Wonder** and **Muse Wonder Full**, positioned as its most capable models for long-running personal/professional agent work (1M-token context; features Meta calls Active Memory and Active Focus). Wonder is on the Meta Model API at a **reported $4 input / $20 output per 1M tokens** (cached input $0.20) â *widely reported at launch but not yet confirmed on Meta's own pricing page; verify before quoting.* Wonder Full is **not on the API** â it is reserved for Muse Code subscribers (Power/Maximum tiers, reported $50/$200/mo).

**Update 2026-10-15 â Wonder pricing CONFIRMED on Meta's own pages:** multiple Oct 13â14 trackers (VentureBeat, DataCamp, and others) now cite Meta's own developer model page / Model API pricing as listing muse-wonder-1.0 at **$4 input / $20 output per 1M tokens, $0.20 cached**, plus built-in web search at **$2.50 per 1,000 searches**. The 2026-10-14 "unconfirmed" flag is retired. Muse Code tiers are also now listed in full: Everyday $5/mo, High $15/mo, Power $50/mo, Maximum $200/mo â standard Wonder is available from Everyday up; **Wonder Full remains Muse Code-only, on Power and Maximum only**, with no per-token API price.

**Update 2026-10-16 (Meta announcement Oct 15, 2026, via TechCrunch and VentureBeat reporting on Meta's model card and launch post):** Meta released **Muse Spark Open**, an open-weight sibling of Muse Spark: 816B total parameters with 42B active per token, 1M-token context, text/image/audio/video input and text output, aimed at coding and agentic work. Weights are free to download under the custom Muse Model License (attribution required; model-as-a-service providers above $20M gross revenue in a 12-month period need a separate agreement; internal and research use are exempt from that clause in the license summary). Hosted access is through launch partners; Together AI lists serverless at $1.25 input / $4.25 output per 1M plus $0.15 cached â a host rate, not a uniform price across partners. Local running is heavy: Meta's desktop example pairs two 512 GB M5 Ultra Mac Studios for about 1 TB of unified memory, a configuration not yet shipping at announcement. Meta's claim that it is the most capable U.S. open-weight model is a vendor claim; its own table scores it below several non-U.S. open models on some benchmarks.

**Update 2026-11-09 (primary: Meta Newsroom, "Design the Muse App, Just for You," dated Nov 6, 2026; read directly 2026-11-09):** Meta has launched **spaces** in the Muse app: a user describes an idea and Muse builds a custom in-app experience around their routines and goals â Meta's nine examples include a small-business order tracker (new orders, customer messages, what needs to ship today) and a freelance client hub (projects, deadlines, invoices), alongside consumer uses like a gift tracker, school dashboard and trip planner. Spaces can be added to the sidebar or main tabs, hidden, and scheduled to appear at chosen times; connected apps keep them updated in the background. Spaces are **available from Nov 6 everywhere Muse is available**; sharing a space with others is only being explored. The announcement states **no price and no usage limit** â Muse plan prices are unchanged. The same post says Meta is **rolling out specialized versions of its models to Muse, trained with learnings from Muse Wonder**; the companion Meta AI Research post (Nov 6, 2026) names the inherited traits **Active Memory** and **Active Focus** and reports **2.4x less forgetting of critical information** and **67% better completion of everyday tasks when unexpected issues arise** â *Meta's own figures from its own tests (vendor claims)*. Announced Nov 6, this was not captured in the Nov 7â8 runs â picked up here.

**Update 2026-11-10 (primary: Meta AI Research, "Introducing Our Smallest, Most Efficient Model: Muse Ember," research.meta.ai, Nov 9, 2026; read directly 2026-11-10):** Meta launched **Muse Ember (`muse-ember-1.0`)**, its smallest and most efficient model, built for **complex reasoning execution and the work agents repeat all day: tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long-document parsing**. It is available today on **Muse Code and the Meta Model API** at **$0.10 input / $0.50 output per 1M tokens, with cached input at $0.004 per 1M** â the headline rates in Meta's own post. Meta's developer documentation, as reported Nov 9â10 by VentureBeat and other launch coverage reading that documentation, adds a **long-context tier: above 200,000 input tokens the whole request prices at $0.30 / $1.50 per 1M (cached $0.012)** â a 3x step-up to budget for, in the same family as Grok's 200K cliff. Ember has a **1M-token context window and up to 512K output tokens**, is natively multimodal (text, images, audio, video in), supports the Responses API, and is **the first model Meta trained using Muse Wonder Full**; Meta's intended pattern is pairing â Wonder or Spark plans, Ember handles the volume. Meta says Ember **matches Claude Haiku 5.5's best Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score (36) at about a third of the cost per task (~$0.14 vs ~$0.41)** and beats GPT-6 Luna at every reasoning setting plotted â *Meta's figures, drawn from Artificial Analysis results dated Nov 9, 2026 (vendor claims, not independently replicated at launch)*. The post also notes Ember, Spark and Wonder are now available on **Google Cloud and Oracle Cloud**. Muse consumer plan prices are unchanged, and Ember has **no contributor-tier variant** per the developer documentation as reported â it is a Standard-mode API model.

**Update 2026-11-14 (TechCrunch, Nov 12, 2026, reporting Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang's Nov 12, 2026 post, primary-attributed; corroborated Nov 13 by Gadgets 360, MakeUseOf and PCMag reporting of the same post):** **Muse can now place outbound calls to US businesses on the user's behalf**, open to **all paid Muse subscribers in the US** after a September 2026 test with a limited group of early adopters who asked Muse for access. Wang's examples are the calls people avoid â insurance, cable, the DMV and dinner reservations â and TechCrunch notes Muse can sit on hold. The feature is **paid-only for the time being**, per TechCrunch attributing the limit to Meta: paid plans are cited at **$20/mo for 500 million Muse tokens per week (Power) and $100/mo for 3 billion tokens per week (Max)**, matching the matrix's existing figures. **No per-call price, duration limit or call cap was published** in the reporting checked Nov 13â14, and Muse plan prices are unchanged. TechCrunch also reports the recently added Muse email-address option will soon roll out to free users. One practical limit from the same reporting: some businesses and receptionists hang up once they realize the caller is an AI â no success rate was published.

**Update 2026-11-20 (Reuters investigation published Nov 18, 2026, as reported by TechCrunch, Nov 19, 2026):** Reuters reviewed more than two dozen internal Meta posts in which **employees testing Muse reported it surfacing material that was not theirs** â childcare requests, career advice, orders and **bank balances** (one post quotes Muse adding "your Capital One balance is $2722.58, your Charles Schwab balance is $5899.57" to a weather chat) â and, in one case, a **reminder about a sister's Signal message** the employee said she had not granted access to. Reuters, working with Signal and the AI Now Institute, **replicated notification-based access** and found the behaviour ongoing; an employee post says Muse on Android is set up **by default with permission to access push notifications**. Two hedges belong with the story: **Reuters could not confirm whether the balances were another person's real data or hallucinations**, and a Meta spokesperson called the examples **isolated incidents**, said employee testing is core to development, and said Meta prioritizes safety and continuously updates protections. Treat this as **reported, unresolved cross-account/notification exposure in internal testing â not a confirmed breach of a named customer's data** â but for SMB advice it hardens the existing caution: a personal agent with broad notification/app access needs its permissions audited before it touches business information. **No Muse price or plan change was published** in the reporting.
