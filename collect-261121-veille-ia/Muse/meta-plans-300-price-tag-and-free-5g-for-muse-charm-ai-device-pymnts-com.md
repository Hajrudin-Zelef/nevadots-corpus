# Meta Plans $300 Price Tag and Free 5G for Muse Charm AI Device

Meta plans to price its soon-to-be-released pocket-sized artificial intelligence (AI) device, Muse Charm, at $300 and offer buyers a free 5G data plan for up to 12 months, The Information reported Thursday (Nov. 19), citing an unnamed source.
