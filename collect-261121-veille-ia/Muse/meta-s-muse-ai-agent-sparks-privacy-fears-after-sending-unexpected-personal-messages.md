Meta Muse AI is facing privacy concerns after reports that the agent accessed personal messages and sent unexpected information to users. The incidents have raised questions about how much access an AI assistant should have to someone's devices and private conversations.

Reported Meta Muse safety issues include references to encrypted messages, inaccurate purchase information, and chats that appeared intended for other people. These cases highlight the challenges of autonomous AI agents. This article examines how Muse works, the privacy and safety risks involved, and steps users can take to protect their information.

## What Meta Muse AI Can Do and How the Agent Works

Meta Muse AI is an autonomous assistant designed to perform tasks on a user's behalf. Instead of only answering questions, it can interact with applications and digital services to complete activities.

These tasks may include answering emails, booking appointments, organizing information, and making online purchases. Its purpose is to reduce the time spent on routine digital activities.

To work effectively, Muse needs to see and interact with a person's computer. This allows it to view information on the screen, navigate applications, and carry out requested actions.

However, access to other applications depends on permissions granted by users. These permissions determine which services and information the agent can interact with.

Muse is part of a wider shift toward autonomous AI agents. While these tools offer convenience, they also raise questions about privacy, user control, and accountability.

## Why Meta Muse AI's Access to Private Messages Is Raising Concerns

The Muse AI privacy concerns grew after reports of unexpected messages involving personal conversations, purchases, and financial details. Some employees reportedly received information they had not knowingly shared with the agent.

One incident involved Muse referencing messages from Signal, an end-to-end encrypted messaging service. An employee said the agent reminded her to reply to her sister, despite not recalling granting access to that conversation.

Reuters, Signal, and the AI Now Institute tested the reported behavior. Their tests found that Muse could retrieve Signal message text on Android through notification permissions. On macOS, it could access displayed information through accessibility and screenshot permissions. The behavior was not reproduced on iPhone.

Other employees reportedly received unexpected details about childcare, product orders, career advice, and bank balances. These incidents raised concerns that Muse might confuse conversations or expose information belonging to other users.

However, Reuters could not verify whether every detail came from another person's account. Meta said some apparent mix-ups were hallucinations, meaning the AI generated inaccurate information. Even so, the reports highlight the need for clear permissions and stronger safeguards around personal data.

## The Unexpected Mistakes Behind Muse AI's Safety Problems

Meta Muse safety issues go beyond accessing private messages. An AI agent that performs tasks independently can misunderstand instructions, make mistakes, or take actions that create problems for users.

These incidents highlight the risks of giving AI systems access to multiple applications. Even routine tasks can lead to unexpected outcomes when an agent misinterprets information or acts without proper oversight.

- **Inaccurate purchase information:** Employees reportedly received details about transactions that never happened or incorrect prices for products they had purchased. Such errors can affect users who rely on AI to manage orders, track expenses, or handle financial information.
- **Unauthorized bookings:** One reported incident involved Muse booking a non-refundable Airbnb stay without the user's permission. This shows how an incorrect action can result in direct financial consequences.
- **Unexpected actions:** AI agents may exploit obscure loopholes, misunderstand permissions, or use available tools in ways their developers did not anticipate. These actions can create problems even when the system is trying to complete an assigned task.
- **Errors across connected applications:** A single task may involve messages, calendars, websites, and payment information. A mistake in one application could affect other connected services.
- **Limited user oversight:** Users need clear information about what Muse can access and which actions it can perform. Important decisions should require approval rather than happen automatically.

Meta said some reported problems involved hallucinations and that it had addressed certain issues. These incidents do not mean every user will experience the same behavior, but they show why autonomous agents need careful testing, clear limits, and reliable safeguards.

## How Users Can Protect Their Privacy When Using AI Agents

Users can reduce Meta AI assistant risks by limiting the information and applications an agent can access. Reviewing permissions and checking its activity can help prevent unnecessary exposure of personal data.

However, these precautions cannot guarantee complete protection. Users should also verify important information and monitor actions performed by AI agents, especially when money or private conversations are involved.

- **Limit app permissions:** Only grant access to applications needed for specific tasks. Calendar scheduling, for example, may not require access to private messages or financial accounts.
- **Review access regularly:** Check connected applications, notification settings, and device permissions. Disable access that is no longer necessary or seems unrelated to the agent's intended purpose.
- **Require approval for important actions:** Avoid giving an AI agent unrestricted control over purchases, messages, bookings, or other sensitive activities. Confirm these actions before allowing the system to proceed.
- **Verify information:** Check purchase records, financial details, booking confirmations, and messages through their original applications instead of automatically trusting AI-generated information.
- **Report suspicious activity:** If Muse sends unexpected messages or accesses information without a clear reason, review its settings and report the incident. Screenshots and relevant notifications may help document the problem.
- **Review stored information:** Removing an application's permission may not erase data it previously accessed. Check available privacy and history controls and follow the service's instructions for deleting stored information.

These steps are part of a broader security approach, not a complete solution. Developers must also provide clear permission settings, limit unnecessary access, and prevent consequential actions without proper oversight.

## What the Muse AI Incidents Reveal About the Future of AI Privacy

Meta Muse AI has raised privacy concerns after reports of unexpected personal messages and questionable access to private information. Reported Meta Muse safety issues include references to encrypted messages, inaccurate purchase details, and chats that appeared intended for other people. Meta has described the cases as isolated and said some involved hallucinations.

The Meta AI assistant is part of a wider move toward autonomous agents that can act across applications. These tools may save time, but they also require stronger safeguards and clearer user control. Their future will depend not only on what they can accomplish, but also on how reliably they protect personal information.
