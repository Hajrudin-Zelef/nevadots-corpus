Meta has been pushing its Muse AI agent hard since its September 2026 launch, and now it appears the company wants users to have a dedicated hardware companion for interacting with it.

A new report has put a price tag on that companion, and it is raising more than a few eyebrows.

## Meta Muse Charm Price Tag Unveiled

The Information reported that Meta is targeting a $300 retail price for the Muse Charm, the AI-powered keychain accessory that Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg described earlier this year as "a joyful little device for talking to your Muse that fits on your keychain." The price was not officially confirmed by Meta, but The Information's report comes from sources familiar with the company's planning.

The Muse Charm was first teased publicly by Zuckerberg when Meta introduced its broader hardware vision for the Muse AI ecosystem. It is described as a small, wearable device equipped with a small screen and microphones, intended to give users a voice-first interface for Muse that does not require pulling out their phone. The form factor puts it in a category that has not yet seen wide commercial adoption, somewhere between a smart badge and a traditional keychain accessory.

The timing of The Information's report coincided with broader attention to Meta's hardware ambitions. According to the same report, Meta delayed its previously planned smartwatch project specifically to prioritize the development and release of the Muse Charm instead, suggesting the company views the AI keychain as a more strategically important piece of hardware for the near term.

## $300 For AI-Powered Keychain From Meta

At $300, the Muse Charm would sit at a price point that is notably high for an accessory, though not unusual for the emerging category of dedicated AI hardware wearables. For context, the Humane Ai Pin launched at $699 in 2024 before being discontinued, and similar category devices have generally landed between $200 and $700.

The $300 target places the Muse Charm at the more accessible end of that range, though it is still more than double the cost of an entry-level Apple Watch SE or a pair of Meta's own Ray-Ban smart glasses.

The price also invites comparisons to other devices in terms of raw value proposition. Muse is already accessible through any smartphone that has the Muse app installed, so the hardware primarily offers a form factor advantage for users who prefer a hands-free or pocketless interaction style. Whether that convenience justifies a $300 commitment will depend heavily on how central Muse becomes to a given user's daily routine.

No official specification sheet has been published by Meta for the Muse Charm, leaving the full picture of what the device includes at this stage based on the CEO's own description and reporting from The Information.

## Meta Muse Charm Will Come With Freebie

The $300 price will reportedly come with an included benefit designed to make the Muse Charm more attractive from day one. According to The Information, Meta plans to bundle the device with a free 5G data plan that is specifically designed to enable faster responses from Muse when the device is in use. That free data plan is described as lasting up to one year from the purchase date.

The inclusion of a free 5G data plan addresses one of the practical questions any always-connected wearable faces: how does it stay online without relying on a paired phone's hotspot or a separate data subscription? By bundling a year of 5G connectivity, Meta is effectively letting new Muse Charm owners experience the hardware at its full potential for the first twelve months before they face any additional monthly cost decision.

What happens after that first year, and what the ongoing data cost would be, was not specified in The Information's report. The free-year model is a common strategy for connected hardware, used in categories ranging from smart security cameras to dedicated kids GPS trackers, though the renewal economics vary considerably by carrier and device.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is Meta Muse?

Meta Muse is an AI agent developed by Meta and launched for US users in September 2026. It was described by Mark Zuckerberg at launch as a personal AI assistant built from the ground up for privacy and security. The agent is designed to autonomously manage tasks across a user's digital life, including reading and composing emails, managing calendar events, handling financial transactions through connected accounts, making phone calls on behalf of the user, browsing the internet, and acting on information drawn from connected apps and devices.

Muse operates through broad device permissions that allow it to access notifications, messages, and other data from other installed apps in order to provide context-aware assistance. Each Muse agent runs in its own cloud virtual machine, gated by a separate security process called Sentinel. The app reached 10 million US downloads in just 40 days after launch, making it one of the fastest-growing apps in App Store history.

### What Is the Meta Muse Charm?

The Muse Charm is a small, wearable AI accessory designed to serve as a dedicated hardware interface for interacting with Meta's Muse AI agent. Mark Zuckerberg described it as a device that fits on a keychain and that users can speak to in order to interact with Muse hands-free and without needing to access a phone. Based on available information, the Muse Charm includes a small screen and microphones.

It is designed to connect to 5G data, with Meta reportedly planning to bundle a free year of 5G connectivity with the device at purchase. The Muse Charm falls into a broader category of AI hardware companions, alongside products like the Rabbit R1 and earlier devices like the Humane Ai Pin, that aim to give users a dedicated physical interface for AI-powered interactions separate from their primary smartphone.

### When Is the Meta Muse Charm Releasing?

No official release date for the Muse Charm has been confirmed by Meta. Zuckerberg had previously indicated the company was targeting a release around the holiday season, suggesting a late 2026 window is at least being aimed for internally, though that timeline has not been locked in publicly.

The pivot away from the smartwatch project, which The Information attributed to a reprioritization in favor of Muse Charm, suggests the product is being treated as a near-term shipping priority rather than a distant future initiative. Meta has not held an official product announcement event for the Muse Charm, and the $300 price remains a reported figure rather than a confirmed retail listing.

### Is the Meta Muse Charm a Security Liability?

The broader Muse ecosystem has already faced significant security and privacy scrutiny in its early weeks on the market, and the Muse Charm inherits many of those concerns by virtue of being a hardware companion to the same platform. Reuters and Ars Technica both reported in November 2026 that internal Meta employees had flagged serious privacy issues with Muse, including incidents of cross-account data confusion during testing, the AI reading Signal messages without direct user instruction, and a vulnerability in the chat-sharing feature that made shared conversations discoverable through public DNS records.

Meta addressed the DNS issue after it was identified by security firm Claroc, temporarily disabling the sharing feature before restoring it with a patch. For the Muse Charm specifically, the introduction of a connected hardware device with microphones and 5G connectivity expands the attack surface of the Muse ecosystem, adding a physical device that must be secured in addition to the app-based access that already exists on a user's phone.
