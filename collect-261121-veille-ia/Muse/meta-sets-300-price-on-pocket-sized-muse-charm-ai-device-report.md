# Meta sets $300 price on pocket-sized Muse Charm AI device: report

Meta Platforms META has set a $300 price on its upcoming AI gadget, Muse Charm, according to *The Information.*

To drive up consumer interests, Meta is also offering a free, 12-month 5G data plan from Verizon VZ, the report said. Meta pushed back the release of its smartwatch until 2027 to get the Muse Charm on the market before Christmas. Earlier this year, Meta set a goal of selling 10M wearable devices during the second half of 2026 as it pushes into a market led by Apple AAPL.

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg introduced Charm during its Connect event in September. Roughly the size of an Apple AirPods case, Charm features an approximately 2-inch OLED touchscreen and built-in 5G connectivity. It expects to begin shipping the devices by early December.

Zuckerberg said Charm brings the company's Muse personal AI agent into a portable device that can be carried on a keychain, in a pocket, or with a wrist strap.

"We pack the whole Muse experience, including the whole real-time voice and avatar stack, into something that fits on a keychain and is always available to talk to," he said.

The device includes speakers, microphones, front- and rear-facing cameras, a USB-C port, and a customizable virtual Muse character, including options for its appearance and voice. Charm is designed as a dedicated interface for Muse rather than a smartphone replacement. It can connect to Muse over 5G when Wi-Fi is unavailable and is paired and synchronized through the Muse app on an iPhone or Android device. It also supports music and video playback but does not offer phone or video calling. Charm devices can interact with each other as well.

In addition to the Charm and the upcoming smartwatch, Meta is also working on an AI pendant. **Disclaimer**
