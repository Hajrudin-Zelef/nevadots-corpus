# Meta Staffers Say Muse AI Agent Made Unauthorized Booking and Accessed Private Apps

Meta employees have told the company that while they were using its personal artificial intelligence (AI) agent, Muse, the agent took actions they didn’t request, supplied them with made-up information and sent them responses that seemed to be meant for someone else, Reuters reported Wednesday (Nov. 18), citing more than two dozen internal company posts.
