Nov 19 (Reuters) – Meta Platforms plans to price its pocket-sized AI device, Muse Charm, at $300 and offer buyers up to 12 months of free 5G data from Verizon, the Information reported on Thursday, citing a person familiar with the matter.

• Verizon did not immediately respond to Reuters’ requests for comment, while a Meta spokesperson said “we’re charmed by the interest in our AI devices, and we’ll have more to share soon”

• The device that carries the viral Muse AI agent into the real world was unveiled in September by chief executive Mark Zuckerberg during the company’s annual conference

• Muse Charm underscores Meta’s broader push to reduce its reliance on Apple and Google’s operating systems by building its own AI platform

• Users can tap the gadget to access Muse directly, bypassing AI apps on their smartphones

• The device features a roughly 2-inch (5-cm) screen that can be navigated with a thumb, with a customizable Muse character at its center

• A built-in 5G modem allows access to Muse even without Wi-Fi, though it cannot make call

• Muse has gained traction among users as Meta expands its push to make its AI assistant a more widely used consumer service

• The AI agent allows users to delegate tasks such as shopping, travel booking and form-filling to an AI agent

• Meta is expected to launch Charm in time for the holiday shopping season in December, according to Zuckerberg’s comments at the company’s Connect event.

(Reporting by Harshita Mary Varghese in Bengaluru; Editing by Shinjini Ganguli)
