# Muse Wonder: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

Muse Wonder (Max) is below average in intelligence and somewhat expensive when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also slower than average per task and somewhat verbose. The model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Muse Wonder (Max) scores 44 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it below average among comparable models (median: 44). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 113M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 65.4M.

Pricing for Muse Wonder (Max) is $4.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $2.00) and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $6.95 per task to evaluate Muse Wonder (Max) on the Intelligence Index.

At 14.0 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Muse Wonder (Max) is slower than average compared with the median of 12.8 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
