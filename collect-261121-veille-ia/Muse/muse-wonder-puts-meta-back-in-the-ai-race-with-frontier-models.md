Meta wants a stronger seat at the AI table.

With the launch of Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, Meta is making a stronger push into frontier AI, putting it in more direct competition with OpenAI, Anthropic and Google.

Released by its Superintelligence Lab on October 12, 2026, the models are designed to handle demanding personal and professional tasks. The launch follows months of rebuilding Meta's AI strategy around more capable assistants and agents that can work through tasks over time.

## What sets Muse Wonder apart

Meta describes Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full as its most capable models so far. They follow Muse Spark, an earlier model designed to be lighter and faster, but are aimed at more complex work involving coding, planning, research and document understanding.

The distinction is important. Rather than simply responding to prompts, these models are designed to support AI agents that can retain context, use tools and work through longer tasks.

## Memory is central to Meta's pitch

One of Muse Wonder's key features is Active Memory, which allows the model to retain important details across conversations and bring them back when relevant. A personal assistant could, for instance, remember a user's preferences, project details or earlier instructions instead of treating every conversation as a fresh start.

Meta is also highlighting Active Focus, which helps the model persist with difficult tasks, try alternative approaches when it gets stuck and ask for clarification when needed.

These capabilities could make a difference in tasks such as planning travel, analysing documents or working across a codebase, where context and continuity matter.

## How the models compare

Meta says the Wonder models achieve benchmark scores comparable to Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra. The company has also highlighted their performance on complex coding tasks that require models to track requirements, make changes and test their work across longer projects.

The official model card lists support for text, image, video and audio inputs, with text as the output format. It also specifies a context length of one million tokens at inference, allowing the model to process a large amount of information in a single session.

## Pricing, access and Meta's agent ambitions

Muse Wonder is available through the Meta Model API and Muse Code. API access costs $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens. Muse Wonder Full, meanwhile, is limited to Muse Code subscribers and will not be offered through the API.

The launch also builds on Meta's broader push to make Muse a personal AI agent capable of carrying out multi-step tasks, including filling out web forms, making purchases and booking travel.

Early interest in the app has offered another measure of that ambition.

Estimates from app intelligence firm Apptopia put Muse at about 1.8 million iOS downloads in the US and Canada in its first 12 days, compared with 1.3 million for ChatGPT over the same period after its mobile launch. These are third-party estimates, not official figures from either company.

## Can Meta turn capability into trust?

Both Wonder models are expected to power Meta's Muse agent. The company says Muse Wonder will initially roll out to subscribers and later help create specialised models for broader Muse experiences.

Meta has also outlined safeguards against misuse, particularly in sensitive cyber and chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear areas.

The launch gives Meta a stronger claim in the frontier AI race, but benchmark results and early downloads will not settle the competition. The real test is whether Muse can deliver reliable results, protect users and handle everyday tasks well enough to become an assistant people trust.
