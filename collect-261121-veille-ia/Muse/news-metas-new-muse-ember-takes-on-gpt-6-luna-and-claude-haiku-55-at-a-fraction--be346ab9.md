Several months ago, Chinese labs released a wave of new AI models that delivered roughly 80-90% of the performance of frontier AI models at a fraction of the cost. OpenAI responded with its GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna models, which outperformed their Chinese rivals while offering similar pricing. Last month, Anthropic introduced the Haiku 5.5 model, targeting high-volume AI workloads at a fraction of the cost of traditional frontier models.
These cost-effective models helped curb the growing popularity of Chinese AI models in the U.S. Now, Meta has announced Muse Ember, its smallest and most efficient AI model yet, taking on OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna and Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5. Meta claims its new model can deliver similar intelligence to Claude Haiku 5.5 at roughly one-third of the cost, while outperforming GPT-6 Luna across several benchmarks.
According to Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 results shared by Meta, Ember scores 36 points, matching Claude Haiku 5.5 and comfortably beating GPT-6 Luna's score of 28. Muse Ember costs approximately $0.14 per task, compared to $0.41 for Claude Haiku 5.5 and $0.15 for GPT-6 Luna, with all three running at maximum reasoning effort. In other words, Ember offers Haiku-level intelligence at nearly one-third of its running cost.
Meta also published benchmark comparisons across agentic workflows, coding, and multimodal tasks:
| Benchmark | Muse Ember | Haiku 5.5 | GPT-6 Luna | 
|---|---|---|---|
| AutomationBench-AA | 37.8% | 13.9% | 20.2% | 
| Terminal-Bench 4.0 | 40.9% | 39.2% | 16.4% | 
| Coding Agent Index-AA | 46 | 37 | 31 | 
| FrontierCode 2.0-AA | 27.2% | 34.9% | 25.2% | 
| OSWorld 2.1 (partial credit) | 69.5% | 68.9% | 50.5% | 
As you can notice in the benchmarks above, Claude Haiku 5.5 remains ahead of Ember in FrontierCode 2.0, which evaluates real-world software engineering tasks. Muse Ember supports text, image, audio, and video inputs. It comes with a 1-million-token context window and supports generating up to 512,000 output tokens.
Muse Ember costs $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.004 per million cached input tokens, and $0.50 per million output tokens. These rates apply to prompts up to 200,000 tokens. For longer prompts, pricing increases to $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.50 per million output tokens.
Muse Ember is now available through the Meta Model API and Muse Code under the model ID muse-ember-1.0. It supports OpenAI's Responses API spec, allowing developers to integrate it into existing agentic workflows.
