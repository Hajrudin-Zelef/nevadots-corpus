The Information reports Meta will charge $300 for its Muse Charm AI pendant and bundle up to 12 months of free 5G from Verizon to spur adoption, as the company targets 10 million wearable sales in H2 2026 and races toward a December launch. The device, unveiled at Connect in September, embeds a 5G modem to run Meta's Muse personal assistant without a tethered phone. Meta delayed its smartwatch to concentrate on shipping Charm for holiday 2026.
