NVIDIA and Microsoft said they are expanding their joint AI stack beyond Azure cloud. New Azure virtual machines will run NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems and give customers access to open‑model services through Microsoft Foundry. The companies also showed AI workloads running locally on Windows PCs with RTX Spark GPUs and on edge Jetson devices. A limited preview of GPU‑accelerated Windows 365 Cloud PCs is open for sign‑ups, with a public preview planned for early 2027.

## Why it matters

Enterprises can now run large‑language models on‑premise or at the edge, reducing latency and dependence on cloud bandwidth.
