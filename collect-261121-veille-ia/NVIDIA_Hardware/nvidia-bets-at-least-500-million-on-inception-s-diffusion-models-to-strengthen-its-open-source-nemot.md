# Nvidia bets at least $500 million on Inception's diffusion models to strengthen its open-source Nemotron lineup

**Nvidia is nearing a deal to acquire AI startup Inception for at least $500 million.** That's according to three people familiar with the talks told The Information. The deal also includes performance bonuses for the more than 30 employees at the two-year-old San Francisco Bay Area startup.

Inception builds language models using the diffusion process known from image generation. Rather than generating responses token by token like the conventional models behind ChatGPT and Claude, they refine a full response step by step. The startup most recently released Mercury 3.

The acquisition aims to bolster Nvidia's open-source Nemotron models and physical models for robots, helping it compete with OpenAI and Anthropic as both build their own inference chips. Palantir and US government agencies already use Nemotron as an alternative to Chinese models. Talks began before Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation and after Microsoft expressed interest. Inception co-founders and professors Stefano Ermon, Aditya Grover, and Volodymyr Kuleshov are expected to join Nvidia full time. Neither company initially commented.
