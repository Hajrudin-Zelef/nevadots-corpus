# Nvidia CEO gave go-ahead on Marvell's mega-acquisition of Lumentum

Before the announcement of Marvell Technology's MRVL proposed acquisition of Lumentum LITE, the CEOs of both companies asked Nvidia NVDA CEO Jensen Huang's opinion, according to Bloomberg.

Huang gave his blessing on the potential tie-up, as consolidation among crucial suppliers in the AI buildout could reduce bottlenecks, the report said. Nvidia invested $2B in Marvell and Lumentum earlier this year.

"I think the reaction from Nvidia has been super positive," said Lumentum CEO Michael Hurlston during an interview with Fox Business on Monday. "We think we can actually bring a lot more product to bear to Nvidia a lot more quickly in this combination."

The all-stock deal is valued at about $115B. The acquisition would further strengthen Marvell's position in optical networking and expand the combined company's total addressable market to $550B. In addition to boosting the combined company's revenue profile and margins, the deal would provide more than $1B in annual run-rate synergies and be accretive to Marvell's adjusted earnings one year after the deal closes.

It combines Lumentum's optical components with Marvell's custom silicon, creating a more vertically-integrated supplier of high-speed connectivity equipment. This is crucial in scaling up increasingly powerful data centers.

"By combining with Lumentum, we will become one of the largest and most strategic optical and photonic engineering organizations in the world," said Marvell CEO Matthew Murphy in a Monday investor call. "The operating scale resulting from this combination will significantly increase our ability to innovate and invest in next-generation solutions for our customers."**Disclaimer**
