**What's the deal?** Nvidia is in advanced talks to acquire Inception, the two-year-old startup behind the Mercury family of diffusion language models, for at least $500 million plus performance-based earnouts tied to targets for the new employees, The Information reports, citing three people familiar with the talks. Nothing has been signed or announced, and neither company has commented.

**Why Nvidia wants it:** Instead of writing text one token at a time like the models behind ChatGPT and Claude, Inception's diffusion models refine many tokens in parallel, which the company says makes them up to 10 times faster and much cheaper to run. One person told The Information that the technology and team could help Nvidia build out its Nemotron family of open models. **Context:** The talks started before Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO, a sign that Nvidia's deal-making and its push into its own models continue under interim leadership. Inception has raised about $50 million from investors including Menlo Ventures, M12, Mayfield and Nvidia itself, and has more than 30 employees. Microsoft had also shown interest in a similar deal, according to the report.
