By Katherine Hamilton

Nvidia introduced Nemotron for Science, an open chemistry dataset and post-training recipes to help researchers build AI agents for scientific discovery.

The platform is designed to help build agents that can reason through scientific problems and use specialized research tools, Nvidia said Monday. Scientists can adapt the model to specialized tasks for conducting experiments in various fields.

Nemotron for Science includes an open dataset with nearly 50,000 questions for training and evaluating language models on chemistry reasoning. It draws on molecular properties and experimental discovery data to predict how molecules behave.

Life sciences companies, including Lila Sciences and Terray Therapeutics, are using Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Super VL for science applications, including extracting chemical knowledge and planning experiments.

The 3.5 Super VL and LitmusBench v0.2 datasets are now available on Hugging Face, Nvidia said.
