# Nvidia no longer shipping licensed Hopper chips to China

Nvidia NVDA is no longer shipping licensed Hopper data center products to China, despite receiving U.S. government approval last year to sell a version of the product in the country.

Nvidia sold $2.4B worth of licensed Hopper data center products to China in the third quarter, interim CEO Colette Kress said during an earnings call on Tuesday.

However, Kress said the company's future outlook “does not have any licensed Hopper data center products included, and we are no longer shipping licensed Hopper data center products to China.”

Kress did not elaborate on why the shipments were ending. The shift comes after years of lobbying by Nvidia, with ousted CEO Jensen Huang a vocal advocate for maintaining access to the Chinese market.

The move also follows increased US scrutiny of chip exports to countries where data centers could provide Chinese companies remote access to advanced AI chips. Nvidia chairman Harvey Jones said the board recognizes that national security concerns are reshaping the market and that the company's next CEO will need to “navigate those waters very carefully.”

Nvidia has also announced the formation of a board committee focused on national security and export controls. The company on Tuesday raised its sales forecast for next year and pledged to improve access to critical supplies, while dismissing speculation that former CEO Jensen Huang could return.**Disclaimer**
