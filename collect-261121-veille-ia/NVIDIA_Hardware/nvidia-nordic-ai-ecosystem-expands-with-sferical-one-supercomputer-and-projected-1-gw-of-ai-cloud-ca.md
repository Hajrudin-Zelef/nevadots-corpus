## Details

Swedish consortium launched Sferical One, a shared supercomputer on two NVIDIA DGX SuperPODs

The system uses 16 racks of DGX GB300 totaling 1,152 GPUs and will use Nemotron open models.

Sferical One is anchored by Ericsson, AstraZeneca, Saab, SEB and Wallenberg Investments

SKF is strategic partner. Pooling compute gives major Swedish firms shared access to a large AI system.

Nordic AI clouds are projected to reach 1 GW of capacity across the region by end of 2027

Named providers are Bitdeer, CoreWeave, Crusoe, Hyperstack, Nscale, Verda and Volta. This is a projection, not current capacity.

Sweden's startups raised $2.8B in H1 2026, with $5B projected for the full year

The Nordics are described as Europe's leader in unicorn density per capita. The full-year figure is a projection.

Elisa is launching a beta of Moontalk AIRI, a meeting assistant built on Nemotron

An example of a telecom operator building an agentic assistant on NVIDIA's open models.

ABB Robotics' RobotStudio HyperReality claims up to 99% simulation accuracy and 80% less commissioning time

Built on Omniverse libraries. These are vendor-reported upper bounds, not typical results.

Spotify uses NVIDIA TensorRT-LLM for podcast recommendations; startups build on Nemotron

Stilta, ConfidentialMind (over 20% cross-lingual retrieval gain claimed) and Agaton all use Nemotron models.

Infrastructure = compute, Partnership = collaboration, Financials = funding, Product Launch = new product, New Tech = capability, Tech Info = usage

#### What This Means

NVIDIA is presenting the Nordics as a growing AI hub, anchored by a shared supercomputer that large Swedish companies use together. The picture mixes real deployments with projections and vendor claims, so read the numbers as promotional. It still shows NVIDIA building regional ecosystems around its hardware and Nemotron models.
