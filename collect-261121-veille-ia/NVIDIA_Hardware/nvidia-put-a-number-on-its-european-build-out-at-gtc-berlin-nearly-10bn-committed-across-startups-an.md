# Nvidia put a number on its European build-out at GTC Berlin - nearly $10bn committed across startups and sovereign infrastructure - with a Swedish industrial consortium supercomputer and a projected 1GW of Nordic AI cloud by end-2027

GTC Berlin, 20-22 October, gave the figure behind the Merz-Huang announcement this ledger recorded on 19 October: Nvidia says it has committed nearly $10bn to Europe's technology ecosystem, spread across startup investments in France, the UK and Sweden, sovereign industrial cloud work with Deutsche Telekom, and an extended Mercedes-Benz automated-driving partnership. No amount is attached to any individual startup deal, which is the main reason this stays at magnitude 3. Two concrete items sit underneath it. Sferical One is a shared supercomputer for a Swedish consortium of Ericsson, AstraZeneca, Saab, SEB and Wallenberg Investments with SKF as strategic partner, two DGX SuperPODs, 16 racks of GB300 and 1,152 GPUs - industrial firms pooling compute rather than each buying their own. And the Nordic and Finnish regional clouds, including CoreWeave, Crusoe, Nscale and Verda, are projected to reach 1GW of AI cloud by the end of 2027, which is the same Nscale that reportedly cannot get UK grid power before the 2030s and is reportedly in talks to sell Anthropic $45bn of capacity. The two-speed-world branch is priced on access splitting by jurisdiction; this is the shape of a second speed being built deliberately, by national consortia, with one vendor's parts.
