# Cognition upgrades Devin Teams with SWE-2 sessions and admin controls

Cognition announced upgrades to the Devin Teams plan, including broader access to Devin Desktop and two concurrent Free sessions per user powered by SWE-2. Team administrators can enable SSO through Okta, Entra ID, SAML or OIDC, configure up to three usage tiers, and request a standard Business Associate Agreement from settings. These capabilities and access terms are vendor-reported.

**In-depth insight**

The update combines expanded access to Cognition’s coding agent with enterprise identity, usage and compliance controls for team deployments.

Today we're upgrading the Devin Teams plan with new features, promotions, and controls:

▪️ Everyone on your team can now use the new and improved Devin Desktop, without having to pay for additional seats ▪️ Every user gets two concurrent Free sessions in our new Free mode, powered by our own model SWE-2 ▪️ Teams Admins can now enable SSO with Okta, Entra ID, or any SAML or OIDC provider ▪️ Admins can also set usage limits per user or per group, with up to 3 different usage tiers ▪️ Teams can request a standard Business Associate Agreement (BAA) from settings

Read more and try it out today: https://devin.ai/blog/devins-new-teams-plan
