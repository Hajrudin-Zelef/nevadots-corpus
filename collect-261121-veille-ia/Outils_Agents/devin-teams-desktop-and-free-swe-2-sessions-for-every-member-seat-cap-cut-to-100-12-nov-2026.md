# Devin Teams: Desktop and free SWE-2 sessions for every member, seat cap cut to 100

~~Up to 200 seats; Devin Desktop seats paid for separately; no Free mode~~Up to 100 members (full and flex seats count); Devin Desktop on every seat; 2 free SWE-2 cloud sessions at a time per member; SSO; 3 usage tiers

Cognition’s Nov 12 update to the Devin Teams plan: every member can now use Devin Desktop without paying for separate Desktop seats, a new Free mode gives each member 2 concurrent cloud sessions powered by SWE-2 at no cost, and admins get SAML/OIDC SSO and per-member usage limits with up to 3 tiers. Teams are now limited to 100 seats, down from 200; teams already above 100 keep their members but can’t add new ones until they’re below 100. Cognition also says it is retiring quota seats on Teams in 30 days, without a date.

Vendor’s framing (quoted, not endorsed): “We’re upgrading the Devin Teams plan.”

Affected plans: Devin Teams (full and flex seats)
