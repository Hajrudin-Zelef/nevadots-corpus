# Windsurf / Devin limits and pricing

Devin (formerly Windsurf) Pro uses a daily plus weekly allowance billed at the token cost of the model you pick; no absolute message figure is published. Max has a weekly allowance only, with no daily cap. Teams full seats get a Pro-equivalent daily and weekly quota; flex seats and overflow draw on shared on-demand credits. Free offers a light quota to code with agents, size not published. 4 plans tracked under Windsurf. Checked 17 Nov 2026. Last change 12 Nov 2026: Devin Teams: Desktop and free SWE-2 sessions for every member, seat cap cut to 100.

**Current limits as of 17 Nov 2026**

- **Windsurf (Devin) Free** $0Usage: Light quota to code with agents (no figure published) · Coding agent: Devin Desktop agents; Devin Cloud is Pro and up · Models: Limited model availability
- **Windsurf Pro** $20/moWindows: Daily + weekly allowance (daily is more than 1/7 of weekly) · How usage counts: Billed at the token cost of the model you pick · Absolute figure: None published
- **Devin Max** $200/moWindows: Weekly allowance only, no daily cap (Pro has daily + weekly) · Usage: A significantly larger weekly quota than Pro; absolute figure not published · Covers: Devin sessions, Devin CLI and Devin Desktop share the quota
- **Devin Teams (full and flex seats)** $80/mo team minimumFull seat: $40/month per seat, with a daily and weekly usage quota equivalent to the Pro plan · Flex seat: Free; draws entirely from the team's shared pool of on-demand credits · Minimum: $80/month; with fewer than two full seats, the rest is charged as prepaid on-demand credits for the team
