Alibaba Cloud released Qwen4-Max on November 16 and made the multimodal model available through Model Studio in six documented regions. Alibaba calls it the Qwen familyâs ânext-generation flagship,â but that label is the companyâs positioning, not an independent comparison with competing models.

Alibaba lists deployments in China (Beijing), Singapore, Germany (Frankfurt), the US (Virginia), Japan (Tokyo) and Hong Kong. Beijing has a Chinese-mainland scope, Singapore has an international scope, and Frankfurt, Virginia, Tokyo and Hong Kong have a global scope.

The company documents a 1,000,000-token context length, with maximum input and output limits that vary by thinking mode. Model Studioâs listed capabilities also include streaming, deep thinking, parallel tool calling, web search, function calling and structured output.

A separate Alibaba Cloud product changelog records Qwen4-Max as available on November 16. Its separate November 11 entry for Qwen4-Max-Preview distinguishes the earlier preview from the production model documented under the `qwen4-max` ID.
