Alibaba has released Qwen4 Max, a proprietary artificial intelligence model that scored 43 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0.1, according to an evaluation published by benchmark platform Artificial Analysis following the model's release in November 2026. The score places the model above the class median of 41 and ranks it 38th among 81 benchmarked models on the platform's composite index.

According to the benchmark data, Qwen4 Max functions as a reasoning model that uses chain-of-thought processing to work through tasks. During the Intelligence Index evaluation, the model generated 127 million output tokens against 9.9 billion input tokens, placing it in the higher verbosity range compared to the peer median of 78.6 million tokens. In cost rankings, the model placed 44th out of 80 tracked systems, with an average evaluation cost of $3.27 per Intelligence Index task.

Specs: Developed by Alibaba, Qwen4 Max is a proprietary, closed-weight model that supports text and image inputs and produces text outputs. It features a context window of 984,000 tokens, equivalent to approximately 1,475 A4 pages of size 12 Arial font. The model is available via Alibaba Cloud API at $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens, alongside a blended rate of $0.80 per million tokens based on a 7:2:1 cache-hit-to-input-output ratio. Prompt caching offers a 95 percent discount on cache hits. On the composite Intelligence Index v5.0.1—which incorporates evaluations such as AA-Briefcase v1.1, AutomationBench-AA, FrontierCode-AA 2.0, and HLE-Diamond—it scored 43 against a peer median of 41. Alibaba has not disclosed the model's parameter count, and performance metrics such as time per task and output speed were listed as unknown.

Background: Qwen4 Max forms part of Alibaba's wider Qwen model family, which includes iterations listed on the platform such as Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, Qwen3.8 27B, Qwen3.5 Omni Plus, and Qwen3 Omni 30B A3B Instruct. Artificial Analysis evaluates proprietary models against peers in similar price brackets using blended token pricing thresholds, comparing systems alongside models from providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. Qwen4 Max is positioned below the category median prices of $2.00 per million input tokens and $10.00 per million output tokens.

Artificial Analysis notes that the model performs above average in intelligence while remaining reasonably priced compared to similarly priced models. However, its high output volume of 127 million tokens per evaluation run reflects higher verbosity than the class median. The platform tracks independent evaluations across several capability indexes, including Finance & Accounting, Legal, and Engineering, using specialized workflow benchmarks.
