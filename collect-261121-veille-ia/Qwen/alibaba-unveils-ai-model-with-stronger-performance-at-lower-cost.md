By Tracy Qu

Alibaba has released an artificial-intelligence model that delivers stronger results at a lower cost, marking the latest push by a Chinese technology company to challenge U.S. leaders in frontier AI.

The Qwen4-Max model boasts 2.4 trillion parameters and activates fewer resources than the previous version for the same task, making it more cost-efficient, the Hangzhou-based company said in a statement Monday.

Alibaba said Qwen4-Max has achieved top rankings across leading benchmarks, particularly in coding, agentic tasks and visual reasoning.

While Qwen4-Max is closed-source, Alibaba plans to make smaller-scale models from the Qwen4 series open-weight soon, allowing developers to download and modify them.

The release comes as Chinese AI developers ramp up efforts to develop frontier models. Beijing-based Moonshot AI released Kimi K3.1 Pro last month, calling it its most capable model to date with 3 trillion parameters.

AI system parameters work like brain cells: The more a model has, the more knowledge it can store, making the count a shorthand for a model's capabilities.

Citi analysts have a positive view on Alibaba, one of the leading AI names under its coverage, according to a research note last week. However, the size of the latest model might disappoint market participants, as some had earlier suggested that Qwen4 could exceed 3 trillion parameters.

Alibaba's Hong Kong-listed stock was 1.2% higher in morning trading, while the Hang Seng Tech Index was 0.7% higher.
