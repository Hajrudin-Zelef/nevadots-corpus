A ball is kicked from the ground at 20 m/s at 35° above the horizontal. Ignoring air resistance, how high does it go, how long is it in the air and how far does it land? Show the steps briefly with g = 9.81 m/s². Write the math as plain text, not LaTeX.

Given:

- Initial speed: v = 20 m/s
- Launch angle: theta = 35 degrees
- g = 9.81 m/s^2
- Assume it lands at the same height it was kicked from.

Velocity components:

vx = 20 cos(35) = 16.38 m/s

vy = 20 sin(35) = 11.47 m/s

1. Maximum height

At the highest point, vertical velocity is 0.

0 = vy^2 - 2 g h

h = vy^2 / (2g)

h = (11.47)^2 / (2 * 9.81)

h = 131.6 / 19.62

h = 6.71 m

1. Time in the air

Time to reach the top:

t_up = vy / g

t_up = 11.47 / 9.81

t_up = 1.17 s

Total time:

t_total = 2 t_up

t_total = 2 * 1.17

t_total = 2.34 s

1. Horizontal distance

range = vx * t_total

range = 16.38 * 2.34

range = 38.3 m

Final answers:

- Maximum height: 6.71 m
- Time in the air: 2.34 s
- Landing distance: 38.3 m
