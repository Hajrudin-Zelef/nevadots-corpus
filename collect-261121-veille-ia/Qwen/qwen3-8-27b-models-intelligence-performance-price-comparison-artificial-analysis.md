# Qwen3.8 27B: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

Qwen3.8 27B (Xhigh) is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but particularly expensive when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also notably slow per task and very verbose. The model supports text, image, and video input, outputs text, and has a 256k tokens context window.

Qwen3.8 27B (Xhigh) scores 20 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 10). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 128M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 81.7M.

Pricing for Qwen3.8 27B (Xhigh) is $0.50 per 1M input tokens (expensive, median: $0.14) and $3.00 per 1M output tokens (expensive, median: $0.32). On average, it costs $3.53 per task to evaluate Qwen3.8 27B (Xhigh) on the Intelligence Index.

At 37.0 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Qwen3.8 27B (Xhigh) is notably slow compared with the median of 13.3 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
