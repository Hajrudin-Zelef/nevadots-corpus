# Qwen4 Max: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

Qwen4 Max is below average in intelligence, but well priced when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also notably slow per task and very verbose. The model supports text, image, and video input, outputs text, and has a 984k tokens context window.

Qwen4 Max scores 43 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it below average among comparable models (median: 44). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 127M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 65.4M.

Pricing for Qwen4 Max is $1.50 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $2.00) and $4.50 per 1M output tokens (moderately priced, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $3.27 per task to evaluate Qwen4 Max on the Intelligence Index.

At 29.8 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Qwen4 Max is notably slow compared with the median of 12.8 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
