## TL;DR

Tesla has unveiled a multi-generation AI chip roadmap under a new "Super Intelligence" brand, targeting an 80X compute leap by 2029-2030 and reviving its Dojo training program with a radically redesigned, space-ready chiplet architecture.

## What happened

- **Tesla disclosed its full SI accelerator roadmap** , renaming all AI chips with "SI" monikers (SI4, SI5, SI6) as Elon Musk repositions the company around "super intelligence" rather than AI.
- **SI6, the 6th-gen inference chip** , targets 17,280 to 46,080 TOPS (17.28 to 46.08 POPS), an**80X compute and 9X memory improvement** over the current SI4 (HW4), with a 2nm-class process and GDDR7 memory; estimated arrival 2029 to 2030.
- **SI5 is nearing production** , built on TSMC 3nm or Samsung 2nm, using a multi-chip module design with 96 GB memory and roughly 40X the compute of SI4.
- **Dojo D3 is back in development** despite Musk canceling the wafer-scale Dojo project in August 2025 and declaring exclusive Nvidia reliance in August 2026; D3 ditches system-on-wafer SRAM for a chiplet design with stacked HBM and 2.5D plus 3D packaging.
- **D3 is explicitly optimized for space deployment** , suggesting radiation-hardened SKUs for extraterrestrial data centers, likely tied to SpaceX orbital compute ambitions.

## Why it matters

- **The 80X claim puts SI6 in direct competition with Nvidia Rubin** , which ships today at roughly 50 NVFP4 PFLOPS; Tesla is betting it can close a multi-year gap by the time SI6 arrives, but Nvidia will not stand still.
- **Dojo's resurrection signals Tesla is not ceding training silicon to Nvidia** , even after two public capitulations; the HBM pivot makes D3 more manufacturable but sacrifices the bandwidth advantage that made wafer-scale integration attractive.
- **The "Super Intelligence" rebrand is a strategic signal** , not just marketing: Musk is aligning Tesla, SpaceX (rebranded SpaceXSI), and xAI under a unified compute narrative that could attract capital and talent.
- **Space-optimized D3 SKUs create a new product category** , radiation-hardened AI training accelerators for orbital infrastructure, a market with no established incumbent.
- **Tesla's vertical integration play intensifies** : controlling inference silicon in every vehicle (SI4 today, SI5 and SI6 in future models) while building proprietary training hardware reduces dependency on external suppliers and improves margin on FSD and robotics workloads.

## What to watch next

- **SI5 production ramp timing** : nearing production now, its yield and volume will validate whether Tesla can execute on advanced packaging at scale before SI6 commitments harden.
- **D3 compute and process disclosures** : Tesla has not revealed D3 performance, memory capacity, or manufacturing node; those numbers will determine whether it is a credible Nvidia alternative or a niche space-focused product.
- **Nvidia's response cadence** : Rubin ships today and Rubin Ultra follows; if Tesla slips SI6 past 2030, the 80X headline advantage over SI4 may look modest against whatever Nvidia ships by then.
