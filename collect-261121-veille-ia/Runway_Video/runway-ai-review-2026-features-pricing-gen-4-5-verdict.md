## What Is Runway AI in 2026?

Runway is a browser-based generative AI platform built for video, image, audio, and creative production. Its current value comes from combining several stages of production inside the same ecosystem rather than forcing creators to use a separate website for every task.

Runway's own tools include Gen-4.5 for video generation, Gen-4 image models, Act-Two for performance capture, and Aleph 2.0 for editing existing footage. Paid subscribers can also access selected third-party models through the same platform.

## Runway Gen-4.5: Features, Quality and Limits

**Gen-4.5** is Runway's current flagship native video-generation model. It supports both text-to-video and image-to-video on Standard plans and above, and Runway lists it at **12 credits per second** of generated video.

Gen-4.5 is designed around stronger prompt adherence, motion quality, visual fidelity, detailed scene composition, and complex camera choreography. It is particularly useful when the prompt includes multiple sequential actions rather than a single static idea.

### 1. Text-to-Video

Text-to-video lets you generate a scene directly from a written description. A prompt can define the subject, environment, action, lighting, composition, timing, atmosphere, and camera behavior. Gen-4.5 currently supports generations from 2 to 10 seconds.

This workflow is useful for concept footage, cinematic B-roll, social content, advertising ideas, mood sequences, previsualization, and short narrative shots. For a broader comparison of this workflow across platforms, see our AI video generators from text guide.

### 2. Image-to-Video

Image-to-video uses a still image as the visual starting point and generates motion around it. This can provide more control over character design, product appearance, composition, or art direction than relying on a text prompt alone.

Runway supports multiple aspect ratios for Gen-4.5 image-to-video, including landscape, vertical, square, portrait, and ultrawide formats. If image-led animation is your main workflow, compare the options in our AI video generators from images guide.

### 3. Resolution and 4K Upscaling

A point that is easy to misunderstand is resolution. Gen-4.5 currently outputs at **720p**. Runway offers a separate **4K upscaling** workflow: Standard includes 4K upscaling, while Pro and Max include unlimited 4K video upscaling. Therefore, Runway should not be described as generating native 4K video directly with Gen-4.5.

### 4. Prompt-Based Camera Choreography

Modern Runway camera control is increasingly prompt-driven. Gen-4.5 can interpret detailed camera choreography, scene timing, and sequential instructions directly in the prompt. This replaces much of the need for older standalone motion-control features.

## What Happened to Motion Brush and the Old Camera Control?

Older Runway reviews frequently mention **Motion Brush**, **Multi-Motion Brush**, or the Gen-3 Camera Control interface as major current features. That is now outdated.

Runway officially deprecated Gen-2 in 2025, and Motion Brush was a Gen-2-specific feature. It is not available on newer models. Runway says newer models rely on improved prompting and prompt adherence to achieve motion results that previously required Motion Brush.

Likewise, the standalone Camera Control associated with Gen-3 Alpha Turbo is no longer the right feature to present as Runway's current flagship motion-control workflow. Gen-3 Alpha and Turbo were retired in July 2026, with Gen-4.5 replacing them for text-to-video and image-to-video.

## Key Runway Features Beyond Gen-4.5

### 1. Act-Two Performance Capture

**Act-Two** is Runway's current character-performance system. You provide a driving performance video and a character image or video, and Act-Two transfers motion, speech, facial expression, and—when supported—body gestures to the character.

With a character image, gesture control can transfer hand and body movement while Runway also adds environmental motion. Act-Two supports clips up to 30 seconds and currently costs 5 credits per second, with a 3-second minimum.

This makes Act-Two useful for stylized characters, digital humans, animated presenters, narrative scenes, and performance-driven creator content. It is broader than a simple lip-sync feature.

### 2. Aleph 2.0 and Edit Studio

**Aleph 2.0** powers Runway's current Edit Studio. Instead of generating a new video from scratch, creators can upload footage and use prompts or edited keyframes to transform the existing shot.

- Replace or remove objects and subjects.
- Change backgrounds, weather, time of day, or lighting.
- Restyle footage or add visual effects.
- Expand a video's aspect ratio.
- Transform single shots or multi-shot sequences.

Edit Studio currently supports video sequences up to 30 seconds, while Aleph 2.0 is priced at 28 credits per second with a 56-credit minimum. That makes it powerful, but potentially expensive for repeated iterations.

### 3. Multiple Models in One Platform

Standard and higher plans provide access to Runway's AI video and image model catalog, including Runway models alongside third-party options available inside the platform. This matters for creators who want to compare different models without maintaining a separate subscription for every provider.

The tradeoff is that every model has its own credit cost and behavior. Runway can simplify access, but it does not make high-end generation inexpensive.

### 4. Image, Audio and Creative Workflow Tools

Runway also includes image generation, text-to-speech, custom voice features on higher plans, audio tools, Apps, and workflow-oriented creative utilities. This broader toolkit is a major reason to consider Runway instead of evaluating Gen-4.5 in isolation.

## Runway AI Pricing in 2026

Runway uses a credit-based subscription model. The official pricing page currently lists Free, Standard, Pro, Max, and Enterprise plans. Standard and above provide access to Gen-4.5 and the wider model catalog.

| Plan | Price | Credits | Main Fit | 
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | 125 one-time | Exploring a limited selection of Runway tools | 
| **Standard** | $15 monthly / $12 annual | 625/month | Light AI video use and access to all models | 
| **Pro** | $35 monthly / $28 annual | 2,250/month | Regular creators and professional workflows | 
| **Max** | $95 monthly / $76 annual | 9,500/month | High-volume production and one-month credit rollover | 
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Teams and organizations | 

### How Runway Credits Work

Gen-4.5 currently costs **12 credits per second**. A 5-second generation therefore costs 60 credits, while a 10-second generation costs 120 credits. Because AI video usually requires several attempts, the effective cost of getting one usable final shot can be much higher than the cost of a single render.

The Free plan includes 125 one-time credits that do not expire, but an important limitation is that **Gen-4.5 requires Standard or higher**. Free therefore works as an introduction to Runway, not as a complete free trial of its flagship model.

Standard and Pro monthly credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over. Max allows up to one month of unused credits to roll over. Purchased additional credits do not expire.

### What Happened to Runway Unlimited?

Runway introduced Max in 2026 and stopped offering Unlimited to new subscribers. Runway later extended eligible legacy Unlimited subscriptions through **November 30, 2026**, after which those subscriptions are scheduled to transition to Max.

For a new subscriber evaluating Runway today, **Max** is the relevant high-volume plan.

## Runway AI Pros and Cons

### Pros

- **Strong flagship generation model:** Gen-4.5 supports both text-to-video and image-to-video with detailed prompt control.
- **Broader than a single generator:** Act-Two, Aleph 2.0, image tools, audio tools, Apps, and third-party models create a more complete production environment.
- **Useful performance capture:** Act-Two can transfer expression, speech, and body gestures to character inputs.
- **AI-powered video editing:** Aleph 2.0 can transform existing footage instead of forcing every project to start with generation.
- **Commercial usage rights:** Runway states that users on all plans retain rights to the content they upload and generate, including commercial use.
- **4K upscale workflow:** Standard includes 4K upscaling, while Pro and Max include unlimited 4K video upscaling.

### Cons

- **Credits can disappear quickly:** High-quality video generation and AI editing become expensive when several iterations are needed.
- **Gen-4.5 is not available on Free:** The flagship model requires Standard or higher.
- **720p native Gen-4.5 output:** 4K is an upscale workflow, not native Gen-4.5 generation.
- **Learning curve:** The growing number of models, Apps, credit rates, and workflows can be harder for beginners than a simple one-model generator.
- **Short generations:** Gen-4.5 clips are currently limited to 2–10 seconds, so longer storytelling still requires shot planning and assembly.
- **Older tutorials age quickly:** Motion Brush, Gen-3, older Camera Control workflows, and other legacy features can create confusion when researching Runway online.

## How Runway Fits a Creator Workflow

Runway makes the most sense when it is treated as part of a production pipeline rather than as a button that creates a complete finished video.

1. **Plan the concept:** define the script, shot list, visual identity, and output platform.
2. **Create or choose reference images:** establish characters, products, locations, or compositions before animation when consistency matters.
3. **Generate shots with Gen-4.5:** use text-to-video or image-to-video depending on how much visual control you need.
4. **Use Act-Two when performance matters:** transfer an actor's expressions, speech, and gestures to a character.
5. **Edit existing footage with Aleph 2.0:** replace objects, alter lighting, restyle scenes, or make other targeted transformations.
6. **Upscale or assemble:** prepare the generated shots for your final editing and publishing workflow.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Runway AI

### Is Runway AI free in 2026?

Yes. Runway offers a Free plan with 125 one-time credits that do not expire. However, the Free tier only includes a limited selection of models and **does not include Gen-4.5**, which requires Standard or higher. Generations made while on the Free tier include watermarks.

### How much does Runway AI cost?

Runway currently lists Standard at $15/month or $12/month with annual billing, Pro at $35/month or $28 annually, and Max at $95/month or $76 annually. Standard includes 625 monthly credits, Pro 2,250, and Max 9,500.

### What is Runway Gen-4.5?

Gen-4.5 is Runway's flagship native AI video model for text-to-video and image-to-video. It supports 2–10 second generations, costs 12 credits per second, outputs at 720p, and is available on Standard plans and above.

### Does Runway still have Motion Brush?

No. Runway states that Motion Brush was specific to the retired Gen-2 model and is not available on newer models. Modern workflows rely more heavily on improved prompt adherence and prompt-based motion and camera instructions.

### What is Act-Two?

Act-Two is Runway's performance-capture tool. It uses a driving performance video and a character image or video to transfer facial expression, speech, motion, and—in supported image workflows—body gestures to the character.

### What is Aleph 2.0?

Aleph 2.0 is Runway's in-context video-editing model inside Edit Studio. It can modify existing footage by changing selected elements while attempting to preserve the rest of the shot, including background, lighting, and surrounding details.

### Does Runway support 4K video?

Yes, through a separate 4K upscaling workflow. Standard includes 4K upscaling, while Pro and Max include unlimited 4K video upscaling. Gen-4.5 itself currently outputs at 720p, so 4K should be understood as an upscale option rather than native Gen-4.5 generation.

### Can Runway videos be used commercially?

Runway states that, as between the user and Runway, users on all plans retain ownership and rights to the content they upload and generate and may use their generations commercially, including monetized YouTube videos, short-form content, ads, and client work.

### What happened to Runway Unlimited?

Runway stopped offering Unlimited to new subscribers after introducing Max. Eligible legacy Unlimited subscriptions have been extended through **November 30, 2026**, after which they are scheduled to transition to Max.

Runway is worth considering when your workflow benefits from more than raw video generation. Gen-4.5 provides the core text-to-video and image-to-video capability, while Act-Two, Aleph 2.0, image and audio tools, and third-party model access make the platform more useful for creators building a broader production pipeline.

Its biggest weakness is not a lack of features—it is the economics of iteration. A few failed generations, higher-cost editing passes, or repeated variations can consume credits quickly. That makes Runway a stronger fit for creators who value production flexibility and can plan their credit use than for users who simply want the cheapest possible AI video output.

For professional creators, filmmakers, visual storytellers, ad producers, and YouTubers who can use the broader toolkit, Runway offers a broad all-in-one production environment in 2026. For simpler or budget-first workflows, a more specialized alternative may offer better value.
