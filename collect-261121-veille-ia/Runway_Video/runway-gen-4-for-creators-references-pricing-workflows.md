## Runway Gen-4 References: one character, many shots

Runway says Gen-4 References takes one or more images and creates new ones using characteristics, styles, characters or objects from those references. It describes References as strong at keeping characters consistent across different lighting, locations and treatments from a single image. You can select up to three active references per generation and tag references to reuse them later.

Runway's help center recommends a clean, neutral character image as the starting reference, describing it as a blank canvas that makes transformation easier, though stylised inputs can work too. For complex changes, it suggests iterating elements one at a time and using each result as a new reference. In newer models, you can also type @ in the prompt to insert uploaded or saved references by name.

## Environments and b-roll from a reference

References are not only for characters. Runway's help center says a reference image can also create consistent environments or b-roll by prompting for different angles, focal points and objects, which helps establish a cohesive setting while keeping the atmosphere of the original. For a persona channel, that means you can build a small library of the character's regular places, such as an apartment, a studio or a favourite cafe, and reuse them across posts.

- **Establishing shot:** a wide view of the location from your reference.
- **Detail shots:** objects from the same scene, such as a desk, a coffee cup or a window view.
- **Angles:** the same room from a new direction, keeping lighting and style.
- **Character in scene:** combine the character reference with the environment reference.

## Prompting tips from Runway's guidance

Runway's help center describes two ways to work with references, and the choice changes how you write the prompt.

- **Single reference:** the reference holds the identity, and the prompt describes everything that changes: outfit, location, lighting, action.
- **Multiple references:** each reference controls an element, and the prompt explains how they combine. Expect more variation and generate a few options.
- **Separate pathways:** for a big transformation, change one element at a time and feed each result back in as a new reference, which Runway says gives more precision.
- **Tagged references:** tag your persona and recurring locations so you can call them up quickly in future sessions.

Keep a short prompt library for your persona: the base description, recurring locations, and lighting styles you like. Reusing the same phrasing across generations is one of the simplest ways to keep a series looking consistent.

## Consent and labelling

A consistent AI character is powerful, which is why the usual rules matter. Build personas that are fictional or based on people who have given clear consent, and do not create references from photos of real people without permission. Platforms also have labelling rules: TikTok says it requires creators to label AI-generated content that contains realistic images, audio and video, and Meta requires people to use its AI-disclosure tool for photorealistic video that was digitally created or altered. Saying in the bio that an account is an AI persona keeps things clear for followers.

## Runway Gen 4: FAQ

### What is Runway Gen-4?

Gen-4 is Runway's family of image and video generation models. It includes Gen-4 Turbo for video, Gen-4 Image, and Gen-4 References, which uses one or more reference images to keep characters, objects or styles consistent. Runway also offers a newer Gen-4.5 video model. Checked October 2026 on Runway's help center and pricing page.

### How much does Runway cost?

Runway's pricing page lists Standard at $15 a month, or $12 billed yearly, with 625 credits a month; Pro at $35 a month, or $28 billed yearly, with 2,250 credits a month; and Max at $76 a month billed yearly. Runway says 625 credits equal about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 or 104 seconds of Gen-4 Turbo. Checked October 2026.

### How do Gen-4 References keep a character consistent?

Runway says Gen-4 References takes one or several images and creates new images using characteristics, styles, characters or objects from them, and that it is strong at consistent characters across lighting, locations and treatments from a single reference. You can use up to three references per generation and tag them to reuse later.

### How many references can I use in Runway?

Runway's help center says you can select up to three active references for a single Gen-4 References generation. A single reference relies on your prompt to describe changes; multiple references give more control over specific elements, with more variation in results.

### Is Runway Gen-4.5 more expensive than Gen-4 Turbo?

Yes, per second. Runway's plan page says 625 credits buy about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 or 104 seconds of Gen-4 Turbo. On the developer API, Runway lists gen4.5 at 12 credits per second and gen4_turbo at 5 credits per second, with API credits at $0.01 each.

### Can I use Runway for an AI influencer?

Yes, as long as the character is fictional or you have the consent of the person it resembles, and you follow platform rules on labelling realistic AI content. A common workflow is to generate a consistent reference image of the persona, then use it as a reference for new images and as a start frame for video.
