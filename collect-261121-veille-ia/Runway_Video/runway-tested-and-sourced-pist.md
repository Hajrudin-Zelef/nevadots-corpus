Runway

# Runway

AI video platform with its own frontier models (Gen-4.5, Aleph, Act-Two) that also resells third-party image, video and audio models through its app and the Runway Dev API.

Known forGen-4.5 video, Aleph video-to-video editing, Act-Two performance capture, a developer API.

Cost

Free plan with 125 one-time credits; Standard $12/mo billed yearly ($15 monthly) for 625 credits, Pro $28/mo ($35) for 2,250 credits, Max $76/mo ($95) for 9,500 credits with one-month rollover. Gen-4.5 costs 12 credits per second; Team and Enterprise plans and credit top-ups (from 1,000 credits) also available.

Features

Text- and image-to-video (Gen-4.5), video editing with Aleph 2.0, image generation, Agent creative collaborator, node Workflows and App Studio, Runway Ads, music, voice and sound effects, 4K upscaling and HDR, Adobe and DaVinci Resolve plugins, MCP and developer API
