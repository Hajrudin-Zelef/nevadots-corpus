Saab has developed its own artificial intelligence agents and has tested them in real flight with the Gripen E fighter. The system piloted the aircraft autonomously in various air combat scenarios beyond the pilot's field of view. The agents, created by the Advanced Programs unit, can assist the pilot in the cockpit or fly from an unmanned aircraft. The initiative aims at air warfare based on networks of manned and unmanned systems that cooperate with each other.
