# Breaking: One UI 9.5 beta is rolling out to Galaxy S26 series right now

- Samsung has pushed out the One UI 9.5 beta update to Galaxy S26 series owners in some markets.
- The update is rolling out in Germany, India, Korea, Poland, the UK, and the US.

We heard that the One UI 9.5 beta could arrive on Galaxy S26 phones today, and we’ve now got some good news for you. The beta software is indeed arriving on Samsung’s early 2026 flagships right now.

Samsung has confirmed that One UI 9.5 is now rolling out to Galaxy S26 series owners in Germany, India, Korea, Poland, the UK, and the US. The update weighs in at 3.49GB to 3.6GB and has a variety of build numbers. These build numbers apparently end in ZZKH, CZKH, ZZKN, CZKN, and CZKF (h/t: Tarun Vats on X).

## What’s new in One UI 9.5?

The changelog reveals a variety of tweaks and additions. For starters, the camera app now offers custom buttons and presets, along with presenting zoom range as focal length. We’ve also got a live depth effect for lock screen wallpapers, with elements shifting as you tilt your device. Staying with the lock screen, you can choose how long the lock screen clock with seconds stays on the screen, while the Now Bar gets sports animations for goals and victories.

Other notable changes include a toggle within Digital Wellbeing that adds a 10-second pause before launching apps, long-overdue App Lock functionality, Privacy Display for secure app features (e.g., Secure Folder, private albums), and auto-split functionality in DeX mode. You can check out the full changelog in our dedicated article here.

Keen on trying out the beta? You can register via the Samsung Members app (if registration is open in your market) and then check for the update by tapping **Settings > Software update > Download and install**.
