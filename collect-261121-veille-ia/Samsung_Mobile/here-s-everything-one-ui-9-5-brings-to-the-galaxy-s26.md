Samsung has announced One UI 9.5, its next major software update for eligible Galaxy phones and tablets. It is still based on Android 17, similar to One UI 9.0. However, it is a much bigger update with respect to the amount of new features and improvements it brings.

 

And we now know what features it will bring to Galaxy devices. Below is the complete changelog for the One UI 9.5 beta update for the Galaxy S26 series. Note that Samsung will likely add even more features to One UI 9.5 when it debuts on the Galaxy S27 series in early 2027.

 



## One UI 9.5 changelog




- Improved camera controls and zoom bar: Quick controls are now laid out on one screen, so every option is visible without scrolling. The layout of the bottom shutter area has also been improved, providing a cleaner and simpler look.

- Enhanced Pro and Pro video modes: You can now create custom buttons and presets, see lens and zoom levels as focal length, and fine-tune manual sliders. *(Available only on models that support Pro and Pro video modes. Not supported on models with Pro Lite and Pro Video Lite.)*

 

**Lock screen personalization**

 


- Live effect for wallpapers: Add depth to your Lock screen wallpaper. As you tilt your device, elements in the image shift at different depths. *(Supported on flagship S/FE, Z, and tablet models.)*

- More customizable Lock screen clock: You can choose how long the clock with seconds appears on your Lock screen. Choose a timeout of 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, 5 minutes, or 10 minutes from the settings in the upper-right corner.

- Sports animations in Now bar: Animation effects are now available for more sports. Celebrate your favorite team’s goals and victories with special visual effects. You can download these effects in Now bar settings.

 



- Pause before app launch: Turn on Pause before app launch to add a 10-second pause before a selected app or app category opens. The pause helps you reconsider whether you really need to use the app.

 



- App lock: Lock individual apps to keep them private. Locked apps require biometrics or your screen lock (PIN, pattern, or password) to open, and their widgets and notification content are hidden, even when someone has access to your unlocked phone.

- Privacy display for secure app features: Privacy display can now turn on automatically for secure features, including Locked apps, Secret mode in Samsung Browser, private albums in Gallery, and locked notes in Samsung Notes. It also protects the notifications panel and Recent previews from any app set to turn on Privacy display automatically. *(Supported on Galaxy S26 Ultra.)*

- Fingerprint and face recognition at the same time: Fingerprint and face recognition now work at the same time if you’ve registered both and the feature you're using supports both verification types. You no longer need an extra tap to use face verification—just look at the screen or touch the fingerprint sensor to verify your identity.

- Redesigned biometric registration: Fingerprint and face registration screens have been redesigned with a cleaner, more refined look.

 



- Improved document support: You can now open PPTX and DOCX files directly in My Files without installing Microsoft Office or other apps. Document Reader already supports PDF and TXT files.

- Easier storage cleanup: When you manage storage in My Files, you now have the option to delete uncategorized files and files that are in use in the Other category.

 



- Optimized window maximizing: Maximizing a window now fills the entire screen, window controls are now integrated with the status bar at the top of the screen, and the app's background color is visible behind the taskbar at the bottom, providing a more immersive experience.

- Auto split and more snap options: You can now snap windows to all four corners in addition to the top, bottom, left, and right. You can also arrange your running apps into 2, 3, or 4 split-screen windows at once.

 



- Enhanced sleep coaching: You can now start sleep coaching with just 3 days of data from the past two weeks. Check your overall sleep status and get personalized coaching tailored to your sleep patterns and goals.

- Scan meals and log them instantly: Take a single photo of a meal before you eat it and get nutrition details, ingredients, and calorie estimates right away. No need to search for and enter every food you ate. *(Available in the U.S. only.)*

- Mindful notes: Clear your mind and record your thoughts with free writing and guided prompts. Your notes sync seamlessly with Mood check-in to reveal your mood trends at a glance.

- Track your medications worldwide: The Medications tracker is now available globally. You can enter your medications, set personalized reminders, and track your daily intake all in one place.

- Reorder cards: You can now change the order of cards in the Activity, Sleep, Vitals, Mindfulness, and Nutrition tabs. Just drag and drop the cards you use the most to make them easier to find.

 



- Intuitive floating accessibility shortcuts: Access your accessibility features from a floating button that's always available, without the need to select additional options. Visual on and off indicators make it easy to see which features are available.

- Improved hearing aid controls: Adjust your connected hearing aids' volume from the notification panel. You can also choose which sounds from your phone reach your ears by opting out of notifications or ringtones and alarms.

 



- Downloadable ringtones: Download past versions of Over the Horizon, Samsung's signature sound, along with a curated selection of ringtones from past and present Samsung devices. Set any of them as your ringtone or use them for alarms.

 



- Audio brief and Moments no longer available: Audio brief and Moments will no longer be available in Now brief after you upgrade to One UI 9.5.
