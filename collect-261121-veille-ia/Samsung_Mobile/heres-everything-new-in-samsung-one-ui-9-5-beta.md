# Here’s everything new in Samsung One UI 9.5 beta

- Samsung has highlighted new features and tweaks in the One UI 9.5 beta.
- Features in the spotlight include app lock functionality, a Mindful Note feature, and various camera changes.
- The full changelog also lists a ton of other tweaks and additions.

Samsung has just launched the One UI 9.5 beta (here’s how to install it) in select markets, including the US. This release gives Galaxy S26 owners a preview of what’s coming when the stable version eventually lands.

What’s actually new in the One UI 9.5 beta, though? Samsung has confirmed some of these features in a post on its Newsroom blog, but we can also confirm changes via the One UI 9.5 changelog.

Perhaps the most notable addition is the long-overdue appearance of an app lock feature. This lets you lock an app in your app drawer behind a biometric scan or PIN. This differs from Secure Folder, which effectively clones an app and then hides the cloned version. Check out the official screenshot below.

Samsung also confirmed that Pro and Pro Video modes in the camera app now offer customizable controls and presets (see below). In fact, the changelog also mentions the ability to change lens and zoom levels to focal lengths. Furthermore, the default camera app has received improved controls and a zoom bar for more intuitive operation.

Samsung’s blog post also highlights Mindful Note functionality in Samsung Health. This is effectively a journal that offers both free writing and guided prompts. Check out the screenshot below.

Other changes highlighted by Samsung’s blog include a dedicated floating shortcut for frequently used controls and improved hearing aid controls in the notification panel (including volume adjustments and audio output controls).

Samsung’s actual changelog highlights a ton more changes, though. This includes live depth effects on the lock screen wallpaper as you tilt your phone, the option to add a 10-second pause before launching specific apps, Privacy Display for secure app features (e.g., private browsing, private albums), PPTX/DOCX support in My Files, and the ability to search for quick panel controls.

## Samsung One UI 9.5 changelog

### Camera

- **Improved camera controls and zoom bar:** Quick controls are now laid out on one screen, so every option is visible without scrolling. The layout of the bottom shutter area has also been improved, providing a cleaner and simpler look.
- **Enhanced Pro and Pro video modes** : You can now create custom buttons and presets, see lens and zoom levels as focal length, fine-tune manual sliders, and monitor your shot with an improved histogram and audio meter.

### Lock screen personalization

- **Live effect for wallpapers:** Add depth to your Lock screen wallpaper. As you tilt your device, elements in the image shift at different depths.
- **More customizable Lock screen clock:** You can choose how long the clock with seconds appears on your Lock screen. Choose a timeout of 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, 5 minutes, or 10 minutes from the settings in the upper-right corner.
- **Sports animations in Now bar:** Animation effects are now available for more sports. Celebrate your favorite team’s goals and victories with special visual effects. You can download these effects in Now bar settings.

### Digital Wellbeing

- **Pause before app launch:** Turn on Pause before app launch to add a 10-second pause before a selected app or app category opens. The pause helps you reconsider whether you really need to use the app.

### Privacy and security

- **App lock:** Lock individual apps to keep them private. Locked apps require biometrics or your screen lock (PIN, pattern, or password) to open, and their widgets and notification content are hidden, even when someone has access to your unlocked phone.
- **Privacy display for secure app features (Ultra model only):** Privacy display can now turn on automatically for secure features, including Locked apps, Secret mode in Samsung Browser, private albums in Gallery, and locked notes in Samsung Notes. It also protects notifications panel and Recents previews from any app set to turn on Privacy display automatically.
- **Fingerprint and face recognition at the same time:** Fingerprint and face recognition now work at the same time if you’ve registered both and the feature you’re using supports both verification types. You no longer need an extra tap to use face verification. Just look at the screen or touch the fingerprint sensor to verify your identity.
- **Redesigned biometric registration:** Fingerprint and face registration screens have been redesigned with a cleaner, more refined look.

### My Files

- **Improved document support:** You can now open PPTX and DOCX files directly in My Files without installing Microsoft Office or other apps.
Document Reader already supports PDF and TXT files.
- **Easier storage cleanup:** When you manage storage in My Files, you now have the option to delete uncategorized and files that are in use in the Other category.

### Samsung DeX

- **Optimized window maximizing:** Maximizing a window now fills the entire screen, window controls are now integrated with the status bar at the top of the screen, and the apps background color is visible behind the taskbar at the bottom, providing a more immersive experience.
- **Auto-split and more snap options:** You can now snap windows to all four corners in addition to the top, bottom, left, and right. You can also arrange your running apps into 2, 3, or 4 split-screen windows at once.

### Samsung Health

- **Enhanced sleep coaching:** You can now start sleep coaching with just 3 days of data from the past two weeks. Check your overall sleep status and get personalized coaching tailored to your sleep patterns and goals.
- **Mindful notes:** Clear your mind and record your thoughts with free writing and guided prompts. Your notes sync seamlessly with Mood check-in to reveal your mood trends at a glance.
- **Track your medications worldwide:** The Medications tracker is now available globally. You can enter your medications, set personalized reminders, and track your daily intake all in one place.
- **Reorder cards:** You can now change the order of cards in the Activity, Sleep, Vitals, Mindfulness, and Nutrition tabs. Just drag and drop the cards you use the most to make them easier to find.

### Accessibility

- **Intuitive floating accessibility shortcuts:** Access your accessibility features from a floating button that’s always available, without the need to select additional options. Visual on and off indicators make it easy to see which features are available.
- **Improved hearing aid controls:** Adjust your connected hearing aids’ volume from the notification panel. You can also choose which sounds from your phone reach your ears by opting out of notifications or ringtones and alarms.

### Even more improvements

- **Search for Quick panel controls:** When editing the Quick panel, you can search by app name or control name to find the controls you want quickly without scrolling through the full list.
- **Downloadable ringtones:** Download past versions of Over the Horizon, Samsung’s signature sound, along with a curated selection of ringtones from past and present Samsung devices. Set any of them as your ringtone or use them for alarms.

### Other changes

- **Audio brief and Moments no longer available:** Audio brief and Moments will no longer be available in Now brief after you upgrade to One UI 9.5.
