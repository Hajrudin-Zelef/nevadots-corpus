Samsung has started the One UI 9.5 beta for the Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra. The first build went out on November 18. It reached owners in Germany, India, Korea, Poland and the UK. The US is not on the first list. The update weighs between 3.49GB and 3.6GB, depending on the model.

## What is in Beta 1

The camera app gets the biggest change. You can compare camera settings in one view instead of scrolling through menus. The row at the bottom of the screen is also easier to use.

Pro mode and Pro video get finer controls. The shutter speed and frame rate ranges are tuned, and the video audio controls are redone. Samsung DeX gets upgrades. The beta is open to the Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra.

The list also includes App Lock, new lock screen options, new Health tools and changes to digital wellbeing and security. Samsung has not published an official changelog yet, so the details come from early reports.

## What is missing

The agent features from the leaks do not show up in the reports I found. That includes the Personal Agent Core, which is said to run tasks across apps. Earlier leaks tie it to the Galaxy S27 launch in early 2027. It may arrive in a later beta, or not at all on the S26.

That matters if you hope to try Samsung’s answer to Apple’s Siri AI on your current phone. Do not expect it in this build.

## How to join

Open the Samsung Members app and look for the beta banner. Only the S26 series is eligible so far. Beta software can have bugs, so back up your data first and think twice before installing it on your only phone.

## Where it goes from here

The US is the open question. Samsung’s earlier One UI 9 beta reached the US in the first week, so the gap this time stands out. Samsung has added markets with later builds before. For the One UI 8.5 beta, owners in Poland and India waited until the second build, which came two weeks after the first.

Samsung began testing One UI 9.5 in August, earlier than it did for One UI 8.5. It has not given a date for the final release. The Galaxy S27 series, due in early 2027, will probably ship with it. Until then, treat Beta 1 as a preview.
