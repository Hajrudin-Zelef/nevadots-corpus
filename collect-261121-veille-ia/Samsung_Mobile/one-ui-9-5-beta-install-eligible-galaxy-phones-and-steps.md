Samsung opened the One UI 9.5 beta on November 18, 2026, and a One UI 9.5 beta install is now possible on the Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra. You enroll through the Samsung Members app, then download the update from your phone’s settings. The catch: the beta is limited to six countries, and going back to stable takes a full reset.

This guide covers who qualifies, what to do before you install, the exact steps, what you get, and how to undo it. If you only own an older Galaxy, the short answer is to wait.

## Is One UI 9.5 beta available for your Galaxy phone?

Only three phones are in the first wave: the **Galaxy S26**, **Galaxy S26+** and **Galaxy S26 Ultra**. The Galaxy S26 FE is not part of the initial announcement. The Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Fold 8 Ultra are also missing from the roster, and older Galaxy S models are not listed.

Region matters just as much as the model. The beta is open in six markets:

- Germany
- India
- South Korea
- Poland
- The United Kingdom
- The United States

Users elsewhere cannot download it. Samsung released the beta in all of these regions on the same day, which differs from earlier betas that arrived country by country.

Samsung has not said when the beta will reach other models, foldables or markets. If you own a different phone, you will have to wait for an announcement.

One more distinction: the beta is not the final software. The stable One UI 9.5 release comes later. It is built on Android 17, the same base as One UI 9.

## Before you install: backup and risk check

Samsung warns that beta software can contain bugs, behave unexpectedly, or cause data loss. Take that seriously, because the way back is not a simple uninstall (more on that below).

Run through this checklist first:

1. **Back up your data.** Save photos, videos, messages and app data to Samsung Cloud, Google One or a computer. Do not rely on a single copy.
2. **Check storage and Wi-Fi.** The download is roughly 3.5 to 3.6 GB, depending on the report. Make sure you have free space and a stable connection.
3. **Charge the phone.** Start with a healthy battery level so the install does not stall.
4. **Install pending stable updates.** The beta may not show up until your phone is current, and other updates may need to go first.
5. **Think about your main phone.** If you depend on this device for work, banking or two-factor codes, a beta is a poor fit.

## How to join the One UI 9.5 beta in Samsung Members

Enrollment happens in the **Samsung Members** app, not in Settings. Make sure the app is up to date from the Galaxy Store or Play Store before you start.

1. Open **Samsung Members** and sign in with your Samsung account.
2. Look on the home screen for the **One UI Beta** banner or the**Beta program** card.
3. Tap **One UI 9.5 Beta** .
4. Tap **Join** and accept the terms and enrollment prompts.

Labels and banner wording can vary by phone and app version, so look for the general beta entry rather than an exact phrase. Reports also say beta slots are limited. Samsung has not said how many exist or how fast they fill, so enroll sooner rather than later if you plan to join.

If you do not see the banner, the usual causes are the wrong model, an unsupported country, or an outdated Samsung Members app.

## How to download and install the beta update

Joining does not install anything by itself. You still have to pull the update.

1. Open **Settings** .
2. Tap **Software update** .
3. Tap **Download and install** .
4. Wait for the download to finish, then let the phone restart and apply the update.

The update may take a little while to appear after you enroll. If it does not show up, install any other waiting updates first and check again later.

The first boot after installing usually takes longer than normal, so be patient. Do not force a restart or unplug the phone mid-install.

## What’s new in One UI 9.5 beta

The beta is not a full redesign, but it adds some practical features.

### Native App Lock

You can now lock individual apps with biometrics or your screen lock. Locked apps also hide widget and notification content, which helps if you hand your phone to someone briefly or worry about prying eyes. Many people previously needed a third-party tool for this.

### Camera changes

The Camera app’s **Pro** and **Pro Video** modes get more granular controls and a faster zoom interface. If you shoot manually, this is probably the most interesting upgrade.

### Other additions

Reporting also points to health and accessibility tweaks, DeX enhancements, a Mindful Note feature and live wallpaper effects.

### What you lose

Audio brief and Moments no longer appear in Now brief after you upgrade to One UI 9.5. If you use either one, factor that in.

## How to go back to One UI 9 if the beta misbehaves

Returning to the previous stable version takes more effort than leaving a typical app beta. You must withdraw from the beta through Samsung Members, then perform a rollback using **Smart Switch** on a computer. The rollback requires a reset that removes beta-generated data.

That has a few practical consequences:

- Back up before you start, and again before you roll back if you have added new data on the beta.
- Expect to restore your phone from a backup afterward.
- Data created while on the beta may not carry over cleanly.

If the phone is essential to your daily life, consider testing on a spare device or skipping the beta altogether.

## Should you install the beta or wait for stable?

For most people, waiting is the better call. The beta is early software. Stable One UI 9.5 is expected to debut alongside Samsung’s next flagship generation early next year, so the wait is not indefinite.

The trade-off is simple. Installing now gets you App Lock and the camera improvements early. Waiting gets you a more polished build and no reset-based exit plan.

Install the beta only if you are comfortable with a full backup and restore, you live in one of the six supported countries, and ideally you have a second phone to fall back on. Otherwise, let early testers find the bugs first.

## Quick troubleshooting

**I don’t see the beta banner.** Confirm you own an S26, S26+ or S26 Ultra, that your phone is set up for one of the six countries, and that Samsung Members is up to date.

**Can I join from outside the six countries?** Reports say no.

**I joined, but nothing is downloading.** Enrolling does not install the beta. Go to Settings, then Software update, then Download and install. Install any other outstanding updates first.

**Where can I find the full changelog?** Check Samsung’s official One UI beta page in the Samsung Members app, which is the best place for the complete list of changes and known issues.
