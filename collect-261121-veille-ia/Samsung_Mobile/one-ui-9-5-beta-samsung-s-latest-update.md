## What’s new with One UI 9.5

Samsung opened the One UI 9.5 beta for the Galaxy S26 series in all six eligible countries on launch day, breaking an eight-year habit of staggered access. This is the first time in One UI beta history that the program reached every participant country on day one rather than in waves.

The change ends the usual pattern where some markets waited weeks and joined on a later build. SamMobile, which maintains affiliate and sponsored partnerships, reported the shift on November 19, 2026, noting that Samsung still has a long way to go because the beta program remains limited and stable updates continue to reach countries at different times.

Samsung launched its first One UI beta in November 2018. South Korea, Germany, and the US formed the first wave, while China, France, India, Poland, Spain, and the UK were listed for later phases; the Galaxy S9 beta took additional time to reach India. As of November 20 the simultaneous day-one rollout marks a clear departure from that original staggered model.

## Who it’s for

The feature beta is intended for Galaxy S26 series owners in the six eligible countries who want an early look at the updated interface. Historically, the first One UI beta gave Galaxy S9 and S9+ owners the same kind of early access to Samsung’s new interface and Android Pie.

In plain English, this means owners of the newest Galaxy S flagships in those markets can try the software ahead of the stable release, just as S9 owners did years ago when the program began. Think of it like a limited-capacity train that now leaves every station at the same time instead of making some passengers wait for the next service.

Our take is that the day-one availability is a practical improvement for Galaxy S26 users in the participating countries, yet the overall beta program is still constrained and stable updates remain staggered. What to watch is whether future builds continue the simultaneous approach or revert to phased country access.
