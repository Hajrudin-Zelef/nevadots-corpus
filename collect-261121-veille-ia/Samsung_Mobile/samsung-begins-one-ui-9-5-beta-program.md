## **What New Creative Capabilities Does One UI 9.5 Bring?**

One UI 9.5 makes advanced creative features more accessible, giving users precise control over their shots.

**Enhanced Pro and Pro video modes** make creative control more intuitive. Customizable controls and presets let users tailor the shooting experience, and more detailed monitoring tools enable precise fine-tuning.

The default camera also offers **improved camera controls and zoom bar** that consolidate key settings on one screen, letting users adjust settings and return to shooting without navigating multiple menus.

## **What Improvements Are Coming to Samsung Health?**

Samsung Health adds **Mind Note,** a space to reflect on thoughts and feelings through free writing or guided prompts.


It also introduces **Medications**, where users can add prescriptions, vitamins, or supplements, then set personalized reminders and track their adherence rates at a glance.

## **How Does One UI 9.5 Make Accessibility Features Easier to Use?**

One UI 9.5 streamlines accessibility with a **dedicated** **floating shortcut** keeping essential controls within reach. **Improved hearing aid controls** can adjust volume from the notification panel and toggle which device sounds reach the hearing aid, including notifications, calls, and alarms.

## **How is One UI 9.5 Strengthening Privacy Protection?**

One UI 9.5 introduces **App Lock**, giving users greater privacy control. Individual apps can be secured with biometrics or a screen lock, and notification content and widgets stay hidden even when others have access to the device.
