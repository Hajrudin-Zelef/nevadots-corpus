On 18 November 2026 Samsung made the One UI 9.5 beta available to owners of the Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra in six countries – Germany, India, South Korea, Poland, the United Kingdom and the United States.

The beta adds a full‑featured Pro camera mode, including manual shutter speed, ISO and white‑balance controls, as well as a zoom bar that places key settings on a single screen.

Samsung Health receives two new modules – Mindful Note, a free‑form journal for mood tracking, and Medications, which lets users log prescriptions, vitamins and set reminders.

Accessibility improvements include a floating shortcut for frequently used controls and a volume‑routing option for users of hearing aids.

A new App Lock feature lets users protect individual apps with fingerprint, face or screen lock, hiding notifications and widgets while the app remains locked.

The beta does not disclose the underlying Android version, though previous One UI 9 beta was built on Android 17, and no Galaxy AI or call‑recording features appear in the current list.

The download size ranges from 3.49 GB to 3.6 GB; participants must enrol via the Samsung Members app and install through Settings → Software update, with a backup recommended as the software is test‑level.

Kenyan Galaxy S26 owners are not eligible for this beta, as Kenya has not been included in the rollout of the last three Samsung beta programs.

Samsung typically releases the final One UI 9.5 update alongside the next‑generation Galaxy S27 early next year, with older models receiving the update later.
