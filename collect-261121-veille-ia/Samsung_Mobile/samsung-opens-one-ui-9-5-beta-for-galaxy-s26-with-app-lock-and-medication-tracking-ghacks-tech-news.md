Samsung has opened the One UI 9.5 beta program for the Galaxy S26 series. The beta gives users an early look at the next version of Samsung's Android interface, less than two months after One UI 9 started rolling out to the Galaxy S26 in late September.

For the first time, the beta opens in six countries at once: Germany, India, Korea, Poland, the UK and the US. Samsung usually starts with the US, the UK and Korea and adds India and Germany later.

## What's New in One UI 9.5

Samsung lists these new features in the first beta:

- App Lock, which locks individual apps behind biometric authentication or the screen lock and hides their notification content and widgets while they are locked
- enhanced Pro and Pro video camera modes with customizable controls and presets
- a zoom bar and key camera settings on one screen in the default camera
- Mindful Note in Samsung Health, for journaling through free writing or guided prompts
- Medications in Samsung Health, for adding prescriptions, vitamins and supplements, setting reminders and tracking whether they were taken
- a floating shortcut button for frequently used controls
- hearing aid volume controls in the notification panel, with an option to choose which sounds go to the hearing aids

App Lock is the biggest change for privacy. Until now, Galaxy users had to move apps into Secure Folder to protect them with a fingerprint or face scan. One UI 9.5 is still based on Android 17.

## How to Join the One UI 9.5 Beta

Galaxy S26 series owners in the six countries can join through the Samsung Members app:

1. Open the Samsung Members app and tap the One UI 9.5 beta banner.
2. Enroll in the program. Slots are limited.
3. Check for a software update on the Galaxy S26 and download the beta.

Early user reports say the beta arrives in two parts: a 3.6 GB update first, then a second update of about 540 MB after the phone restarts. Installing the beta does not erase data, but going back to the stable version resets the phone and wipes it.

First betas usually have bugs, and Samsung typically releases more beta builds before the stable version. The stable release of One UI 9.5 is expected to debut on the Galaxy S27 series early next year before reaching the Galaxy S26 and other flagships. Samsung has not announced which other Galaxy devices will join the beta program.
