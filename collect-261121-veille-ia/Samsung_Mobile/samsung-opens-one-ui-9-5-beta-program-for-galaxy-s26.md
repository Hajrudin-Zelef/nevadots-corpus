The new software brings design changes and new features.

Abhijeet Mishra / SamMobile

We have been hearing about Samsung’s One UI 9.5 update for quite some time now. In fact, leaks and reports about the new software started surfacing even before the company released the stable version of One UI 9.0.

Now, Samsung has officially opened the One UI 9.5 beta program for the Galaxy S26 series, giving users an opportunity to try out the new software before the brand releases its stable build. It brings many UI changes and new features that we will discuss in a moment.

Galaxy S26 owners in supported countries can sign up for the beta program via the Samsung Members app, then download the first beta update from the **Settings > Software update** menu.


The One UI 9.5 update brings several improvements to the camera experience. Quick controls are now easier to access, with the layout of the bottom shutter area also receiving improvements.

Samsung has also added new controls to Pro and Pro Video modes, allowing users to customize buttons and presets, lens tones, zoom levels, and monitor various settings through an improved histogram and audio meter.

Other visual changes include live effects for wallpapers, more lock screen clock customization options, and new sports animations in the Now Bar.

Samsung has also introduced several privacy, security, and digital wellbeing improvements. The new App Lock feature lets users protect individual apps with a PIN, pattern, password, or biometrics, while locked apps also hide their notifications and widgets.

Privacy Display can now turn on automatically for secure apps, and fingerprint and face recognition work simultaneously, eliminating the need for an additional tap during verification.

Users can also pause before app launch to get a 10-second break before opening selected apps or app categories. The update additionally brings improved biometric registration screens.

There are several improvements to Samsung’s productivity and health features as well. My Files now supports direct editing of DOCX and PPTX documents and offers an easier way to organize storage. Samsung DeX gets optimized window maximizing and more window snapping options, making multitasking easier.

Samsung Health receives enhanced sleep coaching, mindful notes for recording thoughts and mood, a worldwide rollout of its Medications tracker, and a rearranged Activity, Sleep, Vitals, Mindfulness, and Nutrition cards.

One UI 9.5 also introduces new accessibility shortcuts, improved hearing aid controls, and search functionality for Quick Panel controls. Users can download additional ringtones from Samsung’s selection of past and present Galaxy device sounds. However, Samsung is discontinuing Audio Brief and Moments in Now Brief with this update.
