Samsung Electronics said on the 29th during its third-quarter 2026 earnings conference call that DRAM prices rose by about 20% and NAND flash prices climbed in the high-10% range, led by server products.

Samsung Electronics explained that strong third-quarter demand related to artificial intelligence (AI) sharply increased demand for AI infrastructure products such as high bandwidth memory (HBM) and server solid-state drives (SSD). In response, it greatly expanded HBM4 sales and actively met demand for server SSDs.

In the third quarter, DRAM bit shipments increased by the low-to-mid single digits from the previous quarter, and NAND rose by the mid-single digits.

Samsung Electronics projected that in the fourth quarter, memory demand will continue to grow on expanded AI infrastructure investment and the launch of new graphics processing units (GPU) and central processing units (CPU). It especially saw a continued memory supply shortage as storage demand rapidly increases with the spread of cloud-based AI agent services.
