## 
        Samsung SDS begins construction of 60 MW AI data centre
        

  
        Samsung SDS has begun construction of a dedicated AI data centre in Gumi, North Gyeongsang Province, South Korea, with an initial power capacity of 60 MW and provision to expand to 120 MW. The facility is scheduled for completion by the end of 2028. The development occupies a site of approximately 46,280 square metres in Gumi's Gongdan-dong industrial district. Construction began following municipal approval in August 2026. Samsung SDS announced an investment of KRW 427.3 billion in the project in January.
