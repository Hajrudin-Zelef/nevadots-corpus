# Samsung SDS begins construction of 60MW AI data centre in Gumi

See how this story connects
Samsung SDS held a groundbreaking ceremony on 2 November 2026 for an AI data centre on the former Samsung Electronics Campus 1 site in Gumi, North Gyeongsang Province, South Korea. The building will stand on a site of about 47,000 square metres, with one basement level and five above-ground floors, and 60MW-class power infrastructure for generative AI and cloud demand. Completion is targeted for December 2028, with full operation from March 2029.

### Why it matters

Gyeongbuk Province and Gumi City expect the project to create jobs in construction, operations and facility management, support AI and cloud training with local universities, and create synergies with regional construction, electronics and semiconductor firms.
