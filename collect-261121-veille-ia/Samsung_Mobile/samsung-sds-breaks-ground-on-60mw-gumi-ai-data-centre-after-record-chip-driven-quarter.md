## Executive brief

In Q3 2026 Samsung Electronics made an operating profit of KRW 107.4 trillion, about $80bn. Its chip division earned KRW 106.5 trillion of that (Samsung), and its HBM supply for 2027 is already sold out (BigGo Finance). The FT reports that Samsung now wants to repeat that success across the rest of the group, with data centres the next target. Only the FT's excerpt was reviewed. The clearest concrete step is Samsung SDS's 60MW AI data centre in Gumi, which can grow to 120MW. It broke ground on 2 November and mainly serves Samsung's own robot and factory-automation work (Seoul Economic Daily).
