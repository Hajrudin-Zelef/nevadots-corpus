GUMI — Samsung SDS (018260.KS) broke ground on the 2nd on an artificial intelligence data center in Gumi, North Gyeongsang Province.

North Gyeongsang Province and the city of Gumi said the data center will be built on the site of Samsung Electronics' (005930.KS) former Gumi Plant 1 in Gongdan-dong, Gumi.

The facility will span about 47,000 square meters and carry 60 megawatts of power infrastructure to serve generative AI and cloud computing demand. Operations are targeted to begin in March 2029.

The groundbreaking turns into actual investment a memorandum of understanding signed in January by the province, the city and Samsung SDS at CES 2026 in Las Vegas.

Officials expect Samsung SDS's capabilities in designing, building and operating data centers to combine with Gumi's electronics and semiconductor manufacturing base, accelerating the AI transition at factories and the recruitment of related companies.

The province and the city plan to widen opportunities for local construction firms and suppliers of materials and equipment during the build, and to strengthen training programs for AI and cloud specialists in partnership with local universities.

"This is the starting point for North Gyeongsang to redraw the industrial map of the AI era," North Gyeongsang Governor Lee Cheol-woo said at the groundbreaking ceremony. "We will concentrate our capabilities on accelerating the AI transition of local industry and on building an AI industry ecosystem in North Gyeongsang through specialist training and follow-on investment by AI-related companies."

Gumi Mayor Kim Jang-ho said the data center project had served as a catalyst. "The construction of Samsung SDS's AI data center became the seed money that led to Samsung Group's large-scale investment plan for Gumi and to the designation of a robotics-specialized complex," he said. "The first shovel on the AI data center will be a solid cornerstone for the development of future industries."
