Samsung has officially started the One UI 9.5 beta programme for the Galaxy S26 series, and India is among the first countries to get it. The update brings a native App Lock, better camera controls, new wellness tools in Samsung Health and a range of smaller improvements.

## Samsung One UI 9.5 Beta Eligible Devices and Countries

Samsung announced the One UI 9.5 beta programme on November 18, 2026. For now, itâs available only for the Galaxy S26 series in India, the US, the UK, South Korea, Germany and Poland.

The beta comes less than two months after the stable One UI 9 update reached the Galaxy S26 series in September 2026. If you need a refresher on what One UI 9 brought, check out our list of the best Samsung One UI 9 features.

## How to Join the Samsung One UI 9.5 Beta

Joining the One UI 9.5 beta programme is simple:

- Open the **Samsung Members app** on your Galaxy S26.
- Tap the **One UI 9.5 Beta banner** and join the beta.
- Go to ***Settings -> Software update -> Download and install*** to get the beta.

According to early user reports, the first beta update is about 3.6 GB, followed by a second update of about 540 MB after you restart your phone. Beta slots are limited, so itâs worth signing up early if youâre keen to try it.

A word of caution from my end: the first beta may have bugs, so itâs best not to install it on your main phone unless youâre comfortable with that.

Installing the beta wonât wipe your data, but rolling back to the stable version will reset your phone, so take a backup first as well. Take it from someone who's had bad experiences in this regard.

## Samsung One UI 9.5 Features

One UI 9.5 is one of the most feature-packed updates released for Samsung Galaxy phones in recent times. So, understandably, a lot is going on here:

### App Lock and Better Privacy

One of the most useful additions is a built-in App Lock. You can now lock individual apps using your fingerprint, face or screen lock, and locked apps will hide their notification content and widgets too.

On supported models, the Privacy Display feature can also turn on automatically when you enter your PIN, pattern or password. Moreover, it now works with private Gallery albums, locked Samsung Notes and selected apps.

Fingerprint and face unlock have also been improved. You can now scan your fingerprint directly on the sensor without an extra on-screen prompt.

### Upgraded Pro Camera Modes

Camera enthusiasts get enhanced Pro and Pro Video modes, with customisable controls and presets for different shooting conditions. You also get more precise shutter speed and frame rate controls.

The default camera controls have also been simplified, with key settings like the zoom bar grouped together on one screen. Note that Pro Lite and Pro Video Lite modes wonât get these upgrades.

### New Samsung Health Tools

Samsung Health gets two new features.

Mindful Note is a journaling tool that lets you reflect on your thoughts and feelings through free writing or guided prompts, similar to Appleâs Journal app and Googleâs Pixel Journal.

The new Medications feature lets you log prescriptions, vitamins and supplements, set reminders and track whether youâre taking them on time.

Samsung Health is also getting sleep coaching based on your last few days of sleep data, along with rearrangeable health cards.

### Lock Screen and Other Changes

The lock screen gets **live depth effects**, where wallpaper elements move at different speeds as you tilt your phone, along with more Now Bar animations, including live sports scores. According to a changelog shared by tipster Ice Universe on X, One UI 9.5 also brings:

- An app opening delay of up to 10 seconds in Digital Wellbeing, to help you cut down on mindless scrolling
- DOCX and PPTX previews in My Files, without needing Microsoft Office
- Expanded snap layouts in Samsung DeX for two, three or four apps
- A floating accessibility shortcut and improved hearing aid controls
- Faster settings search
- Downloadable classic "Over the Horizon" wallpapers from older Galaxy phones

Oh, by the way, Samsung is removing the Audio Broadcast and Enhanced Audio Services features with One UI 9.5.
