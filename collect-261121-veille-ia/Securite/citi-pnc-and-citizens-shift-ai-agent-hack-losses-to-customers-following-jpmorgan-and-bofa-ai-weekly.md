## Summary

        
          Citi, PNC and Citizens updated terms on November 18, 2026 to hold customers responsible for anything third-party AI agents do in their accounts — including activity after credentials are leaked or the agent itself is hacked. Citi carves out an exception for AI agents Citi provides directly. JPMorgan and Bank of America adopted similar policies last month, making five major US banks now pushing agent-hack liability onto end users.
