When Sora 2 arrived in late September 2025, it was one of the most talked-about AI apps of the year. You typed a sentence, and it produced a short video with matching dialogue, sound effects, and music. Most of the world could only watch those clips spread on social media, though, because the app launched only in the US and Canada.

If you’re trying to access Sora 2 outside the US and Canada today, the location is no longer the obstacle. OpenAI closed Sora in 2026, first the app and website, then the API that developers used in their own tools. No VPN can fix that, because there’s no Sora left to connect to, in North America or anywhere else. Here’s what happened to it, and what to use now.

**The Sora 2 app never left North America and Asia.** It started as an invitation-only iPhone app and added a handful of countries over the following weeks:

- **September 30, 2025:** the iPhone app launched in the US and Canada, with access by invitation code.
- **October 6, 2025:** OpenAI released Sora 2 to developers as a preview in its API.
- **November 2025:** Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam joined, and an Android app went live in the same seven countries.

Europe, the UK, Australia, and India never received the app. People there could still use the original Sora on the web through a paid ChatGPT plan, and OpenAI’s support team confirmed in October 2025 that only the Sora 2 app was limited to the US and Canada. The UK’s own history with Sora is in our guide to Sora in the UK.

**The shutdown was announced on March 24, 2026**, and it happened in three steps:

- **April 26, 2026:** the app and the website closed in every country.
- **Late June 2026:** Microsoft removed Sora video generation from Microsoft 365 Copilot.
- **September 24, 2026:** the API and the Sora 2 models were switched off.

OpenAI offered no detailed explanation. Reports pointed to the cost of operating Sora, estimated at about $1 million a day, and to OpenAI’s shift toward products for businesses. Interest had also faded: Sora’s users peaked near one million worldwide and later fell below half that.

Automation pipelines built on Sora have to move to a different video model. These companies sell developer access to their own models:

Each company keeps its own list of supported countries and its own pricing, so check both before you rebuild a workflow around one.

### Is Sora 2 available outside the US and Canada?

No. OpenAI shut Sora 2 down worldwide, so it isn't available in any country. Before closing, the app spread from North America to parts of Asia but never made it to Europe.

### Which countries supported Sora 2?

The Sora 2 app supported the US and Canada at launch, then added Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam. No European country was ever on the list.

### Can the Sora 2 API still be used?

No. The Sora 2 API is gone for developers worldwide. Pipelines now require a different video model, and Google, Runway, Luma AI, Kling, and MiniMax sell API access to theirs.

### Can a VPN unblock Sora 2?

No. Sora 2 no longer operates anywhere, so a US or Canadian IP address only leads to a shutdown notice. A VPN still helps with AI tools that launch in the US first.

### How can old Sora videos be saved?

Sora's sunset page still has an Export button that sends a download link by email. Videos left there are lost for good when OpenAI's final export period is over.

### What is the best Sora 2 alternative?

Google Gemini is the closest replacement for most people, creating short clips with sound from text or images. Runway is built for professional editing, and Kling offers detailed camera and motion control.
