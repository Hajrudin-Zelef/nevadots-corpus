## What is Sora 2?

Sora 2 is OpenAI’s most recent AI video generator. It was released in September 2025, and it effortlessly creates realistic videos from text prompts and images.

Sora features lifelike motion, synchronized audio, and a unique “cameos” feature that lets you insert personalized digital avatars of yourself. It generates videos with multiple shots and consistent scenes for a high level of control over camera angles, style, and scene composition.

Overall, Sora is designed for fast video creation, making it ideal for marketing, education, social media, storytelling, and personalized content.

### Sora vs. Sora 2

When the original Sora was announced in February 2024, it was impressive but somewhat limited in its capabilities. By the time Sora went public in December 2024, competitors had already caught up.

However, Sora 2 is different. OpenAI themselves calls it their “GPT-3.5 moment” for video.

The original Sora was more like a proof of concept. It demonstrated that AI could generate a video that actually looked real. Object permanence began to take effect, which meant things didn’t just disappear randomly.

However, it had issues. Videos were silent, physics got weird, and access was minimal.

### What Makes Sora 2 Unique

Unlike the original Sora, Sora 2 introduced synchronized audio generation right out of the box (dialogue, sound effects, and background music) that match what’s happening on screen. This was a big improvement because adding sound to AI-generated video is always a separate headache.

The physics improvements were also impressive. In older models, if someone missed a basketball shot, the ball would teleport into the hoop. Sora 2 allows things to fail naturally, as in the ball rebounds off the backboard as it should.

### The Technology Behind Sora 2

Sora 2 uses a diffusion transformer architecture. This means that it breaks video generation down into small steps, processing both the visual content and the timing simultaneously.

It also understands context through natural language processing. The model maintains consistency across frames and automatically generates realistic lighting and textures.

Videos can be up to 25 seconds long and reach 1080p resolution, making the quality per second impressive. You can work in different aspect ratios (16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok, etc.), and there’s a “Cameos” feature where you can literally insert yourself into videos after a single recording.

### Why This Actually Matters

The reason OpenAI calls this their “GPT-3.5 moment” is because it represents a shift to an actually useful tool. GPT-3.5 was when ChatGPT became reliable enough for everyday use. Sora 2 is trying to be that for video (reliable enough that you can actually build a workflow around it).

But is it perfect? Nope. It still makes mistakes, sometimes generates unrealistic movements, and the 25-second limit is restrictive.

However, it can go from a text prompt to a polished video with sound in minutes. That’s the kind of leap that changes how content gets made.

### Step 2: Continue with ChatGPT

Next, I hit “Continue with ChatGPT.”

### Step 3: Add a Profile Picture & Display Name

After signing in to ChatGPT, I chose my profile name and image.

### Step 4: Connect Your Contacts

Next, I was asked to connect my contacts in case I wanted to find friends already on Sora. Feel free to skip this.

### Step 5: Get Started

From there, I hit “Get started.”

### Step 6: Record Yourself

After granting Sora access to my camera and microphone, I began recording myself and saying the numbers on the screen.

Sora only took one minute to process my recording.

### Step 7: Adjust Your Settings

Next, Sora asked me who could use my recording:

- Only me
- People I approve
- Mutuals
- Everyone

I kept it on “Only me.”

I could also choose my Cameo preferences. This included my preferred pronouns, how the cameo looks to others, and how my cameo can’t be used.

Once everything was in place, I hit “Done.”

### Step 8: Create a Video

Immediately, I was taken to a TikTok-like content feed of “AI Slop.” I hit the “+” on the bottom to start creating my own.

### Step 9: Describe Your Video

I selected my cameo (the one of myself I’d just created) and described the video I wanted to see:

Show natural lighting in the first half and vibrant sci-fi glow in the second. Use detailed textures, dynamic reflections, soft shadows, and subtle particle effects. End on a wide shot showing both timelines blended together for a moment before the future fully takes over. High realism.”

Immediately, Sora 2 began creating a draft.
