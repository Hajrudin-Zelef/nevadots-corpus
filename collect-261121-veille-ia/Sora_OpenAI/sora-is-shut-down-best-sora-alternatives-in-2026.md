## FAQ

### Is Sora still available?

No. The app and website closed on April 26, 2026, and the Sora 2 models left the OpenAI API on September 24, 2026. Only the export page remains.

### Can I export my Sora videos?

Yes, at sora.chatgpt.com/sunset: click Export and wait for the email. Do it soon, because OpenAI will permanently delete Sora data after any final export window.

### What replaced Sora?

Nothing: OpenAI names no replacement, and leftover credits only work in Codex, a coding tool. For video, switch to a provider such as Google Veo, Kling or Runway.

### What is the best free Sora alternative?

For Sora-style clips with sound, Google Veo: 50 free Flow credits a day cover five Veo 3.1 Lite clips, although free users may be blocked at peak hours. PixVerse and Kling also refill free credits daily, with limits.

### Which Sora alternative is best for long videos?

For generated footage, Kling 4.0 and Pika (both up to 30 seconds). For finished videos of several minutes, InVideo AI or Zebracat (2 or 5 minutes depending on the plan), which add stock footage, voiceover and subtitles.

## Bottom line

Sora is gone: no app, no API, no successor, only an export page.
