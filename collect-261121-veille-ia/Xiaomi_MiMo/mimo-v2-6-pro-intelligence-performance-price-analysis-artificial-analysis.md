# MiMo-V2.6-Pro: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis

### Model summary

MiMo-V2.6-Pro is amongst the leading models in intelligence and well priced when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also notably slow per task and somewhat verbose. The model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

MiMo-V2.6-Pro scores 30 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 23). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 131M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 101M.

Pricing for MiMo-V2.6-Pro is $0.43 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $0.53) and $0.87 per 1M output tokens (competitively priced, median: $2.10). On average, it costs $0.26 per task to evaluate MiMo-V2.6-Pro on the Intelligence Index.

At 36.7 minutes per Intelligence Index task, MiMo-V2.6-Pro is notably slow compared with the median of 15.0 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
